# Project Chief — Product One-Pager

**Category:** Private Chief of Staff  
**Price:** A$69/month founding cohort (14-day trial)  
**Buyer:** High-agency founders, executives, consultants, operators

## The problem

High performers drown in commitments, follow-ups, and meeting prep. Chatbots wait to be asked. Task apps require manual entry. Neither verifies that work actually landed in Gmail, Calendar, or social channels.

## The product

Project Chief is a **local-first personal node** that:

1. **Observes** connected sources on your device (mail, calendar, phone, SMS, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X).
2. **Prepares** decisions with evidence — not essays.
3. **Asks** before consequential mutations.
4. **Executes** only within explicit permission boundaries.
5. **Verifies** external state before marking work done.
6. **Records** an auditable local receipt.

## Gamified progression (sellable differentiator)

Verified work earns **XP**. Daily **streaks** compound. **Ten achievements** unlock as Chief proves outcomes:

- First Move, Trusted Operator, Delegation Master
- Week Warrior, Relentless (streaks)
- Time Liberator, Capacity Multiplier
- Inbox Zero, Word Keeper, Meeting Master

Five **Chief levels**: Associate → Deputy Chief → Chief of Staff → Executive Chief → Master Chief.

Progress is computed **on device**. No public leaderboard. No cloud content.

## Trust architecture

Personal context stays on user-controlled devices. The service cloud handles account, billing, device keys, and content-free telemetry only.
