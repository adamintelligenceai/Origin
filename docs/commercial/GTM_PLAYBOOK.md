# GTM Playbook — Project Chief Founding Launch

## Positioning

**Category:** Private Chief of Staff  
**Enemy:** Chatbots that wait to be asked; task apps that require manual entry  
**Wedge:** Verified agent actions + gamified progression on a privacy-first node

## ICP (first 100 users)

1. Founders (10–200 person companies)
2. Independent consultants / advisors
3. Executives with high commitment surface (mail + calendar + social)
4. Referrals from founding cohort

## Message hierarchy

1. **Wake up with less to do** (outcome)
2. **Approve, then verify** (mechanism)
3. **Chief progression** (retention hook)
4. **Private by architecture** (trust)

## Channels (prioritized)

| Priority | Channel | Asset |
|----------|---------|-------|
| 1 | Founder LinkedIn | 60s demo video + progression screenshot |
| 2 | Direct outreach | One-pager + calendar link |
| 3 | Executive communities | Slack/Discord intros with demo loop |
| 4 | Referral | 1 month credit for referred founding user |
| 5 | Product Hunt | Only after 10+ retained weekly users |

Avoid initially: broad paid social, lifetime deals, free mass launch.

## Launch sequence (14 days)

### Days 1–3: Internal dogfood
- Run quality gates
- Approve 5+ decisions in desktop build
- Capture Progress tab + achievement unlock screenshots

### Days 4–7: Founding cohort (5–10 users)
- Personal onboarding call (15 min)
- Connect Gmail + Calendar (readonly)
- First verified action within 24h

### Days 8–10: Content
- Publish LinkedIn demo (Today → Verified → XP)
- Post `/progress` marketing page
- Share one-pager PDF from `docs/commercial/`

### Days 11–14: Scale founding invites
- Open waitlist batches of 10
- Stripe test → live keys when counsel clears terms
- Track: activation (first verify), D7 streak, achievement unlock rate

## Metrics (content-free telemetry only)

- `app_open`
- `briefing_viewed`
- `decision_approved`
- `action_verified`
- Local: XP, streak, achievements (never sent to cloud)
