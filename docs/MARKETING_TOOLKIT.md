## Demo script (60 seconds)

"Most AI assistants wait for you to remember what to ask. Project Chief starts from the opposite direction.

Overnight it reviewed changes in my email and calendar on my own device. This morning it found three things that need me: a commitment due tomorrow, a meeting missing the document I asked for, and a scheduling conflict.

It has already drafted the follow-up and proposed the calendar change. I approve here.

Now it executes the action, checks the external system again, and only then marks it Verified.

Every action gets a receipt: what happened, why, what source triggered it, what permission allowed it, and whether an external AI provider was used.

The goal is simple: wake up with less to remember and less to do."

## First acquisition channels

Prioritise:
1. founder-led LinkedIn content
2. short product demos
3. executive/consultant/founder communities
4. referral from founding users
5. product-hunt style launch only after retention

- broad Meta/TikTok paid acquisition
- generic SEO farms
- lifetime deals
- free consumer mass launch
