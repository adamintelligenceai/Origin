## 1. Single job-to-be-done

**When** a Finance Director or Head of AP is told to “put AI into Accounts Payable,”  
**they want** a governed way to design, pilot, and measure agents on the stack they already have,  
**so they can** start Monday with a named first agent at L0, a control trail, and a promotion rule —  
**without** buying another ERP, turning on autonomous payments, or buying a prompt pack.

**Economic buyer:** Finance Director / Head of AP / Finance Transformation Director in a 200–10,000+ employee organisation with complex AP (hundreds to tens of thousands of invoices per month), ERP, exceptions, and audit pressure.

**User of the files:** AP Manager, Transformation Manager, Process Excellence, Finance Systems, Internal Audit observer.

**Friday → Monday test:** Purchased Friday. Monday 09:00 they can appoint owners, pick one invoice slice, and run Intake + Duplicate at L0.

## 2. What this product is / is not

**Is:** An implementation-ready operating system: agent charters, exception taxonomy, governance, controls, KPIs, process method, testing/shadow/pilot, business-case model, workshop pack, and a productised Blueprint service.

**Is not:** A ChatGPT pack, ebook, screenshot gallery, theoretical AI report, consulting-jargon PDF, autonomous payment system, accounting system, ERP replacement, fraud-detection product, compliance certification, or SaaS at launch.

**Category sentence (locked):**  
Evidence Room does not replace the AP stack. It helps organisations design the agent layer that operates across it.

## 5. Evidence protocol (non-negotiable)

1. Maintain `09_RESEARCH/RESEARCH_LEDGER.md` with: claim, figure, source, date, URL, exact context, independence, whether used in a customer product.  
2. Customer-facing products may cite **only** ledgered independent (or clearly labelled vendor-originated) facts.  
3. Do not invent statistics. Do not launder vendor blogs as “industry average.”  
4. Separate: independent evidence / vendor claims / ILLUSTRATIVE examples / Evidence Room frameworks.  
5. Software-adopter research (e.g. Hackett touchless rates) must be labelled as such — never as Evidence Room performance.  
6. ACME Manufacturing is the only worked example. Always **ILLUSTRATIVE**.

**Ledgered facts available at lock (20 Sep 2026):**

- APQC, 16 Mar 2026: ~$0.38 vs ~$0.92 AP cost per $1,000 revenue (top vs bottom). Independent.  
- Deloitte Finance Trends 2026, 8 Oct 2025: 63% finance teams fully deployed AI; 21% report clear measurable ROI; 14% using fully integrated AI agents. Independent professional-services research.  
- Hackett Group, 19 Nov 2025: evaluated AP-solution customers averaged 60% touchless; ≥30% touchless associated with 3.5× AP productivity; cycle times improved 59% post-implementation. Independent research firm, software-adopter context.  
- Lemon Squeezy pricing page, retrieved 20 Sep 2026: 5% + 50¢; MoR.

If a number is not on the ledger, it does not ship.

## 6. Architecture locks

**16 agents** (names and jobs locked). Orchestrator sequences; specialists decide within charters.

**Human classes that never leave humans:** payment release, vendor bank-change approval, policy exceptions, legal disputes.

**Autonomy:** L0 Observe → L1 Recommend → L2 Prepare → L3 Execute within guardrails → L4 Managed autonomy. Default is L0. Promotion is per agent × legal entity × invoice slice. One increment at a time. Evidence pack required.

**Hand-off is a packet**, not a chat. Required fields include `case_id`, document keys, entity, vendor, from/to, autonomy applied, decision, confidence rule, `evidence_refs`, exceptions[], human_required, cost envelope.

**Agents cannot be Accountable** on a RACI. A named human is.

**Agent 10** is not a fraud product. **Agent 12** never releases payment. **Agent 05** never invents a goods receipt. **Agent 08** send-gate defaults to human.

**ERP-agnostic.** Map fields. Do not depend on one vendor.

## 7. Page test (delete if fail)

Every page must answer at least one:

1. What should I do?  
2. How should I do it?  
3. Who owns it?  
4. What can go wrong?  
5. How do I control it?  
6. How do I measure it?  
7. What evidence proves it works?

If none, remove it.

2. Could a Finance Transformation leader take the Team Edition into a workshop **tomorrow** and run a credible AP AI session?  
3. Does the buyer immediately understand this is an **operating system for Finance agents**, not a prompt bundle?
