What you receive

A document set you can take to a controller, a CIO, an auditor, or a vendor. Typical contents (scoped in the SOW):

  • Live-process map for the named slice (we will not invent it)
  • Agent (or small set) that should exist — and those that should not
  • Fences, escalations, stop conditions
  • Control and evidence requirements
  • Operating measures and a 30/60/90 reading plan
  • Go / no-go recommendation — no-go is a successful Blueprint
  • Autonomy register draft (rows, not a live system)

You do not receive a bot, a model deployment, ERP configuration, or a change to your bank mandate.

What you do not receive

  • Implementation of Coupa, SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Tipalti, Medius, or any suite
  • Legal, tax, accounting, or audit advice
  • A savings, ROI, fraud, or compliance engagement
  • Autonomous payments
  • Attendance at your Team workshop unless the SOW says so (default: design conversations only)
  • A retainer. When the Blueprint is delivered, the product is complete unless a separate agreement is signed
  • A licence to republish the Evidence Room method as your product
  • Professional or Team zips, unless you also bought them

Who should apply

Most of these should be true:

  • You can name the legal entity, the system of record, and the invoice class
  • A senior finance owner will read the work
  • A toolkit would be rewritten so heavily you would not use it as shipped
  • You want a designed layer before a vendor statement of work
  • You can tolerate a recommendation not to deploy an agent

Do not apply if you want us to “just set up the AI,” if you need a guaranteed business case, or if you need a systems integrator’s hours.

How it runs

  1. You submit the intake questionnaire.
  2. We accept, decline, or propose Team instead. Decline is normal.
  3. If accepted, you pay the agreed fee (hidden Lemon Squeezy link or invoice — counsel may prefer invoice if this is characterised as a service).
  4. Two to four working conversations and one document cycle (see fulfilment workflow).
  5. You receive the pack. Internal use for the named entity. Our unused library and method remain ours.

Typical elapsed time: two to four weeks of calendar, not of consulting days. If we cannot schedule the owner, we pause; we do not invent the map.

Northline as a subject (fictional — not a client)

A Blueprint for Northline Industrial Group would name two plants, SAP NL10 plus a refusal to merge NetSuite Pacific into the same fence, PO invoices under a stated threshold, a Match-and-flag agent, human release, three controls, three measures. It would refuse an agent that issues payment instructions. It would tell the CFO what would be read in 30 days — and what would not be claimed.

That document is the product. Northline has not paid for one. Neither have you, until you apply and we accept.
