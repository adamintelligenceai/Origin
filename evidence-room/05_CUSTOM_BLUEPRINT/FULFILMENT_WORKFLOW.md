# Custom Blueprint — fulfilment workflow

**Evidence Room · Internal**  
**Version:** 1.0 · September 2026  
**Do not ship this file in a customer zip.**  
**Product:** Scoped design. Complete when the pack is delivered. No retainer.

This is how we keep Custom from becoming unpaid consulting, a silent implementation, or a savings engagement.

---

## 1. States

| State | Meaning | Customer sees |
|---|---|---|
| `received` | Intake in inbox | Auto-ack: not a booking |
| `screening` | We read the paragraph and owners | Silence OK for 5 business days |
| `declined` | Team / wait / out of scope | Written decline + reason class |
| `team_redirect` | Toolkit will fit | Link to Team; no fee |
| `accepted_pending_pay` | SOW one-pager sent | Hidden LS link or invoice |
| `paid` | Funds or invoice agreed per counsel | Kickoff date |
| `discovery` | Conversations 1–N | Calendar |
| `draft` | Pack v0.9 | Their comments (one cycle) |
| `delivered` | Pack v1.0 | Product complete |
| `paused` | Owner disappeared | Clock stopped; no extra design |
| `cancelled_pre_work` | Refund path per policy (work not started) | Counsel-approved refund |
| `cancelled_post_kickoff` | No fee refund; deliver what exists or negotiate | As counsel |

Do not invent `retainer` or `phase 2` without a new agreement.

---

## 2. Screening (30–45 minutes)

Read the intake. Score the internal table on the questionnaire.

**Decline immediately if:** they attached invoice images or credentials; they require autonomous payment; they require a guaranteed ROI; there is no named finance reader; the ask is “all finance verticals.”

**Redirect to Team if:** the paragraph is thin and the stack is one entity / one class. Be kind. Team was the right product.

**Accept only if:** slice is namable, owners exist, they can hear no-go, and we can finish in the fee shape without implementation hours.

Record the reason class in a log (not a CRM essay): `decline_scope` / `decline_data` / `decline_claims` / `team` / `accept_1500` / `accept_2250` / `accept_3000`.

## 4. Delivery sequence (after `paid`)

| Step | Who | Timebox | Artefact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kickoff note | Us | Day 0 | Confirms slice, non-promises, conversation count |
| Conversation 1 | Process owner + floor witness | 60–90 min | Live-path draft |
| Conversation 2 | Control + payments + systems | 60 min | Fence + what must stay human |
| Optional 3 | Steward + exec reader | 45 min | Purpose sentence stress-test |
| Optional 4 | Only if dual-ledger / dual-class | 45 min | Cross-walk / second fence *or* a written refusal to combine |
| Draft pack | Us | 5–8 working days after last required call | v0.9 |
| Comment cycle | Them | 5 working days | One consolidated mark-up |
| Final | Us | 3 working days | v1.0 + delivery email |

If they miss two conversations, move to `paused`. Do not fill the map from a vendor deck.

**We do not:** configure their ERP, write production prompts into their tenant, attend their Team workshop (unless the SOW paid for attendance as a separate line — default no), or introduce a model vendor.

---

## 5. Pack contents (v1.0 minimum)

1. Cover: named entity, slice, date, fictional-example disclaimer if Northline teaching slips remain  
2. Live map (their Tuesday, not ours)  
3. Purpose sentence  
4. Fence + escalation + register draft  
5. Agent set: in / out, with reasons (A12 never releases)  
6. Controls + evidence pull-list  
7. Three measures + 30/60/90 *reading* plan (no F6)  
8. Go / no-go with conditions  
9. What we did not do (implementation, opinion, results)  
10. Licence reminder: named-entity internal use; method remains ours  

Quality gate: the pack must survive Q1–Q9 in the refined operating spec. If we cannot name their kill-switch owner, we do not deliver a “go.”

Second pair of eyes: another Evidence Room reader, 20 minutes.

---

## 7. After delivery

State `delivered`. Support: clarification on *our* sentences for 10 business days. Not a new slice. Not a vendor call.

If they want another class, that is a new application or they buy Team and do it themselves.

Do not upsell a second Blueprint on the receipt.

---

## 8. Record-keeping

Keep: intake, screening reason, SOW, payment ref, conversation dates, pack versions, delivery email.  
Do not keep: any production invoices they sent in error — delete and write to them; we are not their processor.
