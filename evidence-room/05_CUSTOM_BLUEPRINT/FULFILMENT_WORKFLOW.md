# Custom Blueprint — fulfilment workflow

**Audience:** Named Evidence Room practitioner  
**Nature:** Highly repeatable. AI-assisted drafts. Human-accountable deliverable.  
**Data:** Client production data stays in the client’s tenant. Our AI tools see only notes the client agreed we may keep (roles, counts, system names — not invoice images or bank details).

This workflow is how we deliver the same quality at $1,500–$3,000 without inventing a unique methodology each time. The *content* is theirs. The *steps* are ours.

---

## 0. Principles

1. Same method as the licensed OS. No special autonomy for paying clients.
2. Payment-always-human in every artefact.
3. AI may draft; it may not be the signatory.
4. If intake shows a guaranteed-ROI sponsor and no willingness to drop it, **decline**.
5. If they send production invoices to our mailbox, **delete, notify, do not ingest.**

---

## 1. Repeatable stages

| Stage | Time (illustration) | Human | Allowed AI assist | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Accept | 30–45 min | Practitioner + (if needed) founder | Summarise intake against decline rules | Mail: accept + price + dates |
| B Prepare | 45 min | Practitioner | Draft session agenda from intake | Folder: agenda, blank charters, band page |
| C Kickoff | 90 min live | Practitioner | Live notes only in client tool if they require | Band draft; path chosen; owners named |
| D Sessions 2–3 | 2 × 90 min | Practitioner | After session: draft map + JDs from *our notes* | Client corrects in-session or by comment |
| E Assemble | 2–4 hours | Practitioner | Merge, consistency check, grep banned phrases | Draft pack |
| F Read-back | 60–90 min | Practitioner | — | Redlines |
| G Final | 60–90 min | Practitioner | Changelog | Delivered pack + closure mail |
| H Close | 20 min | Practitioner | — | No residual client files we should not hold |

Elapsed calendar: 2–4 weeks. Do not add “bonus” agents to be nice.

---

## 2. Decline rules (stage A)

Decline or re-scope if:

- They want a connector or hosted model on their invoices.
- They want us to write a guaranteed savings/ROI.
- No Process Owner will be named.
- They intend to republish the toolkit.
- Scope is “global AP all ERPs all waves” inside $1,500.

Re-scope upward (still ≤ $3,000) if: two ERPs, or InfoSec Q&A added, or Wave 2 sketches.

---

## 3. AI-assisted drafting pattern (stages D–E)

**Inputs to the model (ours):** session notes (roles, system names, volumes as *they stated*, exclusion lists), blank charter template, stack overview IDs.

**Never input:** invoice PDFs, vendor master extracts, bank IBANs, employee IDs, client legal advice.

**Prompts we use internally** (short):

- “Draft a Wave 1 Matching charter from these notes. Autonomy L1. Exclusions must include no GR invent, no post, no flag clear. Payment not in scope. Label any number the notes did not contain as [UNKNOWN].”
- “Turn these exception stories into class A–J. Do not invent classes.”
- “Grep-level pass: list sentences that imply savings, fraud detection, compliance, accuracy, or autonomous pay.”

**Human checklist after every model draft:**

- [ ] Every number appears in notes or is [UNKNOWN].
- [ ] Northline not presented as their baseline.
- [ ] Agent 10 language is hypothesis.
- [ ] Agent 12 ceiling annotate if present.
- [ ] Owners are their names, not ours.
- [ ] Banned-phrase pass clean.

If the model invents a statistic, delete it. Do not “leave it for flavour.”

---

## 4. Session scripts (compress)

**Kickoff:** contract of product; payment sentence; sit or challenge diagnostic; pick one path; name six owners; list informal helpers; schedule views of *their* five invoices on *their* screen.

**Session 2:** walk invoices; harvest codes; classify ten + payment run; start 03 and 10 charters.

**Session 3:** finish 01, 02, 04, 16; shadow calendar; steering page; refusals list.

**Read-back:** page-turn; they speak exclusions; we do not add L3.

---

## 5. Quality gates before send

Same as `00_READ_ME/REFINED_MASTER_PROMPT.md` Q1–Q12, plus:

- Intake declarations on file.
- Price matches `PRICING_AND_SCOPE.md` band.
- Deliverable list matches what was sold.
- No client production data in our cloud.
