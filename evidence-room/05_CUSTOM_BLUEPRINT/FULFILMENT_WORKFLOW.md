## 1. Stages

| Stage | Entry | Activities | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Inquire | Marketing/sales lead | Qualify ICP; send brochure + intake | Intake returned |
| 1 Scope | Complete intake | Fit check; draft SOW & price band | Signed SOW / deposit |
| 2 Mobilise | Payment / PO cleared | Schedule interviews; data room rules | Kickoff held |
| 3 Discover | Kickoff | Interviews; artefact review | Discovery notes approved |
| 4 Design | Discovery done | Workshop(s); workforce design | Draft deliverables |
| 5 Challenge | Drafts out | Sponsor + Audit review cycle | Comments closed |
| 6 Handoff | Final artefacts | Readout; handoff pack; feedback | Engagement closed |
| 7 Aftercare | Closed | Optional 30-day email check-in | — |

## 4. Commerce notes

- Deposit or full fee via Lemon Squeezy where used as MoR (platform **5% + $0.50**; intl **+1.5%**; PayPal **+1.5%**; affiliate **+3%** if referred).  
- Flag VAT/GST treatment for professional confirmation.  
- Refunds: services partially delivered — see Storefront Spec recommendation (pro-rata only before discover starts; limited after).

- [ ] Payment boundary explicit  
- [ ] No guaranteed ROI/fraud/compliance language in customer-facing decks  
- [ ] Industry cites attributed (Ardent 2025 figures only as climate)
