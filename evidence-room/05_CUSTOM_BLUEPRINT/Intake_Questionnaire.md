# AP Transformation Blueprint — Intake Questionnaire

## Section 1: Organisation Profile
1. Company name and industry
2. Number of employees
3. Number of legal entities / business units
4. ERP system(s) in use
5. AP team size (FTE)
6. Shared services model? (Y/N, location)

## Section 2: AP Operations
7. Monthly invoice volume
8. % PO-based vs non-PO invoices
9. % electronic vs paper invoices
10. Current cost per invoice (if known)
11. Current STP rate (if known)
12. Average exception rate
13. Average invoice processing time (days)
14. Number of active suppliers
15. Payment methods (check, ACH, wire, card)

## Section 3: Current Automation
16. Current AP automation tools (list)
17. OCR/intelligent capture in use? (Y/N, vendor)
18. Workflow automation in use? (Y/N, vendor)
19. Any AI agents deployed in Finance? (Y/N, describe)
20. Integration maturity (API access to ERP: full/partial/none)

## Section 4: Pain Points
21. Top 5 AP challenges (ranked)
22. Top 5 exception categories by volume
23. Biggest control concern
24. Month-end close pain points
25. Supplier relationship challenges

## Section 5: Transformation Goals
26. Primary objective (cost reduction / speed / controls / all)
27. Target timeline for first agent deployment
28. Budget range for implementation
29. Executive sponsor (name, title)
30. Internal resources available (FTE)

## Section 6: Governance
31. AI governance framework exists? (Y/N)
32. Segregation of duties documented? (Y/N)
33. Internal audit involvement required? (Y/N)
34. Data privacy requirements (GDPR, etc.)
35. Risk appetite for agent autonomy (conservative/moderate/aggressive)

## Fulfilment Workflow
1. Customer completes questionnaire
2. AI-assisted analysis against AP Agent OS framework
3. Human review of recommendations (2-4 hours)
4. Generate: assessment, architecture, business case, 90-day plan, executive deck
5. Deliver within 10 business days
6. One round of revisions included
