# Custom Blueprint

**Service:** Facilitated design of *this organisation’s* AP agent layer against *this organisation’s* stack  
**Price range:** $1,500–$3,000 USD (see `PRICING_AND_SCOPE.md`)  
**Lockup:** EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS  
**Motion:** Application, not a cart impulse.

A Custom Blueprint is not a download of a “personalised zip” and not implementation consulting for software. It is a scoped, repeatable design engagement. Payment authorisation remains human in every deliverable. The engagement does not promise savings, fraud detection, compliance, accounting accuracy, or ROI.

## When this is the right SKU

- More than one ERP, or a messy acquisition path.
- InfoSec / Audit will not move from a self-serve licence alone.
- Political split (SSC vs plants, AP vs Procurement).
- The buyer already holds Professional or Team and needs *their* names on *their* map.

When this is the wrong SKU: they want a bot built, a connector, a savings guarantee, or a cheap rewrite of the toolkit as their product.
