## The offer

You already have an AP stack. You may already have models. You do not have a written layer: named agents, packets, owners, and a human hold on payment that survives a vendor demo.

A Custom Blueprint is a short, facilitated design of **your** Wave 1 (and, if scoped, Wave 2 sketch) against **your** entities, ERP, and exception mix. You leave with charters that have your names on them — not a second copy of Northline Industrials. Northline in the toolkit is fictional; we will not present it as your peer.

We do not replace Coupa, SAP, Oracle, Dynamics, NetSuite, Tipalti, BILL, or your SSC. We do not connect to your ERP. We do not take production invoices home.

## What you receive

See `DELIVERABLE_OUTLINE.md`. In short:

- Diagnostic band on *your* scores (you sit; we challenge).
- Path map for one agreed path.
- Six Wave 1 charters completed with your owners and exclusions.
- Human-vs-agent classification of ten of *your* exceptions and one payment run (you supply redacted materials in *your* environment).
- Autonomy register starter and two-week shadow plan.
- Steering one-pager and a written list of refusals (including payment).

Optional (upper end of range): Wave 2 sketches; enterprise overlay notes; InfoSec Q&A using the method.

## How it runs

1. You apply (`INTAKE_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`).
2. We accept, decline, or re-scope the price inside the published range.
3. Kickoff (90 minutes). Then structured working sessions (see fulfilment workflow).
4. We may use AI tools **internally** to draft maps and charters from the notes you already approved. A named Evidence Room practitioner is accountable for every sentence. Your production data stays in your systems.
5. Read-back. You own the signed charters.

Typical elapsed time: two to four weeks of calendar, not a six-month transformation.
