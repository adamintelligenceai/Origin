# Custom Blueprint — Deliverable Specification

**Product:** Evidence Room — Custom Blueprint ($1,500–$3,000)  
**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-03-20  
**Acceptance:** Sponsor written accept or email confirmation

---

## D1 — Current-state operating map

**Must include:** lifecycle stages; systems of record; ownership; control hotspots; exception pathways; explicit non-goals.  
**Format:** PDF diagram + narrative (8–15 pages typical).  
**Out of scope:** ERP re-architecture designs; vendor selection RFPs.

---

## D2 — Agent workforce design

**Must include:** prioritised portfolio (typically 5–12 opportunities); wave-1 recommendation (≤3); mode per agent (Observe/Assist/…); heatmap; prohibited actions list; link to library patterns where relevant.  
**Each wave-1 agent:** answers seven product questions at draft quality.  
**Out of scope:** Production prompt engineering inside customer LLM tenants (advisory only unless separately scoped).

---

## D3 — Governance charter

**Must include:** sponsor; decision rights; promotion/demotion rules; cadence; SoD stance; payment boundary; change control for instructions; audit evidence expectations.  
**Format:** 5–10 page charter v1.0 suitable for steering adoption.

---

## D4 — 90-day responsibility roadmap

**Must include:** phased plan; gates; owners; dependencies; training implications; steering dates.  
**Must not include:** guaranteed financial benefits. Optional appendix may cite Ardent 2025 climate (**$9.84** / **$2.65** / **18.4%** / **35.4%** / **44%**) as external context.

---

## D5 — Handoff pack

**Must include:** decision log; RAID; interview theme summary; file index; recommended next commercial step (Pro/Team self-serve continuation vs further advisory).  
**Optional:** annotated Team tracker seeded with customer names.

---

## Acceptance criteria (all)

1. Artefacts use Evidence Room voice (institutional; no hype).  
2. Stack honesty preserved.  
3. Customer internals not redistributed by Evidence Room.  
4. Licence: customer internal use; no resale of Evidence Room IP.  

---

## Change control of deliverables

Post-accept edits require a change note; material scope expands may move price within or beyond $1,500–$3,000 band via new SOW.
