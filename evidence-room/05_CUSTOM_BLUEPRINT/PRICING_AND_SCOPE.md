# Custom Blueprint — pricing and scope

**Currency:** USD list, exclusive of tax.  
**Range:** $1,500–$3,000.  
**Motion:** Application. Not an instant checkout of a “custom zip.”  
**Merchant / contracting:** Confirm with counsel and, if used, Lemon Squeezy or invoice terms. This file is commercial intent, not the legal instrument.

---

## 1. How to pick a band

| Band | Price | Fits when | Sessions | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Blueprint — Path** | $1,500 | One ERP, one path, owners named, Professional already in hand or bought alongside | Kickoff + 2 × 90 min + read-back | Core pack #1–10 |
| **Blueprint — Layer** | $2,200 | One ERP, Wave 2 sketches, or light multi-entity (same ERP) | +1 working session | Core + #11 and/or #12 |
| **Blueprint — Contested** | $3,000 | Two ERPs **or** InfoSec Q&A **or** acquisition path without a map | +2 working sessions | Core + #11–14 as agreed in acceptance mail |

If they need implementation, RPA build, or multi-month change: **out of range**. Decline or refer to a separate statement of work that is not this SKU.

Starter-only buyers can apply; we usually require Professional or Team so they hold the specs the charters point to. The Blueprint does not replace those licences.

---

## 2. Inclusions (all bands)

- Named Evidence Room practitioner.
- Internal AI-assisted drafting per fulfilment workflow.
- Written artefacts listed for that band.
- 10 business days of email clarification on *our sentences*.

## 3. Exclusions (all bands)

- Hosted processing of invoices.
- ERP / bank connectivity.
- Guaranteed savings, ROI, fraud detection, compliance, accounting accuracy, autonomous payments.
- Travel (if ever required: actuals, separately).
- Licence to resell Evidence Room IP.
- Work beyond the agreed path without a written change.

---

## 4. Client responsibilities

- Sit or challenge the diagnostic with artefacts.
- Name Process Owner and Controls Lead.
- Show (in their tenant) five invoices and a payment-run procedure.
- IAM and operator time.
- Sign drafts into their environment if they choose to go live.

If client responsibilities slip by more than 15 business days, we may close the engagement as delivered-to-date or pause. We do not extend unpaid to “wait for the ROI committee.”

---

## 5. Payment and refunds (recommendation to counsel)

- 100% on acceptance, or 50% accept / 50% before read-back — pick one in the acceptance mail.
- Refundable if Evidence Room declines before kickoff.
- After kickoff: not refundable for completed time, subject to law and MoR policy.
- Do not advertise “money back if you don’t save money.”
