# Launch Offer and Affiliates

**Lockup:** EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS  
**Default commercial posture:** published prices are the prices. We do not invent urgency.  
**If a launch offer exists, it is dated, honest, and does not reintroduce banned claims.**

Professional confirmation required before any affiliate pays out (contract, tax, LS programme rules).

---

## 1. Launch offer (recommended)

### Name

**Founding Practitioner — Professional**

### Mechanic

- **What:** Professional at **$199** (full list). Founding buyers are listed (first name + organisation, **opt-in only**) on an internal roll we may later thank, not a public “hall of fame” without consent.  
- **What we will not do:** 50% off, “only 12 licences,” countdown clocks, or “founding price forever.”  
- **Optional, cleaner than a discount:** a **14-day office hour** (one group call, 45 minutes, Q&A on START HERE — not implementation consulting) for Professional and Team purchases in the first 30 days after launch. Capacity capped. No recordings sold later as a SKU that cannibalises Blueprint.

### Why not discount Pro at launch

$199 is already a method price, not an enterprise price. Discounting it trains buyers to wait and makes Team ($499) look like a penalty. If we must discount, discount **Starter ($79 → $49)** as a diagnostic companion for 14 days, then return to $79. Never discount Team below Professional × 2.

### Dates

Write real dates before going live, e.g.:

> Office hours for purchases between {{LaunchDate}} and {{LaunchDate+30}}.

After the window, the page comes down. No zombie “launch” banners.

### Copy (allowed)

Founding practitioners get the same OS everyone else gets. For the first thirty days we will host one optional group office hour on the twelve-step START HERE. No extra fee. No extra promises.

### Copy (forbidden)

“Founding licence pays for itself.” “Founders get fraud modules.” “Lock in ROI pricing.”

## 3. Affiliate programme (phase 2 — off at launch)

Turn on only after:

1. Legal pages are counsel-approved  
2. A one-page affiliate rule is signed  
3. We accept the LS merchant-side **+3%** on referred sales (docs: https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees)  
4. We understand the **+2%** on affiliate payouts (affiliate-side, same page)  
5. We can revoke affiliates who hype  

### Proposed terms (draft for counsel)

- **Commission:** 20% of **net list** (not of tax) on Starter, Professional, Team. **0%** on Blueprint (we will not pay 20% of $3,000 for a form-fill). Confirm whether LS calculates on gross or net.  
- **Cookie:** 30 days (or LS default — confirm).  
- **Payout:** via LS affiliates; threshold {{$50}}; not on refunded orders.  
- **Allowed:** link to Diagnostic, Method, Professional; verbatim quotes from our site.  
- **Forbidden:** guaranteed savings/ROI; fraud-detection claims; “official partner of SAP/Oracle”; implying they sell the OS; bidding on “Evidence Room LLC” or forensic-animation terms; republishing our zip; “prompt pack” repositioning.  
- **Disclosure:** affiliates must disclose the commercial relationship as their jurisdiction requires.  
- **Termination:** immediate for banned claims; keys they pirated are revoked.

### Who we will not accept

Anonymous coupon farms. “AI money” newsletter swarms. Anyone whose landing page contains a banned phrase.

### Who we might accept (later)

Practitioners in finance transformation or AP operations with their own audience, who will use our words.

## 4. Margin check before enabling 20% + LS 3%

Example: Professional $199, US card, no tax.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Gross | $199.00 |
| LS platform (5% + $0.50) | $10.45 |
| LS affiliate merchant extra (3% of total — **confirm base**) | ~$5.97 if on $199 |
| Affiliate 20% of $199 | $39.80 |
| **Residual before payout drag / refunds** | **~$142.78** |

Still acceptable if volume is real and refunds are low. Not acceptable if we also discount 30%.

International + PayPal + affiliate stacking can erase the “quiet premium” of $199. Model before a campaign. See `FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`.
