## 15. Refund policy recommendation

**Digital goods (Starter/Pro/Team):**  
- Full refund within **14 days** if download count is zero / negligible (configure to LS capability).  
- After substantial download, no refund except file defect — replace files.  

**Free:** N/A  

**Custom:**  
- Full refund of deposit if cancelled before Discover kickoff.  
- After Discover starts: refund minus documented hours at agreed rate, or credit to future work.  
- After final accept: no refund.  

**Always:** No refund solely because AI pilot underperformed — outcomes depend on customer execution; we do not guarantee savings/ROI.

**Flag:** Have counsel review final refund text before publish.
