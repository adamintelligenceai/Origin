## 2. Licence families

### 2.1 Evaluation (Diagnostic)

You may complete the diagnostic for your own assessment. You may keep your completed answers. You may not treat the blank diagnostic as a client deliverable or reprint it in a publication.

### 2.2 Individual (Starter)

You may use the materials in your own work for **one** organisation you are employed by or (if a sole practitioner inside that organisation) engaged by as an employee-equivalent. One human user. No sharing of the files as working copies.

### 2.3 Professional

As Individual, plus the full Professional corpus. Still **one practitioner**. Briefing colleagues verbally is allowed. Dropping the zip on a shared drive for the department is not. That is Team.

### 2.4 Team

**One** legal entity (or one clearly named group within it, if counsel prefers). Up to **[eight]** named natural persons (“seats”) may hold working copies and run the workshop **internally**.

You may train non-seat colleagues using the training scripts, provided the files remain with seats.

You may not provide the files to vendors, auditors’ firms as a kit, or other entities. An auditor may *read* an output you created (your completed brief) in the course of their engagement with you. They may not receive our blank toolkit.

### 2.5 Custom Blueprint

The delivered design may be used internally by the named applicant entity. Our underlying method, unused library items, and templates remain ours. You do not receive a Professional or Team licence unless you also bought one.

## 4. What you may not do

You may not, under any of these licences:

1. **Resell** the materials or any substantial part.
2. **Redistribute** the files (email, Slack to another entity, GitHub, Notion public, LMS for another org).
3. **Sublicense** anyone.
4. **Republish** — including as a book, course, Substack, conference talk handout, or “our framework” on a consultancy site.
5. Use the materials to provide **paid services to other organisations** (workshops, implementations, retainers) without a separate written licence from us.
6. Train a **public** machine-learning model on the materials, or feed the corpus into a tool that uses customer content to train foundation models. Internal use of a private instance to *assist a licensed user* with *their* completed documents is a grey area — **counsel must write this sentence**. Until then: do not upload the blank toolkit to a public AI service.
7. Remove copyright notices, licence files, or disclaimers.
8. Claim we certify your controls, your compliance, or your results.
