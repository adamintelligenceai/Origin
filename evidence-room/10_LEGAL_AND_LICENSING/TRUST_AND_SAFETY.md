# Trust and Safety — Evidence Room

**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-03-20  
**Audience:** Internal operators + public trust page source

---

## 1. Commitments

1. **Evidence over hype** in all commercial copy.  
2. **No guaranteed** savings, fraud outcomes, compliance certification, ROI, or autonomous payment safety.  
3. **Stack honesty** — we do not claim to replace major AP/ERP platforms.  
4. **MoR transparency** — Lemon Squeezy processes digital checkout.  
5. **Human responsibility** — agents earn authority; payment boundaries default to human/platform-owned.

---

## 2. Harm scenarios we design against

| Scenario | Mitigation in product |
|---|---|
| Silent wrong posting | Stop conditions; Assist-first modes |
| Hallucinated supplier status | SoR citation rules |
| SoD bypass | Explicit prohibited actions; RACI |
| Autonomy by demo | Promotion hold rules |
| Buyer overclaim in their board pack | Disclaimers + training language bans |

---

## 3. Abuse of our materials

We discourage use of Evidence Room packs to:

- Market ungoverned “fully autonomous AP”  
- Train staff to ignore audit  
- Resell as competing OS  

Respond to abuse reports via trust@evidenceroom.ai.

## 4. Security (digital delivery)

- Prefer signed LS downloads over open Drive links  
- Rotate shared Custom workspace access after handoff  
- No production payment credentials in examples
