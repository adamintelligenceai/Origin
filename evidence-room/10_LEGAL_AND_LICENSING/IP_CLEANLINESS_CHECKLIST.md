# IP Cleanliness Checklist

**Purpose:** Keep Evidence Room — AP Agent OS independently authored and licensable.  
**Status:** Internal operating checklist. **Not a legal opinion.** Counsel should review the zip before launch.  
**Last updated:** 20 September 2026

---

## 1. Authorship rule

Every file we sell or publish as ours must be **independently authored** (including AI-assisted drafts that a human edited) or used under a **written licence** we can point to.

We do not paste:

- vendor documentation from SAP, Oracle, Microsoft, Coupa, Tipalti, BILL, etc.  
- another firm’s control frameworks (COSO, NIST, ISO) as if we wrote them — **refer by name at most, do not reproduce tables**  
- another course’s AP agent pack  
- scraped paywalled research  
- Evidence Room LLC forensic-animation scripts or images (different company)

## 3. Trademarks of others

**Allowed:** descriptive sentences (“maps onto SAP, Oracle, Dynamics, NetSuite, Workday”).  
**Not allowed:** logo walls, “SAP-approved,” using their word marks as our product names, keyword ads that confuse.

**Our lockup** must stay distinctive: EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS. See `TRADEMARK_AND_DOMAIN_NOTES.md`.

---

## 4. People and data

| Do not include | Why |
|---|---|
| Real vendor bank details | Payment-data hygiene; not our dataset |
| Real employee emails beyond optional testimonials **with written consent** | Privacy |
| Real invoice PDFs in “examples” | Create fictional ones or redact until unrecognisable |
| Photos of staff without a release | Publicity rights |

Diagnostic free-text: if someone pasted a real invoice, delete per Privacy and do not file it in the “example bank.”

---

## 5. AI-assisted text

- Run a human edit for voice, banned claims, and factual fee citations.  
- Do not leave model-invented case studies that look real.  
- Do not invent court cases, statutes, or “according to Gartner” numbers.  
- Fee figures must cite LS/Stripe URLs we actually opened.  
- If a passage is too close to a known vendor’s white paper, rewrite from the method, not from memory of their PDF.

---

## 6. Marketplace derivatives

Derivatives (`MARKETPLACE_DERIVATIVES.md`) must be **new artefacts** (posters, pads), not a unzipped Professional. Shop name should not impersonate Evidence Room LLC.

---

## 7. Contributors and contractors

Anyone who writes into the toolkit signs (or is contracted with) an IP assignment or work-made-for-hire **as counsel specifies**. Do not accept “I dropped this prompt pack in the folder.”

---

## 8. Inbound open source

Default: **no inbound copyleft** in the sold zip. If we later add a script, record its licence. Markdown method files stay our copyright.

---

## 9. Outbound licence hygiene

Buyers get a **use licence**, not a right to relicense. If we find our zip on a marketplace:

1. Confirm it is ours (hash / unique phrase).  
2. Revoke keys if identifiable.  
3. Takedown notice — **counsel**.  
4. Do not publicly accuse without review.

---

## 10. Third-party research and numbers

If we cite a study, we keep the citation and the date we read it. If we cannot cite it, we do not use the number. We do **not** publish Diagnostic aggregates as “the state of AP agents” without a methodology and counsel.

---

## 11. Music, icons, photography

Prefer type and line diagrams. If icons: confirm licence allows commercial embedding in a sold PDF. No random Google Image.
