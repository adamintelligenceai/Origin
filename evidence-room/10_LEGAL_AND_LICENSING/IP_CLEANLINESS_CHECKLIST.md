# IP Cleanliness Checklist — Pre-Release

Complete before any public sale or marketplace listing. **Fail any critical item → do not ship.**

---

## Critical

- [ ] No employer or client confidential materials pasted into product files  
- [ ] No copied vendor documentation beyond fair-use quotation with citation  
- [ ] No scraped competitor paid content  
- [ ] No third-party trademarks used in a way that implies endorsement  
- [ ] No stock assets with licence violations; record licence proofs  
- [ ] No personal data of real suppliers/employees in sample invoices (synthetic or fully anonymised)  
- [ ] Charts either use customer-owned data (permissioned) or are labelled illustrative  
- [ ] Research Ledger entries exist for any external statistic cited in customer PDFs  

## Product claims

- [ ] No guaranteed ROI / savings / fraud catch rate / compliance certification  
- [ ] Payment autonomy not promised  
- [ ] Lemon Squeezy fee language matches live pricing page or is caveated  

## Brand & design

- [ ] Fonts licensed for web/desktop use as deployed  
- [ ] Colour/brand distinct from obvious clones of restricted systems  

## Legal pack present

- [ ] Licence Terms linked at checkout  
- [ ] Disclaimers linked  
- [ ] Privacy language linked  
- [ ] Refund Policy linked  

## Marketplace derivatives

- [ ] Derivative SKUs do not exceed licence grant of source tier  
- [ ] Listing copy includes “not a compliance certificate” boundary
