## Citation hygiene

- Numeric claims → Research Ledger / approved list only.  
- Permitted Ardent 2025 cites: **$9.84**, **$2.65**, **18.4%**, **35.4%**, **44%** (and other ledgered IDs).  
- Lemon Squeezy fees: **5%+$0.50**, **+1.5%** intl, **+1.5%** PayPal, affiliate **+3%**, MoR — as merchant education, not buyer surcharges.

---

## Red flags (stop ship)

- “Partner of [AP mega-vendor]” without written agreement  
- Customer logo wall without permission  
- Guaranteed savings/fraud/compliance/ROI language  
- Copied competitor playbooks
