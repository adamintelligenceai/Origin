**Status:** Clearance flags and operating hygiene. **Not a trademark search, not a legal opinion, not a filing. Counsel and a qualified trademark professional must act before we claim a mark or send cease-and-desist letters.**  
**Last updated:** 20 September 2026  
**Lockup we will use:** EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS  
**Intended public domain:** evidenceroom.ai

---

## 1. Why the lockup is long

“Evidence Room” is a strong metaphor (proof is stored; permission is granted elsewhere) and a **weak standalone brand** in the wild:

- A forensic-animation company, **Evidence Room LLC**, uses **evidence-room.net**.  
- **evidenceroom.com** is taken (as of the brand-selection check; **re-verify**).  
- Descriptive English words (“evidence,” “room”) are hard to monopolise.

Commercial mitigation — **not a legal conclusion**:

1. Always pair the room with the product: **AP AGENT OS**.  
2. Always show the category: **Finance Agent Operating System**.  
3. Use **evidenceroom.ai**, not a hyphenated “evidence-room” that collides with the animation firm.  
4. Payment descriptor should read `EVIDENCEROOM.AI` or `ER AP AGENT OS`, not `EVIDENCE ROOM` alone.

## 3. Third-party collision — Evidence Room LLC

**Flag: trademark-clearance item. High priority.**

Public materials (to be verified by counsel, not relied on as a full search) indicate a forensic-animation / courtroom-visuals business using “Evidence Room” and evidence-room.net.

**Risks**

- Search confusion (“evidence room” → animation, not AP)  
- Payment-descriptor confusion  
- Email and invoice mis-delivery  
- Trademark opposition or demand if we file or scale  
- Customer thinking we reconstruct accidents for trial  

**Operating rules until counsel says otherwise**

- First screen of every property: **EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS**  
- About and footer: we are **not** that firm; we do not do forensic animation or courtroom reconstruction  
- Ads: **no** keywords like “forensic animation,” “courtroom reconstruction,” “accident reconstruction”  
- Marketplace shop name: **AP Agent Room** (or similar), not “Evidence Room” alone  
- Do not buy evidence-room.net lookalike ads against their name  
- Do not use a gavel, fingerprint, or courtroom sketch as our mark  

**Counsel should**

- Commission a proper search (US and other launch markets)  
- Decide whether to file, and for which classes (likely educational publications / downloadable manuals — **not** payment software, **not** animation)  
- Consider a coexistence conversation vs a different master brand if the search is ugly  
- Review the lockup as a **composite** mark  

We will **not** send informal legal threats ourselves.
