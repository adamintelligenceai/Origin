## Digital products (Starter $79 · Professional $199 · Team $499)

Because these are **instant digital downloads**, refunds are generally **not available** once files have been accessed or downloaded, except where required by law or in the cases below.

### We may refund or replace at our discretion if:

- Duplicate accidental purchase within 24 hours (same account)  
- File delivery failure we cannot remedy within a reasonable time  
- Material non-delivery of listed core components (verified)

### We typically do not refund for:

- Change of mind after download  
- Insufficient internal adoption  
- Incompatibility with a specific ERP configuration (Products are methodology, not software integrations)  
- Expectation of custom consulting beyond the tier purchased  

## Free diagnostic

No payment; no refund. You may unsubscribe from email at any time.

## Blueprint service ($1,500–3,000)

- Before workshops scheduled: refund minus any non-recoverable processing fees where permitted.  
- After workshops begun: refunds replaced by reschedule or partial credit per statement of work.  
- After final pack delivered: no refund except for verified non-delivery of listed deliverables.
