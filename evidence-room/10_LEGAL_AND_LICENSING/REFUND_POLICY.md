## 1. Recommended policy (paid digital licences)

### Window

**14 days** from the time of purchase for **Starter, Professional, and Team**.

### We will refund (recommended)

1. You never received a working download or key **through no fault of your own**, and we could not re-issue within a reasonable time after you wrote to hello@evidenceroom.ai with your order ID.  
2. The zip is **materially not as described** (advertised modules missing).  
3. A duplicate charge for the same SKU, same buyer, same day.  
4. We, or Lemon Squeezy, cancel the order before fulfilment.

### We will typically not refund (recommended, subject to law)

1. Change of mind after you have **successfully downloaded** the archive and had a reasonable chance to see that it is a document toolkit (not hosted software).  
2. You wanted a result we **expressly disclaim** (savings, fraud detection, compliance, accuracy, autonomous payments).  
3. You bought Individual and now want Team — that is an **additional purchase**, not a refund.  
4. You redistributed the files or published them.  
5. Chargeback already opened — we respond through that process.

**Counsel:** EU/UK and other consumer rules for digital content often require **express consent** to immediate delivery and acknowledgement that withdrawal is lost. Build that into checkout if those buyers are in scope. This draft must yield to those rules.

## 3. Custom Blueprint

| Stage | Recommended treatment |
|---|---|
| Application submitted, we **decline** before kickoff, money taken | Full return of what was taken |
| Application accepted, **kickoff not started**, buyer withdraws | Return minus any non-refundable application fee **only if** a fee was clearly disclosed — default: return |
| After kickoff | Refund only for unused scoped fees as the acceptance note says; completed time is not “a zip you didn’t like” |
| We materially fail to deliver the scoped sessions | Remedy as the note / counsel says |

Blueprint is not a “14-day digital goods” clone if it is a service. **Classification is a professional confirmation item** (it also affects MoR fit).
