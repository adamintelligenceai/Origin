## Commercial intent (for counsel to legalise)

We sell to professionals. We would rather refund a misfit than argue a chargeback. We will not run a “no questions, 30 days” window that turns the zip into a lending library.

Lemon Squeezy will also apply its own dispute process. Align with them.

## Public policy (draft copy)

### Toolkit products — Starter ($79), Professional ($199), Team ($499)

If, within **14 days** of purchase, you email hello@evidenceroom.ai from the address you used at checkout and tell us that:

- the product is materially different from the product page, or
- we failed to deliver a working download or licence key and have not repaired it,

we will refund the amount you paid for that product, and your licence ends. You must delete the files.

If you simply finished reading and would like your money back, **[counsel: state whether we honour that in the EU cooling-off regime for digital goods after delivery/consent, and whether US professional buyers have no such right]. Draft seller preference:** we *may* refund once as a gesture; we will not do it as a habit, and we will not refund after 14 days except for file corruption we cannot repair.

### Diagnostic (free)

No payment. No refund.

### Custom Blueprint ($1,500–$3,000)

If you have paid and **we have not started work**, you may cancel for a full refund of that fee.

Once a kickoff has occurred or we have sent a first working draft, the fee is for scoped work in progress. We will complete the agreed Blueprint or, if we cannot, we will propose a resolution. **We do not offer a 14-day “digital goods” refund after work starts.** Counsel must confirm this is lawful for the buyer’s location.

### Chargebacks

Email us first. A chargeback opened without that conversation, where delivery occurred, may be treated as a licence breach and the key revoked.

### How to ask

Email hello@evidenceroom.ai with:

- order number or LS receipt
- product name
- reason (one paragraph)

We aim to answer in two business days.
