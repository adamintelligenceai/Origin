# Privacy Language — Evidence Room

**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-03-20  
**Status:** Draft privacy notice language — **flag for lawyer review / DPA alignment**

---

## Plain-English summary

We collect what we need to deliver digital products, respond to inquiries, run email sequences you opt into, and improve the site. Lemon Squeezy processes payments as Merchant of Record. We do not sell your personal information.

---

## Categories of data

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Identity & contact | Name, email, organisation | Delivery, support, sequences |
| Transaction | SKU, amount, LS customer id | Fulfilment, accounting |
| Usage | Download events, page analytics | Improve products, security |
| Custom engagement | Intake answers, documents you share | Deliver Blueprint under SOW/NDA |
| Technical | IP, browser, cookies (if used) | Security, basic analytics |

Payment card data is handled by Lemon Squeezy / its processors — not stored on Evidence Room servers in ordinary digital checkout.

## Lawful bases (draft — counsel to confirm)

Contract performance · Legitimate interests (product improvement, security) · Consent (marketing emails where required) · Legal obligation (tax/accounting records).

## Email communications

Transactional messages for purchases. Marketing sequences only with opt-in (or soft opt-in where lawful). Unsubscribe honoured promptly.

## Processors / sub-processors (illustrative)

Lemon Squeezy · email provider · analytics provider · cloud storage for Custom workspaces. Maintain a live list on the Privacy page.
