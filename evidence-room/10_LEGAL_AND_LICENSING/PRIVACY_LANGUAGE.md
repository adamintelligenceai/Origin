## Who we are

Evidence Room (“we”) provides digital products and related services for Finance teams.

## Data we may collect

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|----------|----------|---------|
| Purchase data | Name, email, organisation, billing via MoR | Fulfilment, records |
| Lead data | Email, optional company/role | Send diagnostic, nurture emails |
| Usage communications | Support emails | Customer service |
| Site telemetry | Approximate analytics if enabled | Improve site |

**Payment card data** is handled by Lemon Squeezy (or other MoR/processor)—not stored on Evidence Room servers in the standard digital checkout pattern.

## How we use data

Fulfil orders · send product files · respond to requests · improve products · send optional educational emails (with unsubscribe) · meet legal obligations.

## Sharing

We share data with processors necessary to operate (e.g. email delivery, MoR, cloud hosting). We do not sell personal information.

## Retention

Keep purchase records as required for tax/accounting; delete or anonymise lead data on request where applicable law allows.
