## 2. Scope

This notice covers:

- the marketing site evidenceroom.ai
- emails we send
- Custom Blueprint applications
- information we receive from Lemon Squeezy about orders

It does **not** cover Lemon Squeezy’s own processing, Vercel’s processing as host, or any AI tool a customer uses inside their organisation. Those have their own notices.

We are not a processor of customers’ accounts-payable personal data. We do not want that data.
