## 2. Data we intend to collect

### 2.1 Site operations

Internet Protocol address, user-agent, approximate time, pages requested, referrer. Purpose: security, debugging, abuse prevention. **[Cookies/analytics: none at launch unless counsel and a tool are added. If added, list them and legal basis.]**

### 2.2 Diagnostic and marketing email

Name, work email, role, optional organisation name, volume/ERP bands, free-text you type, opt-in flag. Purpose: deliver the worksheet, answer you, send the sequence **if you opted in**.

### 2.3 Purchase (via Lemon Squeezy)

Name, email, billing address as required for tax, organisation if provided, product, amount, tax line, licence key, order ID, payment metadata **as LS provides to us**. We do not intend to receive or store full card numbers.

### 2.4 Support

Whatever you send. **Please do not send invoice images, vendor bank details, employee records, or other production finance files.** If you send them, we will ask you to stop and will delete when policy and law allow.

### 2.5 We do not need

Your vendor master, payment files, employee HR files, or live invoice corpus. The OS is applied **in your environment**.
