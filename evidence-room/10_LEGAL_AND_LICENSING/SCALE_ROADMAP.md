| **AR** | Cash application assist, dispute triage, dunning draft with SoR cites |

**Non-negotiables persist:** earned responsibility · exception taxonomy · payment/treasury action boundaries · no guaranteed ROI claims · stack honesty vs ERP/TMS/expense vendors.

**Explicit non-goals for Phase 3 marketing until built:** selling a unified “autonomous finance” suite; moving money; replacing banks or ERPs.
