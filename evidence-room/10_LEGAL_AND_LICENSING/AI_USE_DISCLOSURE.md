## Public statement (site footer or /about)

Evidence Room uses contemporary writing and production tools, including large language models, as **assistants** in drafting, structuring, and editing operating materials. Humans direct the method, the claims we refuse to make, and what ships.

The products are **not** an AI service. They do not run a model on your Accounts Payable data. They are documents you apply in your own environment.

We do not use customer invoice data, Diagnostic answers, or Blueprint application attachments to train a public model. Do not send us that data.

---

## What we use AI tools for

- Drafting and tightening prose
- Structuring templates and checklists
- Exploring alternative outlines (the master line “Responsibility is earned.” was selected by humans after alternatives were rejected)
- Production hygiene (consistency of terms, non-promise list)

## What we do not use AI tools for

- Generating fake customer testimonials or case studies
- Inventing Ardent Partners or Forrester statistics
- Creating a “savings calculator”
- Running inference on a customer’s invoices
- Autonomous publishing without a human pass

---

## What customers may do with AI tools

Licensed users often want to drop a template into a private assistant.

**Until counsel finalises `LICENCE_TERMS.md` section 4.6:**

- Do **not** upload the blank toolkit to a **public** consumer AI service that states it trains on uploads.
- A **private**, no-training workspace used by a licensed seat to fill *that organisation’s* documents is the intended spirit of the product.
- Outputs remain subject to the same non-promise list. An assistant that adds an ROI claim is producing a claim we disown.

## Northline

Northline Industrial Group is a **fictional** company written for illustration. It is not a real customer record and was not generated as a “synthetic case study” of a real business.

---

## Third-party models

We do not endorse a model vendor. If a tool is named in an internal workflow, that is operations, not a partnership claim.

---

## Accuracy

AI-assisted drafting can be fluent and wrong. Every shipped document should pass a human check against:

1. The non-promise list  
2. The live product contents  
3. Cited research (Ardent / Forrester only when sourced)  
4. Licence family names and prices  

If a passage cannot survive those four, it does not ship.
