## 1. What this document covers

Two different facts:

1. **How Evidence Room used AI tools** when writing some of the toolkit and commercial copy.  
2. **How buyers may use AI models** later, inside a chartered AP agent, in *their* environment.

They must not be collapsed. Buying Professional does **not** mean we run a model on your invoices.

---

## 2. Our use of AI in creating the materials

Some research collation and draft language for the method, examples, and marketing files was produced with the assistance of AI writing tools. A human editor is responsible for the published method, the payment-hold rule, the banned-claim list, and the decision to ship.

We do not claim the OS was “written entirely by hand” or “written entirely by AI.” Both would be false.

Fictional operating details (Northline Industrials) are invented illustrations, not extracted from a real customer’s books.

**We do not use your Diagnostic free-text to train a public model.** Internal review of answers, if any, is for product clarity and abuse, as Privacy allows.

---

## 3. What the product is, computationally

The licensed zip is **documents and worksheets**. It is not a hosted model, not a plugin that calls a model, and not an automated decision system we operate on your employees or vendors.

The Diagnostic band is a simple score-to-label on answers you typed. It is a reading aid. It is not a credit, employment, or legal decision.

---

## 4. Your use of AI after purchase

The method assumes you *may* later attach models (yours or a vendor’s) to chartered roles. That use is **yours**.

The method requires:

- a named human owner per agent  
- evidence before permission  
- no agent payment release  
- flags treated as hypotheses, not fraud verdicts  
- change control for prompts and model versions  
- sampling rather than faith in fluency  

If you ignore those rules, you are not “using Evidence Room incorrectly” in a technical sense — you are **leaving the method**. The disclaimers still apply.

---

## 5. What you must not do with our files and models

- Do not upload the licensed archive to a **public** prompt marketplace or public training corpus (Licence Terms).  
- Do not tell a model it may “release the run if everything looks fine.”  
- Do not present model output as a **compliance certificate**, **fraud finding**, or **accounting fact** without a named human and a packet.  
- Do not send us production invoices for “the AI to review.”

Private, internal use of excerpts with a model **under your controls** for your implementation is a normal way to apply a written method — it does not expand your licence to publish the OS.

---

## 6. Accuracy and hallucination

Language models invent citations, amounts, and vendor names. The method treats that as expected. That is why L0 exists, why packets are required, and why payment is not on the ladder.

We do not warrant that any model, including ones used to draft our own sentences, is accurate.

---

## 7. Human involvement in *our* operation

Fulfilment of keys and zips is automated by Lemon Squeezy. Support replies are human. Blueprint, if accepted, is human-facilitated. We do not operate an AP agent on your stack.
