## Section: What a Blueprint is

A written operating design for one organisation, covering:

- the live process in scope
- the agent (or small set of agents) that should exist — and those that should not
- fences, escalations, and stop conditions
- control and evidence requirements
- operating measures and a 30/60/90 reading plan
- a go / no-go recommendation

You receive a document set you can take to a controller, a CIO, an auditor, or a vendor. You do not receive a bot, a model deployment, or a change to your ERP.
