## Intro

This page explains what Evidence Room collects when you use evidenceroom.ai and when you buy or download a product. It is written in plain English on purpose.

**This is a draft.** A qualified lawyer must review it before it is relied on. It is not legal advice to you or to us.

The seller brand is Evidence Room. Digital checkout is processed by **Link, LLC f/k/a Lemon Squeezy LLC** (“Lemon Squeezy”) as merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy’s own privacy notice also applies to checkout, payment, tax collection, and download delivery.

Until the selling legal entity is confirmed, treat “we” as the operator of evidenceroom.ai and the author of AP Agent OS.

Contact for privacy questions: **privacy@evidenceroom.ai** (or hello@evidenceroom.ai until that alias exists).

## What we collect

### When you browse the site

- Technical data your browser sends (IP address, user agent, pages viewed).
- Cookies or similar storage if we enable analytics. **Do not enable analytics until counsel and a cookie notice are in place.** Until then, collect as little as the host requires.

### When you request the free Diagnostic or buy a product

Lemon Squeezy typically collects:

- Name and email
- Billing address and tax-relevant location
- Payment method details (handled by Lemon Squeezy / its payment processors; we should not receive full card numbers)
- Product purchased, licence key, and download events

We receive a subset of that information so we can fulfil the order, email you, and support the licence.

### When you apply for a Custom Blueprint

Whatever you choose to write in the application: organisation name, stack, owners, constraints. **Do not send us invoice files, vendor masters, employee personal data, or anything that would be a data-processing engagement.** We are not your processor for AP data.

### When you email us

The content of the email and your address.

---

## What we do not want

We do not need, and you should not send:

- Invoice images or extracts
- Vendor bank details
- Employee or supplier personal data beyond the buyer’s business contact
- Credentials for any system

If you send those, we will ask you to delete the thread and not to resend.

---

## Why we use information

- To deliver downloads and licence keys
- To answer support questions about the product you bought
- To send the email sequence you signed up for (diagnostic and onboarding)
- To maintain licence records (so we know which terms apply)
- To meet bookkeeping and tax records that we, or Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record, must keep
- To protect the site and the licence (abuse, fraud against the store — not “fraud detection” as an AP product claim)

We do not sell your personal information.

## Email

The Diagnostic and paid products include transactional mail (receipt, download, START HERE).

Marketing mail is only sent if you asked for it or if the law where you live treats a customer relationship as sufficient — **counsel must set the rule**. Every marketing mail will have a visible unsubscribe.

Lemon Squeezy email is free to 500 subscribers on their published pricing page as of September 2026. If we use that tool, their processing terms apply as well.

## How long we keep it

- Order and licence records: at least as long as tax and contract law require (counsel to set the period).
- Email list: until you unsubscribe, plus a short suppression record.
- Application materials: until the Blueprint is declined or delivered, then a limited archive if we must keep a contract file.

## Your requests

Subject to the law that applies to you, you may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold, or to object to marketing.

Send the request to privacy@evidenceroom.ai. We may need to confirm you are the person you say you are. Lemon Squeezy may need to handle requests that only they can fulfil (payment records, tax invoices).

If you are in the EEA, UK, or another region with a statutory privacy authority, you may also complain to that authority. **Counsel must add the correct controller identity and lawful bases before this sentence is enough.**
