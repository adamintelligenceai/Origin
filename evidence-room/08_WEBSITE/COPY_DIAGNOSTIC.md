## Section: What you will do

The diagnostic takes a working session, not a coffee.

You will answer for the environment you actually run — ERP, AP tool, invoice mix, exception path, control owners, and current “AI” activity.

You will not be asked for account numbers, vendor files, or anything that should not leave your organisation.

## Section: What you will have afterwards

1. **A readiness level from 0 to 4**  
   From “no designed agent layer” to “governed agents with operating measures and a change process”.

2. **A gap map**  
   Process, ownership, controls, evidence, measurement, and change — marked present, partial, or missing.

3. **A recommended next step**  
   Wait. Map one process. Buy Starter. Buy Professional. Or apply for a Custom Blueprint. The diagnostic is allowed to tell you not to buy.

4. **Language you can reuse**  
   A short paragraph you can paste into a note to a controller or CFO without adding a savings claim.

## Section: The five levels (plain)

| Level | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Unscoped | AI is a topic. No agent has a written job. |
| 1 | Tool-led | A vendor or model is in trial. Ownership and fences are unclear. |
| 2 | Designed | One agent has purpose, fence, and a human owner. |
| 3 | Governed | Controls, evidence, and a review cadence exist for that agent. |
| 4 | Operating | Measures are read on a schedule. Change control exists. Scale is deliberate. |

Most teams that “have AI in AP” are at 0 or 1. That is not an insult. It is a starting coordinate.

## Section: What this diagnostic does not do

- It does not calculate savings or ROI.

If someone needs those, they need their systems integrator, auditor, or bank — not this file.
