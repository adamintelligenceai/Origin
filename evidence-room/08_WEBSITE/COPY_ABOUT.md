## Section: Why this exists

Finance is being offered a great deal of assistance and very little responsibility.

The assistance looks like chat, capture, matching, and “agents”. The responsibility still sits with the controller, the AP lead, the shared-services supervisor, and the auditor who will ask for evidence.

Evidence Room exists to make that responsibility *designed*. Not theatrical. Not guaranteed. Designed.

---

## Section: Brand idea

A room in which a claim about an agent has to survive process, control, and measurement.

If it cannot survive those three, it does not ship as a story on this site, and it should not ship in your operation.

## Section: Voice

Intelligent. Concise. Credible. Executive. Specific. Non-hype.

We prefer nouns from the operation: invoice, exception, fence, steward, evidence pack. We do not prefer “unlock”, “supercharge”, “10x”, or “AI-powered”.

## Section: Aesthetic

Institutional investment research. Bloomberg terminal discipline. Stripe and Linear clarity. Palantir seriousness. McKinsey structure without McKinsey fog.

No robots. No neon brains. No purple gradients.

## Section: What we sell, and what we do not

**We sell**

**We do not sell**

- ERP or AP software
- Payment processing

We do not replace the stack. We help you design the governed agent layer across it.
