- You need a workshop, not just a reading list.
- An executive or audit committee will be briefed.

## Section: What you receive

Everything in Professional, and then:

### Workshop

A facilitated agenda you run yourselves. Timed sessions: live-process mapping, first-agent selection, fence writing, control attachment, measure selection, stop conditions. It is a working session, not a keynote.

### Training

Short modules for the roles that actually touch the work:

- Agent steward (day-to-day owner)
- Control owner
- Reviewer / exception handler
- Executive reader

Each module says what that person must be able to do, not what they should believe about AI.

### Implementation

A sequenced plan from “one mapped invoice class” to “one governed agent in a limited production fence”. Includes entry criteria, exit criteria, and pause rules. It does not include a vendor implementation or a cutover of your ERP.

### Change management

Notes for the people whose work will move: what gets lighter, what gets more precise, what must not be silently removed. Written for AP supervisors, not for a transformation office.

### Executive pack

A briefing an executive can read in twelve minutes: what is being proposed, what is out of scope, which operating measures will be read, what would cause a pause, what is explicitly not being promised (savings, fraud detection, compliance, accuracy, autonomous payments, ROI).
