## How to use this diagnostic

1. Assign a single facilitator (usually Head of AP or Controller).  
2. Answer all **35 questions** honestly. Use “partial” when practice exists but is inconsistent.  
3. Score each question **1–5** (see scoring model).  
4. Calculate dimension averages and overall maturity level.  
5. Plot the opportunity heatmap.  
6. Complete the baseline KPI worksheet with **your** numbers only.  
7. Draft the business-case starter (inputs, not commitments).  
8. Export or copy the scorecard for email exchange with Finance leadership and Audit.

**Time required:** 60–90 minutes with one process owner; 2–3 hours if multi-entity.

## Scoring model

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **1** | Absent or ad hoc — no documented practice |
| **2** | Emerging — informal practice; depends on individuals |
| **3** | Defined — documented; applied inconsistently |
| **4** | Managed — measured, owned, and reviewed on a cadence |
| **5** | Optimising — continuously improved; control evidence retained |

**Dimension score** = arithmetic mean of its questions (round to one decimal).  
**Overall score** = mean of the six dimension scores.

### Maturity levels (overall)

| Level | Overall score | Label | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 1.0–1.9 | Foundational | Stabilise process and data before agent pilots |
| **2** | 2.0–2.9 | Structured | Define one bounded use case; supervised design only |
| **3** | 3.0–3.9 | Controlled | Ready for Starter-tier governed agent design |
| **4** | 4.0–4.4 | Instrumented | Ready for Pro OS install and multi-metric reviews |
| **5** | 4.5–5.0 | Governed | Ready for Team-scale change and responsibility progression |

**Gate rule:** No agent promotion (increased autonomy) if any of Controls, Governance, or Data scores below **3.0**, regardless of overall level.

## Opportunity heatmap instructions

Create a 2×2 (or 3×3) grid for candidate agent work:

**X-axis — Process clarity** (Low → High): How well documented and stable is the work?  
**Y-axis — Control risk** (Low → High): What is the downside of a wrong agent action?

| Quadrant | Placement rule | Recommended stance |
|---|---|---|
| High clarity / Low risk | First pilots | Starter design; supervised operation |
| High clarity / High risk | Second wave | Design with hard stops; no autonomy until Level 4 metrics hold |
| Low clarity / Low risk | Process fix first | Map and standardise before agent JD |
| Low clarity / High risk | Do not agentise | Human-owned until process and controls mature |

**Plot at least five candidate activities**, e.g.: invoice intake triage, validation, matching exceptions, supplier inquiry response drafting (with SoR citation), duplicate flags, approval packet assembly, payment **proposal review** (never silent payment release), vendor statement recon assist, AP reporting drafts, root-cause clustering.

Mark each plot with: Activity name · Monthly volume (est.) · Current human hours (est.) · Heatmap quadrant.

## Ten-agent opportunity overview

Use this as a **menu for later design**, not a commitment to deploy ten agents. Patterns align to the Evidence Room agent library.

| # | Agent opportunity | Typical human pain | Readiness precondition | Suggested first mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Invoice Intake | Unstructured mail / portal mix | Complete packet definition (A3) | Classify + route; no posting |
| 2 | Invoice Validation | Field incompleteness / policy checks | Data retrieval (B4) | Flag fails; human resolve |
| 3 | Matching | Tolerance and PO linkage exceptions | Match rules documented (A4) | Triage exceptions only |
| 4 | Exception Triage | Exception backlog as inbox chaos | Taxonomy exists or will be created | Classify + assign owner |
| 5 | Goods Receipt assist | Missing GR blocking match | Receipt artefacts retrievable | Evidence chase prompts |
| 6 | PO Quality | Bad POs creating AP noise | Procurement liaison named | Flag; do not rewrite PO alone |
| 7 | Approval packet | Slow cycles; incomplete packs | Approval matrix current (C2) | Assemble evidence; never self-approve |
| 8 | Supplier resolution | Inquiry load; status confusion | Single SoR for status (B6) | Draft replies with citations |
| 9 | Duplicate / anomaly flag | Leakage fear without method | Duplicate checks baseline (B2) | Flag only; human confirm |
| 10 | AP Manager orchestrator | Multi-queue coordination | Governance sponsor (F1) | Status synthesis; no payments |

**Defer by default:** silent payment execution, master-data writes, approval impersonation, ERP configuration changes.
