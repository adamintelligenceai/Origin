# Evidence Room — AP AI Readiness Diagnostic

**Tier 0 · Free lead magnet**  
**Time:** 25–40 minutes  
**Best completed by:** Head of AP, Finance Transformation lead, or Controller — with Systems input for data/systems questions.

---

## Why this exists

Most AP AI programmes fail because organisations buy tools before they know whether their process, data, controls, and operating model can absorb agents. This diagnostic produces a **readiness score**, a **maturity level**, and a first-pass **opportunity heatmap** you can take into a steering discussion on Monday.

This is not a vendor scorecard and not a promise of ROI.

---

## How to score

Score each question **1–5** using the scale in the workbook (`spreadsheets/ER_AP_Maturity_Diagnostic.xlsx`) or the abbreviated scale below:

| Score | Meaning |
|------:|---------|
| 1 | Absent / unknown |
| 2 | Ad hoc / heroic |
| 3 | Defined but inconsistent |
| 4 | Managed and measured |
| 5 | Optimising with evidence loops |

**Total** = sum of 35 scores (max 175)  
**Maturity index** = Total ÷ 35

| Index | Level | Interpretation |
|------:|-------|----------------|
| 1.0–1.9 | Initial | Experiment carefully; do not automate payment-adjacent work |
| 2.0–2.9 | Developing | Build foundations: taxonomy, baseline KPIs, one shadow agent |
| 3.0–3.9 | Defined | Ready for governed pilots on well-bounded agents |
| 4.0–4.4 | Managed | Scale agent portfolio with responsibility gates |
| 4.5–5.0 | Optimising | Orchestrated agent workforce with continuous evidence |

## Domains (35 questions)

Use the Excel workbook for full answer anchors. Domains:

1. **Strategy** (Q1–2) — sponsor, OKR linkage  
2. **Process** (Q3–6) — documentation, taxonomy, PO/GR discipline  
3. **Data** (Q7–9) — capture quality, master data, joinability  
4. **Systems** (Q10–11) — landscape, audit trail  
5. **Controls** (Q12–15) — SoD, accountability, change control, incident response  
6. **People** (Q16–18) — capacity, training, change  
7. **Measurement** (Q19–22) — cost/invoice, STP, FP/FN, duplicates  
8. **Agents** (Q23–26) — production use, charters, autonomy, shadow mode  
9. **Suppliers** (Q27–28) — invoice quality, statements  
10. **Close** (Q29–30) — completeness, GRNI/accruals  
11. **Risk** (Q31–32) — bank changes, payment proposal review  
12. **Economics** (Q33) — business case quality  
13. **Governance** (Q34–35) — steering, privacy/security

## Opportunity heatmap (after scoring)

Plot each domain average (1–5) and prioritise agents where:

- **Process + Data ≥ 3** and **Controls ≥ 3** → candidate for early pilot  
- **Controls < 3** → fix controls before raising autonomy  
- **Measurement < 3** → instrument before claiming benefits  
- **Risk domain weak** → keep Payment Proposal Review at Observe/Recommend only

### Ten-agent opportunity overview (starter lens)

| Priority band | Agents to consider first | Why |
|---------------|--------------------------|-----|
| Often first | Exception Triage, Goods Receipt, Invoice Validation | High volume, clear human oversight |
| Early | Duplicate & Anomaly (flagging only), Internal Follow-Up, Approval | Strong detective value |
| After foundations | Matching, PO Quality, Supplier Resolution | Needs policy clarity |
| Later / strict gates | Payment Proposal Review, AP Close | Higher blast radius |
| Portfolio stage | Reporting, Root Cause, Orchestrator | Needs multi-agent evidence |
