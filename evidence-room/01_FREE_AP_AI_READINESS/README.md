# AP Agent Readiness Diagnostic

**SKU:** ER-DIAG · $0  
**Lockup:** EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS  
**Brand idea:** Proof before permission.  
**Time:** About 45–60 minutes with last-quarter facts.  
**Output:** A maturity band (L1–L5), a heatmap, and a suggested first wave at observe/recommend.

This pack is a reading aid. It is not a purchase of the AP Agent OS. It does not certify readiness, compliance, investment return, fraud coverage, or accounting quality. It does not connect to your ERP. Do not paste production invoices, bank details, or employee records into any Evidence Room form.

---

## What you do

1. Read `DIAGNOSTIC_GUIDE.md`. Answer all 36 questions. Score 0–4 with evidence you can reopen.
2. Place the organisation on `MATURITY_MODEL.md`.
3. Use `10_AGENT_OPPORTUNITY_OVERVIEW.md` to see which roles are design options — not a mandate to stand sixteen agents.
4. If you need language for a sponsor, use `BUSINESS_CASE_STARTER.md`. It will not write a savings guarantee.

---

## What the score means

A high score on technology and a 0 on payment control is not “mostly ready.” It is a control gap with software nearby.

The suggested Wave 1 is always the same six names unless you lack owners: Invoice Intake, Invoice Validation, Matching, Exception Triage, Duplicate & Anomaly, Orchestrator — at L0 or L1.

---

## What this pack refuses

- A single “AI readiness” number that hides a payment-hold failure.
- A recommendation to auto-release payments.
- A comparison that treats Northline Industrials (fictional) as your benchmark.
- A certificate you can show an auditor as sufficiency.
