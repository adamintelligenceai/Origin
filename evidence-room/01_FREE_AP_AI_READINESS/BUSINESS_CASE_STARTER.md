# Business-case starter language

**Product:** Evidence Room — AP Agent OS  
**Audience:** Transformation lead, AP Manager, Controller writing a short sponsor note  
**Use:** Paste and edit. Replace brackets. Delete any sentence you cannot defend from your own diagnostic.  
**Not:** A savings model, an ROI calculator, or Evidence Room’s forecast of your results.

Professional contains a full model you can populate with *your* inputs (`03_AP_AGENT_OS_PRO/Business_Case/`). This file is language only.

---

## 1. Rules for this note

1. Lead with the layer you lack, not the money you hope to free.
2. Name the human holds in the same paragraph you mention agents.
3. If you cite external research, use the labelled citations in §6 and no others.
4. Do not attach a payback period.
5. Do not use Northline’s figures as yours. Northline Industrials is fictional.
6. If the sponsor requires a guaranteed savings number as a condition of starting, this product is the wrong artefact to put in the pack.

---

## 2. Subject line options

- AP agent layer — diagnostic reading and proposed Wave 1
- Observe/recommend agents in AP — named owners, human payment hold
- Request: two-week shadow on [path], not a platform replacement

Avoid: “AI ROI,” “touchless AP business case,” “fraud reduction case.”

---

## 3. One-page note (canonical)

**To:** [CFO / CAO / VP Finance]  
**From:** [name, role]  
**Date:** [date]  
**Re:** Accounts Payable agent layer — readiness reading

We were asked to consider AI in Accounts Payable. I sat a structured diagnostic of the *agent layer* — whether we have named roles, inspectable evidence, a permission ladder, and a written human hold on payment — not a review of our ERP.

**Reading.** We are at **maturity [L1–L5 — name]**. [One sentence from the diagnostic band.] This is not a finding that the AP team is weak and not a compliance certificate.

**Holds.** Payment authorisation and vendor bank-detail change [are / are not] evidenced as human in both policy and system. I will not recommend any helper that releases a payment file. Duplicate and anomaly signals, if we raise them, will be hypotheses for [Controls Lead name], not fraud findings.

**Proposal.** Charter six agents at observe or recommend on [path / entity]: Invoice Intake, Invoice Validation, Matching, Exception Triage, Duplicate & Anomaly, Orchestrator. Owners: [six names]. For two weeks the agents write shadow records only. Operators work as they do today.

**Success at 30 days.** Every in-scope invoice has a work object; validation fails are coded; a sample of match worksheets can be reopened; flags have a recorded human outcome; the autonomy register lists every live helper at observe/recommend; payment release still follows [our existing dual-approval procedure]. I am not proposing a cost-takeout target.

**What we would licence.** [Starter $79 / Professional $199 / Team $499] — a method and toolkit (Evidence Room — AP Agent OS). It does not replace [ERP / capture / payment tool]. It does not connect to our systems. Implementation stays with us.

**What I am not asking.** Permission to auto-post, auto-pay, or report a guaranteed return.

**Ask.** [Approve the two-week shadow / name the Controls Lead / book Internal Audit as observer on day 30 / approve the licence].

## 4. Extra paragraphs you may add — and those you may not

### Allowed

**Stack stays.** We keep [ERP]. We keep [capture vendor]. We keep [payment procedure]. The work is to design the layer that sits across them.

**Informal risk.** We already have [copilot / RPA / vendor AI feature] touching invoices without a charter. The diagnostic treats that as informal activity. Listing it is the first control action.

**Industry context (optional, labelled).** Independent AP research is sometimes cited by vendors: Ardent Partners *State of ePayables 2024*, as cited by Tipalti, reported best-in-class electronic cost per invoice of $2.78 versus $12.88 for laggards, and cycle times of 3.1 versus 17.4 days. Ardent *AP Metrics that Matter 2025*, as cited by Tipalti, reported an exception-rate comparison of 22% to 9%. These are **vendor-originated citations of independent research**. They are not our baseline and not a target I am committing to.

**Adoption context (optional, labelled).** McKinsey’s November 2025 finance AI work reported that 44% of 102 CFOs used gen AI for five or more use cases (from 7%), and that 65% were increasing gen AI investment. McKinsey *State of AI 2025* (survey June–July 2025, n=1,993) reported 88% regular AI use, roughly two-thirds not scaling, 62% experimenting with agents, 23% scaling an agent somewhere in the enterprise, and 39% reporting any EBIT impact. Workflow redesign is noted as a high-performer differentiator. I cite this as context for *why the question landed on my desk*, not as evidence that an AP agent layer will move EBIT.

### Not allowed

- “This will save $X.”
- “Payback in Y months.”
- “We will detect fraud.”
- “This makes us SOX compliant / audit-ready.”
- “The agent will be accurate.”
- “We will move to autonomous payments.”
- “Northline achieved …”

## 5. Email to Internal Audit (observer, not operator)

> I am proposing a two-week shadow of six named AP agents at observe/recommend. Payment release remains [our procedure]. I would like Audit to observe packets at day 30 — source, extract, worksheet or coded fail, flag outcome — not to operate the agents. Flags will be labelled as hypotheses. I am not asking you to certify the design.

## 7. If they ask “what’s the ROI?”

Reply:

> I will not put a return number on this request. We can, later, record cost per thousand invoices for the layer and, if we choose, build a model from our own baseline. Evidence Room’s professional pack includes a calculator specification that uses our inputs and three cases — conservative, base, upside — and still does not promise the output. If a number is required to start, it must be ours, labelled as a management judgement, and it cannot be a condition I ask Evidence Room to guarantee.

Then stop talking.
