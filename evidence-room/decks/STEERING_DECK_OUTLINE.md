# Steering Committee Pack (Template)

## Pack contents
1. One-page decision brief
2. Maturity score & heatmap
3. Proposed first agent + autonomy level
4. Control posture summary
5. KPI baseline & gates
6. Investment ask (tools/time) with scenario ranges
7. Risks & kill criteria
8. 90-day roadmap

## Decision asks (examples)
- Approve shadow for Agent X at Level 1
- Approve entitlement set (read-only)
- Approve sampling budget / QA time
- Do **not** approve payment-path execution rights
