## Naming conventions

- Agent files: `AGENT_NN_NAME.md` with a stable numeric ID.
- Exception codes: `EX-XXX` as published in the taxonomy. Do not invent synonyms in working papers.
- Autonomy: `L0`–`L4` with the level name on first use in a document.
- Northline: always labelled fictional or illustrative in first mention of a figure.
