# Customer Fulfilment & Onboarding

## Digital product fulfilment (Lemon Squeezy)

1. Customer completes checkout (or free email capture for Diagnostic).
2. Lemon Squeezy delivers signed download link to ZIP for the purchased tier.
3. Post-purchase email sends START HERE path (see sequence Email 1 pattern).
4. Upsell: Diagnostic → Starter; Starter → Pro; Pro → Team; Team → Custom application.
