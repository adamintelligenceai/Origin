# Financial Model — Evidence Room (Vendor View)

**Purpose:** Internal planning for digital-product economics.  
**Not:** Investor-grade model; not a promise of revenue.  
**MoR fee assumption:** Lemon Squeezy ecommerce **5% + $0.50** per transaction ([pricing](https://lemonsqueezy.com/pricing) — confirm live; other fees may apply).

---

## 1. Unit economics (illustrative)

| SKU | Price | MoR fee ≈ | Net before other COGS |
|-----|-------|-----------|------------------------|
| Starter | $79 | 5%×79+0.50 = $4.45 | ~$74.55 |
| Pro | $199 | $10.45 | ~$188.55 |
| Team | $499 | $25.45 | ~$473.55 |
| Blueprint | $2,000 mid | $100.50 | ~$1,899.50* |

\*Blueprint has delivery labour COGS — see below.

Free diagnostic: $0 revenue; email tool cost only.

## 4. Cost categories (opex)

- Founder time (content, support)  
- Design / PDF packaging  
- Email + site hosting  
- LLM for internal drafting (not customer inference SLA)  
- Counsel / tax review (one-time + refresh)  
- Marketplace fees if Etsy derivatives used  

Marginal cost of digital SKUs after creation ≈ MoR + support minutes.

## 6. Path to SaaS (economics only)

Digital products validate willingness-to-pay and messaging. SaaS later introduces hosting, tenancy, support SLAs, and COGS different from downloads — see `SCALE_ROADMAP.md`. Do not underprice digital if it anchors future SaaS ARPU expectations downward without strategy.
