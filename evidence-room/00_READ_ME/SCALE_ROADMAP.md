# Scale Roadmap — Evidence Room — AP Agent OS

**Lockup:** EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS  
**Date:** 20 September 2026  
**Status:** Publisher strategy. Not a customer promise, not a valuation model, not a build ticket.  
**Rule:** **First make AP exceptional.** Future finance modules are named so we do not pretend they do not exist. **Do not build them.**

---

## 1. Destination, in one line

A licensed AP digital product, then a thin services layer, then a recurring subscription around the same OS, then — only if AP is genuinely exceptional — an Evidence Room **Finance Agent** platform. The platform is a later company. It is not this edition.

---

## 2. Why this order

McKinsey’s independent 2025 research (see `09_RESEARCH/RESEARCH_LEDGER.md`) describes wide AI use, thin scaling, and workflow redesign as a high-performer differentiator. That is **context**, not a warrant to sprawl into AR, treasury, and FP&A before Accounts Payable has a complete, governed, teachable operating system.

Sprawl is how unofficial permission returns: a new module, a new copilot, no packet.

The brand idea — **proof before permission** — applies to the company as well as to agents. The company does not receive permission to become a platform until the AP product has produced evidence: repeatable fulfilment, clean claims, named users who finished Wave 1, and a support load we can stand.

---

## 3. The path (four stages)

```
AP digital product  →  services  →  recurring subscription  →  Finance Agent platform
        ▲
        └── stay here until AP is exceptional
```

| Stage | What we sell | What we do not sell yet | Exit evidence (permission to proceed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 — AP digital product** | Diagnostic $0; Starter $79; Professional $199; Team $499 | Hosted agents, ERP connectors, AR/Close/Treasury modules | Fulfilment works. Claims stay inside the ledger. Buyers finish Friday-to-Monday. Refunds and support are survivable. |
| **S2 — Services** | Custom Blueprint $1,500–$3,000; optional office hours; Team workshops as facilitation of the **same** OS | A general AI-in-finance consultancy; implementation of SAP | Blueprint hours are priced and capped. Deliverables are charters and registers, not unpaid software. No savings guarantees enter the SOW. |
| **S3 — Recurring subscription** | Annual update window, office-hours pool, shared licence refresh, later a member library — **still files and facilitation**, not a runtime | A SaaS that posts invoices | Demand is real (repeat Professional/Team, asked-for updates). Lemon Squeezy subscription extra (+0.5%) and product confusion are accepted **knowingly**. Counsel refreshes licence language. |
| **S4 — Evidence Room Finance Agent platform** | A later hosted control plane for work objects, autonomy registers, and evidence URIs **across** the buyer’s stack | A new ERP | Only after S1 is exceptional. Separate product decision. Separate security, privacy, and payment-boundary engineering. **Not this year of work.** |

S1 is the current company. S2 is already sketched (`05_CUSTOM_BLUEPRINT/`). S3 and S4 are **gated**. Skipping a gate is a brand defect.

## 4. Stage 1 — Make AP exceptional (current work)

### 4.1 What “exceptional” means (no savings number)

AP Agent OS is exceptional when **all** of the following are true:

1. **Complete method.** Sixteen agents, L0–L4, human-vs-agent table, exception atlas, governance cadence, testing path, Friday-to-Monday journey — internally consistent.  
2. **Honest commercial surface.** Site, store, and emails say we do not replace the stack and do not promise ROI, fraud detection, compliance, or payment safety.  
3. **Evidence discipline.** Every external figure traces to `09_RESEARCH/RESEARCH_LEDGER.md`.  
4. **Fulfilment.** A buyer can download, open START HERE, and do useful work without a call.  
5. **Support load.** hello@ answers fulfilment and licence questions. Professional does not secretly become consulting.  
6. **Legal hygiene.** Counsel has seen licence, privacy, refund, and the Evidence Room LLC collision. Domain is registered.  
7. **Designed artefacts exist** for the things we already specified in Markdown: diagnostic worksheet, steering slides, scorecard workbook, website. Until those exist, “exceptional” is **authoring-complete**, not **go-to-market-complete**.

### 4.2 What we build in S1

- The licensed zip (folders `00`–`05` as sold).  
- Publisher packs `06`, `07`, `09`, `10`.  
- Lemon Squeezy store for one-time SKUs. **No LS subscription SKU at launch** (avoids +0.5% and SaaS confusion).  
- evidenceroom.ai (or `apagentos.com` fallback — `09_RESEARCH/DOMAIN_AND_TRADEMARK_NOTE.md`).

### 4.3 What we refuse in S1

- Hosted invoice processing.  
- ERP connectors.  
- An “autonomous payment” feature.  
- Marketplace prompt-pack positioning.  
- New finance domains (see §6).

## 5. Stage 2 — Services (only as a productised overlay)

Services exist to **apply** the OS, not to invent a second method.

| Offer | Job | Hard edge |
|---|---|---|
| Custom Blueprint | Facilitate *their* layer on *their* stack | Application, quoted $1,500–$3,000. Not an instant custom PDF. |
| Office hours (optional later) | Unblock START HERE | Time-boxed. No live payment-file review. |
| Team workshop | One-day working session from the Team pack | Uses `04_AP_AGENT_OS_TEAM/`. Not a transformation programme. |

**Economics reminder** (from the publisher model, not a customer ROI): a Blueprint sold at the low end that consumes many long sessions can have worse labour economics than several Professional licences. Track hours. Do not let S2 eat S1.

**Do not** staff a delivery factory for SAP implementations. That is someone else’s company.

---

## 6. Stage 3 — Recurring subscription (later, conscious)

Recurring is for **continuity of the method**, not for a runtime:

- 12-month update entitlement becoming a true annual membership  
- New agent-charter examples, exception codes, and recertification packs  
- Scheduled office hours  
- Team-seat true-up  

Launch today is a **licence**, not SaaS. Turning on Lemon Squeezy subscriptions adds fee drag and implies hosting we do not operate. Do it only with a written product decision, updated Terms, and a page that still says: files and method, not a login that posts invoices.

---

## 7. Stage 4 — Evidence Room Finance Agent platform (do not build)

A future platform would be a **control plane**: work-object IDs, evidence URIs, autonomy register, SLA clocks — sitting **across** ERP and suites. It would still not be the system of record for accounting or cash.

It would require: security review, data-processing terms, tenant isolation, and a re-statement of the payment ceiling in software, not only in Markdown.

**Permission is withheld.** There is no prototype ticket in this edition.

---

## 8. Future modules — named so we can refuse them

These are **possible later domains** for the same operating-system idea. They are not a roadmap we will staff.

| Module | Why people will ask | Why we will not build it now |
|---|---|---|
| **AR** | Symmetric “agentic” story; Order-to-Cash vendors already exist | Different objects, different fraud story, different cash application politics. Dilutes AP excellence. |
| **Close** | Agent 13 already touches AP close | Full record-to-report is a Controller product, not an AP add-on tab. |
| **Treasury** | Agent 12 already annotates payment proposals | Treasury agents that “optimise cash” collapse into payment authority. Hard no until AP payment holds are boringly true in the field. |
| **Reconciliation** | Agent 11 is a wedge | Subledger-to-GL and bank rec are their own control environments. |
| **Controls** | We already produce evidence | A GRC platform is a different category. We produce packets; we do not declare controls sufficient. |
| **FP&A** | McKinsey’s finance-AI article spends time here | Forecast agents are a different buyer and a different error cost. Not AP. |

**DO NOT BUILD** these modules. Do not open folder `11_AR_AGENT_OS`. Do not sell an “Evidence Room Suite” SKU. Do not staff prompt packs for cash application “while we are at it.”

If a Blueprint applicant asks for AR or treasury, the answer is: **we make AP exceptional first.** Decline or stay inside AP Agent 01–16.

---

## 9. What may be reused later (without building modules)

When — if — a later edition opens another domain, it may reuse:

- Autonomy ladder L0–L4  
- Proof-before-permission gates  
- Human-vs-agent decision classes  
- Orchestrator as work-object system of record  
- Evidence standard and research ledger discipline  
- “Design the layer. Keep the stack.”

It may not reuse AP exception codes, Agent 12’s payment ceiling language as if it applied to collections, or Northline’s payables volumes as an AR TAM.

Reuse of the **method** is allowed in the mind. Reuse of the **SKU** is a new product decision.

## 11. Kill criteria (demote the company plan)

Stop advancing a stage — or retire a draft module — if:

- Copy drifts into ROI, fraud, or touchless promises.  
- Blueprint becomes unpaid software implementation.  
- A “platform” spike starts before AP fulfilment is boring.  
- Counsel flags the master brand and we refuse to rename.  
- Anyone staffs AR because a competitor slide mentioned it.

Stopping is a successful control outcome. The OS says the same to agents.
