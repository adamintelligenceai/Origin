# Scale Roadmap — Evidence Room

**Horizon:** AP digital products → services → SaaS → broader Finance Agent Platform  
**Constraint:** Do not build non-AP verticals until AP motion is proven.

---

## Stage 0 — Now (v1.0)

**Offer:** Free diagnostic · $79 · $199 · $499 · Blueprint $1.5–3k  
**Motion:** Content → diagnostic → paid OS → Team → Blueprint  
**Systems:** Lemon Squeezy · manual fulfilment · Notion/Drive evidence room  
**Success signals:** Repeatable Pro/Team purchases; Blueprint utilisation without delivery collapse; claim hygiene intact

---

## Stage 1 — Harden AP commercial engine

| Work | Outcome |
|------|---------|
| Design-partner interviews | Messaging proof |
| PDF/XLSX packaging polish | Higher perceived value |
| Email + LinkedIn operating rhythm | Predictable pipeline |
| Support playbooks | Low-touch delivery |
| Legal counsel pass | Launch confidence |

**Exit:** 90 days of real conversion data vs financial model placeholders.

---

## Stage 2 — Productised services scale

| Work | Outcome |
|------|---------|
| Associate facilitator bench | Blueprint capacity |
| Fixed packages inside $1.5–3k band | Margin control |
| Team as default for multi-stakeholder | Attach rate |

**Non-goal:** Bespoke software builds.

---

## Stage 3 — AP Agent SaaS (adjacent)

Only if validated:

- Charter + evidence ledger hosted  
- Scorecard productised  
- Exception taxonomy workspace  
- Integration **read** paths (not payment auth)

**Pricing intuition:** Anchor above Team; do not give away OS IP as freemium without strategy.

---

## Stage 4 — Finance Agent Platform (later)

Expand **architecture patterns** (levels, evidence, four metrics, shadow/pilot) to:

AR · Close · Reconciliations · Treasury · Reporting · Controls · Tax · FP&A · Audit  

**Sequencing rule:** New domain requires its own research ledger, competitor landscape, and specialist agent library — do not clone AP names onto foreign processes.

---

## Explicit non-goals (all stages near-term)

- Autonomous payments  
- Being an ERP  
- Guaranteed fraud product  
- Compliance certification mill  

---

## Decision gates

| Gate | Question |
|------|----------|
| G1 | Is Pro differentiation clear in win/loss notes? |
| G2 | Is Blueprint delivery ≤ planned hours? |
| G3 | Do customers ask for hosted evidence/scorecards unprompted? |
| G4 | Is support load compatible with SaaS ambitions? |

Proceed stage-by-stage; skip nothing that protects claim hygiene and payment-boundary principles.
