# Quick Start — Buyer Journey

**Product:** Evidence Room AP Agent OS  
**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-03-20  

Twelve steps from assessment to expanded agent responsibility. Complete them in order unless a prior tier already covered the work.

---

## Journey map

```
Assess → Design → Prove → Govern → Measure → Expand Responsibility
```

| Steps | Phase | Typical tier |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Assess | Free |
| 4–5 | Design | Starter |
| 6–7 | Prove | Starter → Pro |
| 8–9 | Govern | Pro |
| 10 | Measure | Pro |
| 11–12 | Expand Responsibility | Team / Custom |

---

## Step 1 — Confirm the problem is agent authority, not missing software

Inventory your current stack (ERP, AP automation, payments). If capture/workflow/payment gaps dominate, fix those with platform vendors first. Evidence Room applies when the gap is **how AI agents will be scoped, evidenced, and promoted** across tools you already own.

*Checkpoint:* One sentence — “We need an operating model for agents, not another AP suite.”

---

## Step 2 — Baseline with published industry context (not invented targets)

Record your internal baselines for cost/invoice, cycle time, exception rate, and STP if available. For external context only, use ledgered Ardent figures:

- **2025** (Ardent / Bottomline): avg cost **$9.84**, cycle **8.2 days**, exception **18.4%**, STP **35.4%**; BIC cost **$2.65**, exception **11.1%**, STP **51.0%**.  
- **2024** (Ardent / Medius distribution): avg cost **$9.40**, cycle **9.15 days**, exception **14.0%**, STP **32.6%**.

Do not set Evidence Room ROI slides to “we will hit BIC” without your own measured path.

*Checkpoint:* Internal baseline sheet started; sources labelled by year.

---

## Step 3 — Run AP AI Readiness (Free)

Open `01_FREE_AP_AI_READINESS/`. Score process maturity, data quality, control posture, exception load, and AI governance readiness. Identify **one** candidate use case with clear boundaries (document types, value thresholds, systems touched).

*Checkpoint:* Readiness score + single use-case card.

---

## Step 4 — Answer the seven product questions for that use case

Using Master Prompt §5, draft answers for the candidate agent: permitted work, evidence, exceptions, KPIs, promotion rules, failure handling, audit trail.

*Checkpoint:* No blank answers; ambiguities listed explicitly.

---

## Step 5 — Purchase and complete AP Agent Starter ($297)

In `02_AP_AGENT_STARTER/`, turn the seven answers into a governed agent design: role card, input/output contract, exception routes, and Starter KPI sheet. Keep scope to **one** agent.

*Checkpoint:* Starter pack complete; human owner named for exceptions.

---

## Step 6 — Pilot with evidence, not autonomy theatre

Run the agent in a supervised mode. Capture every exception class. Prefer missing a few STP points over silent wrong postings. Align with Ardent’s reality that exceptions remain material (2025 peer exception **18.4%**; BIC **11.1%**) — design for them.

*Checkpoint:* Pilot log with outcomes and exception counts.

---

## Step 7 — Prove the business case with honest maths

Use Pro `Business_Case` artefacts (or Starter economics sheet) with **your** volumes × **your** baselines. You may cite that automation can cost **50–80% less** than manual (Ardent 2024) as industry context — not as a guaranteed customer saving. Include Lemon Squeezy fees if reselling or packaging digital goods internally is relevant to your finance model (platform **5% + $0.50**, plus documented add-ons).

*Checkpoint:* Case signed by Controller (or economic buyer) for continued investment.

---

## Step 8 — Install Pro process maps and controls ($997)

In `03_AP_AGENT_OS_PRO/`, map end-to-end AP touchpoints the agent affects. Attach control objectives (segregation, approval thresholds, master-data change bans). Wire stop conditions for policy conflict.

*Checkpoint:* Process map + control matrix linked to the agent ID.

---

## Step 9 — Stand up governance and responsibility progression

Complete Governance packs: RACI, promotion/demotion rules, change control for prompts/policies, audit evidence retention. Responsibility expands only when KPI thresholds are met for a defined period.

*Checkpoint:* Governance charter approved; first promotion criteria dated.

---

## Step 10 — Operate the KPI OS

Use `KPI_Measurement` definitions: STP contribution, exception rate by taxonomy class, cycle-time contribution, supplier-inquiry deflection (industry context: **21.9%** of time on supplier inquiries — Ardent 2025), and evidence completeness. Review on a fixed cadence.

*Checkpoint:* First KPI review held; actions logged.

---

## Step 11 — Scale people and agents (Team, $2,997)

Use `04_AP_AGENT_OS_TEAM/` workshops and training so AP analysts, approvers, and auditors share one language. Run change management for role shifts. Brief executives with the Executive pack — cite industry AI intent (**44%** adoption; **>75%** expected within 12 months; **65%** hope AI increases automation — Ardent 2025) without overstating your internal state.

*Checkpoint:* Trained cohort + second agent candidate selected under the same OS.

---

## Step 12 — Expand responsibility (Team / Custom)

Promote the first agent only per charter rules. For multi-entity or complex landscapes, engage **Custom Blueprint** (from **$15,000**) for workforce design and a 90-day responsibility roadmap. Never expand scope and autonomy in the same change window.

*Checkpoint:* Written promotion decision; updated agent register; next review date set.
