## What this suite is

Evidence Room publishes **AP Agent OS** — an operating system, in the management sense, for designing, governing, measuring, and scaling AI agents across Accounts Payable.

It sits *across* the stack you already run: ERP, AP automation, OCR or e-invoice networks, bank portals, and shared services. It does not replace Coupa, SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Tipalti, Medius, or the bank. It does not ingest invoices. It does not post journals. It does not pay suppliers.

What it does is make the agent layer *designed* rather than improvised:

- a written job for each agent
- a fence (class, amount, vendor set, entity, system)
- a named human owner
- controls and evidence attached to the work
- operating measures you can read
- a way to pause, demote, or stop

The suite is a product family, not a transformation programme and not a model host.

## The Friday-to-Monday path

A serious owner can move from “we have been asked to add AI to AP” to “we have a fenced first agent and a decision” in four working sessions. That is the path. It is not a promise that production will be live on Monday. It is a promise that the work is sequenced.

| Session | When | Work | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Friday | START HERE 01–03 | Open the room. Take a readiness level. Draw the *live* path. | A sentence of purpose, a level, a map the exception staff recognise. |
| Saturday (or next working block) | 04–06 | Choose one invoice class. Write the purpose sentence. Write the fence and the escalation list. | A box the controller will read aloud. |
| Sunday (or next working block) | 07–09 | Attach three controls and evidence. Choose three operating measures. Run a historical-sample desk test. | You know what would have failed. |
| Monday | 10–12 | Limited production fence *or* a written hold/stop. Brief the executive reader. Decide scale, hold, or stop. | A decision in the change log. |

If Friday tells you to wait, you wait. The operating system is allowed to recommend not buying more and not switching an agent on.

The twelve steps live in `QUICK_START.md` and, in customer-facing form, in `06_SALES_AND_MARKETING/CUSTOMER_ONBOARDING.md`.

2. **Fraud detection** — or fraud prevention, AML, or sanctions screening.

5. **Autonomous payments** — or any instruction to a bank without a human control you own.
6. **ROI** — or payback, NPV, or “the toolkit pays for itself”.

This suite is also not:

- software that installs
- a connection to your ERP
- legal, tax, accounting, audit, or investment advice
- a replacement for your control environment, auditor, or bank mandate
- a certification of a person, a system, or a control

Ardent Partners (2024) reports that Best-in-Class AP organisations run at 78% lower cost, 82% faster cycle time, and 59% lower exceptions than their peers, with a 9% exception rate. Those are industry observations. They are not a forecast for this stack. Measure your own baseline.
