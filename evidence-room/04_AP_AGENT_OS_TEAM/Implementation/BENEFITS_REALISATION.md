**Companion:** `spreadsheets/ER_AP_Benefits_Tracker.xlsx` · Pro Business Case model

---

## 1. Rules

1. Baseline before claiming benefit.  
2. Separate operational outcomes from financial conversion.  
3. Apply realisation % — theoretical ≠ booked.  
4. Attribute carefully; avoid double counting with other programmes.  
5. No guaranteed savings language in exec materials.

## 4. Conversion worksheet (labour)

```
Δ minutes per invoice × annual volume ÷ 60 = hours freed
hours × loaded hourly rate = theoretical $
theoretical $ × realisation % = claimed benefit
```

Document where freed hours went: backlog burn-down · higher-value work · attrition backfill avoidance · other.
