**Principle:** Track evidence of improvement using **internal** baselines. Industry stats are reference columns only.

---

## 1. Benefits policy

1. No benefit is “claimed” without a formula, baseline, and measurement window.  
2. Leading indicators move first; lagging financial metrics later.  
3. Never convert Ardent peers (**$9.84** cost, **18.4%** exceptions, **35.4%** STP, BIC **$2.65**) into booked savings.  
4. Dis-benefits (rework, false flags, change friction) are tracked, not buried.
