5. How to facilitate without us

  1. Read Workshop/WORKSHOP_FACILITATION.md twice. Once for timing, once for failure modes.
  2. Issue PROCESS_OWNER_QUESTIONNAIRE.md and INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md three working days before the workshop. Collect raw notes, not polished decks.
  3. Print EXERCISES.md for tables. No slides required. A whiteboard beats a theme.
  4. Appoint a scribe who is not the facilitator. Artefacts are the product.
  5. Appoint a timekeeper who is allowed to cut a vendor story.
  6. End each block with a written object: map, sentence, fence, RACI row, measure list, stop list.

If the facilitator is also the AP Manager, that is allowed. If the facilitator is “transformation,” they may keep time; they may not own the fence.

6. What “implemented” means for Team

Not: sixteen agents, a model selected, a savings slide.

Yes:

  • Diagnostic compared, disagreements logged
  • One live map the floor recognises
  • One signed purpose sentence
  • One fence and escalation list
  • Three controls with owners and artefacts
  • Three sourced measures
  • Historical-sample plan dated
  • CFO brief delivered
  • Written go / hold / stop
  • Tracker opened with named tasks
  • Floor comms sent if go
