# Steering templates

**Evidence Room · Team · Executive**  
**Version:** 1.0 · September 2026  
**Use:** Working-group steering, not a transformation office theatre  
**Cadence:** Fortnightly while the first fence is live; monthly after it is boring  
**Length:** 30 minutes. If you need 90, the artefacts are not ready.

Agents do not attend. Vendors do not present unless the chair asked a specific question. Northline is fictional. No savings, fraud, compliance, or ROI claims.

---

## 1. Standing agenda (30 minutes)

| Min | Item | Owner | Artefact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Purpose sentence read aloud (unchanged unless a change ticket) | Steward | Brief v# |
| 3–8 | Fence integrity: volume in fence, breaches, pauses | Steward | Register + breach log |
| 8–14 | Operating measures (three). No vanity. | Steward | KPI card |
| 14–18 | Risk-control: R1, overrides, sample status | Control owner | Exception / incident list |
| 18–22 | Decisions needed (promote / hold / stop / change fence) | Chair | Decision log |
| 22–26 | What we will **not** claim this period | Chair | Spoken |
| 26–30 | Actions | Scribe | Tracker IDs |

**Chair.** Process owner or Controller. Not “AI lead” unless that person is the process owner.

**Quorum.** Steward + control owner. If either is missing, the meeting is a huddle, not steering. No promotions.

---

## 2. Decision log (copy each meeting)

| Date | Decision | Options considered | Chosen | Owner | Review date | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | Hold / limited continue / expand class / freeze / stop / other | | | | | No Level 3 in Team go-live |

Promotion to Level 2+ uses the Professional promotion pack. Steering cannot “agree to Level 3 in the room.”

---

## 3. RAG rules (families, not one colour)

Do not give the programme a single RAG. Colour **each family**.

| Family | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Activity | Population as fenced; completeness holding | Completeness late < 1 day | Completeness break ≥ 1 day |
| Operational | Measures moving as designed *or* honestly flat | Override-for-error rising | Silent posting; taxonomy fork |
| Financial | F unsigned, or F6 signed by FP&A | Hours observed, cost base unchanged (say so) | Unsigned “savings” in a pack |
| Risk-control | R1 = 0; sample done | Sample late once | Any hard-control breach; payment attempt |

A green O with a red R is a failed period.

---

## 4. Papers (one pack, ten pages max)

1. Cover: sentence, fence, level, kill-switch name  
2. Measure card  
3. Breach / pause log  
4. Sample status  
5. Decision ask (one paragraph)  
6. What we are not claiming  

Forbidden papers: vendor ROI one-pager, Ardent gaps applied as *our* opportunity, Northline numbers presented as ours.

---

## 7. When steering should not exist

If there is no live fence, do not hold steering. Hold the workshop or a working session. Steering without objects becomes a slide club.
