# Steering Pack — AP Agent Programme

**Cadence:** Quarterly (or at each promotion gate)  
**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Owner:** Programme lead  

---

## Pack contents (assemble before the meeting)

1. One-page scorecard (four metric classes)  
2. Benefits realisation summary  
3. Risk & control memo (samples, incidents, residual risk)  
4. Roadmap (done / next / later)  
5. Decision asks (explicit)  
6. Appendix: charter versions, UAT summary  

---

## Suggested slide / section order

| # | Section | Time | Notes |
|---|---------|------|-------|
| 1 | Purpose & non-goals refresh | 5 | “No autonomous payments” |
| 2 | Outcomes vs plan | 15 | Operational + risk first; financial last |
| 3 | What we learned (failures included) | 10 | Credibility |
| 4 | Control posture | 15 | Samples, evidence %, incidents |
| 5 | Benefits & economics | 15 | Assumption-labelled |
| 6 | Proposal (expand / hold / retire) | 15 | Levels, scope, budget |
| 7 | Decisions & owners | 10 | Live capture |
| 8 | AOB | 5 | |

## Red flags for steering

- Activity metrics celebrated while cycle time flat  
- Evidence completeness < target  
- Unowned exceptions rising  
- Prompt changes without charter amendment  
- Any attempt to automate payment authorisation
