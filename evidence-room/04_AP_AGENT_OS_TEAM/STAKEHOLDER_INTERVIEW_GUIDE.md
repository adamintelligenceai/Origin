# Stakeholder interview guide

**Product:** Evidence Room — AP Agent OS · Team  
**Use:** 35-minute interviews in the two weeks before the workshop.  
**Rule:** Ask for artefacts. Recollection without an artefact is scored later as 0.

Do not interview to build a business case. Interview to see work objects, holds, and informal helpers.

---

## Shared opening (3 minutes)

> I am mapping how invoices, exceptions, and payments move, and where any model or bot already touches them. I am not scoring your team. Payment release stays human. Please do not share live bank details. We can look at your screen.

Consent to notes. No production files to Evidence Room.

---

## AP Operations / Intake

1. Show me yesterday’s inbound. What share email / portal / XML / paper?
2. What becomes a work object today? What ID?
3. Walk one ugly PDF. Who keys what?
4. Any copilot or IDR “AI” on? Who owns the prompt?
5. When capture fails, where does it go?
6. What must never be guessed (PO, vendor, amount)?

**Artefact:** intake report or mailbox rule.

---

## Matcher

1. Walk a 3-way. Where is the tolerance table?
2. Last dummy GR you heard about — what happened?
3. What do you do with an open possible-duplicate?
4. Multi-PO / multi-entity — in or out?
5. If the agent is wrong, how do you notice today?

**Artefact:** one worksheet or ERP match screen; tolerance table.

---

## Exception desk

1. Export ten parks. Why these codes (or why free text)?
2. Aging — invoice date or park date?
3. Owner = person or “AP”?
4. Escalation clocks — written?
5. EX-OTH examples.

**Artefact:** queue extract.

---

## Controls

1. Possible duplicates — register or email?
2. Four outcomes: clear / confirm / escalate / defer — exist?
3. Language on tickets today (“fraud”)?
4. Who clears high value?
5. Informal helpers you fear?

**Artefact:** any flag log; bank-change log (with Controls or Vendor Master).

---

## Payments / Treasury

1. Reconstruct the last run: build / review / authorise / transmit.
2. Two humans — names of roles.
3. Has any tool ever offered auto-release? What did you say?
4. Discount expiry — who decides?
5. Positive Pay / bank file — who clicks?

**Artefact:** last proposal + approval evidence.

---

## Vendor master

1. Last five bank changes — dual control?
2. New vendor — who approves?
3. Would you accept an agent-created vendor? (Expected: no.)

---

## Plant / warehouse liaison

1. Missing GR — how are you chased today?
2. Have you been asked to post a GR to match an invoice?
3. What would a useful packet contain?

---

## Buyer / procurement ops

1. Price mismatch — how do you accept?
2. PO quality defects you already know?
3. Agent 06 later: who would own it?

---

## Controller / CAO (20 minutes)

1. What would you need to see at day 30?
2. Is a savings target a condition of starting? (If yes, record — method will not write it.)
3. Confirm payment and close attestation stay human.
4. Audit observer — will you ask them?

---

## IT / IAM

1. Service accounts for finance bots today?
2. Shared UI logins?
3. Change control for prompts?

---

## Internal Audit

1. Will you observe packets (not operate)?
2. What reconstructability standard do you already use?
3. Any ban on models in finance?

---

## Close (2 minutes)

> Thank you. Workshop on [date]. We will not quote you as supporting a savings number.
