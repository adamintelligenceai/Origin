## 1. Facilitator profile

Ideal: AP process lead or external facilitator fluent in controls language. Must enforce evidence discipline and shut down hype claims in the room.

**Co-facilitator:** Scribe + timekeeper (can be one person).

## 4. Facilitation moves

| Situation | Move |
|---|---|
| Tool vendor debate starts | Park on “stack map”; return to agent authority |
| Someone claims “AI will cut cost 50%” | Redirect to internal baselines; cite Ardent only as climate |
| Audit silent | Direct question on retrieval and SoD |
| Scope balloons past 5 candidates | Force rank; wave-2 parking lot |
| Payment automation enthusiasm | Restate non-goal: no silent payment release |
| Hierarchy freezes discussion | Silent write before open share |

---

## 5. Room norms (read aloud)

1. Decisions need owners and dates.  
2. Ambiguity is written, not smoothed.  
3. Industry stats are context, not targets.  
4. Agents earn responsibility; they are not awarded autonomy for demos.  
5. Psychological safety: challenge ideas, not people.

## 7. Feedback form (copy)

1. What decision today was most valuable?  
2. What remains dangerously ambiguous?  
3. Which control risk worries you most?  
4. Facilitation quality (1–5)  
5. Would you recommend this workshop format internally? Y/N — why?
