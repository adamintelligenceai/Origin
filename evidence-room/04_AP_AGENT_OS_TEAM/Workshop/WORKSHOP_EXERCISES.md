# Workshop Exercises

**Product:** Evidence Room — Team  
**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-03-20  
**Use with:** Facilitation Pack · Workshop Deck

---

## Exercise 1 — Pain tour (Discover)
**Time:** 15 min · **Format:** Silent write → cluster  
**Prompt:** Write one pain per sticky — process, control, supplier, or data.  
**Rule:** No solutions yet.  
**Output:** 5 theme clusters.

## Exercise 2 — Exception coding drill
**Time:** 20 min · **Format:** Pairs  
**Materials:** 12 anonymised exception blurbs (prepare from interviews)  
**Task:** Assign EX-DATA / EX-MATCH / EX-POL / EX-DUP / EX-MDM / EX-AMB / EX-SYS + owner.  
**Debrief:** Where did pairs disagree? That is taxonomy work.

## Exercise 3 — Opportunity heatmap
**Time:** 25 min · **Format:** Plenary  
**Axes:** Process clarity × Control risk  
**Task:** Plot ≥5 activities; star wave-1 cluster.  
**Constraint:** Payment release cannot sit in Low-risk without Audit challenge.

## Exercise 4 — Human-vs-agent decision test
**Time:** 20 min · **Format:** Triads  
**Task:** Apply four-question test to three real steps from your map.  
**Output:** Each step labelled Human / Assist / Observe.

## Exercise 5 — Agent JD sprint
**Time:** 25 min · **Format:** Breakouts (one candidate each)  
**Task:** Complete JD skeleton including prohibited work and stop conditions.  
**Gate:** Incomplete seven questions = not ready for readout.

## Exercise 6 — Stop-condition failure injection
**Time:** 15 min · **Format:** Breakouts  
**Scenarios:** Missing evidence · Conflicting SoR · SoD breach · Suspected duplicate · Value above band  
**Output:** Written stop + escalation for each.

## Exercise 7 — KPI formula lock
**Time:** 15 min · **Format:** Plenary  
**Task:** Write formulas for assisted volume, exception rate, taxonomy coverage, evidence completeness.  
**Rule:** No targets until formulas exist. Optional context: Ardent 2025 exception **18.4%**, STP **35.4%**.

## Exercise 8 — Charter lite assembly
**Time:** 20 min · **Format:** Sponsor + process owner + audit  
**Task:** Fill sponsor, scope, non-goals, promotion hold rule, demotion triggers, cadence.  
**Output:** v0.9 on flipchart.

## Exercise 9 — Change message draft
**Time:** 10 min · **Format:** Individual then pair  
**Prompt:** Write 80 words to AP staff: what changes, what does not, how to raise issues.  
**Ban list:** “revolutionary,” “seamless,” guaranteed savings, “AI will replace.”

## Exercise 10 — Decision log read-back
**Time:** 10 min · **Format:** Facilitator read-aloud  
**Task:** Confirm each decision with owner and date; mark contested items.
