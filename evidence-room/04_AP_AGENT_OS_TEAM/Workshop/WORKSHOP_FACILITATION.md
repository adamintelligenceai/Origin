# Workshop Facilitation — AP Agent Design

**Duration options:** Half-day (exec + managers) · Full day (recommended) · Two-day (map deep + agent design)  
**Ideal size:** 8–14 people including 2–4 processors

---

## Objectives

By the end, the room will have:

1. Agreed scope and non-goals  
2. As-is swimlane validated by processors  
3. Ranked friction / exception volumes  
4. 1–2 agent candidates at Level 0–1  
5. Human gates and control owners named  
6. 30-day action plan with dates  

---

## Pre-work (send 5 business days prior)

- Diagnostic scorecard (completed)  
- Volume & mix snapshot (Template T1)  
- 20 anonymised example invoices / exceptions  
- Org list of systems in AP path  
- Ask processors: “Bring your top 5 workarounds”

## Exercises (detailed)

### Exercise 1 — “Where the minutes go”

Each processor allocates 100 points across stages for a typical week. Compare to managers’ allocation. Discuss gaps.

### Exercise 2 — Exception autopsy

In pairs, take 3 real exceptions: taxonomy code, preventable Y/N, agent-assistible Y/N, human-only Y/N.

### Exercise 3 — Charter lite

Fill Agent Insertion Canvas (Process Mapping T5) for the winning agent. Read aloud human gates.

### Exercise 4 — Red team

One participant attacks the design: “How does this create a duplicate payment?” Room must answer with controls.

---

## Facilitation tips

- Processors speak first on as-is; managers speak first on policy.  
- Ban vendor product demos during design.  
- Photograph every board; publish pack within 48 hours.  
- If scope expands mid-room, park to “Phase 2” list.
