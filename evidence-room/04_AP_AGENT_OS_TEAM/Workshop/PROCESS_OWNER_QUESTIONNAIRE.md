# Process-owner questionnaire

**Evidence Room · Team**  
**Version:** 1.0 · September 2026  
**Return:** three working days before the workshop, to the facilitator and scribe  
**Owner:** the human who owns AP process design (usually AP Manager / AP Director)  
**Not:** an application for Custom Blueprint (that is a different questionnaire)

Complete in writing. “TBD” is allowed; “everyone knows” is not.

We do not need account numbers, vendor files, or employee personal data. Describe systems and volumes. Do not attach invoice images.

Northline Industrial Group in our examples is fictional. Put **your** entity names here.
