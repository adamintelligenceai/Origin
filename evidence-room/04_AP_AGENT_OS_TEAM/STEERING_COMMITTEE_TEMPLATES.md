## 1. Terms of reference (adopt / tailor)

**Name:** AP Agent Layer Steering  
**Purpose:** Commission, promote, demote, and refuse agents under Evidence Room — AP Agent OS method.  
**Non-purpose:** Approve ROI, certify compliance, select ERP vendors, authorise payments.

**Membership:** Chair (Controller or delegate), Process Owner, Controls, Transformation, Payments/Treasury, IAM as needed. Audit: observer.

**Quorum:** Chair + Process Owner + Controls.

**Voting:** Promotions and Wave 2 open require quorum plus Treasury if payment-adjacent. Demotions can be executed by Controls + Process Owner and reported.

**Cadence:** Monthly from shadow start; day 30; day 90; extraordinary on STOP.

**Papers:** Decision log; register; incident log; cost ledger. No single-cell ROI paper.

**Conflicts:** Vendor attendees timeboxed after decisions.

**Review of ToR:** 12 months or on toolkit edition change.

**Sign-off:** Chair date ______.
