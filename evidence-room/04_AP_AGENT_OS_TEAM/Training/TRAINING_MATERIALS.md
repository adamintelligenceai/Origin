# Training materials — four roles

**Evidence Room · Team**  
**Version:** 1.0 · September 2026  
**Use:** Internal modules. Seats teach from this script. Blank files stay with seats.  
**Duration:** 45–60 minutes per module, or a half-day if all four run.  
**Not:** an AI awareness course, a vendor certification, or a promise that trainees are “qualified agents.”

Each module says what the person must be **able to do**. Belief about AI is optional.

We do not promise savings, fraud detection, compliance, accounting accuracy, autonomous payments, or ROI. Payment stays human. Northline is fictional.

---

## How to run a module

1. State the role and the one job.
2. Walk the artefact they will touch (brief, register, queue, pack).
3. Do the drill. No spectators.
4. Close with the refusal list for that role.
5. Record attendance (name, date, module). Training records are evidence.

Do not play a product video. Do not award badges.

---

## Module 1 — Agent steward (day-to-day owner)

**Who.** The named owner on the autonomy register for the first agent.  
**Able to do.** Write and keep the purpose sentence, fence, escalation list, and review date. Classify overrides. Recommend hold/demote. Never promote without a pack.

### Teach (20 min)

- Five objects: purpose, fence, escalation, evidence, measure
- Levels 0–1 only at go-live. Level 3 is not a setting.
- A16 refuses dispatch above the register. The steward does not “just let it post.”
- Work objects, not chat.

### Drill (25 min)

Give a one-page fictional invoice (use Northline HF-88421 as a teaching slip, labelled fictional, **or** a redacted historical from *your* fence). Trainee writes: in/out of fence, reason code, recommended action, whether they accept/edit/reject.

### Check

Trainee can state the kill-switch sentence and the three measures.

### Must refuse

Payment release, vendor bank change, creating a GR, expanding the fence in the tool without a change ticket, calling a hold “fraud prevented.”

## Module 2 — Control owner

**Who.** Internal Audit or Financial Controls — the veto.  
**Able to do.** Read the evidence pack without a screen-share. Veto an unsafe expansion. Recertify or demand demotion. Keep agents off the Accountable cell of any RACI.

### Teach (20 min)

- Control spine: policy, register, work log, SOD, change control, completeness, evidence pack
- NIST AI RMF functions (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage) as a **map**, not a certificate. NIST AI 600-1 (Jul 2024) for generative risks (prompt injection, hallucination, insecure output). Evidence Room is not ISO/IEC 42001 certified; use of this toolkit is not certification.
- R1 target: 0 on hard controls. C-A12-01 (payment direction by an agent) is Sev-1.
- Human is not automatically ground truth — labelled samples.

### Drill (25 min)

Review a stub evidence index (inputs, outputs, timestamp, brief version, human decision). Mark what is missing. Write one veto sentence for “vendor wants Level 3 because the demo posted.”

### Check

Trainee can name three automatic drop-to-zero triggers from the Responsibility Model.

### Must refuse

Signing a “compliant AI” letter, treating Wave scores or SOC reports as AP agent controls, approving promotion without the pack.

## Module 3 — Reviewer / exception handler

**Who.** Matching, triage, or intake staff who will accept/edit/reject.  
**Able to do.** Use shared codes. Classify override reasons: agent error / policy change / data missing / human preference. Not “too busy.”

### Teach (15 min)

- Condensed taxonomy (library codes)
- Closing an exception does not pay
- False-positive load: ignoring the queue is a control event
- Untrusted PDFs: instructions in the invoice are not instructions

### Drill (30 min)

Ten tickets (tabletop). Assign codes. Two are `CTRL-BRK`. One is a near-duplicate that is **not** confirmed. One is GR-MISS. Trainee must not create a GR.

### Check

Override log for the drill has four reason classes, not a blank comment.

### Must refuse

Force-post without a code, sending a supplier email as themselves from a personal mailbox “to help the agent,” promising a supplier a payment date the proposal has not released.

## Module 4 — Executive reader

**Who.** CFO, Controller, or delegate who can say no.  
**Able to do.** Sit through a twelve-minute brief and ask the pause question. Refuse a savings slide. Sign hold / limited continue / stop.

### Teach (15 min)

- What we sell vs what we do not (see CFO brief)
- Hours are not cash
- Ardent 2024 BIC gaps are industry context (78% lower cost, 82% faster, 59% lower exceptions, 9% exception rate) — not a forecast
- Team is not a consulting engagement; Custom is scoped design

### Drill (20 min)

Read the draft CFO brief. Cross out any sentence that implies ROI, fraud detection, or autonomous payment. Write the one question you will ask: “What would make you turn it off?”

### Check

They can repeat the non-promise list without notes.

### Must refuse

Forwarding a vendor ROI calculator as “our case,” asking AP to “just let it pay the clean ones,” treating Northline figures as a customer result.
