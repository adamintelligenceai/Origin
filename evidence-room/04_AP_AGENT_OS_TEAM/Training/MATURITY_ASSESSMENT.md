# Maturity Assessment — Team Programme

**Product:** Evidence Room — Team  
**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-03-20  
**Purpose:** Re-score organisational maturity after workshop and during implementation — beyond the Free diagnostic.

---

## 1. Relationship to Free diagnostic

| Instrument | When | Depth |
|---|---|---|
| Free AP AI Readiness (35Q) | Pre-purchase / entry | Six dimensions, levels 1–5 |
| Team Maturity Assessment | Post-workshop; Day 45; Day 90 | Programme + agent-portfolio maturity |

Reuse Free scores as baseline. Do not discard them.

---

## 2. Scoring

Same 1–5 scale as Free diagnostic. Programme maturity = mean of sections below.

| Level | Score | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.0–1.9 | Ad hoc agents / no portfolio |
| 2 | 2.0–2.9 | Single pilot; weak cadence |
| 3 | 3.0–3.9 | Wave-1 governed; steering exists |
| 4 | 4.0–4.4 | Multi-agent; promotion discipline holds |
| 5 | 4.5–5.0 | Portfolio managed; continuous improvement |

---

## 3. Assessment sections

### S1 — Portfolio discipline (5 items)
1. Documented agent register with owners  
2. Prioritisation method used (not HiPPO alone)  
3. Wave boundaries respected  
4. Non-goals enforced  
5. Custom/multi-entity complexity acknowledged  

### S2 — Control integration (5 items)
1. SoD matrix includes agents  
2. Stop conditions tested  
3. Instruction change control live  
4. Audit sampling performed  
5. Payment boundary explicit  

### S3 — Measurement (5 items)
1. Formulas locked before targets  
2. Taxonomy coverage tracked  
3. False flags reviewed  
4. Baselines internal (not only Ardent context)  
5. Benefits tracker separates leading vs lagging indicators  

### S4 — People and change (5 items)
1. Training paths completed for wave-1 roles  
2. Comms issued without hype claims  
3. Feedback loop from analysts  
4. Role impact discussed openly  
5. Capacity protected for supervision  

### S5 — Governance cadence (5 items)
1. Steering monthly (or agreed)  
2. Promotion/demotion decisions minuted  
3. RAID actively managed  
4. Sponsor engagement evidenced  
5. Escalation paths known

## 4. Evidence required per claim

For any score ≥4, attach artefact ID (charter version, scorecard export, training log, steering minutes). Scores without evidence cap at 3.

---

## 5. Interpretation guide

| Pattern | Action |
|---|---|
| Measurement high, Control low | Freeze promotions |
| Change high, Portfolio low | Stop new agents; finish wave-1 |
| All mid (≈3) | Proceed carefully; schedule Day 90 Custom decision if multi-entity |
| Drop vs Free baseline | Investigate change fatigue or silent scope creep |

**Context reminder:** Peer AI adoption **44%** (Ardent 2025) does not equal programme maturity.
