# Training Outline — AP Agent OS

**Evidence Room · Team tier**

---

## Training principles

- Role-based, not “AI 101” lectures  
- Practice on real (anonymised) cases  
- Emphasise **accept / edit / reject** judgement  
- Payment authorisation and bank changes remain human topics — agents do not take those actions  

---

## Audience curricula

### A. AP Processors (90 min live + 30 min practice)

| Module | Minutes | Content |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Why agents, why gates | 15 | Positioning; what will not change |
| Your agent’s outputs | 25 | Reading confidence, evidence, taxonomy |
| Accept / edit / reject | 25 | Drills |
| Exceptions & escalations | 15 | When to stop trusting |
| Practice cases | 30 | 5 cases |
| Quiz / attestation | 10 | Completion log |

### B. AP Managers (60 min)

Scorecard reading · weekly ops cadence · sampling · coaching processors · when to kill switch.

### C. Approvers / Budget owners (30 min)

What changes in approval UX · high-value paths · how to spot odd agent-assisted requests.

### D. Controllers / Control (60 min)

Charter · controls matrix · evidence standard · promotion criteria · SoD.

### E. IT / Security / ERP (60 min)

Identities · logging · prompt/model versions · kill switch · data paths · untrusted content handling.

### F. Exec sponsor (20 min)

Outcomes vs activity · decision rights · what “good” looks like on the steering pack.

---

## Practice case bank (minimum)

1. Clean match  
2. Tolerance break  
3. Duplicate twin  
4. Missing GR  
5. Messy OCR / low confidence  
6. Supplier email with manipulative wording
