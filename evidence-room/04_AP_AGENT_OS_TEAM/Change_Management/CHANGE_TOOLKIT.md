# Change Management Toolkit — AP Agents

**Evidence Room · Team tier**

---

## 1. Change thesis

AP agents change **how judgement is applied**, not the existence of AP work. Resistance usually comes from fear of blame, opaque “AI”, or prior failed automation — not from laziness.

---

## 2. Stakeholder map

| Stakeholder | What they fear | What they need | Channel |
|-------------|----------------|----------------|---------|
| Processors | Replacement / surveillance | Clarity on gates; skill in review | Huddles, training |
| AP Managers | More noise, worse SLAs | Scorecard that helps coaching | Weekly ops |
| Approvers | Rubber-stamping risk | High-value paths unchanged | Short brief |
| Controllers | Control failure | Evidence + levels | Charter reviews |
| IT | Shadow IT / data leakage | Approved endpoints, IAM | Design sessions |
| Suppliers | Robo-emails | Human-gated comms early | Gradual |
| Exec | Hype backlash | Assumption-labelled outcomes | Steering |

---

## 3. Message house (local)

**Headline:** We’re installing governed assistants that earn responsibility — payments stay human.  
**Proof:** Shadow scores, sample pass rates, kill switch.  
**Ask:** Complete training; use accept/edit/reject deliberately; surface failures fast.

Align with `11_BRAND/01_MESSAGING_HOUSE.md`.

---

## 4. Resistance playbook

| Signal | Likely cause | Response |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| Silent non-adoption | Unclear WIIFM / fear | Manager 1:1s; show time saved on hated tasks |
| “AI is always wrong” | Bad early precision | Drop level; publish error types fixed |
| Workarounds around agent | UX friction | Fix workflow; don’t punish |
| Over-trust / blind accept | Deskilling | Sampling; coaching on rejects |
| Rumour of headcount cuts | Comm gap | Sponsor statement: capacity intent |

---

## 5. Communication calendar (pilot)

| When | Audience | Message |
|------|----------|---------|
| T−10d | All impacted | What’s coming / what’s not |
| T−5d | Processors | Training invite |
| T0 | Processors | Go-live + support channel |
| T+7d | All | First metrics (honest) |
| T+30d | All | What we changed based on feedback |

---

## 6. Adoption metrics (change, not finance)

| Metric | Formula |
|--------|---------|
| Training completion % | Trained ÷ required |
| Active user rate | Users who actioned agent output ÷ enabled users |
| Median time-to-first-reject | Health check that people still think |
| Feedback tickets closed | Responsiveness |

---

## 7. Celebration without hype

Celebrate **caught duplicates**, **cleared backlogs**, **clean audits** — not invocation counts.
