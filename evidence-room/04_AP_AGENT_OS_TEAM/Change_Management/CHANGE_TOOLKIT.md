# Change Toolkit

**Product:** Evidence Room — Team / Change Management  
**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-03-20

---

## 1. Change principles

1. Precision over pep talks.  
2. Task shift honesty — what moves to Assist mode, what stays human.  
3. No replacement theatre and no false job guarantees.  
4. Control voice co-authors messages.  
5. Feedback loops within two weeks of pilot start.

---

## 2. Stakeholder impact matrix

| Group | What changes | What does not | Risk if ignored |
|---|---|---|---|
| AP analysts | Coding exceptions; supervising agent output | Final judgement on EX-POL / payments | Quiet workarounds |
| Approvers | Packet format; challenge duty | Accountability for approvals | Rubber-stamping |
| Controllers | Promotion decisions | Fiduciary accountability | Autonomy by demo |
| Audit | New sample populations | Independence | Late objections |
| IT | Identities, logging | ERP ownership | Shadow tools |
| Suppliers | Possibly clearer status replies | Contractual terms | Hallucinated promises |

---

## 3. Adoption curve tactics

| Stage | Tactic |
|---|---|
| Awareness | Sponsor note (see Executive Comms Templates) |
| Understanding | Training paths + office hours |
| Trial | Supervised pilot with praise for good stops (not only speed) |
| Adoption | Embed taxonomy in daily huddles |
| Reinforcement | Maturity reassessment; demotion stories shared as learning |

---

## 4. Resistance patterns & responses

| Pattern | Response |
|---|---|
| “AI will take our jobs” | Show prohibited list + human-owned judgements; invite into JD design |
| “Another tool” | Clarify documentation/OS layer vs AP suite |
| “We tried RPA” | Separate evidence-led agents from brittle scripts; start smaller |
| “Audit will block us” | Bring Audit in early; show trail design |
| “Just turn on vendor AI” | Compare seven-question completeness |

---

## 5. Change readiness pulse (biweekly, 5Q)

Score 1–5: I understand the agent’s limits · I know how to escalate · I trust the stop conditions · Workload is manageable · Leadership messages match reality.  
Act on any item averaging <3.

---

## 6. Success metrics for change (not financial ROI)

- Training completion %  
- Pulse scores  
- Override reason-code completeness  
- Unauthorised scope attempts detected (should trend down after coaching)
