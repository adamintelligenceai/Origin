# Executive communications

**Evidence Room · Team**  
**Version:** 1.0 · September 2026  
**Use:** Notes a process owner can send upward and across. Short. Specific. No theatre.  
**Do not:** invent customers, savings, or “AI transformation” headlines.

Northline Industrial Group is fictional. Do not paste its volumes as yours. Payment stays human.

---

## Principles

1. The purpose sentence is the message. Everything else is appendix.
2. Name the fence. Unscoped “we are piloting AI in AP” is how rumours start.
3. Say what you will **not** claim in the same note as the ask.
4. One ask per note.
5. Vendors do not send these emails for you.

---

## A. Kickoff to the executive reader (before the workshop)

**Subject:** Workshop on a governed AP agent layer — [entity], not a vendor demo

> We will spend [one / two] day(s) mapping the live invoice path for [candidate class] and writing a fence. Evidence Room Team is a toolkit we run ourselves; they are not facilitating.  
> We will not leave with a savings number, a fraud claim, or a recommendation to pay automatically.  
> I need you for [30 minutes at the end of day 2 / a twelve-minute brief the following week] to accept or reject one sentence of purpose.  
> If you need a guaranteed business case to allow the workshop, say so now — we will not invent one.

---

## B. After the workshop (to the same reader)

**Subject:** Purpose sentence for [class] — please accept or send back

> Sentence: “[paste]”  
> Fence: [entity, system, amount, outs]  
> Steward: [name]. Kill switch: [name]. Level: 0/1.  
> Ask: accept sentence / send back.  
> We are not asking to go live in this email.

---

## C. Limited-production notice (to CFO / Controller / IA)

**Subject:** Limited AP agent fence live — [class] — Level [0/1]

> From [date], [agent] will [observe / recommend] on [fence]. Humans still accept/edit/reject. [Name] still releases payments.  
> We will read [three measures] twice weekly.  
> We will pause if [top two stop conditions].  
> We are not announcing savings, compliance, or ROI.  
> Brief attached (two pages). Decision log will record hold / continue / stop on [date].

---

## D. Hold or stop (send the same day)

**Subject:** AP agent fence — [Hold / Stop] — [class]

> We are [holding at paper / stopping] because [ownership / evidence / pause / sample / control] is not real enough.  
> The register row is [frozen / retired].  
> This is the operating system working. It is allowed to recommend waiting.

---

## E. To peer functions (Procurement, Receiving, Treasury)

**Subject:** You may see chases / packets from a fenced AP agent — what to ignore

> AP is running a **limited** agent on [class]. It may [draft a GR chase / pack an approval / challenge a proposal].  
> It will not create receipts, approve in your name, or send a payment file.  
> If a message looks official and should not have gone, tell [steward] the same day.  
> Please do not “help” by posting a GR you did not receive.

---

## F. To Internal Audit (short)

**Subject:** Evidence index for [agent] — pull list

> You can pull without a screen-share: [register row, work log, sample sheet, kill-switch test date, instruction version].  
> We are not asking you to certify the agent. We are asking you to know where the pack lives.  
> NIST / ISO language in our files is a map, not a badge.

---

## G. All-staff AP note (if the rumour mill is faster than you)

**Subject:** What we are actually doing with “AI” this month

> We fenced one invoice class. A tool will recommend. You will still decide. Nobody is being measured on a savings target from this. Payment does not change. Questions to [supervisor], not to the vendor channel.

---

## H. What never to send

- A chart titled with a percentage saving  
- “Best-in-Class 78%” as *our* outcome  
- Northline as a case study  
- “Autonomous AP”  
- A countdown to go-live as culture comms  
- A request that IA “bless the model”

---

## I. Voice check

Before send, read the note aloud. If it could sit on a vendor blog, rewrite until it could sit in a close file.
