## Template 1 — Sponsor launch note (email)

**Subject:** AP agent programme — supervised start on [date]

Colleagues,

We are beginning a supervised programme to design and govern AI agents in Accounts Payable across the systems we already own. This is an operating-model effort, not a replacement of our AP platform, and not a mandate for autonomous payments.

Agents will start in observe/assist modes with explicit stop conditions, named human owners for exceptions, and measurable promotion gates. We will not claim guaranteed savings, fraud reduction, or compliance outcomes from this launch.

Programme lead: [name]. Steering cadence: monthly. Questions: [alias].

[Sponsor name]

## Template 2 — AP team briefing (email / huddle script)

**Subject:** What changes for AP — and what does not

What changes: selected queues will receive agent-prepared classifications, drafts, or evidence packs. You will code exceptions with a shared taxonomy and flag weak evidence.

What does not change: you remain responsible for judgement on policy conflicts; payment release stays on the existing controlled path; vendor master changes stay with master data owners.

Success includes good stops — refusing to guess is correct behaviour.
