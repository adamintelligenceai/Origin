# Full-day workshop agenda — AP AI transformation (layer design)

**Product:** Evidence Room — AP Agent OS · Team  
**Duration:** 08:45–16:30 (one day)  
**Outcome:** Maturity sentence, hold status, classified work, six draft Wave 1 charters, two-week shadow plan, steering date.  
**Non-outcome:** Platform shortlist, savings target, fraud programme, L3 design.

A leader can run this tomorrow with `FACILITATION_PACK.md` and `WORKSHOP_EXERCISES.md`. Northline column in exercises is the answer key for the facilitator, fictional.

---

## 08:45–09:00 · Arrive and wall

Participants write their role on a card. Facilitator posts the two sentences. No opening video.

---

## 09:00–09:20 · Contract of the day

| Min | Talk |
|---|---|
| 5 | What this is: operating system for an agent *layer*. Keep the stack. |
| 5 | What this is not: ERP project, payment product, prompt class. |
| 5 | Payment sentence. Ask Treasury to repeat it. |
| 5 | Banned claims (savings guarantee, fraud detection, compliance, accuracy, autonomous pay). |

**Check:** Everyone can say “prepare is not execute.”

---

## 09:20–10:20 · Exercise 1 — Diagnostic lightning (team score)

Use `MATURITY_ASSESSMENT.md` / 36 questions. Score as a room. Challenger is Controls.

**Output:** Six dimension averages, vetoes, band sentence on the wall.

**Break rule:** If C1 is disputed, do not average — reconstruct the last payment run after the break.

---

## 10:20–10:35 · Break

---

## 10:35–12:00 · Exercise 2 — Ten exceptions + one payment run

Sheets from `WORKSHOP_EXERCISES.md`. Groups of three (Match + Controls + Transformation; Ops + Payments + Process Owner).

**Output:** Class A–J table; list of demo temptations; at least one class A refusal written.

---

## 12:00–12:45 · Lunch

Controller joins if only afternoon.

---

## 12:45–13:15 · Holds review with Controller / Treasury

Walk C1, C2, dummy GR, DOA climb. Write **used / documented / absent**.

**If C1 absent:** afternoon charters are L0 only; Agent 12 card is marked CLOSED.

---

## 13:15–14:45 · Exercise 3 — Wave 1 charters

Six stations or two rounds of three. Use Starter JD. Facilitator rotates. Exclusions read aloud.

**Output:** Draft JDs for 01, 02, 03, 04, 10, 16 with owners (or VACANT).

---

## 14:45–15:00 · Break

---

## 15:00–15:40 · Exercise 4 — Shadow design

Coverage, sample sizes, language sample, rollback, operator huddle date.

**Output:** Two-week calendar.

---

## 15:40–16:10 · Exercise 5 — Steering and communications

ToR skim. Monday note. Who tells operators. Who tells Audit.

**Output:** Steering date; comms owners.

---

## 16:10–16:30 · Close

Read the wall: band, vetoes, owners, payment sentence, shadow start, STOP list.  
Parking lot assigned.  
Facilitator: “We did not set a savings target. That was on purpose.”

---

## Timing if the Controller only has 45 minutes

Give them 12:45–13:15 and 16:10–16:30. Do not move holds to an email.

---

## Timing if virtual

Same blocks. Exercises 2 and 3 in breakouts of 25 minutes + 10-minute plenary. Send packs the night before. Prohibit live invoice pasting into chats.
