# Process-owner questionnaires

**Product:** Evidence Room — AP Agent OS · Team  
**Use:** Homework for named owners before the workshop. Return 48 hours prior.  
**Time:** 25 minutes each.

Answers are design inputs. They are not attestations of compliance.

---

## Questionnaire A — AP Process Owner (Orchestrator A)

1. Which path should Wave 1 use, and why that one (narrowness, not “value”)?
2. Who is backup if you are out for two weeks?
3. List every informal helper you know (copilot, RPA, vendor AI).
4. What ID will we use for a work object on day 1 (even if a spreadsheet)?
5. What is the Intake SLA you will sign (hours)?
6. Will you pause an agent if its owner is vacant? (Expected: yes.)
7. What must Internal Audit be able to reopen at day 30?
8. Write the payment sentence in your own words.

---

## Questionnaire B — Controls Lead (Agent 10 A)

1. Current possible-duplicate process — steps and system.
2. Will you accept four outcomes only (clear / confirm / escalate / defer)?
3. High-value threshold for your clearance (local DOA — do not copy Northline)?
4. Language you will forbid on tickets.
5. Hours per week you can give the register in month one.
6. Any investigation function you must notify — name the policy, do not invent a fraud SOP.

---

## Questionnaire C — Match Lead (Agent 03 A)

1. Match trees in force (attach table).
2. Object types you will *exclude* from Wave 1.
3. Sample size you can dual-review daily in L0.
4. Last time someone force-matched — what should the SOP say?
5. Backup matcher.

---

## Questionnaire D — Operations Lead (Agent 01 A)

1. Source mix (best estimate + report name).
2. Paper / image retrieval — where?
3. Extract sampling today — yes/no.
4. Out-of-scope document types (T&E, etc.).

---

## Questionnaire E — Payments Lead / Treasury (Agent 12 gate)

1. Last run evidence (attach or describe).
2. Do you agree Agent 12 stays annotate-only? Y/N
3. Conditions under which you would *refuse* to open Agent 12.
4. Who transmits the bank file?

---

## Questionnaire F — Finance Transformation

1. Hours freed for method work in the next 90 days (yours and others’).
2. Will you refuse to write a savings target? Y/N
3. Licence: who is on the named working group list?
4. Custom Blueprint needed? Why / why not.

---

## Return format

One page per questionnaire. Artefact names in footnotes. Vacant answers marked VACANT — do not politely guess.
