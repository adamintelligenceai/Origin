# Change-management toolkit

**Product:** Evidence Room — AP Agent OS · Team  
**Use:** Adopt the layer without headcount theatre or “AI will replace you” messaging.  
**Not:** A culture campaign. The change is decision rights and packets.

---

## 1. What is actually changing

| Changes | Does not change |
|---|---|
| Work objects and coded parks | ERP as accounting SoR |
| Recommendations appear (at L1) | Who authorises payment |
| Sampling time is real work | Employment contracts (unless the org separately decides) |
| Informal copilots become visible | The need for specialists |

Do not brief “efficiency” as the lead story. Brief **inspectability and withheld permission.**

---

## 2. Stakeholder map (fill)

| Person | Role | What they fear | What we say | Owner of the conversation |

---

## 3. Messages that are allowed

- Agents earn responsibility.
- You will see more recording before you see less keying.
- Payment is still yours (Treasury / Payments).
- We will demote what is expensive and wrong.

## 4. Messages that are forbidden

- “This will cut the team in half.”
- “The AI is more accurate than you.”
- “Don’t reject — you’ll break the ROI.”
- “We’re going touchless this quarter.”
- “It detects fraud so you don’t have to look.”

---

## 5. Resistance patterns and responses

| Pattern | Response |
|---|---|
| “Just another transformation slide” | Do ten exceptions in the room |
| “We already have Copilot” | List it on the register; charter or off |
| “No time to sample” | Then no L1 — queue problem |
| “Vendor can turn on auto-post” | Refusal minute |
| “Give me a savings number to motivate staff” | Refuse; motivation by holds and packets |

---

## 6. Rituals that help

- Operator huddle at L0 start.
- Weekly exception mix with *people* aging, not “the bot.”
- Public declined promotion (anonymised) — proves demotion is real.
- Thank-you for a good reject.

---

## 7. Headcount

This toolkit has no redundancy workstream. If the organisation later reallocates hours, that is a management judgement outside Evidence Room’s claims. Change comms must not preview a cut as a benefit of the licence.
