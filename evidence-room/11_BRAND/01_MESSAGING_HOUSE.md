## Subhead

AP teams are deploying AI agents without ownership maps, control catalogs, or KPI baselines. Evidence Room is the purchasable AP Agent OS that adds governance, responsibility, measurement, and evidence — on top of the suites, ERPs, and automation tools you already run.

---

## Value proposition

**For** Controllers, AP leaders, and finance transformation teams  
**Who** must adopt AI in payables without failing an audit or flying blind on outcomes  
**Evidence Room** is an AP Agent OS  
**That** productizes responsibility maps, control catalogs, KPI packs, and evidence discipline for agent-assisted AP  
**Unlike** AP suites (execution), RPA platforms (horizontal runtime), or prompt packs (ungoverned text)  
**It** makes agent work assignable, controllable, and measurable — without promising unverified savings, fraud reduction, compliance certification, or ROI.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| “We already have Tipalti / Coupa / Stampli.” | Those run invoices. Evidence Room runs **accountability** — responsibility, controls, KPIs — portable across tools. |
| “UiPath / Automation Anywhere covers governance.” | Horizontal automation ops ≠ AP-specific ownership, control catalog, and finance KPI packs Controllers will sign. |
| “Can’t we just use ChatGPT prompts?” | Prompt packs have no SoD, no audit trail design, no baseline KPIs. That is the hype path. |
| “Show me the ROI.” | We refuse fabricated ROI. We sell the measurement system so *your* baselines drive decisions. |
| “Will this stop fraud?” | No product should claim that. We catalog controls for agent-related failure modes; residual risk remains yours. |
| “Is this compliance software?” | No. It is an operating system for agent accountability. Compliance determinations stay with your counsel/audit. |
| “Why not wait for our ERP copilot?” | Copilots draft and suggest. They do not ship your responsibility map, AP control catalog, or evidence ledger. |
| “Another framework deck from consultants?” | Productized SKUs (Free → Starter → Pro → Team) with maintained libraries — not a one-off slide SOW. |

## Disclaimers (mandatory customer-facing)

Use on website footer, checkout, pitch decks, and product covers:

1. **No guaranteed savings.** Evidence Room does not guarantee reductions in cost per invoice, headcount, or cycle time.
2. **No guaranteed fraud prevention.** Controls reduce designed-for failure modes; they do not eliminate fraud or misuse.
3. **No compliance certification.** Use of Evidence Room materials does not constitute SOX, audit, legal, or regulatory compliance.
4. **No guaranteed ROI.** Any ROI model must use the customer’s own baselines and remains the customer’s responsibility.
5. **Secondary research.** Benchmark figures from analysts (e.g. Ardent Partners) cited via vendor blogs are **Secondary citations** — not Evidence Room primary research.
6. **Vendor claims.** Competitor AI capability statements are labeled Vendor and are not endorsements or verified results.
7. **Frameworks ≠ evidence.** Responsibility maps, control catalogs, and readiness scores are operating frameworks, not market proof.
8. **Not a payment/AP execution system.** Evidence Room does not capture, pay, or post invoices; it governs how agents participate alongside your systems of record.

**Short footer line:**  
*Evidence over hype. No guaranteed savings, fraud prevention, compliance certification, or ROI.*
