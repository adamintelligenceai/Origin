# Transformation roadmap — Starter

**Product:** Evidence Room — AP Agent OS · Starter  
**Audience:** Practitioner and sponsor  
**Use:** Describe waves without promising cycle time, savings, or touchless AP.  
**Clock note:** Durations are planning illustrations. They are not delivery commitments and not evidence that a wave will pay for itself.

Northline Industrials dates below are fictional.

---

## 1. What “transformation” means here

It means the organisation moves from an undesigned or informal agent situation to a **named, held, evidence-gated layer**. It does not mean the AP team is replaced, the ERP is replaced, or payments become autonomous.

Success criteria are band movement (maturity L1→L3→L4) and operating-discipline KPIs. Financial outcomes, if they later appear, are management judgements from *your* baseline — not roadmap milestones.

---

## 2. Waves at a glance

```
Wave 0  Diagnose, holds, owners
   │
Wave 1  Six agents · L0 shadow → L1 recommend
   │
Wave 2  Prepare packets · Agent 12 annotate only
   │
Wave 3  Periodic / diagnostic agents (Professional)
   │
Wave 4  Rare L3 on a named clean-match class · never payment
```

Do not run waves in parallel as sixteen projects. Do not skip Wave 0 because a vendor is on-site.

---

## 3. Wave 0 — Deserve a charter

**Intent.** Know the band. Prove the holds. Name owners.

**Work.**

- Sit the diagnostic.
- Reconstruct a payment run and five bank changes.
- Classify ten exceptions.
- Map one path.
- Refuse any live model on the payment proposal.

**Exit.** Maturity sentence; veto list; six owner names (or a written gap).

**Illustration.** Northline (fictional) spends one week. The informal matching copilot is listed and set to observe-only.

**Typical duration.** 3–10 working days, depending on artefact availability — not on enthusiasm.

---

## 4. Wave 1 — Observe, then recommend

**Agents.** 01 Intake, 02 Validation, 03 Matching, 04 Triage, 10 Duplicate & Anomaly, 16 Orchestrator.

**Autonomy.** Two weeks L0. L1 only per checklist GATE.

**In scope.** One path, one entity if possible.

**Out of scope.** Payment annotation, supplier send, GR creation, posting by the agent.

**Exit.** Coverage and sample packs; coded fails; flag register; autonomy register; day-30 Audit observer session booked or held.

**Failure mode.** Treating L0 output as the system of record “because it looks good.” That is an informal promotion. Demote the habit, not just the agent.

**Illustration.** Northline Wave 1 on US-OH PO goods. Monterrey paper stays out.

---

## 5. Wave 2 — Packets, not execution

**Agents.** 05 Goods Receipt, 07 Approval, 08 Supplier Resolution, 12 Payment Proposal Review.

**Autonomy.** L1. Selected packets may be prepared (L2) only if Professional gates are in use. Agent 12 ceiling remains annotate.

**Pre-conditions.**

- Wave 1 L0 GATE passed.
- Exception mix is coded enough that EX-MGR, EX-DOA, and supplier queries are visible.
- C1 ≥ 3 before Agent 12 opens.

**Exit.** Receiver packets exist; DOA routing does not invent deputies; supplier drafts are fact-only; payment proposals show holds for open flags and recent bank changes; humans still release.

**Failure mode.** “Just send the supplier mail; it’s only a chase.” External send is a commitment surface. Keep human-send until a charter and sample say otherwise — and never for commercial terms.

---

## 6. Wave 3 — Periodic and diagnostic

**Agents.** 06 PO Quality, 09 Internal Follow-up, 11 Vendor Statement, 13 AP Close, 14 AP Reporting, 15 Root Cause.

**Recommendation.** Use Professional specifications. Starter users should not invent these charters from a one-line description.

**Autonomy.** L0/L1. Close attestation and statement-to-pay confusion are the two common failures. Statements are not invoices. Close is signed by a human.

**Exit.** Periodic objects exist; Root Cause produces proposals, not silent process edits.

---

## 7. Wave 4 — Earned execute (rare)

**What it is.** A named clean-match *class* may be proposed for autonomy L3 execute-within-guardrails (for example: post a 3-way match under a dollar cap, no flags, listed entity). Sampling continues. Recertification is mandatory.

**What it is not.** Touchless AP. Payment release. Vendor-bank change. A reward for hitting a date.

**Pre-conditions.** Professional gate tables; Controller sign-off on any posting class; one-hour rollback; maturity band at least L4.

**Starter users:** do not open Wave 4 on this licence. Write the desire down and buy the gate tables first.

---

## 8. Multi-entity and multi-ERP

Add company codes the way you add object types: remap, re-charter, restart the L0 clock for that class. A Dynamics path that is L1 does not make a newly acquired Oracle path L1.

Team `ENTERPRISE_GOVERNANCE_PACK.md` covers overlay governance. Custom Blueprint exists for political or multi-stack design.

---

## 9. Capacity and people

The roadmap does not include a headcount target. Agents prepare and recommend. Named humans own outcomes. If volume requires more specialists at close, that is a queue decision. It is not evidence for L3.

Plant liaisons (Wave 2) need hours. If those hours are not freed, do not charter Agent 05.

|---|---|---|
| 0 | We diagnosed the layer and listed informal helpers | We will save $X |
