# START HERE — 12 steps (Professional)

Print this. Do not skip to Agent 03 because matching is fashionable.

## Step 1 — Write the payment sentence on a wall (day 0, 20 minutes)

Copy, in ink, where the people who release runs can see it:

> Payment authorisation and release are outside every agent’s authority, at every autonomy level. Agent 12 may annotate a proposal. Named humans approve. A named role transmits the file.

If you cannot publish that sentence internally, stop. Do not charter agents. Email us only if the download is broken — we will not help you design a silent release.

**Done when:** A photograph or a ticket shows the sentence exists in *your* environment.

---

## Step 2 — Appoint two humans (day 0–1)

- **AP Process Owner** — A for Orchestrator and for work-object state.  
- **AP Controls Lead** — A for Duplicate & Anomaly hygiene and for the payment sentence.

Names or role titles, not “the SSC.”

**Done when:** Both names are on the blank autonomy register (file in the Governance folder).

---

## Step 3 — Inventory unofficial agents (day 1, 45 minutes)

List every model, copilot, bot, or prompt already touching invoices, exceptions, vendors, or proposals. One line each: where it lives, who pasted it, what it is allowed to do *in practice*.

You are not required to turn them off today. You are required to see them.

**Done when:** The inventory has more than zero lines or a signed “none.”

---

## Step 4 — Take (or re-take) the diagnostic (day 1, 20 minutes)

Use the on-site diagnostic as a baseline even though you have bought. Band the layer. File the worksheet next to the register.

**Done when:** Band and date are written on the register cover.

---

## Step 5 — Read the stack overview once, slowly (day 1–2, 60 minutes)

File: Agent Library `00_AGENT_STACK_OVERVIEW.md`.

Do not charter from the table alone. Notice the interaction rules: intake does not match; matching does not invent receipts; triage does not resolve; flags are hypotheses.

**Done when:** You can explain those four rules to the Controls Lead without the file open.

---

## Step 6 — Charter Wave 1 only (days 2–5)

Charter, at **L0**, using `AGENT_CHARTER_TEMPLATE.md`:

1. Invoice Intake  
2. Invoice Validation  
3. Matching  
4. Exception Triage  
5. Duplicate & Anomaly  
6. Orchestrator  

Do not open Agents 12 or 13 above the idea of L1. Do not discuss L3.

Use the completed Matching example as a *format* guide, not as your policy.

**Done when:** Six signed charters exist. Exclusions are filled. Owners are named.

---

## Step 7 — Stand L0 for ten working days (days 5–15)

Shadow reports only. No recommendations in the system of record. No ERP writes. No external mail.

Compare a small sample of shadow output to what humans actually did. Note error classes. Do not “tune” by granting permission.

**Done when:** A sample log exists (even a spreadsheet) with dates and residual notes.

---

## Step 8 — Run the rights table on real work (during L0)

File: `HUMAN_VS_AGENT_DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md`.

Apply it to **ten recent exceptions** and **one payment run**. Circle any place a model was treated as accountable.

**Done when:** The marked table is filed. Illegal As have an owner to unwind them.

---

## Step 9 — Promote to L1 only on gates (days 15–30)

Open `AUTONOMY_PROGRESSION.md`. Promote **individually**. If Matching fails the gate, Intake may still pass.

L1 means recommend with a packet. It still means no ERP write and no external send.

**Done when:** The register shows levels that match evidence, not hopes.

---

## Step 10 — Invite Audit as observer (day 30)

Not as operator. Observer.

Show: six charters, the register, ten packets or shadow logs, the payment sentence, last run’s two human approvals.

If this meeting is politically impossible, you do not have a Designed layer. Stay at Informal/Gated and keep sampling.

**Done when:** A date happened or a written refusal is filed (a refusal is information).

---

## Step 11 — Record cost per 1,000 invoices (day 30–45)

Licence (including this one), model/token, exception minutes × your rate. No target. No “after AI” column.

If an unofficial agent from Step 3 is expensive and wrong, retire or demote it in writing.

**Done when:** One recorded month exists.

---

## Step 12 — Decide the next wave, or decide to wait (day 45–90)

Only if Wave 1 packets exist and the payment sentence still holds:

- Optional L2 packets on selected object types (prepare ≠ execute).  
- Wave 2 agents on the same ladder.  
- Payment Proposal Review and Close remain ≤ L1.

Wave 4 (narrow clean-match L3) is optional and often wrong. Payment remains human.

Recertify quarterly (`Recertify-or-Retire`).

**Done when:** A written decision: expand, hold, or demote. Silence is not a decision.

---
