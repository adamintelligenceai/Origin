# Marketplace Derivatives

**Purpose:** Etsy-style and similar digital/printables that attract AP and finance practitioners without selling the core OS cheaply.  
**Rule:** If a listing would let a serious buyer skip Professional, it does not ship.  
**Lockup on every listing:** EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS · evidenceroom.ai  
**Price band:** $7–$29. Never bundle “the full agent library.”

These products are independently authored printables and worksheets. They are not the Professional toolkit and not a licence to resell Evidence Room files.

Counsel should review shop names and the Evidence Room LLC collision before opening a shop.

---

## Positioning on marketplaces

**Shop name (proposed):** AP Agent Room (not “Evidence Room” alone)  
**Banner:** Proof before permission. Printables for AP leaders designing an agent layer. Not an ERP. Not a prompt pack.

**About blurb:**

We sell small, durable artefacts — posters, registers, and meeting sheets — that support a Finance Agent Operating System for Accounts Payable. The full sixteen-agent library and autonomy method are licensed separately at evidenceroom.ai. These listings will not replace that library and are not a licence to republish it.

## What must never be listed

- Any complete agent specification (01–16)  
- The completed Matching charter example  
- Autonomy progression gates in full  
- Human-vs-agent framework with worked payment-run example  
- 90-day plan narrative  
- Diagnostic scoring key as a paid SKU (the diagnostic is free on-site)  
- Northline’s fictional figures presented as industry data  
- Anything titled “Fraud detection kit,” “SOX pack,” “Guaranteed savings tracker”  
- A zip that is a thin rewrite of Professional

## Bundle rules

Allowed: “Wall + Register + Desk Card” at $24.  
Forbidden: “Complete AP Agent Kit” that implies the OS.  
Forbidden: mystery zips.

---

## Listing hygiene (every SKU)

- First sentence: what it is.  
- Second: what it is not (not ERP software, not fraud detection, not the full OS).  
- Third: evidenceroom.ai for the operating system.  
- Tags: accounts payable, AP, finance operations, agent governance, printable.  
- Avoid tags: fraud software, SOX certified, ChatGPT prompts, courtroom, forensic animation.  
- Shop policies: digital delivery, no refund after download except as required by law / Etsy — confirm.  
- Do not scrape buyer emails into the Evidence Room sequence without a lawful opt-in.

---

## Fulfilment

Instant digital download on the marketplace. Do not fulfil Professional files from Etsy. If a buyer messages “is this the full OS?” answer: No. Professional is $199 at evidenceroom.ai. This listing is a [poster/pad/register].

---

## Economics (illustrative, not a forecast)

At $9 average and marketplace fees ~6.5% + payment (Etsy example — confirm current fees), derivatives are traffic and brand objects. Do not run the company on them. If a derivative outsells Professional because it contains too much of the OS, pull the listing.
