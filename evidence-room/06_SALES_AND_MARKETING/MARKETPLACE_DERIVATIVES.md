# Marketplace derivatives — acquisition products that do not cannibalise the core

**Purpose:** Optional small products for Etsy-style and similar marketplaces, used as **acquisition**, not as a shadow AP Agent OS.  
**Rule:** A buyer of a derivative must still need Professional or Team to do the real work.  
**Rule:** Same voice, same non-promise list, same fictional-only example (Northline).  
**Do not** list these on the Lemon Squeezy core storefront at launch.

---

## 1. Why they exist

Some buyers browse “AP checklist” and “AI governance template” on marketplaces. A $9–$29 object can:

- teach one idea
- carry the master line
- send a serious reader to evidenceroom.ai/diagnostic

It must not contain the agent library, the control pack, the testing pack, or the Team workshop. If it does, we have discounted the company.

---

## 2. Guardrails

A derivative is safe if **all** are true:

1. It teaches **one** object (a fence worksheet, a level card, a measure picker).  
2. It is **≤ 8 pages** or one sheet.  
3. It does **not** reproduce a unique sequence from Professional.  
4. CTA is the Diagnostic or Method page, not “buy Professional 80% off”.  
5. Licence: personal / one-workplace print. Still **no resale, redistribution, sublicensing, republishing**.  
6. Disclaimer block in the file.  
7. Northline, if used, is labelled fictional.  
8. No Ardent/Forrester tables copied as if we have a reprint right — cite or omit.  
9. Visuals: paper/ink; no robots.  
10. Support load: if hello@ gets “I bought the Etsy fence, where is the library?”, the listing copy is wrong.

---

## 3. Approved derivative concepts

### D1 — AP Agent Fence Worksheet ($12)

One double-sided sheet: entity, class, amount, vendor set, systems, out-of-scope list, escalation names.  
**Does not include:** agent designs, controls library.  
**CTA:** “When the fence is honest, assess readiness → evidenceroom.ai/diagnostic”

### D2 — Readiness Level Card 0–4 ($9)

A postcard-style PDF of the five levels with one diagnostic question each.  
**Does not include:** the full Diagnostic instrument.  
**CTA:** full Diagnostic.

### D3 — Operating Measure Picker ($12)

A chooser for six allowed measures and a “do not print” list (savings, ROI, fraud, compliance, accuracy, autonomous payments).  
**Does not include:** KPI pack, data dictionary, 30/60/90 plan.

### D4 — Executive Twelve-Minute Agenda ($15)

Timed agenda + prompt questions (“What turns it off?”).  
**Does not include:** Team executive pack narrative or slides.

### D5 — Exception Taxonomy Starter ($19)

One-page taxonomy (missing data, match break, policy, vendor, system).  
**Does not include:** control objectives or test plans.

### D6 — Steward / Control Owner / Reviewer RACI stub ($15)

A three-role RACI with empty names.  
**Does not include:** governance pack or change-control forms.

### D7 — “What this agent will not do” wall card ($9)

Printable A4. Payment, override, close, fraud-omniscience, compliance-stamp — struck through.  
Almost merch. Useful. Harmless.

### D8 — Live-path sketch pad ($14)

Blank process swimlane with our labels (receipt → archive).  
**Does not include:** the mapping method’s worked notes or Northline filled map.

## 4. Rejected derivative ideas (do not make)

| Idea | Why it cannibalises |
|---|---|
| “Mini agent library — 5 bots” | That *is* the product |
| “AP AI business case with ROI spreadsheet” | Violates the brand and undercuts Professional |
| “SOX for AI agents in AP” | Implies compliance we do not sell |
| “Prompt pack: pay matching invoices” | Out of method |
| 40-page “lite OS” at $29 | Team/Pro in a trench coat |
| Using our Professional templates with a watermark | Licence leak |

---

## 5. Listing copy pattern

**Title:** AP Agent Fence Worksheet — Evidence Room (Accounts Payable)  
**First line:** A one-page worksheet to write what an AP agent may touch — and what it must not.  
**Body:** Not software. Not a savings calculator. Not legal or audit advice. Does not replace your ERP.  
**Shop name:** Evidence Room  
**Tags:** accounts payable, finance operations, AI governance, invoice process — not “make money with AI”, not “fraud detector”.

---

## 6. Fulfilment

Separate, ugly-on-purpose file names: `ER-DERIV-FENCE.pdf`.  
Watermark footer: Evidence Room · evidenceroom.ai · Licensed for one workplace · Full OS is a different product.

Do not put licence keys on marketplace SKUs unless the platform forces an account. Terms in the PDF are enough for $9.

Refunds: follow the marketplace. Do not fight a $12 refund.

---

## 7. Path back to the core

```
Marketplace listing
    → PDF
        → last page: Diagnostic
            → email sequence
                → Professional / Team
```

Track with a unique URL: `evidenceroom.ai/diagnostic?from=fence-sheet`  
If 90 days of a derivative produce downloads but zero paid, keep it only if it is cheap to host. If it produces paid **and** “where is the rest?” anger, rewrite the listing to say “this is one sheet, not AP Agent OS”.

---

## 8. When to start

Not in week 1. After the Diagnostic and Professional are clean (suggested: after day 45). One derivative first (D1 or D7). Kill it if brand search starts associating Evidence Room with “Etsy printables” more than with AP.

---

## 9. P&L

Hold marketplace cash in a separate line. Target: curiosity, not 15% of revenue. If a derivative ever outsells Professional in units *and* people treat it as sufficient, discontinue.
