## Carousel 01 — Five objects of responsibility

**Kicker:** METHOD  
**Cover title:** An AP agent is not live because it ran once.

**S2 — Purpose**  
One sentence a controller will sign. If they will not read it aloud, it is not the purpose.

**S3 — Fence**  
Class, amount, vendor set, entities, systems, document types. Outside the box does not exist.

**S4 — Escalation**  
States the agent must not resolve. Name the human who receives them.

**S5 — Evidence**  
Inputs, outputs, timestamp, brief version, human decision, location, reader.

**S6 — Measure**  
Operating numbers and a pause. Not a savings fairy tale.

**S7 — Close**  
Five objects. Five owners.  
Responsibility is earned.
