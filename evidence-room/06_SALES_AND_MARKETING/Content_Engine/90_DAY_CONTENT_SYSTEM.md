# 90-Day Content System — Evidence Room

**Channel mix:** LinkedIn-first (primary buyer habitat) · owned site · email nurture  
**Editorial rule:** **80/20** — 80% teaching / diagnostic / frameworks; 20% product / offer  
**Voice:** Intelligent, concise, credible, non-hype. No guaranteed ROI/fraud/compliance claims.

---

## Operating cadence

| Week pattern | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|--------------|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Standard | Framework or chart | LinkedIn teach post | Carousel or diagnostic | LinkedIn teach post | Light offer / social proof (≤20%) |
| Monthly | Long article | | | | Diagnostic push → free scorecard |

**Volume (90 days):** ~36–40 LinkedIn posts from the banks below (rotate); 10 carousels; 5 articles; charts & diagnostics embedded as posts.
