# 90-Day Content System

**Product:** Evidence Room — Content Engine  
**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-03-20  
**Mix:** ~80% insight / education · ~20% product / offer  
**Channel focus:** LinkedIn organic + owned site resources

---

## 1. Pillars

| Pillar | Share | Themes |
|---|---|---|
| **Authority design** | 25% | Seven questions, scope, non-goals |
| **Exceptions & controls** | 25% | Taxonomy, SoD, stop conditions, audit trails |
| **Measurement** | 20% | KPI formulas, earned responsibility, BIC context discipline |
| **Stack honesty** | 15% | Agent layer vs AP suites |
| **Change & leadership** | 15% | CFO narrative, analyst roles, anti-hype |

Promo content maps offers to pillars without breaking the 80/20 rule across a rolling 30 days.

## 2. Cadence (per week)

| Day | Asset |
|---|---|
| Mon | LinkedIn post (insight) |
| Tue | Short carousel idea OR repurpose |
| Wed | LinkedIn post (controls/exceptions) |
| Thu | Comment-led engagement on peer CFO/AP threads |
| Fri | LinkedIn post (measurement or leadership) |
| Sat/Sun | Optional longform drafting |

**Monthly:** 1 longform article · 2–3 carousels · 1 soft offer post (Free diagnostic or Starter) · 1 Team/Custom mention max
