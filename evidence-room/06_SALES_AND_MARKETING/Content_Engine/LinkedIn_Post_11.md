# LinkedIn Post 11

The 5 questions every CFO should ask before deploying AP agents:
1. What is our cost per exception resolved?
2. What percentage of invoices can STP today?
3. Do we have 12 months of historical data for testing?
4. Who owns agent governance?
5. What is our risk appetite for autonomy?
If you cannot answer all five, start with the diagnostic — not the technology.
