# Marketplace Derivatives — Etsy-style SKUs

**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-03-20  
**Purpose:** Low-price discovery SKUs that **do not cannibalise** core ladder (Free / $79 / $199 / $499 / $1,500–$3,000)  
**Rule:** Derivatives are narrow templates or posters; they never include full Starter Guide, Pro OS, or Team workshop packs.

---

## Cannibalisation controls

1. No SKU may include >20% of Starter Guide content.  
2. No workshop facilitation scripts on marketplaces.  
3. No Custom intake or SOW templates publicly.  
4. Every listing links Free diagnostic as the serious next step.  
5. Price floor $9; never discount core tiers on Etsy.

If sold via Lemon Squeezy instead of Etsy, apply platform fee **5% + $0.50** (+ intl/PayPal/affiliate as applicable). Marketplace channel fees differ — model separately.
