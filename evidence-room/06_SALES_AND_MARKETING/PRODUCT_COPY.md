## Subhead

Evidence Room — AP Agent OS does not replace the AP stack you already run. It is the method and toolkit for designing the agent layer that operates across it: sixteen named roles, an evidence standard, a permission ladder, and a written list of decisions that stay human.

## Value proposition

Finance and transformation leaders are being asked to put AI on invoice-to-pay. Most will do it by pasting prompts into the nearest tool. That produces activity without a workforce model, without evidence gates, and without a payment boundary.

AP Agent OS is the operating system for that layer. You specify each agent, name its human owner, define the packet it must produce, and withhold the next unit of permission until the evidence exists. ERP remains the system of record for accounting. Named humans remain the system of record for payment, vendor bank details, and close attestation.

You keep Coupa, SAP, Oracle, Dynamics, NetSuite, Tipalti, BILL, the shared-service team, and the delegation of authority. You add a designed layer, not another platform to implement.

## Features

Each feature is an artefact the buyer can open. None is a hosted robot.

### 1. Sixteen-agent architecture

A named portfolio, not a blob called “AP AI.”

Invoice Intake. Invoice Validation. Matching. Exception Triage. Goods Receipt. PO Quality. Approval. Supplier Resolution. Internal Follow-up. Duplicate & Anomaly. Vendor Statement. Payment Proposal Review. AP Close. AP Reporting. Root Cause. Orchestrator.

Every agent has a job, a default autonomy, a typical human owner, a list of exclusions, and a place in the invoice lifecycle. Intake does not decide match. Matching does not invent receipts. Triage does not resolve. Payment Proposal Review annotates; it does not release.

### 2. Evidence standard

A written definition of what must sit in the packet before a recommendation is eligible for a human decision: source, extract, match worksheet, correspondence, approval log, flag outcome, owner, SLA clock. Incomplete packets do not travel. Perfect packets still do not pay.

### 3. Permission ladder (L0–L4)

- **L0 Observe** — read, classify, log, shadow-report. No reliance.
- **L1 Recommend** — propose with rationale and evidence. No ERP write. No external send.
- **L2 Prepare** — assemble a complete ready-to-act packet. A human or downstream control executes.
- **L3 Execute within guardrails** — only a named, pre-approved, low-risk action inside a written limit table.
- **L4 Managed autonomy** — the same bounded set as L3, reviewed by exception and recertified.

Default for a new agent is L0 or L1. No agent is commissioned at L3 or L4. Payment-related work has a hard ceiling: recommend and prepare only.

### 4. Human-vs-agent decision framework

A one-page rights table for the decisions that never leave the control environment: payment authorisation and release, vendor bank-detail change, new vendor approval, DOA edits, tolerance-table edits, period-end attestation, material write-off, ambiguous tax, legal settlement language, high-value anomaly clearance, override of a policy hold, and expansion of an agent’s own scope.

### 5. Agent charter template

A blank charter plus a completed Matching Agent example. Monday work: name the owner, the object types, the evidence, the exclusions, and the starting level.

### 6. Exception atlas

Coded break types so fail reasons are analysable. Free-text-only exceptions are treated as a design smell, not a culture.

### 7. Orchestrator model

Every invoice, exception, statement line, and payment-proposal line is a work object with an ID, source, current agent, human owner, status, autonomy context, evidence URI, SLA clock, escalation state, and cost token. The Orchestrator is the system of record for work-object state. The ERP remains the system of record for accounting.

### 8. KPI language that refuses theatre

Operating-discipline measures: capture completeness, coded validation fails, sampled match worksheets, recorded flag outcomes, aging by named owner, dual-human payment release, an autonomy register with no silent promotions, and cost per thousand invoices (licence + model + exception minutes). Not a savings guarantee. Not an ROI calculator.

### 9. Governance cadence

Weekly exception mix. Monthly policy and limit-table review. Quarterly autonomy recertification. Internal Audit as observer, not operator. Model and prompt changes treated as change-controlled configuration.

### 10. 90-day layer plan

Wave 1: six agents at L0/L1 (Intake, Validation, Matching, Triage, Duplicate & Anomaly, Orchestrator). Wave 2: prepare-level packets. Wave 3: periodic agents. Wave 4: earned execute for a narrow clean-match class only after gates — still never payment release.

### 11. Readiness diagnostic

A structured assessment of whether the organisation has an agent layer, a pile of prompts, or neither. Produces a picture and a recommended first wave. Does not certify readiness, compliance, or investment return.

### 12. Fictional operating picture

Northline Industrials — a made-up mid-market manufacturer — is used throughout the library so the charters are concrete. Figures are illustrative. They are not a benchmark and not a promise.

## Outcomes (what improves — stated as operating discipline)

Use these sentences. Do not upgrade them into guarantees.

- **Named workforce.** The organisation can point to sixteen roles instead of “the AP copilot.”
- **Inspectable packets.** A Controller or auditor can open the evidence for a sampled invoice, exception, or proposal line.
- **Withheld permission.** Autonomy is visible on a register. Promotion is a decision. Demotion is available.
- **Held payment boundary.** Two humans still release the run. The method writes that down so a vendor cannot shrug it off.
- **Coded exceptions.** Mix becomes analysable. Root Cause has something to cluster.
- **Visible layer cost.** Licence, model, and exception minutes are recorded per thousand invoices.
- **Shared language.** AP, Controls, Transformation, Treasury, and Audit can disagree on a charter instead of on a vibe.
- **No rip-and-replace.** The stack stays. The layer is designed.

If first-time match or cycle time later moves, treat that as an observed outcome after commissioning — not as a reason anyone bought.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this software that connects to our ERP?

No. AP Agent OS is a licensed digital toolkit and method. You implement with the ERP, capture, workflow, RPA, iPaaS, and human procedures you already have or will choose. The architecture is ERP-agnostic.

### Does this replace our AP platform?

No. Design the layer. Keep the stack. Capture, match, workflow, and payment tools remain. The OS specifies how agents should work across them.

### Will this make payments autonomous?

No. Payment authorisation and release stay human at every autonomy level. Agent 12 (Payment Proposal Review) may annotate a proposal. It may not approve, release, or transmit payment. If a vendor proposes that an agent “just release the run if everything looks fine,” the method’s answer is no.

### Does the Duplicate & Anomaly agent detect fraud?

No. It raises flags — hypotheses — for human review. A flag is not a verdict, not a fraud finding, and not authorisation to hold or release funds on its own. Clearing a high-value flag is a named human control.

### Does using this make us compliant?

No. The toolkit produces evidence a control owner can present. The control owner decides sufficiency. Evidence Room does not provide legal, audit, tax, or regulatory advice and does not certify compliance with any standard.

### Does this guarantee savings or ROI?

No. We do not promise savings, cost-takeout, cycle-time reduction, or return on investment. We give you language and measures for operating discipline. Any later cost discussion is a management judgement.

### Is this just a prompt pack?

No. A prompt is an instruction to a model. An operating system is a set of roles, rights, evidence, refusals, owners, and promotion gates. Professional includes example instruction patterns where they help a charter; they are not the product.

### Who is the buyer?

The practitioner who will design the layer — typically a Finance Transformation lead, AP Manager, Controller, or a small trio of those roles — and the organisation that will licence Team or commission a Custom Blueprint.

### What do I actually download?

A structured file set: architecture, agent specifications, charter template, decision framework, autonomy progression, KPI and governance language, diagnostic, 90-day path, and a START HERE sequence. Formats are documents and worksheets, not a hosted app.

### Can I share it with my team?

Individual and Professional licences are for one practitioner and ordinary internal working use within the named organisation, as described in the licence. Team is the licence designed for a shared AP / Controls / Transformation / Audit working group. None of the licences permit resale, redistribution, or publishing the materials as your own product. Confirm the final licence with counsel before relying on this summary.

### Do you store our invoice data?

The diagnostic and the toolkit, as sold, do not require you to upload invoice files or ERP extracts to Evidence Room. If you paste examples into a form, treat that as your disclosure. See Privacy and the AI-use disclosure.

### What if we are already running agents?

Start with the diagnostic and the autonomy register. Most early programmes have activity without charters, without evidence gates, and with unclear payment holds. The OS is for that situation as much as for a green field.

### Is Northline a customer?

No. Northline Industrials is a fictional company used to make the library concrete.

### Which countries and ERPs?

The method is written in plain finance English and mapped to common invoice-to-pay objects. It is not localised legal advice for any jurisdiction and not a certified adapter for any ERP.

## Objection handling

### “We already have Copilot / an IDP vendor / a bot in AP.”

Keep it. The question is whether you have a workforce model, an evidence standard, a permission ladder, and a human-held payment boundary — or a feature turned on in one screen. The OS sits across tools. It does not ask you to turn them off.

### “We don’t need another framework.”

You need named roles and named refusals before the first model call that touches a payment proposal. A framework that withholds permission is cheaper than an undocumented agent that everyone assumes is “just helping.”

### “Our volume is small.”

Then you can read the whole Professional toolkit in a sitting and still use the decision framework on ten exceptions and one payment run. Small volume is not a reason to skip the payment hold.

### “Our volume is huge; we need software.”

You still need the layer designed before you scale L1 recommendations across 80,000 invoices. Software without charters scales ambiguity. Buy or build software; specify here first.

### “Legal / InfoSec will never allow agents.”

Good. Give them the human-vs-agent table and the exclusions list. Many blocks exist because the proposal was “autonomous AP.” A proposal that starts at L0, names owners, and forbids payment release is a different conversation.

### “Internal Audit will slow this down.”

Invite them as observers at day 30. The product assumes they will sample packets. That is a feature.

### “This feels like consulting.”

Consulting is a facilitated judgement on *your* stack. Professional is a licensed toolkit you apply. Custom Blueprint is the facilitated option, by application, at a stated range. Do not blur them.

### “$199 / $499 seems expensive for documents.”

You are licensing an operating system for a workforce that will sit next to cash. The alternative in the market is a prompt list or a slide. Compare it to a week of undocumented agent experiments, not to a PDF page count.

### “Can we try it on one invoice type?”

Yes. Wave 1 is six agents at observe/recommend. Charter Matching on one clean PO class before you discuss anything else.

### “We need guaranteed outcomes to get budget.”

Then this is the wrong product to put in that business case. We will not write the guarantee. If budget requires a savings number, that number must come from the organisation’s own baseline and judgement — not from our copy.

### “Isn’t ‘Evidence Room’ that courtroom animation company?”

Different organisation. Evidence Room LLC is a forensic-animation firm at evidence-room.net. We are EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS at evidenceroom.ai. We do not do courtroom reconstruction. Trademark clearance remains an open professional item.

## Implementation limitations (say these before the sale)

1. **You still have to do the work.** Charters, owners, sampling, and recertification are organisational habits. Files do not install them.
2. **You still need a stack.** The OS does not capture invoices, post documents, or send bank files.
3. **Models fail.** Extraction will be wrong sometimes. Matching will be wrong sometimes. The method assumes error and designs holds, not perfection.
4. **L3 is rare and narrow.** If your political goal is “touchless AP this quarter,” this product will disappoint you on purpose.
5. **Local law and policy win.** DOA, tax, employment, and payment rules in your entities override any template.
6. **No connector certification.** Mapping to SAP, Oracle, Dynamics, NetSuite, or Workday is a design activity, not a supported integration.
7. **Northline is fiction.** Do not present its figures to a board as comparables.
8. **One practitioner cannot silently bind a group.** If AP, Controls, and Audit must share, buy Team.
9. **Affiliates and partners cannot resell the files.** They can refer a checkout link if an affiliate programme exists.
10. **We will not write your savings slide.** If that is the only artefact your PMO wants, do not buy this.
