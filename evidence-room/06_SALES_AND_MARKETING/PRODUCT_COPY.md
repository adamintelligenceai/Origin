## FAQs

**Is this software?**  
No. It is an operating-model and documentation suite for governing AI agents in AP.

**Will it replace our AP platform?**  
No. It is designed to work across the platforms you already own.

**Do you guarantee ROI?**  
No. We provide structures to measure earned responsibility using your baselines.

**Can agents pay suppliers?**  
Default posture: no silent payment execution. Payment remains human- and platform-owned unless you create a separate explicit charter (not recommended in Starter).

**How is this different from ChatGPT prompt packs?**  
Prompt packs rarely include exception taxonomy, promotion rules, SoD, audit trails, or executive governance. Evidence Room requires the seven product questions before an agent “ships.”

**What is Lemon Squeezy’s role?**  
Merchant of Record for digital checkout. Platform fee **5% + $0.50**; additional fees may include **+1.5%** international, **+1.5%** PayPal, affiliate **+3%** merchant.

**Refunds?**  
See Storefront Spec recommendation — digital goods generally limited refund window after download; services treated separately.

**Who is the ICP?**  
Mid-market to enterprise AP/Controller teams with existing automation/ERP and a need for governed AI — not teams shopping to rip out their suite.
