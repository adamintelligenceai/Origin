# Lead Magnet Funnel — Evidence Room

**Primary magnet:** Free AP AI Readiness Diagnostic + scorecard  
**Destination paid tiers:** Starter $79 → Pro $199 → Team $499 → Blueprint $1.5–3k

---

## Funnel stages

| Stage | Asset | Conversion goal |
|-------|-------|-----------------|
| Attention | LinkedIn teach posts (80%) + diagnostic posts | Profile / site visit |
| Capture | Diagnostic landing / Lemon free product | Email + optional company |
| Nurture | `Email_Funnel/SEQUENCE_7_EMAILS.md` (7 emails) | Starter or Pro purchase |
| Expand | In-product CTAs + Team narrative | Team / Blueprint |
| Retain | Implementation success content | Referrals / renewals (later SaaS) |

---

## Fulfilment path

1. Form or Lemon Squeezy free checkout captures email.  
2. Deliver PDF/MD diagnostic + scorecard (CSV/XLSX).  
3. Enroll 7-email sequence (Day 0–21).  
4. UTMs: `utm_source=linkedin|email&utm_campaign=diagnostic_v1`.  
5. Support alias monitors replies with volume/ERP questions.
