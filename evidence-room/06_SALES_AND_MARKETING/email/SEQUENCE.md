## Email 03 — Responsibility is earned

**When:** Day 4  
**Subject:** Responsibility is earned  
**Preview:** Five objects. Five owners.

The master line is not decoration.

An AP agent is in production when five objects have owners:

1. Purpose — one sentence a controller will sign.  
2. Fence — class, amount, vendors, entities, systems.  
3. Escalation — states it must not resolve.  
4. Evidence — what is kept, where, for whom.  
5. Measure — operating numbers, and a pause.

Missing one of the five is how a pilot becomes an unowned queue with a nicer name.

Governed agents. Measurable outcomes.  
Those are the supporting words. They are also the test.
