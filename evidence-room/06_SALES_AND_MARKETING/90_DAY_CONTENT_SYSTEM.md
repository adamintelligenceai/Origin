# 90-day content system — Evidence Room

**Window:** Launch quarter (illustrative Day 1 = public site + Diagnostic live).  
**Mix:** ~80% insight, ~20% commercial.  
**Assets:** this file sequences the pieces in `linkedin/`, `articles/`, `charts/`, and `email/`.  
**Rule:** Northline is fictional. Ardent/Forrester only when sourced. No savings/ROI/fraud/compliance/accuracy/autonomous-payment promises.

---

## Operating cadence

| Cadence | What |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 3 posts / week (Mon, Wed, Thu) |
| Carousel | 1 / week (Tuesday) |
| Diagnostic-led post | 1 / week (Friday, light) |
| Framework post | 1 / 9 days (from the ten) |
| Longform | 5 articles across 90 days (days 5, 22, 40, 58, 76) |
| Chart | 1 / 9 days, reused as LinkedIn image |
| Email | 7-mail sequence on Diagnostic opt-in; launch list gets a compressed version |
| Site | No blog chrome at first — articles can live at `/method` annex or `/articles/{slug}` |

Do not post every asset you have in week 1. The library is a quarter, not a day.

---

## Voice check (every piece)

Before publish, delete the sentence if it:

- uses “unlock”, “supercharge”, “10x”, “AI-powered”
- invents a customer
- attributes cash savings to Evidence Room
- implies we replace ERP/AP
- uses a robot or neon visual

Master line when a close is needed: **Responsibility is earned.**  
Supporting: **Governed agents. Measurable outcomes.**

## Production notes

- Batch-write LinkedIn on Friday for the next week.  
- Charts: one sentence of source notes under every image.  
- Articles: no lead-magnet interstitial in the first screen. CTA at the end.  
- If a post performs, reply in comments as a human. Do not drop a coupon in the first reply.  
- Kill any derivative that starts to outrank the Diagnostic as the ask.

---

## End of quarter review

Read: Diagnostic count, paid mix, refunds, “is this software?” support rate, which article was finished.

Do not read: impressions as success, or a savings story from a buyer we cannot verify.

Decide: continue weekly cadence, or drop to 2×/week and spend time on Custom quality.
