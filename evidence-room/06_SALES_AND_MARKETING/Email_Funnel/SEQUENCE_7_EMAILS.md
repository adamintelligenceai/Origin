## Email 2 — Day 2 · Problem sharpness

**Subject:** Activity isn’t value  
**Preview:** 40,000 bot runs can still mean a flat touch rate.

Hi {{first_name}},

Most “AP AI” dashboards celebrate motion: documents processed, suggestions generated, workflows triggered.

Controllers should ask different questions:

- Did **cycle time** and **touch rate** move?  
- Did **exception ageing** improve by taxonomy—not by inbox heroics?  
- Can you show **evidence** (version, inputs, confidence, override) on material actions?  
- Did **financial** results move only where the mechanism is explicit—and with a realisation %?

If your score showed weak measurement or governance, that’s not a failure. It’s the work.

## Email 3 — Day 4 · Shadow mode

**Subject:** If it wasn’t shadowed, it wasn’t evidenced  
**Preview:** Demos don’t catch false negatives.

Hi {{first_name}},

Before any agent writes to your ERP path, run **shadow mode**: the agent scores live (or replayed) cases while humans keep doing the job.

You want thresholds on precision—and stricter attention to **recall on critical classes** (duplicates, policy breaches). False positives annoy. False negatives pay twice.

Promotion from shadow → pilot should be a written decision with sample sizes, failure modes, and a tested kill switch.

This is the unfashionable part of Finance AI. It’s also why Evidence Room exists.

— Evidence Room

## Email 5 — Day 10 · Framework teach

**Subject:** Four metric classes (steal this scorecard)  
**Preview:** Keep activity out of your ROI slide.

Hi {{first_name}},

When you report AP agents to a CFO, separate:

1. **Activity** — ran, covered, suggested  
2. **Operational** — cycle time, touch rate, exception age  
3. **Financial** — cost/invoice, discounts, leakage (assumption-labelled)  
4. **Risk/control** — sample pass rate, evidence completeness, unowned exceptions

Mix them on one “savings” slide and you train leadership to distrust the programme.

If you want the formulas and scorecard structure, they’re in AP Agent OS Professional.
