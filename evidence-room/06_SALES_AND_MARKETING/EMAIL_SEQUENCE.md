## Email 5 — Day 10  
**Subject:** Prompt packs fail in invoice-to-pay  
**Preview:** A paragraph cannot hold a delegation of authority.

**Body**

{{FirstName}} —

A prompt is an instruction to a model. It can live inside a charter. It cannot be the charter.

Prompt packs collapse in AP for ordinary reasons:

1. **No owner.** The prompt is in someone’s personal workspace.  
2. **No exclusions.** The model will happily draft a bank-detail change.  
3. **No packet.** The reply looks complete and cites nothing.  
4. **No promotion gate.** Yesterday it summarised. Today it is “allowed to post.”  
5. **No demotion.** When it is expensive and wrong, someone adds another prompt.  
6. **No payment boundary.** The words “be careful” are not dual approval.

If a colleague forwards you a pack titled “100 prompts for Accounts Payable,” ask for the human-vs-agent table. If there isn’t one, you have instructions without an operating system.
