# Email 2 — Biggest Misconception

Subject: The biggest AP automation misconception

Most Finance leaders believe AP automation means replacing their team with software.

It doesn't.

The organisations achieving $2.78 per invoice (vs the $9.40 average) aren't running headless AP departments. They're running governed agent workforces where:

• Agents handle classification, matching, and follow-up drafting
• Humans retain judgment, exceptions, supplier relationships, and payment authorisation
• Agents earn responsibility through demonstrated performance

The question isn't 'how do we automate AP?' It's 'which AP decisions can agents prepare, and which must humans own?'
