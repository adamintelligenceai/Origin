# Email 3 — First Agent

Subject: How to identify your first AP agent

The most common mistake: trying to automate everything at once.

Select ONE agent using these criteria:

1. High volume (most invoices affected)
2. Bounded scope (clear inputs and outputs)
3. Lower risk (mistakes are recoverable)
4. Data available (ERP access confirmed)
5. Measurable (baseline KPI exists)

Best starters: Invoice Validation (AP-VAL-02) or Exception Triage (AP-TRI-04).

Worst starters: Payment execution, fraud detection, or anything requiring full autonomy on day one.

Start at Level 0 (Observe). Earn the right to act.
