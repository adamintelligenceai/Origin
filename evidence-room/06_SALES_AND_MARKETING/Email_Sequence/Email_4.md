# Email 4 — Earn Responsibility

Subject: How agents earn responsibility

Evidence Room's core principle: agents earn responsibility like employees.

Level 0 — Observe: Reviews, cannot act
Level 1 — Recommend: Suggests actions
Level 2 — Prepare: Drafts actions for approval
Level 3 — Execute within guardrails: Low-risk auto-execution
Level 4 — Managed autonomy: Independent with exception oversight

Most organisations should start at Level 0-1 and progress one level at a time over 90+ days.

Never skip levels. Never default to full autonomy.

— Evidence Room
