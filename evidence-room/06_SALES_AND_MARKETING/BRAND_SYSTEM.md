## 1. What the brand must do in five seconds

A finance, AP, audit, or transformation leader should be able to answer four questions from the lockup and one sentence:

1. **What it is:** an operating system for AI agents in Accounts Payable — a design and governance toolkit, not another AP platform.
2. **Who it is for:** organisations that already have an AP stack and are about to (or have begun to) put agents on invoice-to-pay work.
3. **What it improves:** the design, evidence, permission, and oversight of that agent layer.
4. **Why it is different:** it does not replace Coupa, SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Tipalti, BILL, or the shared-service team. It helps the organisation design the agent layer that operates across the stack it already owns.

If a visitor thinks this is a new ERP, a payment product, a fraud engine, or a prompt pack, the brand has failed.

| Specificity | Does it imply a working rule, not a mood? |

### Alternative 8 — “Proof before permission.” **SELECTED**

**Idea:** An agent does not receive the right to recommend, prepare, or act until it has produced the evidence the organisation named in advance.

| Test | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Category fit | 4 | Finance-native. Maps to invoice-to-pay without naming software. |
| Differentiator | 5 | Prompt packs grant permission first and hope for proof later. This reverses the order. |
| Executive tone | 5 | Speaks in the language of DOA, dual approval, and close attestation. |
| Specificity | 5 | It is a rule. You can put it on an autonomy register. |
| Risk | 5 | Promises a standard of work, not a financial or regulatory outcome. |
| **Total** | **24** | Highest score. It is the daily operating idea, the brand sentence, and the reason the product exists. |

**Why it wins**

- It is a **working rule**, not a mood. Autonomy promotion in the AP Agent OS is evidence-gated. The brand idea and the product mechanic are the same sentence.
- It is **anti-hype**. It withholds permission. That is the opposite of “autonomous AP.”
- It is **repeatable**. A Controller can use it in a vendor meeting: “Show me the proof. Then we talk permission.”
- It **does not collide** with savings, fraud, compliance, accuracy, payment safety, or ROI.
- It **explains the name**. A room is where evidence is kept. Permission is granted in another room, by a named human.

**Residual risks (flagged, not fatal)**

- “Proof” must never be stretched to “proof of compliance” or “proof of no fraud.”
- “Permission” must never be stretched to “the product authorises payments.”
- Forensic-animation firm Evidence Room LLC (evidence-room.net) is a clearance item. The distinctive lockup and AP-specific category language are the commercial mitigation, not a legal conclusion. See `10_LEGAL_AND_LICENSING/TRADEMARK_AND_DOMAIN_NOTES.md`.

## 3. Selected brand system

### 3.1 Brand idea

**Proof before permission.**

An AP agent earns the next unit of responsibility only after it has produced the evidence the organisation defined for that unit. Observation is the default. Recommendation is earned. Preparation is earned. Execution inside a written guardrail is rare, sampled, and never applied to payment release.

### 3.2 Brand architecture

| Layer | Expression |
|---|---|
| Master brand | EVIDENCE ROOM |
| Product | AP AGENT OS |
| Lockup | EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS |
| Category | Finance Agent Operating System |
| Brand idea | Proof before permission. |
| Category shorthand | The AP agent layer |
| Stance line | Design the layer. Keep the stack. |
| Method line | Evidence before execution. |
| Domain | evidenceroom.ai |

Do not shorten the public lockup to “Evidence Room” on paid media, storefronts, or the first screen of the site without the product descriptor nearby. On subsequent screens, “Evidence Room” is acceptable if “AP Agent OS” is already in view.

### 3.3 What we sell

A licensed digital toolkit and method for specifying, chartering, governing, and scaling a portfolio of AI agents across Accounts Payable. The toolkit includes the sixteen-agent architecture, autonomy progression, human-vs-agent decision rights, evidence standards, KPI language, diagnostic, and implementation path.

We do not sell:

- an ERP, AP automation platform, or payment rail
- a hosted agent that posts, pays, or changes vendor bank details
- a certification of compliance, fraud coverage, or accounting accuracy
- a prompt pack as the product

### 3.4 Brand personality

| Trait | Means | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| Intelligent | Names the object, the owner, and the hold | Academic fog, unexplained frameworks |
| Concise | One claim per sentence | Manifesto paragraphs |
| Credible | Distinguishes observed from promised | Case-study theatre, invented ROI |
| Executive | Writable into a steering pack | Founder-voice slang |
| Specific | Sixteen agents, five autonomy levels, named human holds | “End-to-end AI transformation” |
| Non-hype | Withholds what we cannot stand behind | Guarantees, magic, 10x, touchless as a commitment |

### 3.5 Visual direction (verbal only)

This is not a design system. It is direction for anyone commissioning one.

- **Colour temperature:** paper, graphite, a single signal red for holds and human-only actions. No neon “AI purple.”
- **Typography:** a serious serif or a quiet grotesque. The lockup is set in small caps or tracked capitals: EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS.
- **Diagrams over photography.** Workforce maps, autonomy ladders, and evidence-pack skeletons beat stock “team high-fiving in glass office.”
- **Empty space is a feature.** The brand should feel like a well-kept file, not a launch party.
- **The Room mark (concept):** a simple rectangular frame — a room — with a gap where a human signature belongs. Do not use a gavel, a fingerprint, a shield, or a robot.

Commission a designer. Do not treat this paragraph as a logo.

## 4. Verbal identity

### 4.1 How we name things

| Object | Preferred name | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| The company / master brand | Evidence Room | ER, EvRo, “the Room” in first mention |
| The product | AP Agent OS | “the AI,” “the bot suite,” “the platform” |
| The full lockup | EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS | EvidenceRoom, evidence-room, Evidence Room LLC |
| A single specified role | Agent 03 Matching; the Matching Agent | “our matching AI,” “the matcher bot” |
| Autonomy | L0 Observe … L4 Managed autonomy | “fully autonomous,” “touchless,” “human-out-of-the-loop” |
| A recommendation packet | Evidence pack | “the AI’s decision,” “the verdict” |
| A possible duplicate | Flag; hypothesis; signal | “fraud alert,” “caught a fraud” |
| The buyer | Organisation; named role (Controller, AP Manager) | “users,” “the community” |
| The existing systems | AP stack; ERP; invoice-to-pay systems | “legacy,” “dinosaur systems” |
| The work we do | Design the agent layer | “implement AI,” “deploy copilot” |

### 4.2 Sentence habits

- Lead with the human hold when the topic is money, vendors, or tax.
- Prefer verbs that produce artefacts: specify, charter, sample, hold, recertify, demote.
- Prefer nouns a Controller already owns: delegation of authority, dual approval, period-end attestation, vendor master, payment proposal.
- When using a number, say whether it is illustrative, observed, or a product count (sixteen agents, five autonomy levels). Do not present fictional Northline figures as benchmarks.
- When refusing a claim, refuse it in the same sentence you introduce the topic. Do not bury the refusal in a footnote the buyer will not read.

### 4.3 First-person and address

- Site and product: “Evidence Room” or “the AP Agent OS.” Occasional “we” is fine.
- Email and LinkedIn: “you” for the practitioner; “your organisation” for the institution.
- Do not write “I” as a founder-hero voice unless the About page is telling a short origin paragraph.
- Do not invent a mascot or a first-name-only guru.

### 4.4 Approved short sentences (use freely)

- Proof before permission.
- Design the layer. Keep the stack.
- Agents earn responsibility.
- Prepare is not execute.
- Payment authorisation stays human.
- A flag is a hypothesis, not a verdict.
- The stack is not the operating system for agents.
- Observation is the default.
- If it is not in the charter, the agent does not do it.
- Evidence first. Then a named human.

### 4.5 Pronunciation and styling

- **Evidence Room:** two words, title case. Not “Evidenceroom.”
- **AP Agent OS:** “A-P Agent O-S.” Not “ape agent oss,” not “Ay-Pee OS.”
- **Em dash in the lockup:** EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS. A hyphen is acceptable in filenames and URLs: `evidence-room`, `evidenceroom.ai`.
- **Autonomy levels:** L0, L1, L2, L3, L4 — always with the name on first use in a piece.

### 5.1 Roof — brand idea

**Proof before permission.**

### 5.3 Beams — four messages that must appear on Home

| Beam | Five-second line | Supporting sentence |

| Why it is different | It does not replace the AP stack. | It helps the organisation design the agent layer that operates across the systems it already runs. |

### 5.4 Rooms — message pillars

**Pillar A — The stack is not the OS**  
Invoice capture, matching, workflow, and payment tools do their jobs. They were not designed as a workforce model for agents. Prompting inside those tools is not an operating system.

**Pillar B — Sixteen named roles, not a blob of “AP AI”**  
Intake, Validation, Matching, Exception Triage, Goods Receipt, PO Quality, Approval, Supplier Resolution, Internal Follow-up, Duplicate & Anomaly, Vendor Statement, Payment Proposal Review, AP Close, AP Reporting, Root Cause, Orchestrator. Each has a charter, an owner, a default autonomy, and a list of things it will not do.

**Pillar C — Evidence-gated autonomy**  
L0 Observe. L1 Recommend. L2 Prepare. L3 Execute within written guardrails. L4 Managed autonomy over that same bounded set. No agent is commissioned at L3 or L4. Payment-adjacent work has a hard ceiling: recommend and prepare only.

**Pillar D — Human-held boundaries**  
Payment release, vendor bank-detail change, new vendor approval, DOA edits, tolerance-table edits, close attestation, material write-off, ambiguous tax, legal settlement, and high-value anomaly clearance remain human. The toolkit writes this down so a vendor cannot talk the organisation out of it.

**Pillar E — Measure the layer, do not invent ROI**  
Good looks like complete work objects, coded fail reasons, sampled match worksheets, recorded flag outcomes, aging by owner, dual human payment approval, an autonomy register with no silent promotions, and a visible cost per thousand invoices. If cycle time or first-time match later moves, that is an observed outcome, not a commissioning promise.

### 5.5 Value proposition (canonical)

Evidence Room — AP Agent OS is the operating system for building, governing, and scaling AI agents across Accounts Payable. It does not replace the AP stack. It gives finance and transformation leaders a sixteen-agent architecture, an evidence standard, a permission model, and a human-held payment boundary so the organisation can design the agent layer that operates across the systems it already owns.

## 6. Proof points we may claim — and the ones we may not

### 6.1 Permitted claims

- The product contains a sixteen-agent architecture with named defaults and exclusions.
- Autonomy is described as L0–L4 with written promotion gates.
- Payment authorisation is specified as a human control in the method.
- The method is ERP-agnostic and intended to sit across an existing AP stack.
- The diagnostic produces a readiness picture, not a score that “certifies” the organisation.
- Northline Industrials is a fictional operating example used to make the library concrete.
- Digital delivery is a licensed toolkit (files, templates, method), not hosted AP software.

### 6.2 Claims we do not make

Do not claim, imply, or allow a partner to claim:

- guaranteed savings, cost-takeout, or ROI
- fraud detection, fraud prevention, or “the agent will catch fraud”
- regulatory compliance as an outcome of using the toolkit
- accounting accuracy as a property of an agent or of the OS
- autonomous payment safety
- “touchless AP” as a committed operating state
- replacement of the AP team, the Controller, or the ERP
- that Evidence Room is a law firm, audit firm, or payment institution

If a sentence would be more persuasive with one of the above, rewrite the sentence. Do not move the claim into a testimonial.

## 7. Banned phrases

Never use these in site copy, ads, emails, carousels, sales calls, or affiliate notes.

### 7.1 Outcome guarantees

- guaranteed savings / guaranteed cost reduction / guaranteed ROI
- “pays for itself”
- “cut your AP cost in half”
- “10x,” “100x,” “unlock millions”
- “touchless AP” as a promise
- “set and forget”
- “agents that never make mistakes”

### 7.2 Control and crime claims

- fraud detection / fraud-proof / “catch fraud before it happens”
- “eliminates duplicate payments” (absolute)
- “sanctions screening solved”
- “audit-ready” as a certificate
- “fully compliant” / “SOX compliant” / “meets [regulation]”
- “accounting accuracy guaranteed”
- “autonomous payment safety”

- “replace your ERP”
- “the last AP platform you’ll need”

- “human-out-of-the-loop payments”
- “just add ChatGPT to AP”

### 7.4 Hype and filler

- revolutionary, disruptive, magic, secret sauce
- “the future of finance is here”
- “AI-powered” as a hollow prefix
- “next-gen,” “cutting-edge,” “world-class,” “best-in-class”
- “supercharge,” “turbocharge,” “unleash,” “empower your team to thrive”

- fake urgency: “only 3 licences left”

### 7.5 Legal and identity collisions

- “Evidence Room LLC” (that is a different company — forensic animation)
- “evidence-room.net” as ours
- “forensic animation,” “courtroom reconstruction,” or any implication we are that firm
- “certified,” “accredited,” or “approved by” any regulator, standard-setter, or ERP vendor unless a real agreement exists (none exists at writing)

## 8. Name, domain, and lockup rules

### 8.1 Why the lockup is long

“Evidence Room” is a strong room-and-file metaphor and a weak standalone trademark position. A forensic-animation company, Evidence Room LLC, operates at evidence-room.net. evidenceroom.com is taken. The distinctive commercial form is therefore:

**EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS**

Always pair the room with the category. Always keep AP visible. This is a brand-hygiene rule and a clearance mitigation, not a substitute for a lawyer’s search.

### 8.2 Domain

- **Use:** evidenceroom.ai
- **Do not imply we own:** evidenceroom.com, evidence-room.com, evidence-room.net
- **Email:** hello@evidenceroom.ai (to be provisioned)
- **Store:** Lemon Squeezy storefront labelled EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS

Availability of evidenceroom.ai was reported at the time of brand selection. Re-check immediately before purchase. Domain registration is not trademark registration.

### 8.3 Clearance flags (professional confirmation required)

1. Evidence Room LLC / evidence-room.net — forensic animation. Risk of name confusion in search and in payment descriptors.
2. evidenceroom.com — taken. Risk of diversion.
3. “Evidence Room” as a standalone word mark in finance — unsearched here.
4. “AP Agent OS” as a descriptive product name — likely descriptive; still confirm.
5. Payment-descriptor text on card statements should include “AP AGENT OS” or “EVIDENCEROOM.AI”, not only “EVIDENCE ROOM.”

See `10_LEGAL_AND_LICENSING/TRADEMARK_AND_DOMAIN_NOTES.md`.

1. Removing the outcome guarantee.
2. Naming the artefact (charter, evidence pack, autonomy register).
3. Restating the human hold.
4. Restoring the differentiator (we do not replace the stack).

## 10. Brand idea in the product

The brand idea is not decoration. It is implemented as:

| Product mechanic | Brand idea in practice |
|---|---|
| Default autonomy L0/L1 | Permission is withheld at commissioning. |
| Evidence gates in autonomy progression | Proof is the ticket to the next level. |
| L2 = complete packet, no execution | Evidence can be perfect and still not be permission. |
| Payment ceiling at recommend/prepare | Some permissions are never for sale to an agent. |
| Duplicate & Anomaly as signal | A flag is proof of a hypothesis, not proof of a crime. |
| One human owner per agent | Permission has a name. |
| Exclusions in every charter | If it is not written, it is not permitted. |
| Demotion and retirement | Proof can fail. Permission is revoked. |

If a future product change severs this link — for example, a default L3 matching agent that posts without gates — the brand idea becomes false and must be retired or the product changed. Do not keep the sentence if the method no longer means it.
