# Evidence Room — commercial funnel

**Product:** AP Agent OS  
**Primary conversion:** Assess Your AP Agent Readiness  
**Secondary:** Explore / buy Professional  
**Voice:** intelligent, concise, credible, executive, specific, non-hype  
**Rule:** the funnel may never grow a promise the product refuses.

---

## 1. The job of the funnel

Move a finance professional from “we are being asked to add AI to AP” to one of four honest outcomes:

1. They take the Diagnostic and wait (we told them to).  
2. They buy Starter or Professional and do the work.  
3. They buy Team and do the work as a group.  
4. They apply for a Custom Blueprint because a toolkit will not fit.

A fifth outcome — they leave because we will not guarantee savings — is a successful filter.

---

## 2. Audience

**Primary:** Controller, AP manager/director, shared-services lead.  
**Secondary:** Internal audit / SOX reader, CFO / transformation lead.  
**Not primary:** AP software sales, general “AI influencer” audience, procurement of office supplies.

One buyer, one user (Professional). A buying group (Team). A named entity (Custom).

---

## 3. Stages

```
Attention        LinkedIn, articles, search, referral
     │
Comprehension    Home / Method / Landing  (5-second test)
     │
Trust            Disclaimer, method, fictional-only examples
     │
Diagnostic       Free LS lead magnet
     │
Paid toolkit     Starter $79 · Professional $199 · Team $499
     │
Designed work    Custom Blueprint $1,500–$3,000
```

There is no “book a demo” stage at launch. Team does not include us in the room. Custom is the only stage that consumes our calendar.

## 4. Offers by stage

| Stage | Offer | Success |
|---|---|---|
| Cold | One insight post or chart. No “comment READY”. | Profile visit or article read |
| Warm | Method page or landing | 2+ minutes or scroll to tiers |
| Hand-raise | Diagnostic | Completed checkout $0 + download |
| Fit | Email sequence + START HERE | They can name their level |
| Purchase | Professional (default), Team if multi-human | Paid + unzipped |
| Expansion | Team from Pro; Custom from Team | Application or second SKU |
| Retention | Onboarding 01–12; quiet excellence | They finish a fenced agent brief |

---

## 5. Messages by stage (do not mix)

**Attention.** “Most AP AI is tool-led, not owned.”  
**Comprehension.** Operating system across the stack you have.  
**Trust.** Responsibility is earned. Here is what we will not claim.  
**Diagnostic.** Level 0–4 and a gap map.  
**Purchase.** Files you can take into a controller conversation.  
**Expansion.** Other people have to move; or the environment will not fit a toolkit.

Forbidden at every stage: guaranteed savings, fraud detection, compliance, accounting accuracy, autonomous payments, ROI.

| They say | We say |
|---|---|
