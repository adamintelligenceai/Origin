## Carousel 1 — Proof before permission  
**Slides:** 8 · **Use:** Method, ads to Diagnostic

1. **Title.** Proof before permission. / EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS / A working rule for AP agents, not a mood.
2. **The usual order.** Permission first: “You are an AP expert. Post the invoice.” / Proof later: hope the tone looks right.
3. **The operating order.** Proof first: a named packet exists. / Permission second: a human grants the next level — or does not.
4. **What counts as proof.** Source. Extract. Worksheet. Correspondence. Approval log. Flag outcome. Owner. Clock.
5. **What never counts.** Fluency. Confidence scores alone. “The model is usually right.” A vendor demo.
6. **What permission is.** L0 observe. L1 recommend. L2 prepare. L3 bounded execute. L4 exception oversight of that same set.
7. **What permission is not.** Payment release. Bank-detail change. Close attestation. Self-expansion of scope.
8. **Close.** Keep the stack. Design the layer. / Assess Your AP Agent Readiness — evidenceroom.ai / Not a savings promise.

1. **Title.** Sixteen agents. One payment boundary.
2. **Do not charter a blob.** “AP AI” is not a role. Audit cannot sample a blob.
3. **Operational path.** 01 Intake → 02 Validation → 03 Matching. Parallel: 10 Duplicate & Anomaly.
4. **Breaks.** 04 Triage → 05 GR / 06 PO Quality / 08 Supplier / 09 Internal follow-up. Non-PO: 07 Approval routes the packet.

6. **Across all of it.** 16 Orchestrator: object, owner, SLA, evidence. Not a second ERP.
7. **Interaction rules.** Intake does not match. Matching does not invent receipts. Triage does not resolve.

10. **Close.** If it is not in the charter, the agent does not do it.

## Carousel 4 — Why prompt packs fail  
**Slides:** 8 · **Use:** Competitive frame, Email 5 companion

1. **Title.** A prompt is an instruction. An OS is a workforce.
2. **No owner.** The text lives in a personal workspace.
3. **No exclusions.** The model will draft a bank-detail change if you do not forbid it.
4. **No packet.** The reply cites nothing you can open.
5. **No gate.** Yesterday it summarised. Today it “posts.”
6. **No demotion.** Failure produces another prompt, not a retirement.
7. **No payment boundary.** “Be careful” is not dual approval.
8. **Close.** Keep useful prompts *inside* charters. Do not let them replace charters.

## Carousel 5 — Permission levels L0–L4  
**Slides:** 8 · **Use:** Method, steering packs

1. **Title.** Autonomy is a ladder with a missing rung: payment.
2. **L0 Observe.** Read, classify, log, shadow-report. No reliance.
3. **L1 Recommend.** Propose with evidence. No ERP write. No external send.
4. **L2 Prepare.** Complete packet. Human or downstream control executes.
5. **L3 Execute within guardrails.** Named low-risk action. Written limits. Sampled.
6. **L4 Managed autonomy.** Same bounded set as L3. Exception review. Recertify. Not a new class of work.
7. **Hard ceiling.** Payment authorisation stays human at every level.
8. **Close.** Default is L0/L1. Commissioning at L3 is a failure of nerve, not a sign of maturity. / evidenceroom.ai/method

## Carousel 6 — Exception atlas (starter set)  
**Slides:** 9 · **Use:** Operations

1. **Title.** If it is free text, it is not a mix you can manage.
2. **Capture.** Unreadable image. Missing page. Wrong vendor identity.
3. **Validation.** Incomplete header. Tax fields ambiguous. Currency mismatch.
4. **Match.** Price. Quantity. Missing GR. Missing or defective PO.
5. **Duplicate / anomaly.** Possible duplicate. Unusual amount. Unusual vendor-on-PO pairing. *Hypothesis, not verdict.*
6. **Approval.** No DOA path. Over-tolerance. Substitute approver missing.
7. **Supplier / internal.** Awaiting reply. Awaiting receipt. Awaiting PO fix.
8. **Triage rights.** Name the type, owner, next action, clock. Do not resolve in the classify step.
9. **Close.** Code the break. Then Root Cause has something to cluster.

## Carousel 7 — KPIs that are not ROI theatre  
**Slides:** 9 · **Use:** Controllers, PMO

1. **Title.** Measure whether the layer is real.
2. **Work objects** exist inside the Intake SLA.
3. **Fail reasons** are coded.
4. **Match worksheets** exist for a defined sample.
5. **Flags** have clear / confirm / escalate recorded.
6. **Aging** is by named owner, not only by queue.
7. **Payments** show the required human approvers. Every time.
8. **Register** lists every live agent and level. Cost per 1,000 invoices is written down.
9. **Close.** If cycle time later moves, it is an observation — not why you commissioned.

## Carousel 8 — Responsibility model  
**Slides:** 8 · **Use:** RACI conversations

1. **Title.** Agents prepare. Humans own.
2. **R versus A.** An agent may be R for assembly. A named role is A for the outcome.
3. **One owner per agent.** “The SSC” is not a name.
4. **Payment.** Agent annotates. Humans authorise. Separate rows. Always.
5. **Vendor master.** Agents assemble a request pack. They do not create the vendor or change the bank.
6. **Close / tax / legal.** Prepare the file. Do not attest. Do not take a tax position. Do not settle.
7. **Scope.** Agents do not promote themselves.
8. **Close.** Write the table before the first L1 recommendation. / Professional includes the full framework.

## Carousel 9 — Ninety-day layer plan  
**Slides:** 7 · **Use:** Transformation

1. **Title.** Ninety days to a designed layer — not to unsupervised AP.
2. **Days 1–14.** Appoint Process Owner and Controls Lead. Charter Wave 1 at L0.
3. **Days 15–45.** Promote to L1 only on gates. Ten exceptions. One payment run. Audit observes.
4. **Days 46–75.** Selected L2 packets. Wave 2. Payment review and Close stay ≤ L1.
5. **Days 76–90.** Publish the register. Record cost/1,000. Write the payment refusal again.
6. **Wave 4.** Optional. Narrow clean-match class. Still not payment. Many should skip it.
7. **Close.** Observation is a successful quarter if that is what you can honestly run.

## Carousel 10 — What the diagnostic band means  
**Slides:** 7 · **Use:** After Email 1, Diagnostic page

1. **Title.** A reading, not a certificate.
2. **Undesigned.** Activity without charters. Start Wave 1 at L0.
3. **Informal.** Some roles, implied permission. Put autonomy on a register.
4. **Gated.** Packets exist; sampling or payment ink is thin. Invite Audit.
5. **Designed.** Recertify. Blueprint only if entities are the hard part.
6. **What no band means.** Not compliant. Not safe-to-pay-autonomously. Not an ROI score.
7. **Close.** Assess Your AP Agent Readiness — free, no invoice upload. / evidenceroom.ai/diagnostic
