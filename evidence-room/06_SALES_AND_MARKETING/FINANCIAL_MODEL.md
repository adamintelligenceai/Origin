**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-03-20  
**Prices locked:** Free $0 · Starter $79 · Pro $199 · Team $499 · Custom $1,500–$3,000  
**Commerce:** Lemon Squeezy MoR — platform fee **5% + $0.50**; intl **+1.5%**; PayPal **+1.5%**; affiliate **+3%** merchant (when used)

## 5. Scale path

```
Phase A — AP digital products (now)
  Free → Starter → Pro → Team → marketplace derivatives
Phase B — Services attach
  Custom Blueprint + optional advisory retainers (future pricing TBD)
Phase C — Subscription (future)
  Updates, template library, community office hours — only after digital PMF
Phase D — Finance Agent platform (future only)
  Expand patterns to AR, Record-to-Report / Close, Treasury, Expense
  Still evidence-led; not a payment rail; not promised in v1 GTM
```

Document Phase D only as roadmap language in `10_LEGAL_AND_LICENSING/SCALE_ROADMAP.md`. Do not sell Phase D SKUs now.

- +10 pts international share ≈ −1.5% list on those orders

- Refund rate: monitor; high refunds signal overclaim in copy — fix copy, not fees
