# Meeting Guide — AP Agent Programme

Use these agendas as-is; time-box ruthlessly.

---

## A. Kickoff (60 min)

**Attendees:** Sponsor, Controller, Head of AP, IT, Automation lead  

| Min | Topic | Outcome |
|-----|-------|---------|
| 0–10 | Problem & non-goals | Shared scope |
| 10–25 | Diagnostic / baseline | Agree top metrics |
| 25–40 | Candidate agents Level 0–1 | Shortlist 1–2 |
| 40–50 | Governance & payment rule | Confirm hard gates |
| 50–60 | 30-day plan & owners | Dated actions |

---

## B. Daily AP stand-up (15 min)

1. Exceptions ageing > SLA (by taxonomy)  
2. Agent errors / timeouts  
3. Blockers needing buyer/supplier  
4. One improvement experiment  

No status theatre.

---

## C. Weekly agent ops (45 min)

**Inputs:** Scorecard, sample of false +/−, change log  

| Topic | Decision type |
|-------|---------------|
| Metric movement | Observe / investigate |
| Threshold tweaks within charter | Approve / defer |
| Defects | Prioritise |
| Training gaps | Assign |

---

## D. Monthly control review (60 min)

- Sample results vs CTL plan  
- Override themes → root cause  
- Evidence completeness  
- IAM / version attestations  

---

## E. Quarterly steering (90 min)

Use `04_AP_AGENT_OS_TEAM/Executive/STEERING_PACK.md`.

1. Benefits realisation vs plan  
2. Risk posture  
3. Promotion / retirement decisions  
4. Budget & roadmap  
5. Asks for sponsor  

---

## Facilitation rules

- Separate activity praise from outcome metrics.  
- Every meeting ends with owners + dates.  
- Park “AI strategy” digressions to a parking lot.
