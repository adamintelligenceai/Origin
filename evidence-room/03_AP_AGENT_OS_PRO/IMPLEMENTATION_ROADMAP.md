# Implementation roadmap — Phases 0–10

**Evidence Room · AP Agent OS Pro**  
**Version:** 1.0 · September 2026  
**Licensed material · ERP-agnostic**  
**Answers:** *What should I do? Who owns it? What can go wrong? How control? What evidence?*

This is a **control sequence**, not a Gantt you are warranted to hit. Dates are local. Order is not. Team’s tracker (`G0–G9`) is the working-group compression of the early phases. The library’s waves 0–6 sit inside Phases 4–9.

We do not promise a go-live date, savings, or that sixteen agents will exist. Payment stays human. Northline is fictional.

---

## How to read the phases

Each phase has an **entry**, **work**, **exit artefact**, and a **stop**. If the stop trips, you do not “crash the plan.” You write hold.

Do not stand up sixteen agents in Phase 4. One processor plus A16 is a complete first journey.

---

## Phase 0 — Open the room

**Entry.** Someone has asked for “AI in AP.”  
**Work.** Read the non-promise list. Name the owner. Complete the Diagnostic.  
**Exit.** Declared level; wait Y/N; sentence: class + system (or an honest blank).  
**Stop.** No named owner. Unsafe business case (E05=E06=0) used as the reason to start.

Owner: future process owner.

---

## Phase 1 — See Tuesday

**Entry.** Phase 0 done. If wait, this phase *is* the work.  
**Work.** Live-process map with a floor witness. Channel list. Dual-ledger honesty.  
**Exit.** Map the floor recognises.  
**Stop.** Map is only the SOP.

Owner: process owner + floor witness.

---

## Phase 2 — Freeze a slice

**Entry.** A Tuesday map.  
**Work.** One class, outs, amount cap a controller will sign.  
**Exit.** Slice note.  
**Stop.** “All AP.” Combining two ledgers because the team is shared.

Owner: process owner.

---

## Phase 3 — Write the job

**Entry.** Slice frozen.  
**Work.** Purpose sentence, fence, escalation, job description, instruction v1, register row at 0 or 1. Human-vs-agent table.  
**Exit.** Signed sentence. Named kill switch.  
**Stop.** Sentence contains pay / fraud-detect / ensure-compliant.

Owner: steward + executive reader (sentence).

---

## Phase 4 — Controls and evidence

**Entry.** Brief exists.  
**Work.** Three controls minimum; SOD including system identities; untrusted-input note; evidence index. Align to control matrix.  
**Exit.** Control card.  
**Stop.** Vendor SOC offered as the only control. Shared robot user planned for later writes.

Owner: control owner.

Wave 0 of the library (A16 + register + work-object schema + inbound completeness) **completes here** even if A16 is conceptual.

---

## Phase 5 — Measure

**Entry.** Controls exist.  
**Work.** Three sourced operating measures. Baseline lock for the slice. No F6.  
**Exit.** Measure card + cadence.  
**Stop.** Vanity metrics. Ardent gaps as targets.

Owner: steward + (if needed) FP&A for definitions only.

---

## Phase 6 — Test

**Entry.** Instruction version frozen.  
**Work.** Desk sample; technical UAT U01–U12; shadow as appropriate. See `Testing/TESTING_UAT_SHADOW.md`.  
**Exit.** Write-up: “we would have been wrong about X.”  
**Stop.** Cannot obtain a sample. Sev-1 in UAT. Exact-key FN.

Owner: steward; gate: control owner.

---

## Phase 7 — Limited production

**Entry.** Phase 6 pass or Observe-only waiver.  
**Work.** Level 0/1 inside the fence. Pause rehearsed. Floor job aid. Steering ToR if a group.  
**Exit.** Change log: on.  
**Stop.** Pause requires a committee. Any prod write not on the canvas.

Owner: steward + kill-switch owner.

Library waves 1–2 (A01/A02/A10 then A03/A04) start **only** as the *chosen* processor, not as a bundle.

---

## Phase 8 — Read and decide

**Entry.** 14+ operating days of log (or the charter’s minimum).  
**Work.** Classify overrides. Read O and R families. CFO brief.  
**Exit.** Hold / limited continue / stop.  
**Stop.** Sponsor demands a savings slide to continue. Ownership became “the team.”

Owner: executive reader.

---

## Phase 9 — Earn or refuse responsibility

**Entry.** Continue, and you are considering Level 2.  
**Work.** Promotion pack (Responsibility Model). Calendar + volume gates. 25-error sample.  
**Exit.** Level 2 *or* a written refusal.  
**Stop.** Demo promotion. Weekend burst of volume. A16 approving itself.

Owner: AP Manager; Controller for Level 3+ (not this phase by default).

Later library waves (A05/A08/A09, A06/A07, A12 observe, A11/A13, A14/A15) **queue behind** a boring first agent. Order stays as in the stack overview.

---

## Phase 10 — Recertify and (maybe) expand

**Entry.** A stable fence.  
**Work.** Recertification cadence. Optional *second class* if 7–9 were boring. Expand envelope only with the same approver as the current level.  
**Exit.** Recert pack or a new slice starting at Phase 2.  
**Stop.** Scale because a steering slide needed “momentum.” Level 4 without IA acknowledgement.

Owner: process owner + control owner.

Phase 10 is not “the Finance Agent platform.” Other verticals are out of scope for this product.

## 4–6 week single-agent illustration

**Status: illustrative. Evidence Room framework. Not a commitment. Not a result. Not your plan.**

This shows **one** way a prepared team *might* walk Phases 0–8 for a single processor. It assumes Diagnostic is not a wait, a floor witness exists, and C01 is already written. If any assumption fails, the calendar stretches or stops. It does **not** include Level 2, NetSuite, A12 execute, or benefits realisation.

### Assumptions (Northline fictional shape — replace)

- Slice already almost obvious: one company code, PO, one currency pair, amount cap  
- Steward at 30–40% time, not “Friday afternoons only”  
- Extract of historical invoices available in week 2  
- No ERP project colliding  

### Calendar (illustration only)

| Week | Phases | Work | Artefact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–2 | Diagnostic compare, Tuesday map, freeze slice | Map + slice note |
| 2 | 3–5 | Sentence, JD, register, three controls, three measures | Brief v1 |
| 3 | 6 | Desk n=50, UAT U01–U12 in sandbox | Test write-up |
| 4 | 6–7 | Shadow *or* skip to Observe-only limited live if shadow blocked | Shadow log or waiver |
| 5 | 7 | Level 1 limited fence, twice-weekly measures | Operating log |
| 6 | 8 | Brief, decision: hold or continue at Level 1 | Decision log |

A 4-week version **drops shadow** and stays Observe for week 4, or it is a lie. A 6-week version is the more honest illustration when systems access is slow.

### Caveats (read them)

1. **This is not a service level.** Custom Blueprints do not include implementation. Team does not include us in the room.
2. **Systems access** (read identity, sandbox, extract) routinely adds weeks. That is not “resistance.”
3. **Receiving and procurement** are not in AP’s gift. A05/A06 will not follow this calendar.
4. **Two ledgers** doubles Phase 1–2. Do not compress them into week 1.
5. **Promotion clocks** (20/40/60/90 operating days) are *longer* than this illustration. A 6-week live period does not earn Level 2, let alone 3.
6. **Volume gates** (500 / 1,000 objects) may not be met in six weeks even at 18,000 group invoices/month if the *slice* is small. Good.
7. **Do not** use this illustration in a vendor SOW as “Evidence Room says six weeks to value.”
8. **Do not** attach a savings ramp to these weeks.

### Northline walkthrough (fictional)

Priya and Diego used six weeks to G8 on A03 Level 1, SAP NL10 only. Week 3 found UoM defects. They changed the instruction, not the level. Week 6 decision: continue Level 1, refuse Level 2 (calendar gate). NetSuite Pacific still Phase 1. That is a successful roadmap use.

## What “done” means on this roadmap

A written decision at Phase 8, with evidence from Phase 6, on a fence from Phase 2. Sixteen agents is not done. A savings number is not done.
