## Page 1 — Decision

**Title:** Accounts Payable agent layer — design and holds  
**Prepared by:** [name] · [date] · Toolkit: Evidence Room — AP Agent OS (licensed method, not software)

### What we are doing

We are specifying a named agent layer across the AP stack we already run ([ERP]). Six Wave 1 agents will observe and then, if evidence allows, recommend: Intake, Validation, Matching, Exception Triage, Duplicate & Anomaly, Orchestrator. Payment authorisation remains human. Vendor bank-detail change remains dual-controlled human. We are not replacing the ERP, the shared-service team, or the bank.

### Why now

People are already dropping models into invoice work (ours: [list informal helpers / none]). McKinsey *State of AI 2025* (survey June–July 2025, n=1,993) reported 62% of respondents experimenting with agents and 23% scaling an agent somewhere in the enterprise, with roughly two-thirds not scaling AI generally. That is context for **undocumented activity**, not a reason to expect EBIT from this programme. (McKinsey also reported 88% regular AI use and 39% any EBIT impact across uses — not an AP result.)

### What “success” is in 90 days

Inspectable packets; coded exceptions; recorded flag outcomes; an autonomy register at observe/recommend; dual-human payment release still true. Not a cost-takeout target.

### What we are not asking the board to believe

That this toolkit detects fraud, makes us compliant, guarantees accounting accuracy, authorises payments, or produces a stated ROI.

### Money we will show — and money we will not

We can show **layer cost** (tools + implementation + sampled minutes) per thousand invoices. We can, if Finance wants, populate a three-case model from *our* baseline (`BUSINESS_CASE_MODEL.md`). Conservative with a 0% efficiency input shows year-1 net as a cost. That is an acceptable board outcome.

We will not table a single ROI, a fraud-loss avoided, or Ardent/Tipalti cost-per-invoice figures as *our* target. If context is useful: Ardent Partners *State of ePayables 2024*, as cited by Tipalti (vendor-originated citation of independent research), reported $2.78 vs $12.88 cost per invoice and 3.1 vs 17.4 days; Ardent *AP Metrics that Matter 2025*, as cited by Tipalti, reported an exception comparison of 22% to 9%. Those are not our numbers.
