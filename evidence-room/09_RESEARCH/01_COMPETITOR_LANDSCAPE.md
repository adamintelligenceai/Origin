# Competitor Landscape — AP Agent OS / Evidence Room

**Question this doc answers:** Who sells adjacent capability, and why none of them is a purchasable **AP Agent OS** for governance, responsibility, KPI, and control.

**Category definition (Evidence Room):** A buyable product that makes AP AI agents *accountable* — ownership maps, control catalogs, measurement packs, exception taxonomies, and evidence trails — independent of which capture/ERP/RPA stack the customer already runs.

## Segment notes (executive)

### 1) AP automation suites (Tipalti, Coupa, Basware, Esker, Yooz, Medius, Stampli, AvidXchange, HighRadius)

- Sell **invoice execution**: capture, match, approve, pay, often with vendor network or global payments.
- AI is a **feature layer** that improves *their* workflow — not a portable operating system for agents the customer builds elsewhere.
- Controls and KPIs live **inside** the suite. Exporting a responsibility model, control catalog, and measurement system as a standalone purchasable OS is not the SKU.

### 2) Spend / bill-pay platforms (Ramp, BILL)

- Optimize card spend, bill pay, and cash ops.
- Overlap with AP *tasks*; do not productize agent governance as a category.

### 3) ERP giants (SAP, Oracle, Microsoft)

- Remain **system of record** and approval authority.
- Embedded copilots do not create a cross-tool AP Agent OS. Customers still invent ownership, KPI baselines, and evidence packs themselves (or via SI).

### 4) Horizontal automation (UiPath, Automation Anywhere)

- Powerful agent/RPA runtimes with enterprise ops features.
- AP competence is **content + SI**. There is no turnkey “AP Agent OS” product that packages finance responsibility, AP KPI packs, and control catalogs as a purchasable artifact for finance leaders.

### 5) Generic AI prompt packs

- Cheap, fast, ungoverned.
- No dual-control, no audit trail design, no KPI instrumentation, no RACI, no exception taxonomy tied to measurement.
- Highest hype-to-evidence ratio in the landscape — primary foil for Evidence Room messaging.

## Gap statement

**There is no widely marketed, purchasable AP Agent OS whose job is governance, responsibility, KPI, and control** — portable across ERP, AP suite, RPA, and LLM tools.

What exists today:

| What buyers can buy | What they still invent |
|---|---|
| Invoice capture & matching | Who is accountable when the agent proposes a post |
| Approval workflows inside one suite | Cross-tool SoD for human + agent actors |
| RPA bots / copilots | Baseline KPIs, instrumentation, review cadence |
| Prompt packs | Evidence packs that survive audit questions |
| SI custom governance decks | Repeatable productized control + KPI library |

---

## Differentiation — Evidence Room / AP Agent OS

Evidence Room does **not** compete as another invoice engine, payment network, or RPA runtime.

It sells the missing layer:

1. **Responsibility** — explicit ownership for propose / review / approve / post / pay when agents participate.
2. **Governance** — policies, SoD, escalation, and kill-switch design for AP agents.
3. **Controls** — a catalog of preventive/detective controls tailored to agent failure modes (hallucinated PO, duplicate pay, vendor change fraud, silent threshold bypass).
4. **KPI & evidence** — baseline → target → measure → review, with claims tied to a research ledger — not unverified ROI slides.
5. **Portability** — works *with* Tipalti/Coupa/ERP/UiPath/prompt-driven pilots; does not require rip-and-replace.

**One-line:** Suites execute invoices. RPA runs bots. Prompt packs suggest text. **Evidence Room makes AP agents earn responsibility — with measurable outcomes and controls you can show an auditor.**
