## How to use this ledger

1. Copy claims **verbatim in meaning**; do not round into new implied precision.  
2. Always retain year (2024 vs 2025) and distributor association when citing Ardent.  
3. Mark **Vendor-associated** where a software vendor distributes or co-brands the report — still usable, never presented as Evidence Room primary research.  
4. **Independent** here means analyst/research firm primary authorship (Ardent Partners), even when a vendor distributes.  
5. Structural gap rows are **Evidence Room analysis**, not survey statistics — do not convert them into “X% of market” claims.
