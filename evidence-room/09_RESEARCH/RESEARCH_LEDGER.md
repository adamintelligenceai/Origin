**Rule:** Do not invent statistics. If a number is not in this ledger, it does not ship in customer-facing copy.  
**Not:** A licence to treat any figure as an Evidence Room customer result, a savings guarantee, or a typical outcome.

Independence labels used here:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `independent` | Primary publisher is a research or advisory firm; we read its page (or a same-house republication) and the figure is not a software vendor’s own product claim. |
| `vendor` | First-party commercial page (pricing, fees, acquisition announcement). True as a vendor statement; not third-party research. |
| `vendor-citing-independent` | A software vendor’s page quoting an independent study. Cite both hops. Do not drop the vendor hop. |
| `unverified` | Named but not opened in this review. **None of the rows below are unverified.** |

Qualitative competitor observations live in `COMPETITOR_REVIEW.md` and are labelled editorial analysis. They are not statistics and are not copied into this table.

| **Publication** | *The State of ePayables 2024: Money Never Sleeps* (19th annual). Tipalti article: “How AI Invoice Processing Powers AI Invoice Automation,” by Kelly Kennedy, page updated 3 September 2026. |

| **URL** | https://tipalti.com/resources/learn/ai-invoice-processing/ · Bottomline file: https://www.bottomline.com/download_file/84ec3c2a-cc1b-4d5d-8457-ce18d337b7b4/1547 · Bottomline landing: https://www.bottomline.com/resources/the-state-of-epayables-2024 |
| **Independence** | `vendor-citing-independent` |

Ardent defines Best-in-Class as **the 20% of enterprises with the lowest average invoice processing costs and shortest average invoice process cycle times**. Sample is AP professionals who responded to Ardent, not a census. **Do not present $2.78 as a target, a typical result, or an Evidence Room outcome.** |
