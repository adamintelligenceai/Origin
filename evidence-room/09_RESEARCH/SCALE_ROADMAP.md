# Scale roadmap

**Evidence Room · Internal**  
**Version:** 1.0 · September 2026  
**Rule:** Do **not** build other verticals now. AP is the product. Other finance domains are a later *platform* question, not a 1.1 feature list.

This file stops the suite from becoming “agents for everything” because a LinkedIn comment asked for AR.

---

## The only ladder

```
AP digital product
        →
productised services (Custom Blueprint)
        →
subscription (not at launch)
        →
Finance Agent platform (still AP-first)
```

Each arrow requires the previous layer to be **boring**: fulfilment works, claims hold, counsel has named the entity, refunds are not a lifestyle.

---

## Stage A — AP digital product (now)

**In.** Diagnostic $0 · Starter $79 · Professional $199 · Team $499  
**Job.** Teach and equip a practitioner or working group to design a governed AP agent layer.  
**Not.** Hosted software. ERP. Implementation hours inside Team.

**Scale motions that are allowed**

- Better zip hygiene and `00_START_HERE`  
- Translations later, if a seat asks — not a new vertical  
- Spreadsheet renders of scorecard / tracker  
- Counsel-final legal  
- evidenceroom.ai live + Lemon Squeezy overlay  

**Forbidden motions**

- AR, expense, close-the-books, FP&A, treasury-trading “agents” as SKUs  
- A $9 prompt pack  
- Subscription membership to unlock PDFs  
- Open affiliates  

**Exit to Stage B.** We can deliver Custom without inventing a second product, and we have declined applications without drama.

---

## Stage B — Productised services (Custom, already designed)

**In.** $1,500 / $2,250 / $3,000 Blueprint. Application. Hidden checkout.  
**Job.** Write the operating design for *one* named AP environment.  
**Not.** SI hours. On-site Team facilitation (unless a separate SOW). Retainer.

**Scale motions that are allowed**

- A second Blueprint author with the same fulfilment workflow  
- Invoice path if counsel says Custom is a service  
- A written “attendance day” SKU *later*, priced so it cannot be confused with Team  

**Forbidden**

- Staffing a delivery team that implements Coupa  
- Outcome-based fees (savings share)  
- Using Northline as if it were a case study to win a Blueprint  

**Exit to Stage C.** Blueprints complete on time without scope bloom, and we still tell people to buy Team.

---

## Stage C — Subscription (not at launch)

**Why wait.** Lemon Squeezy **+0.5%** on subscriptions (LEDGER-011). A membership that re-sends the same zip is a fee for nothing. Subscriptions need a *reason*: living KPI dictionary, register hosting we are willing to run, or a recertification calendar with human review.

**Possible later objects** (design, do not build)

- Hosted autonomy register + evidence index (still not the ERP)  
- Quarterly method updates with a changelog  
- Named-seat Team as a yearly recert  

**Hard gates before any SKU**

- We can support it without Slack community as a second product  
- Claims remain the non-promise list  
- We do not paywall the method page  
- Counsel reviews consumer/subscription law  

If the reason is “recurring revenue looks better,” the gate has failed.

---

## Stage D — Finance Agent platform (AP-first)

**Meaning.** The *same* responsibility model, taxonomy style, and earned-autonomy register applied to more finance processes — **after** AP charters have survived contact with real buyers (not Northline).

**Still AP-first means**

- The brand and domain stay Evidence Room / evidenceroom.ai  
- New domains of work get new libraries; they do not dilute A01–A16  
- Payment-like actions in other domains inherit the same “human releases cash” instinct (collections write-offs, payroll — do not casualise)  

**Not implied**

- A general “agent OS for the enterprise”  
- HR, CX, or sales agents  
- A model we host on customer invoices  

**When to open a second vertical (all required)**

1. AP product is operationally boring (fulfilment, refunds, questions).  
2. Quality scorecard still ≥ 9 on Q3 (non-promise) after real tickets.  
3. A second vertical has a *named* taxonomy and a *named* thing that stays human.  
4. We are not opening it because a Wave or a VC deck listed “TAM.”

Until then, the answer to “do you do Order-to-Cash agents?” is: **not now.**
