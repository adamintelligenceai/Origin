## The gap we actually occupy

What we consistently do **not** see packaged together, ERP-agnostically, as a buyable operating system:

1. **Operating model** — purpose, fence, owner, evidence, measure, pause — as the product, not a professional-services PDF after the licence.
2. **Earned autonomy** — Levels 0–4 with promotion/demotion evidence, not a toggle labelled “autonomous.”
3. **Exception taxonomy + governance in one spine** — shared codes that route *and* that Internal Audit can test.
4. **ERP-agnostic agent layer** — charters that sit *across* SAP / D365 / Oracle / NetSuite / Workday / a capture suite, with one adapter per ledger, without replacing the book of record.

If a vendor later ships that bundle, we still do not copy their UI. We keep the room.

## AP invoice automation / payables suites

Public category: capture, match, workflow, often payments. Job: run invoices. Not: earn autonomy.

| Name | Public job (plain) | Typical buyer note | Overlap with us | Do not clone | Our stance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tipalti** | Global AP + mass payments | Mid-market / growth; payee complexity | Payment *operations* they run; we never release cash | Their payee network, tax forms, UI | Design A12 as challenge-only across *their* proposal if that is the stack |
| **Coupa** | Spend + invoicing community | Enterprise spend platform | Capture/match/community AI messaging | Community data claims, Wave marketing | Layer on; do not impersonate community AI |
| **Basware** | Network e-invoicing + AP | Enterprise, compliance-heavy | E-invoice completeness (A01/A02) | Network, tax engines | Persist statutory IDs; we do not take tax positions |
| **Esker** | Document process automation incl. AP | Order-to-cash + AP shops | Capture and workflow | Vertical process IP | Fence whatever they already automate; do not re-do a rule |
| **Yooz** | AP automation, often mid-market | Speed-to-value messaging | Capture/workflow | Their “touchless” claims | Measure O7 on the customer definition, not theirs |
| **Medius** | AP automation | Wave shortlist (existence) | Match + exceptions | Product modules | Taxonomy maps *to* their reason codes |
| **Stampli** | AP with conversational / “coworker” framing | Collaborative AP | Human-in-the-loop marketing | Persona, chat UX | Work objects, not chat, are our standard |
| **AvidXchange** | Mid-market AP + payments | US mid-market | Payments adjacency | Buyer/supplier network | Same A12 rule |
| **HighRadius** | “Autonomous finance” marketing; O2C + AP | Office of the CFO suites | The word *autonomous* | Their autonomy definition | Our Level 4 is managed, recertified, never default |
| **Ramp** | Cards, spend, bill pay | Digital-native finance | Bill pay, not industrial AP | Card graph, rewards | Different first slice; do not stretch Northline into Ramp |
| **BILL** | SMB bill pay | SMB / accounting firms | Simple payables | Firm marketplace | Out of our primary buyer; do not discount to win SMB |

**Shared miss (all of the above):** they can be excellent *systems*. They do not sell an ERP-agnostic **responsibility model** you apply when you already have one of them — or two ledgers — and have been told to “add agents.”

**Marketplace derivatives** (`06_SALES_AND_MARKETING/MARKETPLACE_DERIVATIVES.md`) stay **off** the Lemon Squeezy core store. Do not ship a $9 impulse PDF that cannibalises Starter.
