## 3. Third-party collision — Evidence Room LLC

**Flag: trademark-clearance item. High priority.**

Public materials (to be verified by counsel; not a full search) indicate a forensic-animation / courtroom-visuals business using “Evidence Room” and **evidence-room.net**.

**Risks**

- Search confusion (“evidence room” → animation, not AP)  
- Payment-descriptor confusion  
- Email and invoice mis-delivery  
- Opposition or demand if we file or scale  
- Customers thinking we reconstruct accidents for trial  

**Operating rules until counsel says otherwise** (same as the legal pack)

- First screen of every property: **EVIDENCE ROOM — AP AGENT OS**  
- About and footer: we are **not** that firm; we do not do forensic animation or courtroom reconstruction  
- Ads: **no** keywords such as “forensic animation,” “courtroom reconstruction,” “accident reconstruction”  
- Marketplace shop name: **AP Agent Room** (or similar), not “Evidence Room” alone  
- Do not buy lookalike ads against their name  
- Do not use a gavel, fingerprint, or courtroom sketch as a mark  
- Do not send informal legal threats ourselves  

**Counsel should**

- Commission a proper search (US and other launch markets)  
- Decide whether to file, and in which classes (likely educational publications / downloadable manuals — **not** payment software, **not** animation)  
- Consider coexistence vs a different master brand if the search is ugly  
- Review the lockup as a **composite** mark
