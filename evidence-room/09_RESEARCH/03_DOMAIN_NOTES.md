# Domain Notes — Evidence Room

## Recommendation

**Primary candidate: `evidenceroom.ai`**

Subject to **registrar / WHOIS availability and price check** before purchase. Do not announce the domain on website, Lemon Squeezy, or decks until registration is confirmed and DNS is controlled by Evidence Room.

### Why `.ai` first

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Brand fit | “Evidence Room” + `.ai` signals AI category without purple-robot aesthetics |
| Category clarity | Buyers scanning finance+AI remember short, literal names |
| Product story | Aligns with agents/AI while brand system stays institutional (see `11_BRAND`) |
| Differentiation | Separates from generic `.com` SaaS noise; still professional for CFO buyers |
| Risk | `.ai` pricing and renewal can be higher; some enterprise email filters historically stricter — mitigate with matching `.com` defensive registration when budget allows |

---

## Alternate domains (priority order)

| Priority | Domain | Use if… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (primary) | **evidenceroom.ai** | Available at acceptable registration + renewal cost |
| 2 | **evidenceroom.com** | `.ai` taken, trademark conflict, or renewal economics fail; strongest institutional default |
| 3 | **getevidenceroom.com** | Exact `.com` / `.ai` taken; classic SaaS “get-” pattern for marketing site |
| 4 | **evidenceroom.finance** | Finance-vertical emphasis; use as redirect or campaign domain, not primary brand if avoidable (TLD familiarity lower for some buyers) |

**Redirect policy:** Whichever is primary, 301 all owned alternates → primary. Keep one mailbox domain only (primary) to avoid split reputation.

## Naming constraints

- Product name in UI/copy: **Evidence Room**
- Category name: **AP Agent OS** (Pro/Team SKUs)
- Do not stylize as EvidenceRoom / evidence_room in customer-facing wordmark (see Brand System).
- Avoid lookalikes that imply certified “evidence room” legal/forensics services.
