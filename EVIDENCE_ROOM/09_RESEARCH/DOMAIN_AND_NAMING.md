## 2. Why “Evidence Room”

The name is a place, not a feature.

An evidence room is where proof is stored before a decision is allowed to stand. That is the product: packets, hashes, samples, promotion files, override logs. The name tells a Controller what the company believes before they read a feature list.

It is not:

- A robot name  
- An “AI + finance” portmanteau  
- A fake Latin product  
- A workflow vendor (“flow”, “span”, “reach”, “pay”)  
- A marketplace name (“prompts”, “pack”, “gpt”)

**How to say it in speech:** “Evidence Room” (two words). Not “ER” in public. “ER” is acceptable only in internal file paths after first mention.

**How to write it:** Evidence Room (title case). Never *evidenceroom* as a brand word except in the domain and email local-parts.

| Names of ERP modules used as if they were ours | IP and accuracy |

### File and repo language

Internal folders stay numbered (`03_AP_AGENT_OS_PRO`). Customers see product names, not folder codes, except in the download manifest where codes help navigation.

## 6. International and legal notes

- “Evidence Room” is ordinary English. Still run a trademark search in the UK, EU, and US before spend on ads. **Flag for lawyer.**  
- `.ai` is the ccTLD of Anguilla. Registration and renewal rules differ from `.com`. Confirm renewal price and any local presence rule with the registrar. **Flag for lawyer / registrar.**  
- Do not imply the company is incorporated in Anguilla because of the TLD.  
- “OS” is used descriptively (operating system for agents). It is not a claim of a software runtime the buyer installs.
