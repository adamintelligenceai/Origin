# Evidence Room — Research Ledger

**Document ID:** `09_RESEARCH/01_RESEARCH_LEDGER`  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Single source of truth for every external market, fee, or governance claim used in Evidence Room commercial materials.  
**Rule:** No statistic ships in marketing, business-case templates, or website copy unless it appears here with source, date, URL, exact context, and independence flag.

---

## How to read this ledger

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Claim** | The precise statement Evidence Room is allowed to repeat |
| **Source org** | Publisher / originator |
| **Date** | Publication or stated research date |
| **URL** | Primary source link |
| **Exact context** | What the source actually measured or stated (verbatim paraphrase of scope) |
| **Independence** | `independent` · `vendor` · `unknown` |
| **Evidence Room usage note** | Where and how the claim may be used; hard limits |

### Independence definitions

- **independent** — Research firm, standards body, regulator, or government publication. Still not a guarantee of buyer outcomes.
- **vendor** — Primary commercial claim from a seller (pricing page, product docs, marketing). Use for commercial mechanics only; never as market proof of AP performance.
- **unknown** — Attribution incomplete, sponsorship unclear, or second-hand citation. Do not elevate to headline proof without primary verification.

### Non-evidence flag

Entries marked **ILLUSTRATIVE / NON-EVIDENCE** are scenario arithmetic, product pricing, or internal method design. They must never be presented as research findings, industry averages, or customer results.

## F. Claim hygiene checklist (mandatory before publish)

1. Is the claim in this ledger with a stable ID?
2. Is independence correctly labelled (`independent` / `vendor` / `unknown`)?
3. Does the usage note forbid outcome guarantees where required?
4. If the number is scenario math, is it flagged **NON-EVIDENCE**?
5. Is attribution present in the customer-facing sentence?
6. Would removing Evidence Room’s brand still leave the claim true and sourced?

If any answer is no — do not publish.
