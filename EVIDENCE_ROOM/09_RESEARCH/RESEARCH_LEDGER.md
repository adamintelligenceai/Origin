# Evidence Room — Research Ledger

**Product:** Evidence Room / AP Agent OS  
**Purpose:** The only figures Evidence Room may cite in commercial, educational, or customer-facing materials.  
**Rule:** Do not invent statistics. Do not present software-adopter research as Evidence Room results. Do not place third-party figures inside the customer product as if they were the buyer’s baseline.  
**Last reviewed:** 20 September 2026  
**Owner:** Brand / Research (commercial). Product files use buyer-measured KPIs only.

---

## How to use this ledger

| If you are writing… | You may… | You may not… |
|---|---|---|
| Website, landing page, LinkedIn, email, articles | Cite a row below, with source, date, and the independence note | Recast the figure as “our customers save…” or drop the context |
| Customer product (charters, KPI dictionary, scorecards) | Point the buyer to measure their own ledger | Embed these figures as targets, norms, or control thresholds |
| Sales conversation | Use the figure to frame a *market* condition | Promise the buyer will match the figure |
| Financial model | Use Lemon Squeezy fee facts as cost assumptions, flagged | Treat fee facts as legal or tax advice |

Every public citation must carry:

1. The figure, exactly as scoped below.  
2. The source name and date.  
3. The URL.  
4. One sentence of exact context.  
5. Whether the source is independent or vendor-evaluated.

If a claim is not in this table, it is not a citable Evidence Room fact.

## Claims that are *not* in this ledger (and must not be invented)

Do not publish, imply, or “round up” any of the following unless a new ledger row is added with source, date, URL, and independence:

- Evidence Room customer savings, ROI, fraud-catch rates, or cycle-time improvement.  
- Guaranteed touchless rates, cost-per-invoice reductions, or headcount reductions.  
- Regulatory compliance rates, audit-pass rates, or “SOX/GAAP/IFRS coverage.”  
- Accounting accuracy guarantees.  
- Autonomous payment safety.  
- Competitor win rates, NPS, or fabricated price lists.  
- Domain-appraisal values, search-volume figures, or TAM numbers not sourced here.  
- Any conversion of APQC / Deloitte / Hackett figures into Evidence Room product KPIs.

## Citation patterns (copy these; do not improvise)

**APQC (website, article, deck):**  
> APQC (16 March 2026) reports that top-performing organisations spend about $0.38 per $1,000 of revenue to process accounts payable, versus about $0.92 for bottom performers. Source: APQC, independent. This is a cost-performance spread in AP, not an Evidence Room result.

**Deloitte (website, article, email):**  
> Deloitte’s Finance Trends 2026 release (8 October 2025) found that 63% of finance teams have fully deployed and actively use AI, while only 21% report clear, measurable ROI, and 14% are using fully integrated AI agents. Source: Deloitte, independent professional-services research. This describes market practice, not Evidence Room performance.

**Hackett (website, article — always with qualifier):**  
> The Hackett Group (19 November 2025) reported that customers of evaluated AP solutions averaged 60% touchless invoice processing; companies with 30%+ touchless processing had 3.5× higher AP productivity; and average AP cycle times improved 59% post-implementation. This is software-adopter research, not Evidence Room results.

**Lemon Squeezy (internal only, or store FAQ with flags):**  
> Lemon Squeezy’s public pricing page (retrieved 20 September 2026) lists 5% + 50¢ per transaction and describes Merchant of Record services for digital products. Additional fees may apply. Tax and payout treatment must be confirmed with a qualified adviser. Stripe acquired Lemon Squeezy in July 2024; 2026 materials discuss Stripe Managed Payments.

## Independence labels (use exactly)

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Independent | Research organisation with no commercial interest in Evidence Room. |
| Independent professional-services research | Firm that also sells services; the cited document is a survey release, not an Evidence Room engagement. |
| Independent research firm (vendor-evaluated) | Research firm evaluating *other vendors’* software. Useful as market context. Not our proof. |
| Vendor first-party terms | A supplier’s own pricing or product page. Use for commercial planning. Confirm legally. |

## Review cadence

| Event | Action |
|---|---|
| New public claim drafted | Check this ledger. If the figure is absent, delete the figure or add a sourced row. |
| Source URL changes or page is updated | Re-retrieve. If the figure moves, update the row and the date retrieved. |
| Product KPI file is edited | Confirm no ledger figure has been copied in as a target. |
| Launch or price change | Re-read Lemon Squeezy pricing and Stripe Managed Payments terms; flag deltas for adviser review. |

Proof before permission. Agents earn responsibility. Evidence decides.
