# How to read this ledger

Every material market or performance claim in customer-facing files must appear here. Classification:

- **Independent** — analyst, academic, statutory, or unsponsored professional body
- **Sponsored independent** — analyst report underwritten by a vendor; method is the analyst's, distribution is sponsored
- **Vendor** — vendor marketing, product page, or vendor-commissioned figure
- **Aggregator** — secondary blog restating primary research; use only as a pointer back to primary
- **Illustrative** — Evidence Room worked example, not a market fact
- **Framework** — Evidence Room method, not an empirical claim

Do not invent statistics. If a figure cannot be tied to a source, remove it from customer copy.

# What we deliberately did not claim

- Guaranteed savings or payback
- Guaranteed fraud or duplicate-payment prevention
- That Evidence Room is a registered audit methodology
- That any ERP is certified
- A single official AP automation market size
- That agentic AP is already the market standard (Ardent 2025 still shows AI adoption at 44% and mostly tactical)
