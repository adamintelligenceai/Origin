# The category map

Four markets sit on top of each other. Evidence Room sells into the fourth.

| Layer | What buyers buy | Typical vendors | What they optimise |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. ERP financials | System of record | SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, NetSuite, Workday | Posting, master data, DOA, close |
| 2. AP automation / ePayables | Capture, match, workflow, pay | Tipalti, Coupa, Basware, Esker, Yooz, Medius, Stampli, AvidXchange, HighRadius, BILL, Ramp | Cycle time, STP, supplier enablement |
| 3. Automation platforms | Bots and integrations | UiPath, Automation Anywhere, Power Automate, vendor iPaaS | Task automation across systems |
| 4. Agent operating system | How humans design, govern, measure the agent layer | **Almost empty. This is Evidence Room.** | Responsibility, evidence, controls, economics |

# What competitors actually sell

Research is based on public product pages and analyst restatements as of September 2026. No proprietary material was used.

## Invoice-to-pay suites

**Tipalti / BILL / Ramp** — mid-market AP + payments. Strong on supplier payments, tax forms, card, and close-adjacent cash-out. Weak on enterprise three-way match complexity and on an explicit agent-governance layer that is portable across ERPs.

**Coupa** — spend platform. AP is one module inside a broader procurement cloud. Buyers already paying for Coupa do not need another capture engine. They still lack a method for deciding which agentic features to turn on, at what autonomy, with what evidence.

**Basware / Esker / Yooz / Medius** — invoice automation specialists. Public language in 2025–2026 has shifted to “autonomous AP” and “agentic AI” (Medius is explicit). Accuracy percentages are vendor-stated. Governance content is product-feature shaped (audit log, approvals), not operating-model shaped (job descriptions, earned autonomy, control matrix, KPI dictionary).

**Stampli** — collaboration hub + “AI employee” positioning. Human oversight is a stated design principle. Still a system you implement, not a toolkit you use to design agents that may live in Stampli *or* SAP *or* a copilot.

**AvidXchange / HighRadius** — enterprise scale, often with payments and cash-application adjacency. HighRadius markets “AP agents” inside a broader finance-automation suite. Claims such as “100% audit readiness” are vendor language and are not used in Evidence Room products.

## ERP natives

SAP (Invoice Management / Ariba / BTP), Oracle (IDP + AP), Microsoft (D365 + Copilot + Power Platform), Workday, NetSuite. These will be the default place many agents actually run. They do not sell an ERP-agnostic operating system for a shared-services leader who must govern agents *across* a mixed estate.

## Automation platforms

UiPath and Automation Anywhere now sell “agents” beside robots. They are excellent at execution plumbing and weak at AP domain taxonomy, exception science, and Finance control design.

## Prompt / template marketplaces

Etsy, Gumroad, generic “CFO AI prompt” packs. Price $9–$49. No control matrix, no earned-autonomy model, no ERP-aware exception taxonomy, no business case that a controller would defend.

## Consulting toolkits

Big Four and boutiques sell AP diagnostics as services at $50k–$500k. The IP stays in the firm. There is no serious $199 product that a Finance Director can buy on Friday.

# Common claims (do not copy)

- “Touchless AP”
- “Autonomous accounts payable”
- “99% capture accuracy”
- “AI employee”
- “100% audit ready”
- “Go live in weeks”

These claims collapse three different things: capture accuracy, workflow completion, and payment authority. Evidence Room separates them.

# Price signals

| Offer type | Observed public signal | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Prompt packs | $9–$49 | Anchors “AI toolkit” as cheap and unserious |
| Niche finance templates | $29–$149 | Buyers will pay for spreadsheets if they look usable |
| AP automation software | typically $20k–$250k+/year, usage or document based | Software budget is large; operating-model budget is usually zero |
| Transformation diagnostic | bundled into SI statements of work | Creates the $1.5k–$3k productised-service gap |

# Over-served

- Invoice capture and OCR accuracy races
- Native ERP connectors
- Payment execution and card rails
- “AI will do AP for you” landing pages

# Under-served

- A portable **agent job architecture** that works in any stack
- **Earned autonomy** with promotion evidence
- An AP **exception taxonomy** written for agents, not just ticket queues
- **Control matrices** that name hallucination, prompt injection, model change and override
- KPI dictionaries that separate activity from outcomes
- A $79–$499 product a transformation lead can take into a workshop tomorrow
- Honest language about what must remain human — especially payment authorisation

# Differentiation lock

Evidence Room does not replace the AP stack.

It helps organisations design the **agent layer that operates across it**.

If a buyer cannot repeat that sentence after the homepage hero, the page has failed.

# Strategic implication

Launch as a digital OS + productised blueprint. Do not attempt to out-capture Medius or out-pay Tipalti. Become the standard language Finance uses when those vendors turn on agent features.
