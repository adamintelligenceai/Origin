## 1. The category mistake most buyers make

Accounts Payable already has software. What it usually lacks is an **operating system for agents**: charters, autonomy gates, human classes, evidence packets, promotion rules, and a measurement standard that cannot be gamed by activity.

Vendors below sell **software or services** that process invoices, route approvals, or move money. Prompt marketplaces sell **text**. Evidence Room sells the **governance and earned-responsibility layer** that decides what an agent is allowed to do on top of whatever stack the buyer already paid for.

If a buyer asks “are you like Tipalti / Coupa / UiPath?”, the accurate answer is:

> No. Those products execute work. We design the agent layer that is allowed to operate across them — and the evidence standard that decides when it may.

## 2. Market map

Four bands. Evidence Room is Band D. It is complementary to A–C. It is a substitute only for “we bought prompts and hoped.”

### Band A — Systems of record and suite AP

| Name | What they actually sell | Where they stop | Evidence Room does not… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SAP** | ERP, invoice verification, GR/IR, payment programme, often Ariba on the edge | A module and a configuration. Not an agent charter or an autonomy ladder. | Replace S/4, invert GR/IR logic, or copy SAP transaction codes into the library as if they were universal. |
| **Oracle** | Fusion / E-Business AP, iProcurement, payments | Same: a system of record with workflow. | Replace Fusion. |
| **Microsoft** | Dynamics 365 F&O / Business Central AP | Same. | Replace D365. The product is explicitly ERP-agnostic. |

**Over-served:** core posting, matching configuration, payment proposal *inside* the ERP.  
**Under-served:** a written rule for when an *external* model may draft, park, or (rarely) execute — and the evidence pack that proves it.

## 3. Common claims in the category (and what we refuse)

These are patterns visible in public marketing. They are not quotations of any vendor’s protected copy.

| Common market claim | Why it spreads | Evidence Room response |
|---|---|---|
| “Touchless AP” as a default destination | Buyers want a number for the board | Touchless is a *measured rate on a named slice*, with evidence. Full autonomy is not the default. |
| “Autonomous finance” / “AI that pays” | Category heat | Payment release stays human at every level. Agent 12 reviews; a human authorises. |
| “Guaranteed savings / ROI in 90 days” | Procurement needs a business case | Banned. Deloitte (8 Oct 2025) reports only 21% of finance teams with deployed AI claim clear, measurable ROI. We design measurement; we do not invent the result. |
| “Fraud detection” as a product feature | Fear sells | Agent 10 flags exact and near-duplicates and unusual patterns. That is **not** a fraud finding and not a guarantee. |
| “Compliant / audit-ready / SOX out of the box” | Audit anxiety | We provide control design and evidence *shapes*. We do not certify compliance. |
| “Works with one prompt” | Prompt-marketplace economics | A charter is an operating document. The instruction skeleton is a starting instruction, not a magic prompt. |
| “Replace your AP team / ERP” | Transformation theatre | We do not replace the AP stack or the people accountable for it. |

## 5. What is missing in the market (the actual white space)

Across Bands A–C, public materials are strong on:

- Capture accuracy  
- Workflow screenshots  
- Supplier networks  
- Payment coverage  
- “AI” as a feature label on extraction or coding  

They are consistently weak or absent on:

1. **A buyer-owned agent charter** — purpose, exclusions, L0–L4 rights, forbidden tools, packet fields, first-90-day slice.  
2. **An autonomy progression that cannot be silently upgraded** — config outside the model; evidence pack; downgrade without ceremony.  
3. **Four permanent human classes** — payment release, vendor bank-change, policy exception, legal dispute — forced by reason code, not confidence.  
4. **A hand-off that is not chat** — `evidence_refs`, `human_required`, `cost_envelope`.  
5. **Measurement that vetoes promotion** — risk-control family separate from activity.  
6. **ERP-agnostic operating design** the finance team can take from D365 to SAP without buying a new “AI module.”  
7. **Prompt-injection and hallucination treated as AP controls**, not as AI-safety blog posts.  
8. **A written kill-switch and fallback** so AP continues when the model vendor is down.

That list *is* the product.
