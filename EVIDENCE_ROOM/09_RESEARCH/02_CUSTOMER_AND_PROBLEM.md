# Customer and problem definition

## Economic buyer
The person who will be blamed if AP AI posts a duplicate, pays a diverted account, or walks into audit with no evidence: CFO, Finance Director, Controller, Head of AP / Shared Services, Transformation or Finance Systems lead.

## Job-to-be-done
“Give me a credible way to put AI into AP by Monday without losing control of payments, master data, or the audit trail.”

## Buying trigger
ERP already in place. Exception rate unknown or stubborn. A vendor just demoed “autonomous AP.” Internal audit asked how agents will be controlled. Shared services needs capacity without a headcount case.

## Anti-persona
Prompt hobbyists. Teams that want a payments product. Anyone whose first sentence is “turn on AI and remove AP.”

## Problem physics (why an OS, not a tool)
Most of the money and risk sits in exceptions, GR discipline, duplicates, approvals, statements, and pre-payment review — not in another capture licence. Agents help only if they have jobs, exclusions, owners, levels, and evidence.
