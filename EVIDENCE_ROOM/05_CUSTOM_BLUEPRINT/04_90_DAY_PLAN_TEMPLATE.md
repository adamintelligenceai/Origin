## 1. What to do

Run Professional START HERE 01–10 (through Shadow) for **one** increment in ~90 days of **elapsed** time, accepting that Phase 5 often consumes the slack.

**How.** Weeks below. Cut weeks only if well-bounded tests **all** pass — then you may *attempt* a 4–6 week observe-to-shadow path, still without automatic Pilot.

**Who.** Buyer Accountable = Head of AP. Task R from their RACI. Evidence Room practitioner is **not** on the critical path after delivery.

**Wrong.** Day 90 = supplier mail sending. Second agent “in parallel.”

**Control.** Caveat log. Gate IDs. No execute verb in week 12.

**Measure.** Exit packs created; caveats triggered.

**Evidence.** Buyer `/Evidence/Roadmap/` + this plan version.
