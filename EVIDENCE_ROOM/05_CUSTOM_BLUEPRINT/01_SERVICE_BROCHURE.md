## 4. Method (Observe → Structure → Agentise — bounded)

1. **Intake questionnaire** completed  
2. **Document & data room** (process maps, ageing samples, DOA summary — masked)  
3. **Interviews** (typically 4–6 × 45 min)  
4. **Blueprint drafting** (AI-assisted analysis + expert operating design)  
5. **Working review** with Process Owner  
6. **Executive readout** + decision log  
7. **Handoff** of markdown/PDF pack + template completions  

We use AI to accelerate structuring and drafting. **Judgement on controls, ceilings, and payment boundaries remains human** — yours and ours in review.
