## 1. What to do

The buyer coordinator fills this form. The Accountable signs the declaration.

**How.** Tables below. One increment only.

**Who.** Head of AP / Controller signs; AP Manager supplies volumes; FinSys supplies access-lead-time estimates.

**Wrong.** Sixteen agents in scope. Live personal data. Asking for L3.

**Control.** Completeness gate in `02_FULFILMENT_WORKFLOW.md`.

**Measure.** Fields complete vs blank; ASSUMED count.

**Evidence.** Intake ID `CB-IN-[YYYYMMDD]-[buyer-code]`.
