# What the customer buys

A repeatable, AI-assisted assessment — not an open-ended consulting engagement.

**They provide** a structured intake (this folder) plus optional exports.

**They receive** within an agreed window (illustrative: 10 business days after a complete pack):

1. Current-state assessment  
2. AP AI maturity score (diagnostic method)  
3. Opportunity map  
4. Recommended agent architecture (subset of the 16)  
5. Prioritised use cases  
6. Operating model sketch  
7. KPI baseline framework  
8. Controls framework (first agents)  
9. Business case using their numbers (conservative/base/upside)  
10. Transformation roadmap  
11. 90-day implementation plan  
12. Executive presentation

# Intake questionnaire (minimum)

**Organisation.** Industry, employees, entities, ERP(s), AP automation already in place, SSC vs local.

**Volumes.** Invoices/month, channels, PO vs non-PO, credits, statements.

**Labour.** AP FTE, fully loaded cost, who approves, who releases payment.

**Performance.** Cycle time, exception rate, top codes, STP if known, GRNI, late payments.

**Controls.** DOA source, bank-change process, audit findings (yes/no only — do not send confidential reports).

**Data.** What can be exported this month? Sample of 20 sanitised exceptions.

**Constraints.** Cannot-touch list. Works councils. Data residency. Live projects.

**Ambition.** What “good” means in two quarters. Budget envelope if they wish to share.

# Fulfilment workflow (internal)

1. Completeness check (reject if volumes + payment authority + 20 exceptions are missing).  
2. Score diagnostic.  
3. Map opportunities to the 16-agent stack.  
4. Draft architecture and 90-day plan.  
5. Run their numbers through the business-case model.  
6. QA: no invented stats; no payment autonomy; IP-clean.  
7. Deliver PDF + Excel + PPTX.  
8. Optional 60-minute readout (include in $3,000 band).

# Pricing posture

$1,500 for complete written pack, no call. $2,500 written + readout. $3,000 multi-entity or messy estate. Not legal or implementation services.

# What we refuse

Production invoice dumps with personal bank accounts. Live system access. Doing their first agent for them under this SKU (that is implementation, separately scoped).
