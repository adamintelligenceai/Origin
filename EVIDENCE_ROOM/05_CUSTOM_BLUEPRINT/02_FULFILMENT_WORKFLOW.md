# Custom Blueprint — Fulfilment Workflow

**Evidence Room** · Internal operations

## Stages

| Stage | Owner | SLA target | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Intake received | Ops | — | Questionnaire complete; no secrets in clear text |
| 1 Qualify & quote | Lead designer | 3 business days | Band selected; SOW/email confirmation |
| 2 Kickoff call | Lead designer | Scheduled within 7 days of payment | Recording/notes filed |
| 3 Draft artefacts | Designer | 10 business days (Focused); 15 (Complex) | Internal checklist pass |
| 4 QA | Second reviewer | 2 business days | Claims & control review pass |
| 5 Deliver + walkthrough | Designer | Agreed date | Pack sent; session held |
| 6 Revisions | Designer | 5 business days from feedback | Change log closed |
| 7 Close | Ops | — | Feedback captured; upsell note logged |

## Payment & storefront

- Collect via Lemon Squeezy (or agreed invoice) before Stage 2
- SKU reference: `ER-CUSTOM`
- No outcome-based pricing

## QA checklist (mandatory)

- [ ] No guaranteed savings / fraud / ROI language
- [ ] Ardent citations only if used — and only $9.84 / 18.4% / 35.4%
- [ ] Hard-gate warnings present for money-path designs
- [ ] Evidence standard specified per agent
- [ ] Forbidden actions explicit
- [ ] Pilot plan has control KPI
- [ ] Brand voice: institutional, no AI clichés
- [ ] No customer secrets retained beyond retention policy

## Escalations

| Issue | Action |
|---|---|
| Scope creep beyond band | Re-quote before continuing |
| Missing policy artefacts | Pause draft; request; adjust timeline |
| Request for payment auto-release | Refuse; redesign to Propose readiness + human release |
| Security incident / leaked creds in intake | Follow security runbook; scrub; notify sponsor |

## Handoff to Pro / Team

If delivery reveals need for training at scale or full library, note recommendation in close report — do not auto-charge.
