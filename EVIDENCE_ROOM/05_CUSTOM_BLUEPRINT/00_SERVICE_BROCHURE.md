**Product:** Evidence Room Custom Blueprint  
**Brand idea:** Proof before permission. Agents earn responsibility. Evidence decides.  
**Service type:** Productised advisory deliverable — **not** software implementation, **not** a managed AP service  
**Indicative price:** **US$1,500–3,000** (fixed band; see scoping)  
**ERP stance:** Agnostic

This brochure is for a buyer who already has (or will buy) Professional and/or Team materials and wants a **buyer-specific** first-increment blueprint. It does not promise savings, ROI, fraud reduction, compliance certification, or autonomous payments.

---

## 1. What you buy

A written **Custom Blueprint** for **one** AP agent increment on **one** named slice of *your* operation:

- First-slice allow-list  
- As-done notes from your intake answers (and optional 60-minute call)  
- Charter skeleton at **L0** for one agent  
- Control / human-class confirmation  
- Test and shadow outline with **your** strata (counts left for you to fill)  
- 90-day evidence plan with caveats  
- Business-case input sheet populated with **your** figures or explicit ASSUMED flags  

You do **not** buy: ERP configuration, model hosting, labelled historical scoring done for you, a Steer committee, or permission to execute.

**How.** Intake (`01`) → fulfilment (`02`) → deliverable (`03`) → you execute the 90-day plan (`04`) with Professional OS.

**Who.**

| Side | Role |
|---|---|
| Buyer Accountable | Head of AP / Controller (must be named) |
| Buyer coordinator | Transformation or AP Manager |
| Evidence Room fulfilment | Named practitioner (not an agent) |
| Agent | Not a party |

**Wrong.** Treating the PDF as a go-live certificate. Asking us to grant L3 on paper.

**Control.** Scope lock in the intake. Anything outside the band is a **new** engagement.

**Measure.** Deliverable completeness vs outline; buyer sign-off of receipt (not of agent quality).

**Evidence.** The Blueprint file set plus intake ID. Buyer stores it under their Evidence Room path.

## 2. Why the price band is narrow

| Band | When it applies | What is in |
|---|---|---|
| **US$1,500** | One entity, one channel, one type; questionnaire complete; no call; no extra agents | Written blueprint from intake only |
| **US$2,250** | Typical: 60-minute call + questionnaire + one revision | As 1,500 + call notes + one redline cycle |
| **US$3,000** | Two related entities **or** two channels **or** a second charter *skeleton* (still one increment to run first) + call + one revision | Stated on the order; still **no** execution |

Above this band: Team workshop facilitation or multi-increment programme design — **not** this SKU.

Price is a **service fee**, not a share of savings. No success fee. No guaranteed payback.

## 3. What / how / who / wrong / control / measure / evidence (service-level)

**What.** A buyer-specific plan to start AP Agent OS without inventing a 16-agent transformation.

**How.** Structured intake; map to Professional artefacts; write the outline in `03`; stop.

**Who.** Human practitioner. The buyer remains Accountable for AP.

**Wrong.** We “approve” their production agent. We copy another client’s workflow (we will not; ACME is fictional).

**Control.** Fulfilment checklist; no L2+ recommendation without their existing Measure pack (almost never present at this SKU).

**Measure.** Cycle time of fulfilment (target **ILLUSTRATIVE** 5–10 working days after a complete intake). Quality = outline sections present, not “they went live.”

**Evidence.** Intake hash/version; deliverable version; revision log.

## 4. Fit / do-not-buy

**Fit.** You can name an AP Accountable. You can describe channels. You will not require us to access your ERP. You accept L0 as the start.

**Do not buy if.** You want us to release payments, pick a model vendor as a certified stack, produce a board ROI, or implement in your tenant.

Privacy: do **not** send live IBANs, full invoice PDFs with personal data, or employee files in the intake. Use counts, redacted examples, or fictionalised samples.
