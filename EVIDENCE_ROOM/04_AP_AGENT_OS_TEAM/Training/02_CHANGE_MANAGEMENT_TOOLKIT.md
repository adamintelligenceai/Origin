# Change Management Toolkit — AP Agent OS Team

**Evidence Room** · Team · Training / Change  
**Purpose:** Adopt the governed agent path without fear theatre or hype  
**Companion:** `../Change_Management/01_EXECUTIVE_COMMS_TEMPLATES.md`

## Change thesis

People follow the path that is clearer, fairer, and safer than the workaround.  
Agents that create silent risk will be bypassed — correctly.

## Resistance playbook

| Signal | Likely cause | Move |
|---|---|---|
| Ignoring agent queue | UX friction / distrust | Fix path; do not mandate blindly |
| Rubber-stamping | Time pressure | Sample QA + coaching |
| Inflating exceptions to avoid agent | Fear of visibility | Leadership clarity: visibility is the point |
| “Show me ROI” mid-pilot | Expectation mismatch | Restate illustrative vs baseline rules |
| Shadow ChatGPT processes | Tool vacuum | Provide approved Observe tools + policy |

## Training → adoption bridge

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Training day | Knowledge check + kill-switch drill |
| +1–3 | Buddy shadow sessions |
| +7 | First evidence review huddle |
| +14 | Pulse survey (5 questions) |
| +30 | Adoption retrospective |

### Pulse survey (1–5)

1. I understand the agent’s stage limits.  
2. Confirm/reject is clear.  
3. I know how to escalate unsafe behavior.  
4. The agent path is usable for in-scope work.  
5. I trust leadership to demote when evidence is weak.

## Manager talking points (5 minutes)

- We are not installing “autonomous AP.”  
- Responsibility is earned with evidence.  
- Demotion is professional, not punitive.  
- Industry averages (e.g., Ardent 2025 cost **$9.84**, exception **18.4%**, STP **35.4%**) contextualize the industry — our KPIs are **ours**.  
- No guaranteed savings story in team meetings.
