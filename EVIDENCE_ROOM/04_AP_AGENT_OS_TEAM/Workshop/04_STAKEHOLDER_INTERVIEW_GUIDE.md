# Stakeholder Interview Guide

**Product:** Evidence Room — AP Agent OS Team  
**Document ID:** `ER-TEAM-WS-INT`  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Use:** T–10 to T–3 before workshop; 30–45 minutes per interview  
**Output:** Interview notes → facilitator synthesis one-pager

---

## 1. Purpose

Discover operating reality, veto points, and political constraints **before** the workshop so in-room time is design, not archaeology.

---

## 2. Interview set (minimum)

| # | Role | Focus |
|---|------|-------|
| 1 | CFO / Finance Director or Controller | Risk appetite; claims discipline; ceiling |
| 2 | Head of AP / Process Owner | Process truth; exceptions; owners |
| 3 | AP team lead / exception desk | Daily friction; shadow tools |
| 4 | Procurement ops / buyer lead | PO quality; dispute paths |
| 5 | IT / Finance systems | Integration; logging; environments |
| Optional | Internal Audit | Evidence expectations |
| Optional | Shared Services lead | Multi-entity variance |

---

## 3. Opening script (2 minutes)

> “We’re preparing an AP agent operating-model workshop. Evidence Room is a governance and design toolkit — not payment software. I’d like your candid view of how AP works today, where control risk sits, and what ‘good’ would look like if agents assisted under human accountability. We will not ask you to endorse guaranteed savings or autonomous payments.”

Confirm confidentiality: notes synthesised; no gotcha quotes without permission.

---

## 4. Core questions (all roles)

### Context
1. What is your role relative to invoice-to-pay?  
2. Which entities / volumes matter most in the next two quarters?  
3. What ERP / AP automation is in place today?

### Pain
4. Where does work actually pile up (be specific)?  
5. Which supplier or internal behaviours create the most noise?  
6. What broke recently that embarrassed Finance?

### Controls
7. How does payment authorisation work end-to-end?  
8. How are vendor bank changes verified?  
9. Where is SoD strongest / weakest?  
10. What is your reaction to AI touching AP?

### Data & exceptions
11. Are exceptions coded today? If yes, are codes trusted?  
12. Which reports do you actually use weekly?  
13. What baselines exist for cycle time, ageing, STP?

### Change
14. Who must say yes for a pilot to start? Who can kill it?  
15. What prior automation / RPA / AI attempts taught you?  
16. What would make this workshop a waste of time?

### Close
17. What decision do you want the workshop to force?  
18. Anything we must not put on a slide?

---

## 5. Role-specific probes

### CFO / Controller
- What autonomy ceiling would you sign for 90 days?  
- What evidence would you need before any L2 discussion?  
- Which Board narrative are you trying to avoid?  
- Non-negotiables on payment and master data?

### Head of AP
- Who owns process design vs firefighting?  
- Top 5 exception types by effort (even gut-ranked)?  
- Channel mix and known capture issues?  
- Team sentiment toward AI?

### Exception desk / processors
- Walk me through yesterday’s hardest invoice.  
- What workarounds exist that policy doesn’t know?  
- Any use of ChatGPT / public AI on live work?  
- What escalations are ignored?

### Procurement
- PO quality defects you already know?  
- Willingness to own `price_mismatch` / `missing_po`?  
- Supplier communication norms / tone risks?

### IT / Systems
- Read paths for invoice, PO, GR, vendor, approvals?  
- Non-prod realism? Masking?  
- Logging retention? Change control for bots/prompts?  
- Identity / secrets handling for any agent tooling?

### Internal Audit
- What would you expect to re-perform?  
- Prior findings related to AP automation?  
- Preferred involvement model (inform / review / gateway)?

## 7. Synthesis one-pager (facilitator)

After interviews, produce:

1. **Consensus pains** (≤5)  
2. **Disputed facts** (resolve in Exercise 1)  
3. **Ceiling hypothesis**  
4. **Likely pilot slice**  
5. **Political map** (sponsors, blockers)  
6. **Data gaps**  
7. **Claims to ban explicitly in-room**  

Share synthesis with Process Owner only (not full group) 24 hours before workshop.

---

## 8. Ethics

- Do not collect personal employee performance gossip as programme input.  
- Do not copy live bank details into notes stored in open folders.  
- Offer interviewees the chance to correct factual errors before workshop.
