# Evidence Room — AP Agent OS Team Edition

## Workshop — 04 Stakeholder interview guide

**Product:** Evidence Room AP Agent OS — Team Edition  
**Module:** Workshop  
**Standard:** Proof before permission  
**Audience:** Transformation lead, Process Excellence  
**Version:** 1.0  

Interviews **prepare** the workshop. They do not replace Observe on the floor (`../03_AP_AGENT_OS_PRO/Process_Mapping/01_PROCESS_DISCOVERY_GUIDE.md`).

---

## 1. What to do

Run 45–60 minute interviews with the roles below **before** the one-day workshop. Produce a briefing note of facts, tensions, and banned ideas.

**How.** Scripts per role. Record only with consent. Redact bank and personal data in notes.

**Who.** Transformation interviews; AP Manager schedules; Privacy confirms recording.

**Wrong.** Interviewing only the sponsor. Treating opinions as SOP. Promising an agent in the meeting.

**Control.** Same question set for comparable roles; speaker map.

**Measure.** Interviews completed vs planned; open questions carried to E2.

**Evidence.** `[BUYER]/Evidence/Team/Interviews/` + consent.

---

## 2. How — universal open and close (5 min + 5 min)

**Open.** “This is discovery for Evidence Room AP Agent OS. We are not evaluating you. We will not implement from this conversation alone. Proof before permission. I will not record promised savings.”

**Close.** “What should I not get wrong in the workshop? Who else sees the unofficial path? May I quote you in the room (Y/N)?”

**Always capture:** name, role, deputy, systems they touch, whether they can attend the workshop.

## 3. Role scripts

### 3.1 Head of AP / Shared Services lead (60 min)

**What you need.** Accountability appetite; slice prejudice; political landmines.

Ask:

1. Which entity/channel would you **defend** as a first slice, and which would you refuse?  
2. Where is the SOP fiction worst?  
3. Who is actually Accountable if a duplicate is paid?  
4. What has already been tried (OCR, BPO, RPA, prior “AI”)? What evidence remains?  
5. What would a “successful 90 days” look like **without** a savings number?  
6. Who will pressure you to go live before Test?  
7. Kill-switch: will you use it on a sponsor’s demo day?

**Wrong answer to watch.** Success = board AI story.

**Evidence.** Slice prejudices logged as hypotheses for E3.

### 3.2 Controller (45 min)

1. Cost-model existence; what is in AP CPI today?  
2. Rules for publishing hours as money?  
3. DOA owner and last version date?  
4. Close calendar vs any agent window?  
5. Appetite for unsigned “opportunity” on Steer packs? (Should be none.)  
6. Tax and IC sensitivities?

**Measure.** Whether CPI can be frozen in 30 days (Y/N/partial).

### 3.3 AP Manager (60 min)

1. Walk the queues. What ages?  
2. Exception taxonomy today vs informal names.  
3. Payment-proposal ritual.  
4. Who the processors actually escalate to.  
5. Overtime / contractor pattern (mechanism for later benefits).  
6. Month-end behaviours that break controls.

### 3.4 Processor / exception handler (45 min) — **mandatory**

Use the same questions as discovery interview in `Templates/PROCESS_DISCOVERY.md` §7. Add:

7. If a tool told you to post, what would you still check?  
8. Have you ever been blamed for a system match that was wrong?  
9. What metric already makes people game the queue?

**Control.** Manager not in the room unless the processor asks.

### 3.5 Control / Risk / IA (45 min)

1. Last AP findings.  
2. SoD exceptions live.  
3. Evidence retention reality vs policy.  
4. What they need to see to **not** object to Shadow.  
5. Injection / bank-path concern.

### 3.6 Finance Systems (45 min)

1. Identity model; time to a new service account.  
2. Can you technically allow-list entity/channel/value?  
3. Logging today; who can edit prompts?  
4. Approved integration path vs shadow SaaS already in use.  
5. ERP change freeze calendar.

**This interview often kills the 4–6 week fantasy.** Write the real lead time on E8.

### 3.7 Procurement / GR owner (30–45 min)

1. After-the-fact PO culture.  
2. Who creates GR; plant vs desk.  
3. Price-tolerance **document** location — or POLICY GAP.  
4. Will they accept chase mail from a template?

### 3.8 Treasury / payments authoriser (30 min)

1. Who releases; dual control.  
2. Bank-change path today.  
3. What they will never delegate.  
4. If Agent 12 appears in conversation: confirm L1/L2 max for now.

### 3.9 Privacy / DPO (30 min)

1. Invoice/vendor as personal data.  
2. Cross-border model hosting.  
3. Training-data opt-out.  
4. Redaction standard for transcripts.

### 3.10 Sponsor (CFO/FD) — 30 min, **after** you have floor facts if possible

1. Why this programme exists in **one operational sentence**.  
2. What they will accept as a Hold.  
3. Whether they will attend Steer and read Conservative first.  
4. Confirm they are not asking for autonomous payments.
