# Evidence Room — AP Agent OS Team Edition

## Workshop — 02 One-day agenda

**Product:** Evidence Room AP Agent OS — Team Edition  
**Module:** Workshop  
**Standard:** Proof before permission  
**Audience:** Participants and facilitator  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Duration:** 08:30–17:00 local (ILLUSTRATIVE). Compress only by dropping the optional 14:30 cost block — **do not** drop human classes or decision.

Times include movement. Exercises are specified in `03_WORKSHOP_EXERCISES.md` (E1–E8).

---

## 1. What to do

Run the day as a **working session**. Phones down for exercise blocks. Outputs are written artefacts.

**How.** This timetable. Facilitator uses `01_FACILITATION_PACK.md`.

**Who.** See facilitation pack §4. Head of AP must be present at blocks marked **DECIDE**.

**Wrong.** Morning keynote + afternoon “working groups” with no plenary decision.

**Control.** Timekeeper; artefact list on the wall all day.

**Measure.** Start on time; exercises finished or explicitly cut with Accountable consent.

**Evidence.** Timed agenda initialled; board export.

---

## 2. Objective (read at 08:30)

By 17:00 we will have a **first-slice allow-list**, a **first agent at L0**, a **risk and baseline list**, and a **30-day evidence plan** — or a documented **Hold/Stop**. We will not select a vendor, set an ROI target, or grant execution rights.

## 4. Opening script (08:30, 3 minutes — facilitator)

> This is Evidence Room Team Edition. Proof before permission. Agents earn responsibility. Evidence decides.  
> We will not promise savings. We will not call this fraud detection or a compliance programme.  
> Payment release, vendor bank changes, policy exceptions, and legal disputes stay human.  
> The Head of AP is Accountable for decisions today. I am not.  
> If you state a number, say whether it is extracted, estimated, or illustrative.

---

## 5. Parking-lot rules

Parking items get an **owner and a date** at 16:00 or they are deleted.

Forbidden parking: “define fraud approach,” “pick the LLM,” “set 30% efficiency.” Those are either out of product scope or belong in Test/Measure, not a lot.

---

## 6. Remote / hybrid variant

Same times. Two facilitators if >8 on video (content + board). Processor must have a working microphone. Breakout rooms only for E2/E8, 15 minutes max, then plenary. Export the board before anyone leaves.

---

## 7. Half-day emergency cut (not preferred)

If you only have 4 hours: E1 (15), E2 (40), E3 (35), E5 (25), E8 (40), DECIDE (20). You **must** schedule E4/E6/E7 within five working days or the slice is not Agentise-ready.

**Wrong.** Declaring the programme launched after a half day.

**Control.** Calendar hold for the missing exercises before people leave.

---

## 8. What can go wrong

| Failure | Control |
|---|---|
| Sponsor speech eats 08:30–09:30 | Sponsor gets 5 minutes inside Open |
| E2 skipped because “we know our process” | Processor seat mandatory |
| Decision rushed at 16:55 | Playback starts 16:00 even if E8 is ugly |

## 9. Measure / evidence

Attendance; start/end actuals; eight artefacts; decision page; photos.
