## 1. What to do

Design the room, pre-work, materials, roles, and parking-lot rules so the day produces **signed artefacts**, not a mural.

**How.** Checklists below.

**Who.** Facilitator Responsible; Head of AP Accountable for decisions taken in the room; Control owner may halt a path that implies execute-on-payments.

**Wrong.** Facilitator becomes the process owner. Vendor runs the day.

**Control.** Decision page; artefact checklist at 16:30.

**Measure.** Artefacts complete / 8; decision enum recorded.

**Evidence.** `[BUYER]/Evidence/Team/Workshop/[DATE]/`.

## 3. How — materials

**Must have**

- Agenda (02) on one page  
- Exercise cards (03)  
- Blank slice sheet, RACI translation, risk catalogue (`../03_AP_AGENT_OS_PRO/Templates/`)  
- Exception taxonomy index  
- Sticky notes or digital equivalent in **four colours**: Fact / Assumption / Decision / Parking  
- Decision page (`Templates/MEETING_GUIDE.md`)  
- Kill-switch and four-human-classes poster  

**Nice to have**

- 10 redacted invoice packets (real buyer data, privacy-cleared)  
- ACME ILLUSTRATIVE pack only if buyer data is forbidden — label it  

**Do not bring**

- A savings target  
- A 16-agent implementation Gantt for sign-off  
- Vendor pricing as the frame of the day

## 5. How — facilitator behaviours

**Do**

- Ask “what evidence would we file?” after every proposal.  
- Separate Fact / Assumption with colours.  
- Read the human-classes line aloud when someone says “just pay it.”  
- Timebox; park strategy debates.  
- Quote operators verbatim onto the as-done list.  

**Do not**

- Rescue a bad slice because the calendar needs a win.  
- Let the senior person overwrite the processor without logging dissent.  
- Translate “we usually tolerate a couple of percent” into a number.  
- Allow “the AI will handle exceptions.”  
- Promise ROI.  

**Interventions (scripts)**

| You hear | You say |
|---|---|
| “Let’s automate all of AP this quarter.” | “Professional START HERE is one increment. Write the allow-list or we stop at parking.” |
| “We’ll skip testing — it’s low risk.” | “Then it stays L0. Test is the permission mechanism.” |
| “Hackett says 60% touchless.” | “That is software-adopter research, not our baseline. Our STP is `[extract or unknown]`.” |
| “The agent can approve if confidence is high.” | “Confidence cannot widen DOA. That’s a control break.” |
| “We’ll use my ChatGPT login.” | “Out of order. Approved-tool list or we do not run live work.” |

## 6. What can go wrong

| Failure | Control |
|---|---|
| Happy-path theatre | Processor seat; redacted ugly invoices |
| Decision by the loudest | Decision-maker named on the page |
| Parking lot as a second workshop | Lot has owners and dates before close |
| Digital board not exported | Facilitator exports before leaving the call |
| Informal promises in the corridor | “If it is not on the decision page it did not happen.” |
