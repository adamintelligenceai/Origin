## Slide 5 — Brand idea: Operating Evidence

**Title:** Operating Evidence

**Bullets:**

## Slide 10 — Hard stops

**Title:** Agents never…

**Bullets:**
- Authorise / release / transmit payments  
- Invent DOA approval  
- Approve vendor bank changes unilaterally

**Speaker notes:** Violation → pause fleet + Controllership notify. Confirm kill-switch owner later.
