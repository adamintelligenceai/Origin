## Slide 9 — Earn-up ladder

| Stage | Agent may | Human must |
|---|---|---|
| **Observe** | Summarize, classify, flag | Decide all actions |
| **Recommend** | Propose coding, match, route | Approve / reject |
| **Draft** | Prepare emails, packs, notes | Send / post |
| **Execute (narrow)** | Pre-approved micro-actions | Own outcome |
| **Expand** | Broader scope after gates | Explicit promotion |

Default: start left. Demote freely.

*Speaker notes:* Map Starter language (Draft/Propose/Bounded act) to this ladder for continuity.

---

## Slide 10 — Hard gates (money path)

Before Propose/Execute near payments:

- [ ] Dual control on payment release remains human  
- [ ] Vendor bank change never agent-alone  
- [ ] SoD matrix reviewed  
- [ ] Kill-switch named and tested  
- [ ] Evidence retention path exists  

Fail any → stay Observe / Recommend / Draft.

*Speaker notes:* Controls partner owns the checklist verbally.

## Slide 14 — Exception reality

## Slide 15 — Selecting a pilot

Good pilots are:

- High volume **or** high pain — not both unbounded  
- Clear invoice class (e.g., domestic PO < threshold)  
- Observable evidence  
- Willing human owner with calendar time  

*Speaker notes:* Kill “boil the ocean” proposals politely.
