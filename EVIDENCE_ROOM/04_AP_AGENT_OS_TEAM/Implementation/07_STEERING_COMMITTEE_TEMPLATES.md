## 1. Terms of Reference (ToR)

### Name
AP Agent Operating Model Steering Committee

### Purpose
Govern scope, autonomy ceilings, control posture, and gateway decisions for AP agent programmes. Ensure Operating Evidence standards. Prevent unlabelled benefit claims and autonomous payment drift.

### Authority
- Approve / revise autonomy ceilings within Board-delegated risk appetite  
- Start, pause, demote, or retire agents  
- Approve pilot slice changes  
- Escalate residual risk to CFO  

**Not in authority:** Authorising individual supplier payments; replacing treasury dual-control.

### Membership

| Seat | Role | Voting |
|------|------|--------|
| Chair | Sponsor (CFO/Controller/designate) | Y |
| Process Owner | Head of AP | Y |
| Controllership | Controls lead | Y |
| IT / FinSystems | Platform owner | Y |
| PMO | Transformation | N (secretariat) |
| Procurement | Ops lead | Optional Y |
| Internal Audit | Observer | N |
| Agent Owners | As invited | N |

Quorum: Chair + Process Owner + Controllership.

### Cadence
Monthly in pilot; quarterly in BAU; ad hoc within 24h on critical incidents.

### Inputs
CFO Steering Pack · Implementation Tracker · Benefits Tracker · Risk/incident log · Autonomy registry extract

### Outputs
Decision log · updated ceiling · actions with owners/dates

### Claims discipline
Secretariat rejects pack language that guarantees savings, fraud detection, compliance, or autonomous payments.
