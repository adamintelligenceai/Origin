# What Team adds

Professional is a practitioner OS. Team is the same OS plus the artefacts required to move a **group**:

- Workshop deck and run-of-show
- Interview guides and process-owner questionnaires
- Exercises and maturity facilitation
- Enterprise governance pack
- Steering templates
- Implementation and benefits trackers
- Training modules
- Change-management and executive communication

Licence: internal use in **one named organisation** (see legal).

# Facilitator rules

- No vendor demo in the morning.
- No “autonomous AP” language.
- If data access is unknown, the first agent stays at Level 0.
- Capture parking-lot software wishes; do not design around them.

# Pre-work (send 10 days out)

1. Diagnostic questions A1–F6  
2. Last quarter volume, exception top 10, FTE  
3. Current SOP (if any)  
4. Who may authorise payment (names/roles)  
5. List of tools that already touch AP
