## 1. What Team is for

Team is the edition you open when a Finance Director says:

> “Can we run a credible AP agent workshop tomorrow — with Controllership, AP, Procurement, and IT in the room?”

It packages facilitation-ready materials, training, executive artefacts, and implementation trackers on top of the Professional operating-system depth (16-agent library, governance, taxonomy, KPIs — included by purchase ladder or cross-reference to Pro artefacts you already hold).

**Team is not** software, an ERP replacement, or autonomous payment tooling.

## 3. Who should be in the room

| Role | Mandatory? | Why |
|------|------------|-----|
| Executive sponsor (CFO / Controller) | Opening + close (or full day) | Risk appetite; autonomy ceiling |
| Head of AP / Process Owner | Yes | Design authority |
| Exception / team leads | Yes | Reality check |
| Controllership / Internal Control | Yes | SoD, payment boundary |
| Procurement (buyer ops) | Recommended | PO quality, disputes |
| IT / Finance systems | Recommended | Integration, logging |
| Internal Audit (observer) | Optional | Assurance awareness |
| Shared Services lead | If GBS model | Multi-entity standards |

**Ideal size:** 8–14 people. Above 18, split into design pods.

## 5. Outcomes you should refuse to fake

By end of workshop, the organisation should have **draft** (not theatre):

1. Autonomy ceiling and payment boundary statement  
2. Prioritised agent backlog (≤5) with owners  
3. Adopted exception code list for pilot slice  
4. Baseline KPI list + data owners  
5. 90-day plan with named workstreams  
6. Steering cadence and kill-switch owner  

If Controllership will not sign the payment boundary, **stop expansion talk** — document as blocker.

## 7. Quality bar — “usable tomorrow”

Before facilitating:

- [ ] Read Facilitation Guide end-to-end  
- [ ] Pre-interview 3–5 stakeholders (`04_STAKEHOLDER_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md`)  
- [ ] Process Owner completes questionnaire  
- [ ] Pull sample exception ageing (even rough)  
- [ ] Confirm room tech: whiteboard + shared decision log  
- [ ] Print Exercise sheets  
- [ ] Agree with sponsor: no savings/fraud/compliance guarantees on slides  

---

## 8. Voice & claims discipline (facilitator)

**Allowed:** design, charter, baseline, shadow, escalate, measure, demote.  
**Banned in room:** guaranteed ROI, autonomous AP, “AI will find all fraud,” “compliance solved,” magical productivity %.

When executives press for a number, open **Benefits Realisation Tracker** and label assumptions **NON-EVIDENCE**.
