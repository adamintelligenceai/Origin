## 1. Change thesis

AP agent adoption fails less from model quality than from:

- Unclear payment boundaries → fear or reckless trust  
- Exception folklore defending itself against taxonomy  
- Hero processors losing status without new craft  
- Executives demanding unverified savings narratives  

**Change goal:** Build a culture of **Operating Evidence** — agents assist under charters; humans remain accountable; autonomy is earned.

## 4. Messaging house

### Master narrative (approved language)
> We are designing AP agents with owners, exclusions, and scorecards. Agents will shadow and recommend first. Payment authorisation stays with authorised humans. We will expand responsibility only when evidence supports it.

### Message by audience

| Audience | Key line |
|----------|----------|
| AP team | You gain a triage and evidence system — not a silent replacement. |
| Controllership | Autonomy is gated; payment line untouched; logs re-performable. |
| Procurement | Clearer ownership of PO/price defects; fewer anonymous chases. |
| IT | Operating layer first; controlled integration; change control for instructions. |
| All-company | No claim of guaranteed savings or fraud elimination. |

### Banned phrases (comms QA)
Revolutionary · autonomous payments · guaranteed ROI · AI that never misses fraud · seamless AP · replace the AP team.

## 6. Resistance playbook

| Signal | Likely cause | Response |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| “Codes slow me down” | Unclear definitions | Pair coding; refine top codes only |
| “I’ll just ignore the agent” | Low trust / poor outputs | Shadow QA; fix instructions; don’t mandate accept |
| “This is about cutting jobs” | Rumour | Sponsor truth: productivity redesign TBD; no guarantee narrative either way; focus on control + craft |
| “Let it auto-pay small amounts” | Convenience pressure | Controller reaffirm boundary |
| Quiet sabotage of logging | Fear of surveillance | Explain evidence vs policing; sample coaching not gotchas |
| Executive asks for $ savings slide | Board pressure | Benefits tracker with NON-EVIDENCE labels only |

## 7. Pulse survey (10 questions)

Score 1–5; anonymous; Day 45 and Day 90.

1. I understand what agents may and may not do.  
2. I understand the payment authorisation boundary.  
3. Exception codes are usable in daily work.  
4. Agent outputs are worth reviewing.  
5. I can escalate without punishment.  
6. Training prepared me for real cases.  
7. Leaders judge success by evidence, not hype.  
8. I know who owns each pilot agent.  
9. I have enough time to code exceptions properly.  
10. I would trust expanding this agent’s scope *somewhat* — based on evidence so far.

**Open:** What should stop / start / continue?

Interpretation: Items 2 and 5 below 3.5 average → pause expansion.

## 9. Manager conversation guides

### 1:1 — anxious processor
- Acknowledge skill pride  
- Clarify payment line unchanged  
- Invite them into taxonomy examples  
- Agree one craft goal (e.g., mentor coding)  

### 1:1 — dismissive processor
- Review specific missed evidence cases  
- Set expectation: overrides need reasons  
- Pair with QA sampling duty  

### Skip-level rumour control
- Restate master narrative  
- Share decision log facts  
- Invite Controllership visibility
