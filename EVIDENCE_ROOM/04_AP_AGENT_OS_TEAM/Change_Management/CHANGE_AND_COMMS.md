# Position the change

AP is not being replaced. Repeatable analysis and drafting move to agents under governance. Humans concentrate on judgment, suppliers, exceptions that matter, and control.

Banned line: “Turn on autonomous AI and remove your AP team.”

# Audiences

| Audience | Fear | Message | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| AP analysts | Job loss; being measured unfairly | Agents start at recommend; scorecards are of the agent | Team huddle |
| Buyers / receivers | Email storms | Cadence caps; clustered chases | Intranet + manager |
| Approvers | More mail | Stall visibility, not auto-approve | Workflow banner |
| Suppliers | Bots making commitments | Humans still send; payments still human | Only if outbound changes |
| Controller / IA | Uncontrolled model | Charter, logs, SoD, kill switch | Steering |
| CFO | Hype and spend | One agent, four KPIs, conservative case | One-pager |

# Training outline (4 × 45 minutes)

1. Language: agent, level, taxonomy, evidence  
2. How to review a recommendation (and how to reject)  
3. Hostile content and privacy  
4. Using the scorecard and raising incidents

# Executive communication templates

**Sponsor email (launch shadow):**  
We are shadowing one AP agent (Goods Receipt, Level 1). It cannot post, pay, or email suppliers. We will compare its recommendations to current practice for three weeks and report accuracy, noise, and ageing. No FTE change is proposed.

**Steering update:**  
Items: queue, sample accuracy, incidents (including zero), cost, decision (hold / fix / promote / retire), next entity.

# Benefits realisation

Track only what you baseline. Capacity hours, cycle, exception mix, control exceptions. Do not convert hours to dollars without Finance’s method.
