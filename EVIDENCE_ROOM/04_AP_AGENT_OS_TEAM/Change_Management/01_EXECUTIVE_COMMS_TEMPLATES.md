## Template 1 — Sponsor launch note (email)

**To:** Finance leadership, AP, IT app owners, Controls  
**Subject:** Governing AP agents — programme launch

Colleagues —

We are launching a governed approach to AI assistance in Accounts Payable using Evidence Room’s AP Agent OS Team method. Principle: **agents that earn responsibility**.

What this means:

- Agents start in limited stages (Observe / Recommend) with named human owners  
- Payment release and vendor bank changes remain under existing dual-control processes  
- We will measure our own baselines — not market averages presented as our results  
- Demotion is a normal control when evidence is weak

## Template 10 — FAQ blurbs

**Are we replacing Coupa/Tipalti/SAP?**  
No. Agents sit beside systems of record.

**Will this cut our cost to $9.84?**  
$9.84 is an Ardent 2025 industry average, not a promise.

**Can the agent pay invoices?**  
No. Payment release remains human-controlled.

**What if the agent is wrong?**  
Reject, document, escalate; we demote when patterns fail.
