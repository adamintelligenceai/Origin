# Evidence Room — AP Agent OS Team Edition

## Change management — 02 Executive communications

**Product:** Evidence Room AP Agent OS — Team Edition  
**Module:** Change Management  
**Standard:** Proof before permission  
**Audience:** Authors of sponsor, leadership, and all-hands notes  
**Version:** 1.0  

Short, reusable messages. Adapt `[BUYER]` fields. Do not add hype in the last edit.

---

## 1. What to do

Issue the right note at the right gate. Each note states **what is true**, **what is not permitted**, and **where evidence lives**.

**How.** Copy a template; Head of AP signs; Change lead sends.

**Who.** Head of AP Accountable for AP content; CFO for anything that could be read as a financial forecast; Change lead Responsible for distribution lists.

**Wrong.** All-hands “we launched AI in AP” on Shadow day.

**Control.** Language list in `01_CHANGE_TOOLKIT.md`. No number without a pack ID or ESTIMATE/ILLUSTRATIVE label.

**Measure.** Notes sent vs gates; corrections issued (should be rare).

**Evidence.** `[BUYER]/Evidence/Team/Change/Comms/`.
