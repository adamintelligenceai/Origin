# Evidence Room — AP Agent OS Team Edition

## Change management — 01 Toolkit

**Product:** Evidence Room AP Agent OS — Team Edition  
**Module:** Change Management  
**Standard:** Proof before permission  
**Audience:** Change lead, AP Manager, Head of AP, Communications  
**Version:** 1.0  

Change work makes the **operating rules usable** on the floor. It does not “drive adoption” of execution rights the agent has not earned.

---

## 1. What to do

Map stakeholders, impacts, resistance, and messages. Time communications to **gates**, not to a launch party. Measure understanding and behaviour (do-not-wait in Shadow), not sentiment about AI.

**How.** Instruments below.

**Who.** Change lead Responsible for the plan; Head of AP Accountable for what is said about AP work; AP Manager for floor rhythm.

**Wrong.** Campaign: “AI frees you for higher-value work” as a promise. Adoption KPIs that reward rubber-stamping.

**Control.** Language list §6. Comms signed by Head of AP.

**Measure.** Training before Shadow; incident from “I thought it could pay”; unofficial tool use.

**Evidence.** `[BUYER]/Evidence/Team/Change/`.

## 2. How — impact map (complete per increment)

| Group | What changes in their day | What does **not** change | Risk if we mess up the message | Owner of message |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Processors | Extra pane; new codes; review minutes | They still own the live item in Shadow | They wait or obey | AP Manager |
| Exception owners | Packet quality expectations | They still dispose their family | They dump UNMAPPED | Exception lead |
| Buyers / GR | Possible template chases later | They still receipt / own PO | They ignore mail as spam | Procurement / GR |
| Payment authoriser | Maybe a better proposal file | They still release | They think the agent paid | Treasury |
| Master data | More EX-PAY-001 | Dual control remains | They take PDF IBANs | MDM |
| Controllers | Scorecards | Still sign cash | They see ROI theatre | Controller |
| Sponsor | Holds are normal | Not a demo factory | Date pressure | CFO + HoAP |

## 3. How — resistance (treat as information)

| Pattern | What it often means | Do | Do not |
|---|---|---|---|
| “It will take my job” | No mechanism conversation | Tell the truth: no FTE promise in this OS | Promise safety you cannot keep **or** promise “no jobs ever” |
| “We tried OCR” | Prior evidence ignored | Show what is different: gates, not a new engine | Dismiss |
| “Just go live” | Sponsor pressure | Point at Test pack | Soft launch |
| Silent workaround | Packet unusable | Fix packet (UAT defect) | More posters |
| Rubber-stamp AGR | Gaming autonomy | Sample QA; intervention KPI | Celebrate low intervention |

---

## 5. How — manager conversation (15 min script)

AP Manager to team:

1. Why this slice (operational fact).  
2. What the agent **cannot** do this month.  
3. How you will be measured (not “AI usage”).  
4. How to reject.  
5. How to raise an incident.  
6. “If I ever tell you to follow the agent instead of the SOP, that is a defect — tell Control.”

---

## 6. Language list

**Use**

- Proof before permission.  
- Agents earn responsibility.  
- Evidence decides.  
- Shadow is not an instruction.  
- Hold is a valid outcome.  
- ESTIMATE (for hours).  
- Human release.

**Do not use**

- Autonomous AP / autopilot / set-and-forget.  
- Guaranteed savings / ROI / “2× productivity.”  
- Fraud-proof / fraud detection as a product claim.  
- Compliance guaranteed / audit-ready as a certificate.  
- World-class cost (unless a dated buyer-owned study).  
- “The AI decided.”  
- Industry statistics you did not source (and never invent).

External research, if needed: label Deloitte 8 Oct 2025 (63% deployed / 21% measurable ROI) and Hackett Nov 2025 **software-adopter** touchless figures as **not our results**.

## 7. What can go wrong

| Failure | Control |
|---|---|
| Change success = adoption % | Ban; use override quality + FNR |
| Comms outrun the charter | Head of AP sign-off |
| Floor hears a different story than Steer | Same facts, different length |

## 8. Measure change work

| Question | Metric |
|---|---|
| Did people hear the rules? | Training pass before Shadow |
| Did they wait? | Shadow contamination notes |
| Did they invent authority? | Incidents tagged messaging |
| Did we over-claim? | Language review of sent comms |

**Evidence.** Plan, signed messages, review log.

Proof before permission.
