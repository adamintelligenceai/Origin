## 1. Purpose of this briefing

To inform the Board / CFO of the **governed approach** to AI agents in Accounts Payable: progressive responsibility, human accountability, and evidence standards. This is an operating-model programme beside existing AP/ERP platforms — not a platform replacement.

## 2. Principle

**Agents that earn responsibility.**  
Responsibility is progressive, evidenced, and revocable. Demotion is a designed control, not a failure narrative.
