## 1. What to do

Provide an **oversight** note: what programme exists, what cannot be delegated, how assurance works, what has been evidenced, what is asked of the board (usually *note* or *endorse funding envelope*).

**How.** Use the template in §2. Controller and Head of AP co-sign facts. General Counsel / Privacy may add a sentence on data — they do not bless “compliant AP.”

**Who.** CFO presents or circulates. Control owner reviews risk paragraph.

**Wrong.** “AI transformation” narrative. Hackett 60% as our achievement. Autonomous payments.

**Control.** Every number has a pack ID or is removed. Research citations labelled *external*.

**Measure.** Board paper without unsigned savings; human classes stated.

**Evidence.** `[BUYER]/Evidence/Team/Executive/Board/` + board pack number.

## 2. Paper template

**Title:** Accounts payable agents — permission system (note / endorse envelope)  
**Period:** `[BUYER]`  
**Classification:** Internal. Not an audit opinion.

### Purpose of this paper

To inform the Board that Finance is applying a controlled operating system to any AP agents: they start observing, they earn verbs through evidence, and they do not release payments.

### What we are doing

- Named agents with human Accountables (Evidence Room AP Agent OS).  
- First slice: `[entity / channel / type / agent / L0]`.  
- Path: test on labelled history → user acceptance → shadow (no posting/sending) → optional narrow pilot.  
- Four duties remain human: **payment release, vendor bank-change approval, policy exceptions, legal disputes.**

### What we are not doing

- Delegating payment authority to software.  
- Claiming fraud detection or regulatory certification.  
- Publishing ROI as a commitment.

### Assurance

| Question | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Who is accountable? | Head of AP / Controller; never the agent |
| How do we stop? | Kill-switch; identity disable |
| How do we know it is right? | Buyer-owned labelled tests; dictionary KPIs |
| How do we stop silent change? | Model/prompt hashes; change classes |
| How is data handled? | Approved tools; Privacy review of each charter |

Internal Audit may inspect the Evidence Room. They do not operate the agents.
