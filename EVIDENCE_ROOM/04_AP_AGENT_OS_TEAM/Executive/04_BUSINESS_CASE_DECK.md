## Slide 8 — Benefit hypotheses (qualitative)

Possible *directions* (not guarantees):

- Faster triage of known exception types  
- Cleaner evidence for audit conversations  
- Reduced reliance on tribal workarounds  
- Clearer promotion decisions for AI assistance  

Each must be tied to a measurable operational KPI you already trust.

## Slide 13 — Control value (often the real case)

Boards fund **risk reduction in how AI is used** even when throughput gains are uncertain.

Value: known stage ceilings, kill-switches, evidence packs, demotion culture.
