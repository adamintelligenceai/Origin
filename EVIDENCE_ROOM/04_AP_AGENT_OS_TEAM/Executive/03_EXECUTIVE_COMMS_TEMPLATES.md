## Template 1 — Sponsor launch note (email)

**Subject:** AP Agent Operating Model — pilot scope and control principles

Colleagues,

We are starting a governed pilot to design how AI agents may assist Accounts Payable. This is an operating-model effort: agent charters, exception coding, controls, and measurement. It is not a deployment of autonomous payments and not an ERP replacement.

**Principles**
- Human accountability first  
- Responsibility earned gradually (observe → recommend → prepare under guardrails)  
- Payment authorisation remains with authorised humans  
- We will not claim guaranteed savings, fraud detection, or compliance outcomes

## Template 2 — AP team announcement

**Subject:** How AP agents will work with us (and what will not change)

Team,

Following our workshop, we will introduce agent **assistance** on [slice]. Your expertise remains central.

**What changes**
- We will code exceptions with a shared taxonomy  
- Some agents will propose triage, chase drafts, or duplicate *candidates* for your review  
- We will log accepts, amendments, and rejects as operating evidence  

**What does not change**
- You are not asked to trust black boxes blindly  
- Payment authorisation and bank release stay with authorised humans  
- Vendor banking changes still require enhanced human verification  

**Training:** [dates]  
**Feedback channel:** [link / office hours]  

Thank you for precise work — especially good escalations.

[Head of AP]
