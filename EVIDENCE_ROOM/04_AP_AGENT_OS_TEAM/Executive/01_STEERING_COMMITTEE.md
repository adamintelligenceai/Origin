# Evidence Room — AP Agent OS Team Edition

## Executive — 01 Steering committee

**Product:** Evidence Room AP Agent OS — Team Edition  
**Module:** Executive  
**Standard:** Proof before permission  
**Audience:** Sponsor (CFO/FD), Head of AP, Controller, Control owner, Transformation, FinSys (as needed)  
**Version:** 1.0  

Steering **releases permission** (fund, hold, increment) or **stops** work. It does not design packets in the room.

---

## 1. What to do

Constitute a steering group. Meet at programme gates and monthly once live. Decide from Evidence Room packs, not vendor slides.

**How.** Terms below. Pack order is mandatory.

**Who.**

| Seat | Role in Steer |
|---|---|
| CFO / FD or delegate | Sponsor; A for L3+ / payment-adjacent |
| Head of AP | Operating A; presents |
| Controller | Cash lines; cost model |
| Control owner | Veto on risk-control |
| Transformation | Tracker; actions |
| FinSys | Hashes, access, vendor |
| IA | Optional I |
| Vendor | By invitation, last agenda item, 10 min max |

**Wrong.** Steer as an AI showcase. Upside business case as the cover.

**Control.** Pack template §4. Decision log.

**Measure.** Meetings with dictionary IDs; unsigned $ spoken as fact (0).

**Evidence.** `[BUYER]/Evidence/Team/Steer/`.

---

## 2. How — terms of reference

**Purpose.** (1) Confirm first-slice and funding of discovery/test. (2) Accept or reject gate exits from Test / Shadow / Pilot / Measure. (3) Accept one increment or refuse. (4) Unblock access/privacy. (5) Hear incidents Sev-1/2.

**Not in scope.** Prompt wording. Vendor bake-off as a substitute for a charter. Setting industry-benchmark targets.

**Cadence.** Gate-driven + monthly once any agent is in Shadow or later. **ILLUSTRATIVE** 45 minutes.

**Quorum.** Head of AP + Control + (Controller if money on the pack) + sponsor or documented delegate.

**Kill-switch.** Steer cannot overrule an operational disable; it can only review after contain.

---

## 3. How — pack order (mandatory)

1. **Risk-control** — breaches, high-risk FNR, incidents, hash match, identity drift.  
2. **Operational** — vs **this buyer’s** baseline, mix table.  
3. **Cost** — AIC, CPI if current, HHR labelled ESTIMATE.  
4. **Activity** — last, descriptive.  
5. **Decision requested** — enum.  
6. **Appendix** — Conservative business case if still funding; research citations labelled if used.

A green operational story with red risk-control is a **failed** period (`00_KPI_FRAMEWORK.md`).
