### What this is

We are adopting Evidence Room’s AP Agent OS: named agents with charters, human accountability, tests, shadow, and a narrow pilot path. **Proof before permission. Agents earn responsibility. Evidence decides.**

We are not implementing “autonomous AP.” Payment release, vendor bank-change approval, policy exceptions, and legal disputes stay human at every autonomy level.

### Why now (operational, not hype)

`[BUYER: 4–6 factual bullets — volume, exception ageing, overtime, control findings. No invented industry stats.]`

Those are **not** our targets and **not** Evidence Room results.

### What I need from you

1. Confirm sponsor role and that Hold is an acceptable outcome.  
2. Acknowledge that L3+ or payment-adjacent expansion will come back to you.  
3. Confirm Controller ownership of any cash/P&L claim (KPI-FIN-SAV).

5. Confirm we will not use personal or unapproved model tools on invoice data.

### How we will talk about money

- Conservative / Base / Upside model — Conservative on the cover.  
- Hours released stay **ESTIMATE** until a time study exists.  
- Hours become cash only with a mechanism (overtime, contractor, vacancy).  
- Duplicate *flags* are not savings. Discounts *available* are not savings.  
- False precision is a defect.
