## 3. Agentic AP (what we mean — and refuse)

**Overline:** DEFINITION

### Agentic AP is a workforce design. It is not a feature flag.

An agent, in this house, is a **job with a charter**: purpose, exclusions, tools it must not hold, outputs that cite evidence, and a human owner. It is not a chat window with access to post.

The stack is ERP-agnostic. Use it on D365, SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Workday, or a mix. Map your fields. Do not copy another company’s workflow.


Full autonomy is not the default. An agent starts at **L0 Observe**. It moves when evidence shows it is safe.

## 6. Responsibility model

**Overline:** EARNED SCOPE

### Five levels. Four things a model never decides.

| Level | Name | Agent may | Agent may not |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L0** | Observe | Shadow the process; write what it would have done | Surface work into the live queue |
| **L1** | Recommend | Put a recommendation and evidence pack in front of a human | Draft the ERP transaction or the outbound email |
| **L2** | Prepare | Draft the posting, match, email, or GR proposal in a park status | Commit, send, or post |
| **L3** | Execute within guardrails | Commit only when amount, vendor, exception type, and confidence gates pass | Anything outside the published gate |
| **L4** | Managed autonomy | Execute across the published scope with sampling and a live kill-switch | The four human classes; anything after kill-switch |

Promotion is per **agent × legal entity × invoice slice**. Never promote “the stack.”

HOLD — These stay human at every level:

1. **Payment release** — bank file, payment run, positive-pay, cheque.  
2. **Vendor bank-change approval** — and other payment-master changes.  
3. **Policy exceptions** — off-policy spend, after-the-fact PO above threshold, related-party, missing DoA.  
4. **Legal disputes** — formal claim, solicitor letter, statutory demand.

Confidence may stop an execute. Confidence may not clear a human class.

RULE — No agent posts, sends, or closes a case without `evidence_refs`.

## 7. KPI

**Overline:** MEASUREMENT

### Four families. Never one “AI score.”

| Family | Question | May justify promotion? |
|---|---|---|
| Activity | What volume did we touch? | **No** |
| Operational | Did cycle time, first-pass yield, ageing improve vs *your* baseline? | Supporting only |
| Financial | What did inference cost, and what cash or hours did the Controller validate? | Only signed lines |
| Risk-control | Did we breach, override, or miss a high-risk item? | **Veto** |

Measure these from your own data. Do not invent a savings number.

- Intake yield  
- First-pass yield  
- Touchless match rate **with evidence**  
- Cycle time (one clock, kept)  
- Exception ageing  
- GR/IR age  
- Unit cost (inference + tool + human exception minutes)  
- Evidence completeness  

The Hackett Group (19 November 2025) reported that customers of evaluated AP solutions averaged **60%** touchless invoice processing; companies with **30%+** touchless had **3.5×** higher AP productivity; average AP cycle times improved **59%** post-implementation.

SOURCE — The Hackett Group, independent research firm (vendor-evaluated). **This is software-adopter research, not Evidence Room results.** We do not import these figures as your targets.

## 8. Governance

**Overline:** CONTROL

### The model does not hold accountability.

Governance in AP Agent OS is a binding operating standard for the buyer to complete — not a certificate we issue.

It binds:

- Human accountability (no vendor, mailbox, or agent as Accountable)  
- Segregation of duties on people **and** agent identities  
- Least privilege as an allow-list  
- Approval boundaries — agents do not become DoA  
- Prompt injection: invoices are untrusted text  
- Hallucination: facts without a source are defects  
- Output validation before any operational action  
- Immutable-enough audit logs the agent cannot write  
- Versioned prompts, models, and workflows  
- Incident, override, fallback, BCP  
- Evidence retention **your** policy sets  

We do not certify legal, regulatory, accounting, or security compliance.

**Overline:** FIRST NINETY DAYS

### Do not stand up the stack. Stand up a slice.

1. Appoint owners. One human per agent you will touch.
