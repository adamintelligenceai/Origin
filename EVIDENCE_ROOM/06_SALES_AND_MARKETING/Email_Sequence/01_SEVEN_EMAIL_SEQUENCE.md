## Email 3 — First agent

**Send:** Day 4  
**Subject options:**
1. The first AP agent to charter (and why)  
2. Don’t start with payments  
**Preview:** Intake, Validation, Matching, Exception Triage.

### Body

Hi {{first_name | "there"}},

If you charter only one agent this month, do **not** start with payment proposal.

Start where work is frequent, observable, and reversible:

1. **Invoice Intake** — classify and route; do not post  
2. **Invoice Validation** — completeness and policy checks; recommend, don’t override controls  
3. **Matching** — surface mismatches with evidence  
4. **Exception Triage** — classify before people firefight  

A usable first charter answers:

- Job description  
- Inputs / tools / data  
- Responsibilities **and exclusions**  
- Human owner  
- Escalation path  
- Output standard  
- Starting responsibility level (usually **L0 Observe** or **L1 Recommend**)

Payment authorisation stays human. Always.

**Preview:** Shadow before execute. Payments stay human-gated.

Hi {{first_name | "there"}},

Autonomy without a ladder is how AP AI projects lose trust.

Evidence Room uses a simple responsibility model:

| Level | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | Observe | Watch and log |
| L1 | Recommend | Suggest; human decides |
| L2 | Prepare | Draft artefacts; human releases |
| L3 | Execute within guardrails | Act only inside written limits |
| L4 | Managed autonomy | Earned only, with evidence |

**Full autonomy is never the default.**  
**Payment authorisation stays human.**

The method is deliberate: Observe → … → Shadow → Controlled Pilot → Measure → Expand responsibility.

If you cannot show who owned the agent and what changed in the KPI pack, you do not expand.

2. How to think about cost and capacity — carefully  
**Preview:** Labelled illustrative. Your numbers will differ.

### Body

Hi {{first_name | "there"}},

Leaders ask for numbers. Fair. Here is how we recommend thinking — without inventing guaranteed ROI.

**Illustrative model (NON-EVIDENCE — for structure only):**

Suppose a mid-complexity AP team processes a large monthly invoice volume with a material exception rate. If agents at **L1/L2** reduce handle time on a **subset** of exceptions — not all work — capacity frees for higher-value control and supplier work. Software subscription and change cost still exist. Net benefit is **scenario-dependent**.

What Evidence Room provides is not a promised percentage. It provides:

- A **business case model** where *you* enter baselines  
- KPI definitions so “improvement” is measurable  
- Explicit labelling of assumptions  

Market context (e.g. industry benchmark studies on cost-per-invoice or STP) belongs in our Research Ledger with sources — not as your personal results.
