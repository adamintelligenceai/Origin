## 10. FAQs (canonical)

**Is this software?**  
No. It is an implementation-ready digital toolkit and, for Blueprint, a productised design service. It does not replace your ERP or AP automation stack.

**Will it pay invoices automatically?**  
No. Payment authorisation stays human. Agents may observe, recommend, or prepare within guardrails — never authorise payment by default.

**Is this just ChatGPT prompts?**  
No. Prompts, if present, are secondary. The core is agent charters, governance, exception taxonomy, KPIs, and methodology.

**Which ERPs does it support?**  
It is ERP-agnostic by design — useful across Dynamics, SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Workday and peers. Blueprint can fit entity- and ERP-specific constraints.

**Do you guarantee savings or ROI?**  
No. We provide measurement design and cite market context from named sources in the Research Ledger. Your results depend on your process, data, and controls.

**Who is it for?**  
Controllers, Heads of AP, Shared Services leaders, and Finance Directors in complex, ERP-backed organisations (roughly 200–10,000+ employees; hundreds to tens of thousands of invoices/month).

**What do I get immediately after purchase?**  
Downloadable package (PDF/Markdown/Excel as applicable) plus onboarding email. Team and Blueprint include additional mobilisation or delivery steps.

**Can consultants use this with clients?**  
Only under the licence terms that apply to your purchase. Client resale or redistribution of the files as a product is not permitted. Contact us for multi-client or advisory licensing if needed.

**What is the preferred website?**  
`evidenceroom.ai` is the preferred domain — **availability needs verification.**
