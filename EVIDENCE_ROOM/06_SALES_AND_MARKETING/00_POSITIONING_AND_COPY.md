## Value proposition

Finance teams are deploying AI faster than they can prove it. Deloitte’s Finance Trends 2026 release (8 October 2025) found that 63% of finance teams have fully deployed and actively use AI, while only 21% report clear, measurable ROI, and 14% are using fully integrated AI agents. The gap is not another capture tool. The gap is an operating system: named jobs, evidence packets, autonomy that starts at observe, and a written rule for what a model is never allowed to decide.

Evidence Room sells that operating system. AP Agent OS is the design for a 16-agent stack, the governance that binds it, the controls that sit on it, the KPIs that may not be gamed, and the method that turns a live AP operation into a governed workflow. You keep SAP, Oracle, D365, NetSuite, Coupa, Tipalti, or the mix you have. You stop granting permission in a kickoff deck.

## Outcomes (honest)

What a competent team *can* use the OS to do:

1. **Name the work.** Every agent has an owner, a job, and a forbidden list.  
2. **Start without theatre.** First slice at L0. Measure precision, miss rate, and exception cost on *your* invoices.  
3. **Promote only with a file.** Evidence pack, two signatures for L3+, sampling plan, rollback.  
4. **Keep cash human.** Agent 12 reviews payment proposals. A human authorises the run.  
5. **Measure what matters.** First-pass yield, touchless *with evidence*, cycle time, exception ageing, unit cost, evidence completeness — from your ledger.  
6. **Survive vendor downtime.** Fallback is the SOP without the agent lane. The agent is not the system of record.  
7. **Speak to audit.** Packets, hashes, overrides, and promotion files exist as a designed trail.

What we do **not** promise as an outcome: savings, ROI, fraud catch, compliance, accounting accuracy, or payment safety.

Market context you may place *near* outcomes, never as *your* outcomes:

- APQC (16 March 2026): top-performing organisations spend about $0.38 per $1,000 revenue to process AP versus about $0.92 for bottom performers. Independent. A spread exists. We do not claim to close it.  
- The Hackett Group (19 November 2025): customers of evaluated AP solutions averaged 60% touchless invoice processing; companies with 30%+ touchless had 3.5× higher AP productivity; average cycle times improved 59% post-implementation. **Software-adopter research, not Evidence Room results.**

## FAQs

**What is Evidence Room?**  
The company. We publish AP Agent OS: the operating system for building, governing and scaling AI agents across Accounts Payable.

**What is AP Agent OS?**  
A complete operating design: 16 agent charters, autonomy policy, governance, controls, KPI dictionary, and the method to implement them. ERP-agnostic.

**Does this replace our AP software?**  
No. It designs the agent layer that operates across the stack you already have.

**Will this make AP autonomous?**  
No. Full autonomy is not the default. Agents start at L0 Observe and earn each level with evidence. Payment release stays human.

**Do you guarantee savings or ROI?**  
No. Deloitte (8 October 2025) reports that only 21% of finance teams who have deployed AI claim clear, measurable ROI. We give you a measurement standard. We do not invent your result.

**Is this a prompt pack?**  
No. Each agent file is a charter. The instruction skeleton is labelled as a starting operating instruction, not a magic prompt.

**Which ERPs?**  
Designed for D365, SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Workday, or a mix. You map field names. You do not copy another company’s workflow.

**Can a consultancy use this with clients?**  
Not under Professional. Team is one legal entity. Multi-client use needs a written licence. Email licence@evidenceroom.ai.

**What stays human?**  
Payment release, vendor bank-change approval, policy exceptions, and legal disputes — at every autonomy level.

**How do you use the research numbers on the site?**  
APQC and Deloitte as independent market context. Hackett only with the sentence: software-adopter research, not Evidence Room results. None of those figures are product targets.

**What is the diagnostic?**  
A free AP Agent Readiness assessment. It scores operating design, not your invoices. Primary CTA on the site.

**Refunds?**  
14-day fitness refund on Starter and Professional as recommended above, subject to legal review.
