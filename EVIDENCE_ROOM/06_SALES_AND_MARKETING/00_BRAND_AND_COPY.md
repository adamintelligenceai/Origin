# Brand idea

**Responsibility is earned.**

Supporting line: Evidence Room turns Finance AI from experimentation into governed operating performance.

Product: **AP Agent OS** — the operating system for building, governing and scaling AI agents across Accounts Payable.

# Alternatives evaluated

See refined master prompt. Rejected as primary: “Evidence over hype” (kept as supporting), “From workflow to workforce” (replacement risk), “Governed agents. Measurable outcomes” (generic).

# Voice

Intelligent, concise, specific, executive. Prefer “charter”, “evidence”, “owner”, “level” over “unlock”, “supercharge”, “revolutionise”.

# Homepage in five seconds

**What:** An operating system for AP agents.  
**Who:** Finance and AP leaders in complex, ERP-based operations.  
**Improves:** How you design, govern and measure agents — not how you print cheques.  
**Different:** It does not replace the AP stack. It designs the agent layer across it.

# Headlines

**Main:** Responsibility is earned.  
**Sub:** The operating system for building, governing and scaling AI agents across Accounts Payable.  
**Alt hero:** Buy Friday. Map Monday. Do not turn payment loose.  
**Value proposition:** Evidence Room gives Finance a method, a 16-agent architecture, an exception taxonomy, and the control and KPI system required to put agents to work without losing accountability.

# Comparison vs prompt packs

Prompt packs sell sentences. This sells an operating model: owners, exclusions, evidence, promotion gates, and the artefacts a controller will read.

# FAQs

**Is this software?** No. It is a commercial toolkit and, optionally, a productised assessment.

**Will it work on SAP / Oracle / Dynamics / NetSuite / Workday?** The method is ERP-agnostic. Agents usually run inside your existing stack or copilot. We do not require a single ERP.

**Does it pay invoices?** No. Humans authorise payments.

**Do you guarantee savings or fraud detection?** No.

**Can we resell the templates?** No. See licence.

**What do I get immediately?** A download pack (PDF/XLSX/PPTX/DOCX) and a START HERE path.

**Refunds?** Recommended: 14 days if files are unopened / unused, at operator discretion. Confirm with counsel and Lemon Squeezy policy.

**Privacy?** Do not upload production invoices to Evidence Room. The diagnostic does not require personal supplier bank data.

| We have Copilot | Copilot is a tool. This is the job architecture and controls. |

# Disclaimers (customer-facing)

Not legal, tax, accounting, audit or investment advice. Not a guarantee of outcomes. Not an autonomous payment system. Statistics are cited in the Research Ledger and are not your baseline.

# AI-use disclosure

Evidence Room materials were authored as an original product. Customers using AI tools with these templates must apply their own privacy and model-risk rules. Invoice content is untrusted data.

# Implementation limitations

Results depend on data quality, access, controls, integrations, and whether humans actually review work. A 4–6 week first agent is illustrative for a bounded case.
