# Marketplace Derivatives (Etsy-style Acquisition)

**Evidence Room** · Acquisition SKUs that must **not** cannibalise core

## Principle

Sell small, self-contained artefacts that introduce the method and route serious buyers to Free kit → Starter ($79) → Custom. Do **not** upload the full Starter/Pro libraries to low-trust marketplaces.

## Allowed derivative SKUs

| SKU | Price band | Contents | Funnel job |
|---|---|---|---|
| ER-MKT-SCORE | $9–$19 | Printable scorecard + scoring sticker sheet | Awareness → email for full Free kit |
| ER-MKT-GATES | $12–$19 | Four hard gates one-pager + checklist | Controls-minded buyers |
| ER-MKT-RACI | $15–$25 | Human vs agent RACI poster (PDF) | Shareable office artefact |
| ER-MKT-LADDER | $15–$25 | Responsibility ladder wall chart | Brand language seeding |
| ER-MKT-KPI | $19–$29 | Baseline KPI worksheet (lite) | Lead into full kit via QR |
| ER-MKT-CHARTER | $19–$29 | Blank agent charter template (1 agent) | Upsell Starter for ten blueprints |

## Disallowed on marketplaces

- Full `02_AP_AGENT_STARTER` pack
- Pro / Team libraries
- Custom client packs
- Anything implying guaranteed savings or fraud prevention
- Trademark-infringing “Ardent” branded products (cite in text only when educational)

## Listing copy rules

- Title pattern: `AP Agent [Artefact] — Evidence Room`
- First line of description: *Agents that earn responsibility.*
- Include link/QR to evidenceroom.[domain]/readiness
- Palette in mockups: ink `#0B1F2A`, paper `#F7F4EE`, amber `#C47A2C`, teal `#1F5C5C`
- One clear sentence: “Not a substitute for AP Agent Starter or Custom Blueprint.”

## Cannibalisation guardrails

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Marketplace SKU >40% of Starter content | Remove or shrink |
| Support tickets asking why Starter differs | Clarify listings; add comparison chart |
| Price of derivative ≥ $79 | Reprice down or pull |

## Operations

- Separate marketplace store email
- Watermark PDFs lightly with Evidence Room
- Quarterly review: attach UTM `utm_source=marketplace`
