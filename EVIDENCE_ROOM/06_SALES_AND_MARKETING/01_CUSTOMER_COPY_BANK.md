## FAQs

**What is Evidence Room?**  
A product studio for AP agent operating systems — readiness diagnostics, blueprints, and evidence standards.

**Is this software that posts invoices for us?**  
No. You receive methods and designs. Your systems and controls remain yours.

**Will this guarantee lower cost per invoice?**  
No. We help you measure and pilot. Ardent Partners 2025 average cost/invoice **$9.84** is industry context only.

**Can agents release payments?**  
Not in Starter designs. Payment readiness may be proposed; release stays human unless a separately controlled Custom/Pro design with dual control says otherwise.

**How is this different from ChatGPT prompts?**  
Prompts are text. We provide charters, stages, hard gates, RACI, and evidence requirements.

**Do you claim to stop fraud?**  
No. Duplicate Payment Guard flags suspects for investigation. No fraud-certainty marketing.

**What is Custom Blueprint?**  
A fixed-fee ($1,500–$3,000) engagement delivering tailored agent blueprints and a walkthrough.
