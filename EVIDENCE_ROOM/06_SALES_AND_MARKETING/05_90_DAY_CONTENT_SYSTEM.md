# Evidence Room — 90-Day Content System

**House:** Evidence Room  
**Channel:** LinkedIn first. Site second. Email only for people who asked.  
**Mix:** **80% insight / 20% invitation** (not 20% discount codes).  
**Voice:** Intelligent, concise, credible, executive, specific, non-hype.  
**Citations:** Research ledger only.  
**Date:** 20 September 2026 — Day 1 is the week evidenceroom.ai goes live.

This system feeds `LinkedIn/30_POSTS.md`, `Carousels/10_CAROUSELS.md`, `Articles/05_LONGFORM.md`, and `Charts/10_EXECUTIVE_CHARTS.md`. Do not invent a second editorial calendar in a spreadsheet that contradicts this file.

---

## 1. Pillars (five)

Every public piece maps to one pillar. If it maps to none, it is noise.

| ID | Pillar | Question it answers | Typical form |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Permission** | What may an agent decide, and only after what file? | Posts, carousel 01–03 |
| P2 | **Workforce** | What are the sixteen jobs, and who owns them? | Posts, carousel 04–05 |
| P3 | **Measurement** | What may appear on a steering pack? | Posts, charts, article 02 |
| P4 | **Control** | Injection, hallucination, SoD, kill-switch, fallback | Posts, carousel 06–07, article 03 |
| P5 | **Category** | OS vs software vs prompt pack; market facts | Posts, article 01, 04, 05 |

**Invitation pieces (the 20%)** still teach. They end with Diagnostic or the OS page. They do not open with a price.

## 4. Insight vs invitation (the 20%)

Invitation slots in this plan: Week 6 Thu, Week 9 Thu, Week 12 Thu, Week 13 Thu, plus the closing line of each article.

**Invitation copy pattern (do not deviate into launch-spam):**

> If you need a score before anyone grants a write verb: evidenceroom.ai/diagnostic  
> The OS is a licensed instrument, not a prompt pack.

No “only until Friday.” A stated launch offer, if any, lives on the store page and in *one* post during the offer window — still with a teaching paragraph first.

- [ ] No banned promise  
- [ ] No banned aesthetic

## 7. What we do not add in 90 days

- A second social network  
- A podcast  
- A weekly newsletter beyond the 7-email sequence  
- Guest posts that require us to praise a capture vendor  
- “Day in the life of an AI agent” fiction  
- Other verticals (AR, expense, payroll)
