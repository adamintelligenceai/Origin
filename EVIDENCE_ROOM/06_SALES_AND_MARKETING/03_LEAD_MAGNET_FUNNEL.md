## 2. LinkedIn as the top of funnel

**Who we speak to:** CFO, Controller, Head of AP, Shared Services, Control, Finance Systems, transformation leads who refuse prompt packs.

**What a post must do:** teach one operating rule, cite ledger research only when used, end with a calm invitation — not “link in comments 👇🔥”.

**Allowed endings**

- “If you need a score before you grant write verbs: evidenceroom.ai/diagnostic”  
- “The OS is published. The default level is L0.”  
- No ending. Some posts are complete.

**Not allowed**

- Fake urgency (“only 11 kits left”).  
- “Comment READY for the pack.”  
- Ungated PDFs that *are* the Professional library.  
- Invented customer results.

Use the 30 posts and 10 carousels in this folder. 80% insight, 20% invitation. The 90-day system holds the calendar.

## 3. The magnet (diagnostic)

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Name | AP Agent Readiness Diagnostic |
| Price | $0 |
| Time | ~12 minutes, 18 items |
| Data | Name, work email, role, organisation, country, answers |
| Deliverable | Score in four bands + recommended first slice + Controller one-pager |
| Explicit refusal | Production invoices, supplier personal data, bank details |

**Why this magnet, not an ebook.** An ebook is unread. A score is forwardable. The Controller can disagree with the score; that disagreement is useful. A “50 prompts for AP” magnet would train the wrong buyer and cannibalise nothing of value.

**Scoring (do not hide the logic):**

| Band | What it measures | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owners | Named humans vs mailboxes / vendors | Missing | Partial | Named + deputy |
| Evidence | Source file, IDs, packet habit | Posted invoice only | Some IDs | File + hash habit / IDs |
| Measurement | Baseline for yield and cycle time | None | Informal | Frozen slice baseline |
| Permission | Human classes + identity + fallback written | Model would use a human login | Partial policy | Written human classes + named identity + fallback |

Band scores 0–8. Publish the method on `/diagnostic`. Do not gamify with badges that say “Autonomous-ready.”

**Recommended first-slice rule (in the PDF):**  
If Permission < 4: stay at policy and owners. Do not sell them L2.  
If Permission ≥ 4 and Owners ≥ 4: suggest 01/02/03/10/16 at L0.  
If they asked for payment automation in free text: HOLD paragraph — Agent 12 reviews; humans release.

## 4. Email capture

**Form fields:** First name · Work email · Role (select) · Organisation · Country · “I want the score and the short sequence. I can unsubscribe at any time.”

**Role select:** CFO / Finance Director · Controller · Head of AP · AP Manager · Control / Audit · Finance Systems · Transformation · Other.

**Validation:** work-like email preferred; do not reject personal email (some practitioners buy Individual from a personal address) but copy should ask for work email.

**Double opt-in:** recommended in the EU/UK. **Flag for lawyer.** Until counsel decides, use single opt-in with a one-click unsubscribe and a first email that restates why they hear from us.

**Sync:** email tool + Lemon Squeezy lead magnet feature if used. Lemon Squeezy supports lead magnets on its pricing page (retrieved 20 September 2026). Confirm at launch. Do not store card data for a free product.

## 6. Professional → Team

**Trigger to invite Team**

- They bought Professional and emailed “can I share with Control?”  
- Diagnostic role mix shows AP + Control + FinSys from the same domain.  
- Email 6.

**How:** licence@evidenceroom.ai with a credit policy: if Professional was bought within 30 days, the $199 can be credited toward Team $499. Write this on the Team page so it is not a secret. **Flag for accountant** (revenue recognition of the credit).

Do not auto-upgrade. Team is a consent to name seats.

## 7. Team → Custom Blueprint

**Trigger**

- Multi-entity, multi-ERP, or “draw this on our DoA.”  
- They ask for implementation in the ERP.  
- They refuse self-serve because Internal Audit wants a named designer.

**How:** application form, not a checkout link. Floor US$1,500–3,000. We refuse “autonomous payments” as the success criterion.
