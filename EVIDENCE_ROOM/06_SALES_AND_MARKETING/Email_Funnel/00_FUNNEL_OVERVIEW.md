# Email Funnel Overview

**Evidence Room** · Free kit → Starter → Custom

## Goal

Help AP leaders complete readiness, understand the method, and choose Starter or Custom — without spam tactics.

## Entry points

| Source | Opt-in promise |
|---|---|
| Readiness landing | Free AP AI Readiness Kit delivery |
| Webinar / briefing | Kit + method one-pager |
| LinkedIn lead form | Same kit |

## Sequence map (7 emails)

| # | Day | Theme | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Deliver kit + how to score | Open diagnostic |
| 2 | 2 | Hard gates & why they matter | Score controls dimension |
| 3 | 4 | Heatmap: pick two pilots | Complete heatmap |
| 4 | 7 | KPI baseline vs industry context | Fill KPI worksheet |
| 5 | 10 | Responsibility ladder story | Read method |
| 6 | 14 | Starter walkthrough ($79) | Buy Starter |
| 7 | 21 | Custom vs DIY decision | Intake or wait |

## Compliance

- Explicit opt-in; easy unsubscribe every mail
- Identity: Evidence Room, physical/contact identity per regs
- No fake scarcity (“only 2 licences left”)
- No guaranteed savings language
- Ardent figures only when educational and attributed

## Segments (simple)

| Segment | Rule | Path |
|---|---|---|
| A | Downloaded, not purchased by day 14 | Emphasise Starter |
| B | Clicked Custom | Emphasise intake |
| C | Unengaged after em3 | Soft break; quarterly briefing only |

## Metrics

Delivery, open, click, kit completion survey (optional), Starter conversion, Custom inquiries — not vanity “AI engagement scores.”
