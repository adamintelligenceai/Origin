# Evidence Room — 10 Executive Chart Concepts

**Document ID:** `06_SALES_AND_MARKETING/Content_Engine/05_10_EXECUTIVE_CHARTS`  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Visual:** Measure `#1F4E79` primary; Ink muted comparisons; zero baseline; source footnotes when using ledger data.  
**Rule:** Scenario charts labelled **NON-EVIDENCE / ILLUSTRATIVE**.

---

## Chart 1 — Responsibility ladder adoption

**Type:** Horizontal step / stacked bar by agent count at each level  
**Caption:** “Most early programmes should show mass at L0–L2. A pyramid heavy at L3/L4 is a control smell, not a maturity badge.”  
**Use:** Carousel cover, LinkedIn, Method page  
**Data note:** Illustrative distribution only unless plotting a real (scrubbed) estate.

---

## Chart 2 — Exception class mix

**Type:** 100% stacked bar or treemap  
**Caption:** “If ‘Other’ dominates, you do not have an exception OS — you have a junk drawer.”  
**Use:** Taxonomy posts  
**Data note:** Use anonymised internal sample or clearly synthetic mix.

---

## Chart 3 — Shadow disagreement rate

**Type:** Line over pilot weeks  
**Caption:** “Shadow mode tracks where agent recommendations disagree with human decisions. Falling disagreement with stable quality is evidence; silence is not.”  
**Use:** Testing / UAT narrative  
**Data note:** ILLUSTRATIVE.

---

## Chart 4 — Cycle time baseline vs pilot corridor

**Type:** Dual bar before/after for one corridor only  
**Caption:** “Measure one corridor. Do not average the whole AP estate into a vanity ‘AI win.’”  
**Use:** KPI article  
**Data note:** Requires buyer data; template empty state in product.

---

## Chart 5 — Touch types: straight-through vs handled vs exception

**Type:** Stacked bar  
**Caption:** “Agents should change the shape of work — not hide exceptions in a new queue name.”  
**Use:** Executive briefing  
**Data note:** Define STP explicitly in footnote.

---

## Chart 6 — Review tax vs automation minutes

**Type:** Two-series bar  
**Caption:** “Minutes saved in posting can reappear as review tax. Cost-to-serve includes both.”  
**Use:** Economics email / Article 4  
**Data note:** NON-EVIDENCE scenario.

---

## Chart 7 — Control gate pass rate by agent

**Type:** Heat table (agent × gate)  
**Caption:** “Expansion is a controlled event. Gates are binary on purpose.”  
**Use:** Governance pack preview  
**Data note:** Show framework, not fake compliance scores.

---

## Chart 8 — Owner load (agents per human owner)

**Type:** Bar by role  
**Caption:** “If one AP manager ‘owns’ sixteen agents, ownership is fictional.”  
**Use:** Team mobilisation  
**Data note:** Illustrative span-of-control warning.

---

## Chart 9 — Preventable exceptions trend

**Type:** Line + annotation for PO Quality agent introduction  
**Caption:** “Upstream charters should move preventable exceptions — if they don’t, fix the charter before adding models.”  
**Use:** Root cause narrative  
**Data note:** ILLUSTRATIVE annotation.

---

## Chart 10 — Product ladder revenue mix (internal)

**Type:** Waterfall or stacked area (internal dashboards only)  
**Caption:** “Commercial health: Diagnostic volume → Professional core → Team expansion → Blueprint services. Do not publish vanity GMV without context.”  
**Use:** Internal GTM reviews — **not** customer marketing unless scrubbed and approved  
**Data note:** Planning model; see `00_READ_ME/03_FINANCIAL_MODEL.md` (internal only).

---

### Caption style rules
- One sentence insight + one sentence implication.  
- Footnote sources or ILLUSTRATIVE label.  
- No 3D, no neon, no “AI brain” pictograms.
