**Post 1 full:**  
Ardent Partners’ 2025 ePayables study (204 AP organisations) puts “approvals take too long” at 49% of top challenges. Before you add an approval copilot, publish the stall report: whose queue, how old, what value, whether a delegate exists. Agents are useful here at Level 1. They should not approve.

**Post 2 full:**  
18.4% exception rate is the average in that same study. If your codes are free text, you cannot run a root-cause agent. Start with ten codes. Missing PO, price, qty, missing GR, duplicate, tax, approval, coding, aged, system.

**Post 3 full:**  
Straight-through processing at 35.4% average vs 51% Best-in-Class. The gap is usually PO discipline, supplier enablement and GR — not a missing prompt. Map those three before you staff an orchestrator.

**Post 4 full:**  
$9.84 average all-in cost per invoice. $2.65 Best-in-Class. $12.42 everyone else. Use these as orientation. Your number needs your labour and your volume. I built a conservative model that is allowed to say “don’t spend yet”.

**Post 5 full:**  
44% of AP teams already use some form of AI. The failure mode I see is unchartered use: someone pastes invoices into a consumer tool. Write the approved-model list first. Then write the agent.
