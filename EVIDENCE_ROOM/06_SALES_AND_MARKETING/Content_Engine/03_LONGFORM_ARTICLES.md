## Article 2 — The Four Hard Gates Before Money-Path Agents

### Full introduction

If an agent can influence payment or vendor remittance data, four controls questions are not optional: segregation of duties, dual control on bank changes, reconstructable audit trails, and vendor payment-field quality.

Fail any gate and the correct stage is Observe or Draft — not Bounded act. This article turns those gates into a practical stop/go test you can run in a single working session with AP, Controls, and IT.
