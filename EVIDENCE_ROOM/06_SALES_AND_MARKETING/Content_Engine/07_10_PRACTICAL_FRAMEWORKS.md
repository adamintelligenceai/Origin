# Evidence Room — 10 Practical Frameworks

**Document ID:** `06_SALES_AND_MARKETING/Content_Engine/07_10_PRACTICAL_FRAMEWORKS`  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Use:** LinkedIn frameworks, workshop one-pagers, Resources hub.  
**Each framework:** Name · When to use · Steps/grid · Failure mode · CTA (optional)

---

## Framework 1 — Corridor Selection Grid

**When:** Choosing the first pilot slice  
**Grid axes:** Volume × Exception complexity × Reversibility × Control sensitivity  
**Rule:** Pick high volume, medium complexity, high reversibility, manageable control sensitivity.  
**Failure mode:** Starting on payment-critical, low-reversibility corridors.  
**One-liner:** Narrow corridor beats heroic estate-wide launch.

---

## Framework 2 — Charter One-Pager

**Sections:** Job · Inputs · Tools/Data · Responsibilities · Exclusions · Owner · Escalation · Output standard · Starting L-level · KPIs  
**Failure mode:** Responsibilities without exclusions.  
**One-liner:** Exclusions are the control.

---

## Framework 3 — Responsibility Gate Card

**Columns:** Proposed L-level · Evidence required · Approver · Expiry/review date  
**Failure mode:** Permanent L3 with no review date.  
**One-liner:** Levels are controlled events, not vibes.

---

## Framework 4 — Exception Class Tree

**Top nodes:** Match · Master data · Policy · Supplier · Duplicate/anomaly · Process lag · Fraud-suspect · Other (time-boxed)  
**Rule:** “Other” requires weekly burn-down.  
**Failure mode:** Infinite Other.  
**One-liner:** Taxonomy before triage automation.

---

## Framework 5 — Evidence Pack Checklist

**Minimum artefacts:** Charter version · Decision log sample · Exception tags · KPI snapshot · Change log · Access list  
**Failure mode:** Screenshots without owners.  
**One-liner:** If Audit asked tomorrow, what folder do you open?

---

## Framework 6 — Shadow Scorecard

**Metrics:** Agreement rate · Material disagreement count · Time-to-review · False positive burden · Safety incidents (should be zero)  
**Gate:** Pre-agreed thresholds before L-level increase.  
**Failure mode:** Shadow with no scoring.  
**One-liner:** Shadow is a measurement system.

---

## Framework 7 — RACI for Agent Change

**Rows:** Scope change · Prompt/tool change · L-level change · Incident response  
**Roles:** AP Owner · Controller · IT/Automation · Audit (consulted)  
**Failure mode:** Engineers changing scope silently.  
**One-liner:** Who can change the agent is a control question.

---

## Framework 8 — Cost-to-Serve Sketch

**Inputs:** Model/API cost · Human review minutes · Exception spillover · Rework  
**Output:** Cost per successful assisted transaction (define success)  
**Failure mode:** Counting only “minutes saved.”  
**One-liner:** Review tax is real money.

---

## Framework 9 — Escalation Ladder

**Lanes:** Agent → AP Specialist → AP Manager → Controller (as needed)  
**Timers:** Class-based SLAs  
**Failure mode:** Everything escalates to the same hero.  
**One-liner:** Escalation design beats heroic inboxes.

---

## Framework 10 — Executive Brief Spine

**One page:** Scope · Exclusions · L-levels · KPIs · Ask · Non-negotiable (human payments)  
**Failure mode:** 40-slide “vision.”  
**One-liner:** One page that can survive the CFO’s calendar.
