# Evidence Room — Marketplace Expansion (Non-Cannibalising)

**Document ID:** `06_SALES_AND_MARKETING/Content_Engine/08_MARKETPLACE_EXPANSION`  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Optional derivative products (e.g. Etsy, Gumroad-adjacent marketplaces) that **feed** the core funnel without undercutting Professional/Team.  
**Preferred brand domain:** evidenceroom.ai — **verify availability** before printing on marketplace creatives.

---

## 1. Strategy thesis

Marketplaces are for **awareness wedges** and **low-price taste tests** — not for selling the full AP Agent OS.

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| Sell narrow printables / one-pagers | Sell the full 16-agent OS or Team pack |
| Price US$9–29 | Discount Professional to marketplace norms |
| Drive traffic to Diagnostic / Pro | Train buyers that US$199 should be US$19 |
| Use “Evidence Room” + Operating Evidence | Confuse brand with generic “AI prompts” shops |
| IP-clean, original frameworks | Scrape employer/client artefacts |

---

## 2. Cannibalisation rules (hard)

1. **No full product mirrors** of Starter / Professional / Team / Blueprint on Etsy-like markets.  
2. **No licence that allows redistribution** of core OS files.  
3. **No “almost Professional” bundles** within 40% of Pro content coverage.  
4. Every marketplace SKU includes a tasteful link/QR to the free Diagnostic or Method page.  
5. If a SKU starts stealing Pro conversions in analytics, **raise price or retire** — do not expand the SKU.

---

## 3. Approved derivative SKU concepts

| SKU | Price band | Contents | Funnel job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AP Agent Charter One-Pager (printable)** | US$9–12 | Blank charter template + example (non-confidential) | Taste + Diagnostic |
| **L0–L4 Wall Card** | US$9 | Responsibility ladder poster (PDF/PNG) | Brand mnemonic |
| **Exception Taxonomy Starter Sheet** | US$15–19 | 1-page class list + mapping worksheet | Lead into Pro taxonomy |
| **Friday→Monday Checklist** | US$12 | One-corridor pilot checklist | Soft Pro upsell |
| **Executive Brief Template** | US$19 | 1-page CFO brief spine | Authority + Pro |
| **Shadow Mode Scorecard (lite)** | US$19–29 | Simple scoring sheet (not full UAT pack) | Pro bridge |
| **“Not a Prompt Pack” explainer PDF** | US$9 | Category education | Positioning |

**Explicitly excluded from marketplaces:** full agent library, governance framework pack, Team workshop, Blueprint deliverables, Excel control matrices from Pro.

---

## 4. Listing copy pattern (Etsy-style)

**Title pattern:**  
`AP Agent Charter Template | Finance Operating Evidence | Printable PDF | Not a Prompt Pack`

**Description must include:**
- What it is / isn’t  
- “Complement to Evidence Room AP Agent OS — full OS sold separately at evidenceroom.ai (domain pending verification)”  
- Licence: personal/internal business use; no resale  
- Disclaimer: not software; not accounting/legal advice; no autonomous payments  

**Tags (examples):** accounts payable, finance template, AI governance, controller, shared services — avoid misleading “ChatGPT prompts” as primary tag unless the SKU truly is prompt-adjacent (prefer not).

---

## 5. Packaging & brand

- Covers per Brand System (Ink/Paper/Measure).  
- No robot/brain clichés (Etsy AI aesthetic pressure — resist).  
- Watermark footer: © Evidence Room · Operating Evidence  
- File delivery: PDF download; no customer PII required beyond marketplace norms.

---

## 6. Metrics

| Metric | Healthy signal |
|---|---|
| Marketplace → Diagnostic CTR | Rising |
| Marketplace buyers later buying Pro | Some conversion within 60 days |
| Pro conversion rate overall | Stable or up |
| Support tickets “I thought Etsy was the full OS” | Near zero — fix copy if not |

---

## 7. Decision log (launch gate)

Before publishing any marketplace SKU:

- [ ] IP cleanliness checklist passed  
- [ ] Cannibalisation rules reviewed  
- [ ] Price ≥ value floor (not race-to-bottom)  
- [ ] CTA to owned funnel included  
- [ ] Licence + disclaimer included  
- [ ] Counsel/brand review if using trademarks externally
