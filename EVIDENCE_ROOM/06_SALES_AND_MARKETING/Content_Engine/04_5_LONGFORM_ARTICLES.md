## Article 3 — Exception Taxonomy as Operating System

**Working title:** Stop Managing an Exception Pile — Build an Exception OS  
**Primary CTA:** Free Diagnostic  
**Pillar:** P3

### H2 outline & key points

1. **The “Other” trap**  
   - Unclassifiable work cannot be improved or taught to agents.

2. **Design principles for a taxonomy**  
   - Mutual exclusivity where possible; business meaning; agent-consumable tags.

3. **Core class families (illustrative)**  
   - Price/qty/GR/master data/policy/duplicate/fraud-suspect/supplier — labels as design aid, not universal standard.

4. **Triage agent behaviour**

6. **Evidence for Audit**  
   - Why taxonomy logs beat inbox archaeology.

7. **Implementation sequence**
