**Channel focus:** LinkedIn primary; site resources secondary; email supports funnel  
**Brand idea:** Operating Evidence

---

## 1. Objective (90 days)

1. Establish Evidence Room as the category owner of the **AP agent operating layer**.  
2. Drive qualified Diagnostic opt-ins.  
3. Convert a share of engaged readers to **Professional (US$199)** without hype.  
4. Build a reusable library of frameworks, carousels, and longform for ongoing nurture.

## 3. Cadence

| Format | Frequency | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn text posts | 4–5 / week | From `02_30_LINKEDIN_POSTS.md` then evergreen recycle |
| Carousels | 1 / week | From `03_10_CAROUSELS.md` |
| Longform (LinkedIn article or site) | 1 / 2 weeks | From `04_5_LONGFORM_ARTICLES.md` |
| Executive chart posts | 1 / week (alternate) | From `05_10_EXECUTIVE_CHARTS.md` |
| Diagnostic CTAs | 1 pinned + soft every 10–14 days | From `06_10_DIAGNOSTIC_POSTS.md` |
| Framework posts | 1 / week | From `07_10_PRACTICAL_FRAMEWORKS.md` |

**Weekly minimum viable:** 3 text posts + 1 carousel OR chart + 1 soft CTA.

---

## 4. The 80/20 rule

**80% teach / 20% invite.**

- 80% of posts: pure operating insight, frameworks, misconceptions, method steps — **no product pitch** in the body. Soft mention allowed in last line occasionally.  
- 20% of posts: explicit Diagnostic or product CTA (still non-spammy).

If engagement drops, raise teaching share — do not raise pitch frequency.

---

## 5. 90-day phases

### Days 1–30 — Authority install
- Publish first 15 LinkedIn posts + 4 carousels + 2 longform outlines as articles  
- Pin Diagnostic  
- Start email sequence for new leads  
- Goal: consistent voice; 1–2 thoughtful comment threads per post

### Days 31–60 — Conversion engine
- Complete posts 16–30; rotate charts & frameworks  
- Add Professional comparison post monthly  
- Collect FAQs from comments → update site FAQ  
- Goal: steady Diagnostic weekly volume; first paid conversions

### Days 61–90 — Expansion narrative
- Team / Blueprint stories (process, not confidential client detail)  
- Marketplace-safe derivative teasers only if IP-clean (`08_MARKETPLACE_EXPANSION.md`)  
- Repackage top 10 posts into a Resources hub  
- Goal: Team inquiries; refine creative based on saves/comments

---

## 6. Production workflow

1. **Draft** in brand voice (Banned language list enforced).  
2. **Evidence check** — any market number → Research Ledger ID or delete.  
3. **Design** — Brand System tokens; institutional covers; no purple AI kitsch.  
4. **Schedule** — batch Sunday; publish Tue–Thu peak for finance audiences (test).  
5. **Engage** — 30 minutes post-publish for replies.  
6. **Log** — what resonated → feed next month’s calendar.
