## Carousel 09 — Packet standard (held)

**Slide 1**  
# A hand-off is a packet.

**Slide 2**  
Required  
`case_id`  
document keys  
legal entity  
`agent_from` / `agent_to`

**Slide 3**  
`autonomy_level_applied`  
is the level used on *this* case.  
Not the agent’s ceiling.

**Slide 4**  
`decision`  
observe · recommend · prepare · execute · hold · escalate

**Slide 5**  
`evidence_refs`  
Document IDs. ERP IDs. Email IDs. Hash of source.  
Empty refs → reject to sender.

**Slide 6**  
`human_required` + reason  
`next_action` — one verb, one owner, one time  
`cost_envelope` — tokens + human minutes

**Slide 7**  
No agent changes another agent’s decision.  
It can reject the packet.

**Slide 8**  
Chat is not a control.  
The packet is.
