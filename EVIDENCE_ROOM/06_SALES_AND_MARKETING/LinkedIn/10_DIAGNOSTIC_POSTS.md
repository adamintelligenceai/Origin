# 10 diagnostic posts

Each post is a single question a Finance leader can answer in the comments or privately. 80% useful. Soft CTA: the free diagnostic.

1. **Volume honesty.** How many invoices did you actually process last month — all entities, all channels — and how long did it take someone to get that number?

2. **Exception rate.** If you cannot state your exception rate for the last 90 days, you do not have an AI problem yet. You have a measurement problem.

3. **STP.** What share of invoices posted last month with no human exception touch? If the answer is “our vendor says we’re touchless,” ask for the denominator.

4. **Missing GR.** How many three-way invoices are waiting on a receipt right now, and who owns the oldest ten?

5. **Duplicates.** How many confirmed duplicate *payments* in the last 12 months — not duplicate invoices caught in time?

6. **Bank change.** Walk the last vendor bank-detail change. Was verification out-of-band, or in the same email thread that requested it?

7. **DoA.** Is delegation of authority a system control or a spreadsheet with a “current” in the filename?

8. **Statements.** How many strategic vendors are reconciled to statement, and what do you do with a statement line that has no invoice?

9. **SoD.** Can the same person prepare and release a payment proposal in practice, not in the policy?

10. **AI today.** Which AP task already uses a model — and at what responsibility level, written down?

CTA (optional, not on every post): The AP AI Readiness Diagnostic is 35 questions. Unknown is a valid answer.
