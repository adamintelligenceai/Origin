# 10 practical frameworks (shareable)

1. **Responsibility ladder** — L0 Observe → L4 Managed autonomy. Promotion is a management decision with a four-week evidence window.

2. **Human / Agent / Deterministic** — Maths and keys are services. Classification and drafts are agents. Money and master-data writes are human.

3. **Risk before amount** — EX-BK-001, EX-DU-001, EX-AP-002 outrank invoice value in the triage sort.

4. **Untrusted document rule** — Invoice, email, portal, statement never become the agent’s policy.

5. **Copy-from-source** — Amounts, tax, vendor IDs and bank accounts are copied from fields or deterministic services, never generated.

6. **Two-key duplicate** — Exact key is a rule. Near key is an indicator. Language is “review required,” never “fraud.”

7. **GR owner order** — Receiver → requester → PO creator → cost-centre owner. Third touch is a manager, not a third identical mail.

8. **SoD triangle** — Preparer ≠ A12 reviewer identity ≠ payment releaser.

9. **FIN-06 rule** — No dollar benefit in a steer pack without Controller attestation. Capacity is not cash.

10. **Kill criteria** — Unauthorised send, dummy GR, payment-adjacent write, SoD break, planted exact-duplicate miss → Level 0 the same day.
