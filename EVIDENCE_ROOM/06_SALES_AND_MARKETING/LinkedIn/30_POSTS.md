## Post 13 — P3 Measurement  
**Type:** Insight

Four KPI families. Never one “AI score.”

Activity — volume. May not justify promotion.  
Operational — yield, cycle time, ageing, vs *your* baseline.  
Financial — inference cost and Controller-signed benefit only.  
Risk-control — breaches, overrides, misses. **Veto.**

If risk-control fails, the agent does not go up a level. No matter how busy it was.
