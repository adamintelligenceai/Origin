**System:** Brand chart spec in `../../11_BRAND/BRAND_SYSTEM.md`.  
**Ground:** `--paper` or `--white`. Primary series `--ink`. Comparison `--mark`. Risk `--hold`.  
**Type:** Title Noto Serif. Labels Inter. Figures JetBrains Mono.

## Chart 01 — Autonomy ladder (not a metric)

**Use:** Hero of Method, Post 01 week, Blueprint decks.  
**Type:** Horizontal step diagram (not a bar of “% autonomous”).  
**Title:** Agents earn each level.  
**Subtitle:** Default is L0. Human classes never enter L3/L4 decision rights.

| Y (level) | X label | Fill | Annotation |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | Observe | `--observe` | Shadow file. No live queue. |
| L1 | Recommend | `--recommend` | Evidence pack. Human acts. |
| L2 | Prepare | `--permit` | Park only. |
| L3 | Execute within gates | `--execute` | Config gates. Sampling. |
| L4 | Managed autonomy | `--ink` | Kill-switch live. Still not cash. |

**Caption:** Promotion is per agent × legal entity × invoice slice. Never promote “the stack.”  
**Footnote:** Not a performance chart. A rule.  
**Do not:** turn this into a thermometer that implies the buyer is “behind” if they are at L0.

## Chart 08 — Unit cost identity (formula, no invented numbers)

**Use:** Article 02, CFO appendix.  
**Type:** Formula plate, JetBrains Mono.

```
unit_cost = (inference + tool + human_exception_minutes × loaded_rate)
            / invoices_in_slice
```

**Secondary line:**  
`evidence_completeness = cases_with_complete_packet / cases_closed`

**Title:** Cost is earned per correct outcome, not per touch.  
**Caption:** Use your rates and your counts. The OS does not supply a benchmark.  
**Footnote:** Loaded rate is the buyer’s. If you do not have one, report minutes, not currency.
