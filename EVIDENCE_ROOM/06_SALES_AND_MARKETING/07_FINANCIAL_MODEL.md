## 7. 90-day revenue scenarios (digital + one Blueprint in stretch)

**Conservative**

- Completes 80 × 4% = 3.2 → **3** paid OS  
- Mix: 1 Starter, 2 Professional, 0 Team  
- Derivatives: 40 × $19 ≈ $760 list  
- Blueprint: 0  
- **List digital:** 79 + 398 + 760 = **$1,237**  
- **Est. LS net:** ≈ 75 + 377 + 702 = **$1,154**  
- After support + refund reserve: **about $1,000**

**Base**

- Completes 200 × 8% = 16 paid OS  
- Mix: 4 Starter, 10 Pro, 2 Team  
- Model without upgrade first:  
  - List: 4×79 + 10×199 + 2×499 = 316 + 1,990 + 998 = **$3,304**  
  - + derivatives 120×19 = **$2,280**  
  - **List total digital $5,584**  
  - LS net ≈ 4×75 + 10×189 + 2×474 + 120×18 ≈ 300 + 1,890 + 948 + 2,160 = **$5,298**  
- Blueprint 1 × $2,250 (mid-band) invoice (confirm fees)  
- **Combined top line ~ $7.8k list** — a validation quarter, not a firm.

**Stretch**

- 450 × 14% ≈ 63 paid OS  
- Mix 9 Starter, 38 Pro, 16 Team  
- List OS: 9×79 + 38×199 + 16×499 = 711 + 7,562 + 7,984 = **$16,257**  
- Derivatives 300×19 = **$5,700**  
- Blueprint 2 × $3,000 = **$6,000**  
- **List ~ $28k** before fees  
- LS fee on ~$22k digital ≈ **$1.2k** (5% + $0.50 × units)

These are **scenarios**, not commitments. Do not publish them as traction. At locked launch prices the digital product validates demand; Blueprint and later subscription carry the practice. Do not raise list prices in the store to make a slide look larger.

Lemon Squeezy describes payouts **twice a month** (bank wire / PayPal) on the pricing page retrieved 20 Sep 2026. Model a **14–21 day** cash lag. Do not spend Blueprint deposits until the SOW says they are earned. **Flag for accountant.**
