## Post 18 — Day 18

## Post 20 — Day 20
**Pillar:** Transformation Economics | **Type:** framework

ROI model discipline:

Conservative: Assume 50% of projected efficiency gains
Base: Assume 75%
Upside: Assume 100%

Present all three to your CFO.

Include:
→ Implementation cost (one-time)
→ AI/tool cost (ongoing)
→ Change management cost (often forgotten)
→ Payback period for each scenario

Never present upside as the headline number.
