## 1. Funnel thesis

Finance leaders do not buy “AI magic.” They buy **operating evidence** after a low-risk proof of seriousness.

The funnel qualifies intent with a free diagnostic, educates with a seven-email sequence, converts on Professional as the economic core, expands to Team for mobilisation, and routes complex estates to Custom Blueprint.

```
LinkedIn content / profile CTA
        ↓
Free AP AI Readiness Diagnostic (lead magnet)
        ↓
Email sequence (7) + nurture content
        ↓
Starter US$79  ──or──  Professional US$199  ← primary conversion
        ↓
Team US$499 (workshop / multi-owner need)
        ↓
Custom Blueprint US$1,500–3,000 (fit / multi-entity)
```

## 2. Stage definitions

| Stage | Definition | Success signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Buyer sees a specific AP-agent operating problem named clearly | Profile visit, post engage, site session |
| **Interest** | Buyer requests Diagnostic | Email captured; scorecard delivered |
| **Consideration** | Buyer opens emails / resources; compares vs prompts & vendors | Click to product pages; time on Method |
| **Purchase** | Checkout Starter or Professional | Paid order; download |
| **Expansion** | Needs mobilisation or multi-owner rollout | Team purchase or upgrade inquiry |
| **Services** | Needs entity/ERP/control-specific design | Blueprint SOW booked |

## 3. Channel: LinkedIn (top of funnel)

### Why LinkedIn first
- Buyers (Controller, Head of AP, FD, Shared Services) are reachable and professional.  
- Long-form operating posts outperform generic AI hype in this niche.  
- Carousels and frameworks travel; they match “Operating Evidence” aesthetic.

### LinkedIn → Diagnostic path copy

**Profile headline (example)**  
Evidence Room — Operating evidence for AP agents | Charters · Controls · KPIs · Earned responsibility

**Featured CTA**  
Assess Your AP Agent Readiness — free diagnostic → [link]

**Post CTA variants (rotate; never spammy)**  
- “If you want the scorecard we use internally, it’s free — Assess Your AP Agent Readiness.”  
- “Link in comments: free AP AI Readiness Diagnostic (no software pitch).”  
- “Curious where you sit on process, data, controls, and ownership? Take the diagnostic.”

**Conversion notes**
- Lead with one operating insight; CTA last.  
- Never promise ROI percentages.  
- Pin one diagnostic post monthly.  
- Reply to comments with clarifying questions, not hard sells.  
- Target ~3–5 substantive posts/week (see Content Engine).

### LinkedIn ads (optional later)
Only after organic proof. Creative = framework carousel + Diagnostic CTA. Exclude “autonomous payments” language.

## 4. Free Diagnostic (capture)

**Offer:** AP AI Readiness Diagnostic (scorecard)  
**Price:** Free  
**Gate:** Email (+ optional role, invoice volume band, ERP family)  
**Delivery:** Instant download + Email 1 of sequence  

### Path copy — landing hero

**H1:** Assess Your AP Agent Readiness  
**Deck:** A free diagnostic across process, data, controls, and ownership — so you know what to charter before autonomy expands.  
**CTA:** Get the Free Diagnostic  
**Microcopy:** No autonomous payment software. No ERP replacement. Instant download.

### Form fields (minimal)
1. Work email (required)  
2. Role (optional dropdown)  
3. Approximate invoices/month band (optional)  
4. Primary ERP family (optional)

### Conversion notes
- Keep form ≤4 fields.  
- Show sample scorecard screenshot (branded, non-confidential).  
- State limitations near CTA.  
- GDPR/privacy link adjacent to submit.  
- Tag lead source = `linkedin_organic` | `linkedin_ads` | `site_direct` | `referral`.

### Qualification bands (internal)
| Score band | Meaning | Default next offer |
|---|---|---|
| Low readiness | Foundations missing | Nurture + Method content; soft Starter |
| Medium | Ready for scoped pilot design | **Professional** primary |
| High / complex multi-owner | Needs mobilisation | Team nudge; Blueprint if multi-entity |

## 5. Email middle (consideration)

**Sequence:** 7 emails — see `Email_Sequence/01_SEVEN_EMAIL_SEQUENCE.md`  
**Goal:** Teach the operating model; convert to Starter or Professional without spam.

| Email | Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | Deliver diagnostic + set expectations |
| 2 | Biggest misconception (prompts ≠ OS) |
| 3 | First agent to charter |
| 4 | Earn responsibility (L0–L4) |
| 5 | OS intro (Professional) |
| 6 | Illustrative quantified model (**labelled NON-EVIDENCE / illustrative**) |
| 7 | Team / Custom CTA |

**Conversion notes**
- One CTA per email.  
- Unsubscribe easy and visible.  
- No fake urgency countdowns.  
- Launch offer (if any) stated factually with end date.

## 6. Paid conversion (Starter / Professional)

### Primary SKU: Professional — US$199
Position as the default for Controllers redesigning the agent operating model.

**Checkout path copy**  
“AP Agent OS — Professional — US$199. Instant download. ERP-agnostic. Payment authorisation stays human.”

### Secondary SKU: Starter — US$79
For buyers who want a smaller first kit (one corridor, ~3 agents).

**When to push Starter**
- Solo AP lead, early exploration  
- Budget friction on Professional  
- Diagnostic suggests start narrow  

**When to skip Starter**
- Buyer already clear they need full OS → Professional direct

### Conversion notes
- Side-by-side compare on `/ap-agent-os`  
- Show licence difference plainly  
- Lemon Squeezy checkout (MoR) — see `07_LEMON_SQUEEZY/`  
- Post-purchase: onboarding email + Friday→Monday path pointer  

---

## 7. Expansion: Team — US$499

**Trigger signals**
- Multiple owners / workshop needed  
- Shared Services language in replies  
- “Need to train the team” support tickets  
- Professional buyers who stall on mobilisation  

**Path copy**  
“If Professional gave you the OS, Team equips the room — workshop agenda, training decks, executive brief, change artefacts.”

**Conversion notes**
- Offer upgrade credit policy if commercially desired (optional; document clearly).  
- Do not imply live facilitation is included unless sold separately.

---

## 8. Services: Custom Blueprint — US$1,500–3,000

**Trigger signals**
- Multi-entity / multi-ERP  
- Heavy control constraints  
- Board/Audit deadline with incomplete design  
- Team buyers requesting facilitation  

**Path**  
Intake form → scoped proposal → SOW → delivery milestones  

**Path copy**  
“Productised design service: we fit the AP Agent OS to your entities, ERP constraints, and control environment.”

**Conversion notes**
- Price band stated as range; final quote after intake.  
- Not a full SI implementation.  
- Payment may be invoice or MoR depending on ops choice — document which.

## 9. Metrics & targets (directional — revise with real data)

| Metric | Directional target (first 90 days) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn → Diagnostic CVR | 2–5% of profile/link clicks | Content quality dependent |
| Diagnostic → email open (Email 1) | ≥55% | Deliverability hygiene |
| Sequence → product page CTR | ≥12% cumulative | Across emails 5–7 |
| Product page → purchase | 1.5–4% | Professional primary |
| Professional → Team (90d) | 8–15% of Pro buyers | Expansion |

These are **planning assumptions**, not forecasts or guarantees. Label as NON-EVIDENCE in financial model.
