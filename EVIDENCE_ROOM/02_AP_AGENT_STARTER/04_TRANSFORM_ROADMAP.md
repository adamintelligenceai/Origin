## Decision rubric

| Outcome | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Expand | Evidence standard met; control KPI stable; staff adoption real |
| Revise | Value present but error modes clustered — fix policy/data |
| Stop | Hard-gate breach, or no operational value, or unmanageable noise |
