**House:** Evidence Room  
**Product:** AP Agent OS

## 2. Eight brand-line alternatives — evaluated, then closed

These were written to test the idea. They are **not** campaign options. The lock is final.

| # | Candidate | What it gets right | What it gets wrong | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Proof before permission.** | Order of operations in four words. Sounds like a Controller. Implies a gate, not a gadget. Works on a cover, a stamp, and a board slide. | Slightly abstract until the product name sits next to it. Solved by pairing with “AP Agent OS.” | **LOCK.** This is the brand idea. |
| 2 | Evidence first. Authority second. | Same sequence. Clear. | “Authority” can read as managerial ego, not as earned scope. Weaker as a stamp. | Reject. Keep as an explanatory sentence inside Method if needed. |
| 3 | No proof, no promotion. | Accurate to the autonomy ladder. Memorable. | Sounds punitive. Reads like a slogan for a controls police, not an OS. Too narrow for the house brand (it is only the promotion rule). | Reject as house line. Allowed as a *method* sentence in autonomy materials. |
| 4 | Responsibility is earned, not assigned. | True. Close to the tagline. | Longer. “Not assigned” can insult sponsors who *do* assign owners — we *want* named human owners. Ambiguous. | Reject. The tagline already covers “earn.” |
| 5 | The agent does not decide. The file does. | Sharp. Good for a carousel closer. | Over-states. Humans still decide cash, bank-change, policy, legal. The file *evidences*; it does not replace judgement. | Reject as house line. Usable in Agent 12 / human-class education if qualified. |
| 6 | Control the agent layer, not the invoice. | Correct category. Differentiates from capture vendors. | Sounds like a consulting tagline. “Control” as the first verb is colder than “proof.” | Reject as house line. Usable in competitor-contrast paragraphs. |
| 7 | Design the work. Then let software do it. | Honest about sequence. | Could be any process shop. No Evidence Room specificity. Soft on permission. | Reject. |
| 8 | Govern first. Automate second. | Board-safe. | “Govern” and “automate” are category words every vendor already uses. No proof. No permission. Easy to steal. | Reject as house line. Allowed as a supporting pair in Method. |

**Why #1 wins.** It is a rule, not a mood. It survives a CFO, an auditor, and a tired AP manager. It forbids the two failure modes of this market: agents that act without a file, and programmes that grant L3 in a kickoff deck.

**Closed.** Do not A/B the brand idea on the homepage. A/B CTAs and diagnostic length, not the sentence the company is built on.

## 4. Voice

### Character

Intelligent. Concise. Credible. Executive. Specific. Non-hype.

Write as if a Controller will forward the page to Internal Audit without adding a disclaimer of their own.

### Rules

1. **Prefer nouns that exist in the ledger.** Invoice, packet, slice, ceiling, kill-switch, owner.  
2. **Prefer short sentences.** One idea. Then the next.  
3. **Name the human.** “AP Manager,” “Payments Lead,” “Controller.” Not “stakeholders,” not “the business.”  
4. **Label numbers.** ILLUSTRATIVE in product examples. TARGET only when the buyer set it. Third-party research only from `../09_RESEARCH/RESEARCH_LEDGER.md`, with source and independence.  
5. **Refuse the demo lie.** Full autonomy is not the default. L0 is.  
6. **Complement, do not conquer.** We design the agent layer. We do not replace SAP, Coupa, or the AP team.  
7. **Second person for instruction.** “Appoint one human owner.” First person plural only for the house (“We do not certify compliance.”).  
8. **No exclamation marks** in product or legal. One is allowed in a diagnostic confirmation email, not in the OS.

### Rhythm

- Headline: serif, period at the end if it is a sentence.  
- Subhead: what it is, who it is for, what changes.  
- Body: proof, then permission, then the limit.  
- Close: a verb the buyer can do Monday (assess, appoint, measure, promote).

## 5. Banned words and promises

### Banned promises (commercial and product)

Do not say, imply, or “soft-guarantee”:

- Guaranteed savings  
- Guaranteed fraud detection / “fraud-proof”  
- Regulatory compliance / “SOX-ready” / “audit-passed” as a product outcome  
- Accounting accuracy as a guarantee  
- Autonomous payment safety  
- Guaranteed ROI  
- “Autonomous AP” as a default or destination you have already reached for the buyer  
- “Replace your AP team”  
- “Set and forget”

### Banned aesthetics language

Do not write or art-direct:

- Robots, bot friends, humanoid mascots  
- Neon brains  
- Glowing circuits  
- Purple AI clichés, electric violet gradients, “neural” wallpaper  
- Sparkles, magic wands, “alchemy”  
- Cyberpunk cityscapes  
- Stock photos of glowing server rooms used as a metaphor for judgement

### Banned filler

game-changing · revolutionary · next-generation · cutting-edge · seamless · magical · unlock (as a metaphor) · supercharge · turbocharge · disrupt · leverage (unless it is invoice leverage in an AP sense, which we still avoid) · synergy · world-class · best-in-class · “the future of finance” · “your AI workforce” as a headcount replacement claim · “intelligent automation” as a substitute for a named agent

### Words we use carefully

| Word | Allowed when | Not allowed when |
|---|---|---|
| Autonomous / autonomy | Named level (L3, L4), “managed autonomy,” “autonomy ladder” | “Autonomous AP,” “autonomous payments” |
| Touchless | Buyer-measured rate, or Hackett citation with qualifier | A promised destination |
| Control | Control framework, control owner | “We control your AP” |
| Compliant / compliance | “This document does not certify compliance” | “Become compliant” |
| Savings | Buyer-validated, Controller-signed | Marketing headline |
| Agent | A charter with an owner | A mascot |

## 6. Typography

Licensed / available in this environment and for the site: **Noto Serif**, **Inter**, **JetBrains Mono**. Do not introduce a fourth family without a brand revision.

| Role | Face | Weight | Size guidance | Tracking | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display / covers / brand idea | **Noto Serif** | 500–600 | 40–64 px digital; 36–48 pt print | -0.01 to -0.02 em | Headlines, cover line, pull quotes |
| Section titles | **Noto Serif** | 500 | 28–36 px | -0.01 em | H1/H2 |
| UI, body, navigation | **Inter** | 400 body; 500 labels; 600 buttons | 16–18 px body; 14 px UI | 0 | All running text, forms, FAQs |
| Overlines / product family | **Inter** | 500–600 | 11–13 px | +0.08 to +0.12 em, uppercase | “EVIDENCE ROOM”, “AP AGENT OS” |
| Data, IDs, levels, tables of figures | **JetBrains Mono** | 400–500 | 13–15 px | 0 | L0–L4, `case_id`, KPI IDs, packet fields, prices, hashes (truncated) |
| Legal / footnotes | **Inter** | 400 | 12–13 px | 0 | Disclaimers, source lines |

**Do not** pair a decorative script, do not italicise entire paragraphs, do not set body in serif on long web pages (serif is for display). Product PDFs may set long-form in Noto Serif 11.5–12 pt if the page is paper-like; the website body stays Inter.

**Alignment:** Titles left. Covers may centre the brand idea only if the rest of the page is a quiet field — never a centred wall of text.

**Dark mode:** Not required at launch. If added later, invert to `--ink` ground and `--paper` text; do not invent a neon dark theme.

## 8. Iconography

**System:** Line icons, 1.5 px stroke, square caps, 24 px grid. No filled “AI” orbs.

**Motifs that belong**

- Stamp / seal (permission)  
- Open file with a clip (packet)  
- Gate / stile (autonomy ceiling)  
- Ladder with five rungs (L0–L4)  
- Ledger ruling  
- Hash / short mono ID  
- Kill-switch as a physical switch, not a skull  
- Named seat (simple chair + label)  
- Building / legal entity  
- Envelope as *mail channel*, not as “newsletter fun”

**Motifs that are banned**

- Robots, androids, cute bots  
- Brains, neural nodes, circuit boards  
- Rockets, unicorns, lightning bolts  
- Fingerprint-as-AI  
- Credit-card “ka-ching”  
- Shields that imply guaranteed security

**Agent marks:** Use the agent number in JetBrains Mono (`01`–`16`) inside a 24 px square with a 1 px `--rule` border. Do not invent 16 mascots.

## 12. Callout system

Four callouts. No more.

| Type | Left rule | Label (Inter 11 tracked) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RULE** | `--ink` | RULE | Operating rule the buyer must not skip |
| **EVIDENCE** | `--mark` | EVIDENCE | What must be in the file |
| **HOLD** | `--hold` | HOLD | Human class, kill-switch, legal flag |
| **SOURCE** | `--slate` | SOURCE | Ledger citation |

Do not create a “TIP” or “PRO TIP” callout. If it is not a rule, evidence, hold, or source, it is body text.

**Lawyer flags** in commercial files: a HOLD callout that begins with **Flag for lawyer** or **Flag for tax adviser**. Never styled as a cute badge.

## 13. Photography and motion

Launch: **none required**. Paper, type, tables, and one stamp.

If photography is added later: desks, files, plant floors, goods-receipt bays, people at a table with paper — documentary, daylight, no stock handshake. No glowing laptops used as a metaphor for intelligence.

Motion: 180–220 ms ease, fade and short translate only. No looping “neural” animations.
