# How to use this diagnostic

Complete it with the AP Manager and one process owner in the same room. Allow 60–90 minutes. Guessing alone in a laptop bag produces a vanity score.

You will produce:

1. A readiness score out of 100
2. A maturity stage (0–4)
3. An opportunity heatmap across ten agent families
4. A baseline KPI worksheet
5. A business-case starter
6. A one-page scorecard you can send to a CFO

> DO: Answer against **current evidenced practice**, not the roadmap slide.

> RISK: A high score on ambition and a low score on data access means you are not ready for execution agents.

# Scoring rules

Each question is 0–3.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Absent or unknown. No reliable data. |
| 1 | Informal. Heroics. Tribal knowledge. |
| 2 | Defined. Partially evidenced. Uneven across entities. |
| 3 | Standard. Measured. Owned. Auditable. |

There are 36 questions in six domains (6 × 6 = 36). Maximum 108 raw points, scaled to **100**.

`Readiness = ROUND( raw / 108 × 100 )`

# Maturity model

| Stage | Score | Name | What it means | First move |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0–19 | Unobserved | AP is a queue. Exceptions are folklore. | Baseline KPIs and process walkthroughs |
| 1 | 20–39 | Documented | SOPs exist. Data is patchy. AI is ad hoc. | Exception taxonomy and data access list |
| 2 | 40–59 | Instrumented | Metrics exist. Workflow exists. Agents would have inputs. | Charter one Level 0–1 agent |
| 3 | 60–79 | Governed pilot | One or two agents in shadow or controlled recommend mode | Expand only with evidence packs |
| 4 | 80–100 | Earned operating model | Agents are roles with owners, KPIs, controls, promotion gates | Scale the workforce, not the demos |

# Who should not start agents yet

If **Data & systems** or **Controls** scores below 30% of their domain maximum, stay at Observe. Buy this diagnostic's next action (process mapping), not a copilot rollout.
