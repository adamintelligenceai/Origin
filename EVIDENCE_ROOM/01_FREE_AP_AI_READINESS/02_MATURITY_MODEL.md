# AP Agent Maturity Model

**Evidence Room** · Free tier · Levels 1–5

This model translates diagnostic totals into operating posture. It answers: *What may agents do here, under what human oversight?*

## Level definitions

### Level 1 — Manual & opaque (0–27 / 140)

**Reality:** Spreadsheets and inboxes dominate. Exceptions are tribal. Evidence for approvals is thin.

**Agent posture:** Observation only. No autonomous routing. Use the kit to map processes and KPIs.

**Safe next step:** Document intake channels; define exception catalogue; start baseline KPI worksheet.

### Level 2 — Documented but fragile (28–55 / 140)

**Reality:** SOPs exist but compliance is uneven. Matching and approvals still break under volume spikes.

**Agent posture:** Assisted drafting (summaries, checklists) with 100% human action. No write-backs.

**Safe next step:** Stabilise vendor master and approval matrix; pilot Invoice Intake *read-only* enrichment.

### Level 3 — Controlled operations (56–83 / 140)

**Reality:** Core controls hold. Systems of record are in place. Measurement is partial.

**Agent posture:** Propose actions inside workflow (match suggestions, exception triage) with mandatory human confirm for money movement and master-data change.

**Safe next step:** Pilot Three-Way Match + Exception Triage with explicit SLA for human review.

### Level 4 — Measurable & integrable (84–111 / 140)

**Reality:** KPIs are trusted. Integrations support matching. Audit trails are usable.

**Agent posture:** Bounded autonomy for low-risk classes (e.g., clean PO matches under threshold) with sampled QA and kill-switches.

**Safe next step:** Expand agent catalogue; introduce Payment Readiness *recommendations* (not release); formalise governance board.

### Level 5 — Agent-ready with governance (112–140 / 140)

**Reality:** Evidence packs are routine. Hard gates pass. Leadership accepts agent accountability model.

**Agent posture:** Portfolio of agents with RACI, evidence standards, and continuous control monitoring. Responsibility is *earned* per agent and per invoice class.

**Safe next step:** Custom blueprints; Team workshops; roadmap toward multi-process Finance Agent platform (still AP-first).

## Dimension lens (readiness bands)

Use dimension % bands from the questionnaire:

| Band | Implication for agents |
|---|---|
| Fragile | Fix foundation before any pilot |
| Forming | Read-only / draft-only pilots |
| Capable | Propose-and-confirm pilots |
| Strong | Bounded autonomy for narrow classes |
| Evidence-ready | Scale with governance and sampling |

## Mapping maturity → product

| Level | Recommended Evidence Room product |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Stay on Free kit; optionally Starter for operating model literacy |
| 3 | AP Agent Starter → selective Pro blueprints |
| 4 | AP Agent OS Pro + Custom for edge cases |
| 5 | Team change packs + Custom + platform roadmap |

## What maturity is not

- Not a credit rating
- Not permission to skip controls
- Not a promise that agents will lower cost/invoice toward the Ardent Partners 2025 average of **$9.84**
- Not a substitute for audit opinion
