## Definitions (agree before measuring)

| KPI | Definition | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per invoice | Fully loaded AP processing cost ÷ invoices processed in period | Currency / invoice |
| Exception rate | Invoices requiring manual intervention after initial capture ÷ invoices | % |
| STP / touchless rate | Invoices processed without human touch after intake ÷ invoices | % |
| Cycle time | Timestamp receipt → payment-ready (or paid, if you choose — pick one) | Days |
| First-pass match rate | Invoices matching PO/GR without correction ÷ PO invoices | % |
| Duplicate suspect rate | Invoices flagged as potential duplicates ÷ invoices | % |
| On-time payment rate | Invoices paid by due date (or discount date if elected) ÷ invoices | % |
| Ageing > N days | Open invoices older than policy threshold ÷ open invoices | % |
