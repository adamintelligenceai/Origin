# AP AI Readiness Kit — Start Here

**Evidence Room** · Free tier · *Agents that earn responsibility.*

This kit helps accounts payable leaders assess whether their process, data, controls, technology, people, and measurement practices can support agents that earn responsibility — not agents that improvise.

## Who this is for

- AP managers and shared-service leads
- Controllers and finance transformation sponsors
- Process excellence / automation teams partnering with AP
- Consultants preparing a structured readiness conversation

## What you will produce

1. A scored diagnostic across six dimensions
2. A maturity level (1–5) with plain-language meaning
3. An opportunity heatmap for where agent work is plausible
4. A baseline KPI sheet grounded in *your* data
5. A business-case starter for leadership discussion
6. A one-page scorecard you can share

## Ground rules

- Score honestly. Inflated scores produce unsafe pilots.
- Use your own invoice volumes and cycle times. Industry benchmarks (Ardent Partners 2025: **$9.84** cost/invoice, **18.4%** exception rate, **35.4%** STP) are reference points only — not your results.
- Do not claim guaranteed savings, fraud prevention, or ROI in any artefact you produce from this kit.
- Agents that cannot show evidence should not receive responsibility.
