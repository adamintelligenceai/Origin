## The ten steps

1. **Observe** — Watch real work; refuse slides-as-truth.  
2. **Transcribe** — Capture as-is steps and exceptions.  
3. **Extract** — Separate rules, judgements, and artefacts.  
4. **Structure** — Map corridors and handoffs.  
5. **Agentise** — Assign work to chartered agents with exclusions.  
6. **Test** — Scenario and failure tests offline.  
7. **Shadow** — Agent recommends; humans decide; score disagreement.  
8. **Controlled Pilot** — Narrow corridor; written caps; rollback ready.  
9. **Measure** — KPI pack with definitions; blanks allowed.  
10. **Expand responsibility** — L-level changes as controlled events only.
