# Method

**Route:** `/method`

## Headline

How agents earn responsibility.

## The ladder

1. **Diagnose** — Process, Data, Controls, Technology, People, Measurement  
2. **Charter** — Purpose, bounds, evidence, kill-switch  
3. **Stage** — Observe → Draft → Propose → Bounded act  
4. **Sample** — QA on a schedule, not a vibe  
5. **Expand or stop** — Charter board decides with artefacts  

## Hard gates

Money-path and master-data agents do not advance if segregation of duties, vendor bank dual control, audit trails, or vendor payment-field quality fail.

## Evidence standard

Every material agent action leaves a trail: inputs referenced, rule/model version, decision, human gate, timestamp.

## Why institutional tone

AP sits on cash, suppliers, and audit. Method language stays precise. No carnival AI vocabulary.
