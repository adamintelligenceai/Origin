## 3. Agentic AP (reframed)

**H2:** Agentic AP is an operating problem first  

Agents can draft, classify, reconcile, and recommend. That does not make payment autonomy safe — or desirable as a default.

Evidence Room treats agentic AP as **workforce design with controls**: jobs, exclusions, escalations, measurement, and gates.

Keep your AP automation and ERP. Add the operating layer they don’t productise.

## 6. Responsibility

**H2:** Responsibility is earned  

| Level | Name | Default posture |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | Observe | Watch and log |
| L1 | Recommend | Suggest; human decides |
| L2 | Prepare | Draft; human releases |
| L3 | Execute within guardrails | Only inside written limits |
| L4 | Managed autonomy | Earned only |

**Hard rule:** Payment authorisation stays human. Full autonomy is never the default.
