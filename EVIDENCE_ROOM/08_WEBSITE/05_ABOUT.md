# About Evidence Room

**Route:** `/about`

## Brand

**Evidence Room**  
*Agents that earn responsibility.*

## Why we exist

Finance teams are offered agents without operating models. We publish the missing layer: diagnostics, charters, blueprints, and evidence standards for accounts payable first.

## What we believe

- Accountability stays human  
- Responsibility is earned per agent and invoice class  
- Controls are design inputs, not afterthoughts  
- Industry benchmarks contextualise — they do not sell  

## Aesthetic

Institutional finance: ink `#0B1F2A`, paper `#F7F4EE`, amber `#C47A2C`, teal `#1F5C5C`. Calm surfaces. No neon AI chrome.

## Roadmap posture

AP first. Then adjacent finance processes as the platform matures — without diluting evidence discipline.
