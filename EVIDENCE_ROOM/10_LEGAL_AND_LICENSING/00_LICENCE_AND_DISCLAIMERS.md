# Licence tiers

| Tier | Who | What they may do | What they may not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual (Starter default) | One named person | Use for their employer’s internal AP work or their own learning | Share the file tree as a team library; resell |
| Professional (Pro default) | One named person | Use in professional/commercial work (employee or independent consultant) to advise a client *in live workshops they facilitate* | Leave the source files with the client as the client’s product; photocopy as a coursepack for sale |
| Team | One named legal entity / one defined AP or transformation team | Internal copies for employees of that entity within stated seat guidance (illustrative: up to 25 internal users) | Use across a whole consultancy’s client base; publish; sublicense |
| Evaluation (Free) | Downloader | Personal evaluation | Production redistribution |

# Prohibited (all tiers)

- Resale, sublicensing, or republishing templates as the purchaser’s product  
- Training a competing commercial product on the materials where enforceable  
- Removing Evidence Room attribution from customer-facing slides sold onward  
- Using the materials to claim Evidence Room certified an environment

# Refund (recommendation)

14 days if unused. No refund after a Team workshop is delivered or Blueprint work has started. Confirm consumer-law treatment for EU/UK digital content.

# Privacy language

Evidence Room’s digital products are downloads. Do not send production invoices, bank details, or employee personal data to Evidence Room unless a later service agreement says otherwise. The free diagnostic is completed locally.

# AI disclosure

Use of third-party AI tools with these templates is the customer’s choice and risk. Invoice text must be treated as untrusted data.

# IP-cleanliness checklist (before each release)

- [ ] No employer or client names, screenshots, or process maps  
- [ ] No confidential volumes or vendor performance data  
- [ ] No copied consulting slides or copyrighted frameworks reproduced beyond fair citation  
- [ ] Statistics have ledger IDs  
- [ ] Vendor accuracy claims labelled vendor-stated or removed  
- [ ] Examples are fictional (A. Rahman, Entity A/B, $50k caps)  
- [ ] Licence and disclaimer embedded in PDFs  
- [ ] No implication of partnership with SAP, Oracle, Microsoft, Ardent, NIST, COSO, Lemon Squeezy  
- [ ] Trademarks of third parties used only for identification  

# Employment safeguard

Generic professional knowledge may inform structure. No current or former employer confidential information is included. If a future contributor has a restrictive agreement, they do not add artefacts until counsel reviews.
