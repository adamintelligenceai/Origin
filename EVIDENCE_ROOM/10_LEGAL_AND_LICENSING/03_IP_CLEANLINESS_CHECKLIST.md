# IP Cleanliness Checklist — Evidence Room

**Purpose:** Prevent copyright, trademark, trade-secret, and licence contamination in all Evidence Room materials before publish or sale.  
**Owner:** Product lead + author of each pack.  
**Cadence:** Run before every SKU release and after any research refresh.

---

## 1. Original work

- [ ] Frameworks, taxonomies, agent role cards, and diagrams were **created for Evidence Room** (not pasted from employer-confidential decks).  
- [ ] No ex-employer or client confidential playbooks reused.  
- [ ] Contributors confirm they have rights to submit the content (contractor IP assignment on file — **FLAG legal**).  

---

## 2. Analyst & research figures

- [ ] Every statistic appears in `09_RESEARCH/01_RESEARCH_LEDGER.md`.  
- [ ] Ardent 2024 / 2025 figures are **paraphrased** with citation — **no** photographed or OCR’d report tables pasted as images for redistribution.  
- [ ] No substantial verbatim excerpt from Ardent, Forrester, or Grand View Research reports.  
- [ ] Vendor hosting noted (Medius-hosted / Bottomline-hosted) where relevant to independence.  
- [ ] Forrester blog points used **qualitatively** only.  
- [ ] Grand View / market-size claims omitted or heavily caveated.  

---

## 3. Competitor materials

- [ ] No competitor logos in product files without written permission.  
- [ ] No screenshots of Tipalti, Coupa, SAP, etc. UI copied into packs.  
- [ ] Nominative fair use only for names in comparisons (“works beside Coupa”).  
- [ ] No scraped help-center text, training PDFs, or proprietary prompt libraries.  
- [ ] Competitive claims match `09_RESEARCH/02_COMPETITOR_LANDSCAPE.md` (gap = governance layer).  

---

## 4. Third-party licences (fonts, photos, icons, code)

- [ ] Fonts: Fraunces / Source Serif / IBM Plex — licences allow embedding in PDF/web as used; attribution if required.  
- [ ] Stock photos: licence covers commercial digital product use; no “editorial only.”  
- [ ] Icons: MIT/Apache/CC with terms met — or original.  
- [ ] No unlicensed Canva/PPT theme laundering of third-party IP.  
- [ ] Code snippets (if any) licence-compatible; no copyleft contamination of proprietary packs without review.  

---

## 5. Trademarks & branding

- [ ] “Evidence Room” and product names checked for obvious conflicts in launch markets (**FLAG trademark search**).  
- [ ] No impersonation of SAP, Oracle, Microsoft, Lemon Squeezy, etc.  
- [ ] Disclaimer of affiliation present where competitor lists appear.  

---

## 6. People, privacy, and data in examples

- [ ] Examples use **synthetic** vendor names, invoice IDs, and emails.  
- [ ] No real personal data, live IBAN/account numbers, or customer invoices.  
- [ ] No faces of real non-consenting individuals in marketing.  

---

## 7. AI-assisted authoring hygiene

- [ ] Authors did not paste confidential third-party docs into public AI tools to “rewrite into the product.”  
- [ ] AI-generated text reviewed for hallucinated citations — only ledger sources remain.  
- [ ] No generated “quotes” attributed to Ardent/Forrester that are not real.  

---

## 8. Licence & notice hygiene in files

- [ ] Copyright notice on covers.  
- [ ] Licence tier stated (Individual / Professional / Team).  
- [ ] Disclaimers included (`02_DISCLAIMERS.md` short form).  
- [ ] Do not strip notices when exporting PDF/PPTX/XLSX.  

---

## 9. Store & marketing

- [ ] Lemon Squeezy listing copy does not use competitor trademarks in SEO spam patterns.  
- [ ] Claims match verbal identity bans (no guaranteed savings/fraud/compliance).  
- [ ] Images in LS and site pass the same logo/screenshot rules.  

---

## 10. Incident response (if contamination found)

1. Quarantine the SKU (unpublish if live).  
2. Document the source and scope.  
3. Replace with original content.  
4. Counsel on notice obligations if distributed.  
5. Re-run this checklist before republish.
