## 1. Plain-English core disclaimer (paste-ready)

Evidence Room products provide **educational and operational templates** for designing, governing, and measuring AI agents that assist Accounts Payable work. They are **not** legal, tax, audit, accounting, investment, or compliance advice. They are **not** a substitute for your ERP, AP automation platform, payment provider, or professional advisors.

**AI agents can be wrong.** They may hallucinate, misclassify, miss fraud signals, or propose unsafe actions. **Humans remain accountable** for approvals, postings, payments, supplier master changes, and representations to auditors and regulators.

We **do not guarantee** any reduction in cost per invoice, cycle time, exception rate, fraud losses, compliance findings, or ROI. Published industry benchmarks (including Ardent Partners *State of ePayables* figures) are **third-party survey averages for context**, not predictions of your results and not Evidence Room performance claims. Worksheets labeled **illustrative** are models for you to populate with your own data — not promises.

- Ardent Partners averages (e.g., 2024 cost/invoice **$9.40**; 2025 **$9.84**; BiC vs All Others gaps) describe **survey populations**, not your company and not our product outcomes.  
- Best-in-Class figures are **Ardent’s cohort construction**, not a service level we sell.

## 4. No guaranteed fraud detection or prevention

Checklists, red-flag prompts, and anomaly-review aids are **decision support only**. They:

- Do **not** detect all fraud, social engineering, or duplicate payments;  
- Do **not** replace bank-confirmation controls, call-backs, or SoD;  
- Do **not** create a warranty against loss.

Forrester and other analysts may discuss vendor **agentic** capabilities for fraud detection as **market direction** — that is not a quantified guarantee for Evidence Room or for any vendor.

## 6. No guaranteed ROI

ROI and business-case pages are planning tools. They omit many real-world costs (change management, licences, errors, exceptions). **Do not** use them as investment advice or as a basis for financial reporting.

## 7. Human accountability (non-negotiable)

For every agent in production use, you should be able to name:

1. **Human owner** (role + name)  
2. **Allowed stage** (Observe / Recommend / Draft / Execute narrow / Expand)  
3. **Evidence retained**  
4. **Demotion trigger**  

If you cannot, do not promote the agent. Evidence Room’s methodology assumes this discipline; we cannot perform it for you via a digital download.
