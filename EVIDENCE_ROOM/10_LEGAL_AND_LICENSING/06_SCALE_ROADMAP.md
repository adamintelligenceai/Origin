# Evidence Room — Scale Roadmap

**Date:** 20 September 2026  
**Rule:** **Do not build other verticals now.** Accounts Payable until the AP OS is earned.  
**Brand idea remains:** Proof before permission.

This is a company sequence, not a customer promise and not a date-stamped delivery contract. Do not put “Q3 AR Agent OS” on the website.

---

## The only path

```
AP digital product
    → AP services (Custom Blueprint)
        → AP subscription (versioned OS + seats)
            → Finance Agent platform (still governance-first; AP remains the proof)
```

Each arrow requires **evidence** that the previous layer works: refund rate, repeatable fulfilment, Blueprint utilisation, and buyers who can show *their* promotion files — not our invented ROI.

---

## Stage 1 — AP digital product (now)

**Object:** Starter, Professional, Team, Diagnostic, derivatives.  
**Channel:** evidenceroom.ai + Lemon Squeezy.  
**Evidence you are ready to leave this stage:**

- Professional fulfilment is boring (broken-download rate near zero)  
- Licence questions have standard answers  
- Refund rate is a fitness signal, not a revolt  
- Counsel has accepted terms, privacy, refund  
- Domain and marks are registered or actively filed  

**You do not need:** a hosted app, an ERP connector, or an AR pack.

**Forbidden at this stage:** “Finance OS” as a current SKU; payroll; expense; FP&A agents.

---

## Stage 2 — AP services (Custom Blueprint)

**Object:** Draw the OS onto one buyer’s entities, channels, DoA. Floor US$1,500–3,000.  
**Evidence to enter:** a few Professional/Team buyers who ask for mapping, not for magic.

**Still AP.** A Blueprint that “just starts with expenses because it’s easier” is a vertical sneak. Refuse.

**Still not software.** You may sit with Finance Systems. You do not become their SI of record unless a separate, later decision — and even then, AP only.

**Evidence you may consider Stage 3:**

- Two or more Blueprints completed without inventing a new agent 17 to dodge Agent 12  
- A written refusal log (you turned down autonomous-payments work)  
- Delivery hours priced with margin, not heroics  

---

## Stage 3 — AP subscription

**Object:** Versioned updates to the OS + named seats + a simple cadence of change notes. Monthly or annual. Still documents-first. Maybe a private changelog and a seat admin page. **Not** a workflow engine.

**Why this exists:** Teams already pay $499 and then ask “what changed in autonomy policy?” Subscription is how you stop emailing zips from a laptop.

**Evidence to enter:**

- A real VERSION_HISTORY buyers read  
- Demand for v1.1 that is not just typo-fix  
- A lawyer-reviewed subscription agreement (auto-renew, cancellation, seat true-ups)  

**Lemon Squeezy** can do subscriptions; confirm fees (still model 5% + 50¢ until terms change) and MoR on recurring digital goods. **Tax adviser.**

**Do not** subscribe people to “autonomous AP insights” as a newsletter upsell. The sequence stays opt-in and finite unless they ask for more.

---

## Stage 4 — Finance Agent platform

**Object (later):** A platform that hosts **charters, ceilings, evidence packets, and promotion files** — the operating system as software. Agents may *connect*; the platform’s job is still permission and proof.

**This stage is not:**

- an invoice OCR product  
- a payment company  
- an RPA recorder  
- AR, treasury, close, or payroll “modules” launched to complete a suite  

**AP remains the reference implementation.** If the platform cannot show an AP slice at L0 with a kill-switch, it does not launch.

**Other verticals** (AR, expense, procurement intake, close) are **allowed to be designed only after**:

1. The AP digital product is clean.  
2. Blueprint work has not corrupted the public OS with client IP.  
3. Subscription exists or is deliberately skipped with a written reason.  
4. A new brand-line decision (the house may stay Evidence Room; the *product* may become Finance Agent OS).  
5. The same bans: no guaranteed savings, fraud, compliance, accuracy, or cash autonomy.

Until those five are true, the answer to “can you do AR?” is **no**.

## What we will not do to “scale faster”

| Temptation | Why we refuse |
|---|---|
| Prompt marketplace dump of the 16 skeletons | Cannibalises the OS; trains the wrong buyer |
| White-label for a capture vendor | We become a feature. Permission gets lost. |
| Per-invoice pricing | We are not their processor |
| Guarantee Hackett-like touchless rates | Software-adopter research is not our result |
| Open five verticals in a winter | Unearned permission |
| Raise L3 in the marketing site because competitors say “autonomous” | Brand breach |

---

## Capital and attention

Spend, in order: domain and counsel → store test purchases → diagnostic quality → Professional onboarding → one Blueprint done honestly.

Do not spend on a platform rewrite while START_HERE is still the untested path.

---

## Review questions (every 90 days)

1. Are we still AP?  
2. Did we invent a statistic?  
3. Did we grant a promise we banned?  
4. Can a new buyer run Monday from the zip?  
5. What evidence would justify the *next* stage — not the next idea?

If (1)–(4) fail, stay on Stage 1.

Proof before permission. Agents earn responsibility. Evidence decides.
