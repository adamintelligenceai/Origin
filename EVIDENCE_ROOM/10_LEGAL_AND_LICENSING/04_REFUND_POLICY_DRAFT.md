## Recommended commercial position

### Digital products (Starter US$79 · Professional US$199 · Team US$499)

- **14-day** refund request window from purchase.  
- Refunds processed via the merchant of record (e.g. Lemon Squeezy) where used.  
- We may decline refunds where there is evidence of abuse (e.g. file redistribution, chargeback fraud patterns, bulk account farming).  
- Partial downloads do not automatically void rights that law requires — **counsel to localise.**

### Free Diagnostic

No payment — no refund.  

### Custom Blueprint (US$1,500–3,000)

- Governed by SOW.  
- **Recommendation:** Deposit non-refundable after kickoff; unused future milestones may be cancellable per SOW.  
- Digital-product 14-day policy does **not** auto-apply to services.
