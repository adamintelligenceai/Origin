# Evidence Room — AI-Use Disclosure

**Version:** 1.0 — 20 September 2026  
**Status:** Public-facing disclosure. Not a security certificate.  
**Related:** Privacy language, disclaimers, governance (prompt injection / hallucination) in the OS.

We say this in plain language because buyers, auditors, and counsel will ask. We do not hide the tools. We also do not claim that using a model made the OS “intelligent” in a way that replaces human accountability.

---

## 1. How Evidence Room uses AI

Evidence Room uses AI writing and editing tools to **draft, restructure, and proof** operating materials, commercial copy, and internal checklists.

**Humans** decide:

- the brand idea and the banned promises  
- the autonomy model (L0 default; four human classes)  
- what is a charter versus a prompt  
- which research may be cited  
- whether a file ships  

A model does not hold Accountable on any Evidence Room file. A model does not set prices, licences, or refunds.

---

## 2. What we do not do with AI

- We do not generate **legal opinions** and present them as counsel. Drafts are flagged for a lawyer.  
- We do not generate **tax or MoR conclusions**. Lemon Squeezy / Stripe facts are taken from public pages and flagged.  
- We do not invent **customer results**, ROI, or fraud-catch rates.  
- We do not scrape a vendor’s help centre or a former employer’s SOP into the library.  
- We do not use **your diagnostic answers** or **your support mail** to train a shared public model.  
- We do not ask you for production invoices so we can “fine-tune.”

---

## 3. What you should assume when you buy

The files are **designed artefacts**. They may have been drafted with model assistance. They are not a live agent you have purchased. You still:

- appoint human owners  
- map fields to your ERP  
- pin *your* model IDs  
- keep payment release human  
- treat invoice text as untrusted  

If you paste AP Agent OS into a consumer chatbot, you may **publish** our licensed text and you may **expose** anything you paste alongside it. That is a licence and privacy problem. Use sanctioned tools. Do not put supplier personal data into unsanctioned UIs.

---

## 4. AI in *your* implementation (not us)

When you implement the OS, you will likely use model vendors, OCR vendors, and perhaps an RPA platform. Those are **your** processors. Our governance files tell you to:

- keep an approved-tool list  
- default to no training on customer data unless Controller + Privacy sign  
- pin model IDs and treat swaps as releases  
- fail closed on writes  

That is your programme. We are not a party to your model contract.
