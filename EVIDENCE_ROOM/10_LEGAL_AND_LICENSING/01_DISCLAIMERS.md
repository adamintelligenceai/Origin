## Long form

### 1. Nature of the product

Evidence Room publishes **AP Agent OS** and related materials (diagnostics, derivatives, Custom Blueprint deliverables as specified). They are **information and templates for operating design**. They are not:

- hosted software, an ERP, a payment rail, or a capture product  
- a professional engagement unless a signed statement of work says so  
- a certification, audit opinion, SOC report, or ISO certificate  
- legal, tax, accounting, investment, or regulatory advice

You remain responsible for your books, payments, vendors, employment duties, and regulators.

### 2. Banned outcomes (we do not promise these)

We do **not** warrant, represent, or imply:

| Promise | Why it is refused |
|---|---|
| Guaranteed savings or cost reduction | Your ledger decides. APQC’s spread is not our result. |
| Guaranteed ROI | Deloitte (8 Oct 2025) reports many teams deploy AI without clear measurable ROI. We will not invent yours. |
| Guaranteed fraud detection | Agent 10 flags patterns. That is not a forensic or legal fraud finding. |
| Regulatory compliance | We give control *design*. We do not make you compliant with any statute or framework. |
| Accounting accuracy | Models hallucinate. Validators and humans remain yours. |
| Autonomous payment safety | Payment release stays human. We do not authorise funds. |
| “Autonomous AP” as a delivered state | Default is L0 Observe. |

If a salesperson, affiliate, or marketplace listing says otherwise, **that sentence is unauthorised**.

### 3. Examples and numbers

- “ACME” and similar stories are **fictional and illustrative**.  
- Figures labelled **ILLUSTRATIVE** are not benchmarks.  
- Figures labelled **TARGET** are the buyer’s until measured.  
- KPI definitions are methods, not promised results.

Model vendors change models, retain data, or fail. You pin IDs, approve tools, and run fallback. We are not the model vendor.

Services are scoped in a statement of work. Unless that SOW *expressly* says so, Blueprint work inherits these disclaimers. We may refuse work whose success criterion is autonomous payment release.
