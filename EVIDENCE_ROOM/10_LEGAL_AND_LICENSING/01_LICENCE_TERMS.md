## Purpose

Plain-English licence summary for Evidence Room digital products. Final enforceable terms must be drafted/approved by qualified counsel and aligned with Lemon Squeezy (or other MoR) checkout terms.

---

## Shared rules (all paid digital tiers)

1. **Internal use** for designing and operating your organisation’s AP agent model.  
2. **No resale** of the files as a product, course, template pack, or competing toolkit.  
3. **No public redistribution** of source files (public Git repos, marketplace listings, “free download” mirrors).  
4. **Adaptation allowed** for internal AP design (fill-in, rename agents, map to your ERP fields).  
5. **Attribution** optional in internal decks; do not remove copyright notices from redistributed excerpts shared with advisors under NDA.  
6. **No trademark licence** beyond nominative reference to Evidence Room as the source of the toolkit.  
7. **Payment systems:** Licence does not grant rights to build or market autonomous payment products using our brand.

## Individual / Starter (US$79)

**Who:** One named individual purchaser.  
**May:** Use for their employer’s internal AP design work; share limited filled artefacts with internal colleagues for that work.  
**May not:** Transfer the master toolkit to a second company; use as a consultant’s reusable client deliverable library without a multi-client licence; train a public course from the files.

---

## Professional (US$199)

**Who:** Named purchaser plus limited internal collaborators as stated on the checkout / licence certificate (counsel to set a numeric cap if desired — e.g. up to 5 internal collaborators).  
**May:** Deploy artefacts across one organisation’s AP / finance transformation workstreams.  
**May not:** Seat unlimited contractors as distributors; republish the OS; include files in a paid community without written permission.

---

## Team (US$499)

**Who:** One organisation or named business unit within seat/entity caps (counsel to define — e.g. one GBS node / one legal entity group).  
**May:** Use in internal workshops; distribute internally to participants of those workshops.  
**May not:** Sell workshops that redistribute Evidence Room source files as attendee takeaways beyond licence caps; franchise the curriculum as a third-party product.

---

## Custom Blueprint (US$1,500–3,000)

Governed by **Statement of Work**.  
Typical pattern (to be confirmed by counsel): Client owns deliverables produced specifically for them; Evidence Room retains background IP (methods, templates, frameworks). Client may use deliverables internally; resale of background IP not granted.

## Free Diagnostic

Personal evaluation use. Do not repackage as a paid scorecard product.

Breach of no-resale / redistribution rules may terminate the licence. Surviving clauses per counsel draft.
