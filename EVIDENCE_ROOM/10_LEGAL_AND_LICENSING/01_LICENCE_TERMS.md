## 1. Individual / Starter Licence

**Applies to:** AP Agent Starter (and any SKU labeled Individual / Starter).

### Grant
We grant you a **non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable** licence to download and use the Materials for:

- Your **personal** professional use; **or**
- Use within **one** legal entity, by **one** named primary user,

to design and evaluate governed AP agents for that entity’s internal operations.

### Seat / sharing
- **One primary user.** Reasonable viewing by a direct manager or Internal Audit reviewer for evaluation is allowed; systematic multi-user sharing is not.
- Do **not** upload Materials to a company-wide wiki, shared drive for a team, or LMS as a shared playbook — that requires **Team**.

### Allowed
- Customize templates with your company data for internal use.  
- Use prompts with your approved AI tools under your employer’s policies.  
- Print/PDF for internal workshops you facilitate as the licensee.

### Not allowed
- Resell, republish, or white-label the Materials.  
- Share login/download links publicly or with unpaid third parties.  
- Use Materials to deliver paid consulting deliverables to **other** companies as if you authored a competing product (see Professional carve-note below).  
- Remove copyright, licence, or disclaimer notices.

### Term
Perpetual for the version purchased, subject to termination for breach. Updates, if any, may be separate purchases unless stated at checkout.

## 2. Professional / Pro Licence

**Applies to:** AP Agent OS Pro (Professional).

### Grant
Same baseline as Individual, expanded to:

- Use by **one primary practitioner** (e.g., AP Manager, Controller, transformation lead) inside **one** legal entity (and its controlled affiliates **only if** affiliates are under the same AP operating model **and** counsel confirms affiliate language — **FLAG**).  
- Produce **internal** steering decks, policies, and Evidence Room artifacts derived from the Materials for that entity.

### Consulting carve-out (narrow)
If you are an independent consultant:

- You may use Pro to advise **one named client at a time** only if you purchased Pro **in your consulting entity’s name** and you **do not** leave the full Materials library with the client as a redistributable pack.  
- Deliverables you create for the client must be **your work product**, not a wholesale copy of Evidence Room files.  
- Client-wide rollout / multi-seat shared library → client must buy **Team** (or Custom).  
**FLAG FOR LAWYER:** refine consulting use; consider a separate Consultant licence if channel matters.

### Not allowed (in addition to Individual)
- Multi-team shared services standardization using one Pro seat.  
- Training courses selling Evidence Room content as the curriculum pack.
