# Trust & Safety Positioning

**Document ID:** `10_LEGAL_AND_LICENSING/05_TRUST_AND_SAFETY_POSITIONING`  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Audience:** Marketing, support, website, sales  

---

## 1. Trust thesis

Evidence Room earns trust by **refusing dangerous claims** and by making control artefacts the product — not vibes.

---

## 2. Public non-negotiables

| Topic | Position |
|---|---|
| Payments | Human authorisation only |
| Autonomy | Earned L0–L4; never default full autonomy |
| Outcomes | No guaranteed ROI / fraud catch rates |
| Category | Operating toolkit — not payment software |
| Evidence | Sources for market claims; illustrative labels otherwise |
| Advice | Not legal/tax/audit advice |

---

## 3. Safety language bank (approved)

- “Payment authorisation stays human.”  
- “Agents earn responsibility.”  
- “Shadow before execute.”  
- “Exclusions are the control.”  
- “Blank KPIs beat invented KPIs.”  

## 4. Banned safety-adjacent hype

- “Autonomous AP payments”  
- “Set and forget invoice paying”  
- “Guaranteed fraud-proof”  
- “Compliant out of the box”  
- “Replace your Controller”  

---

## 5. Support posture

When buyers ask for payment automation:
1. Restate boundary.  
2. Point to Payment Proposal Review as human-gated.  
3. Offer Blueprint if they need control design — not a bot that pays.

When buyers paste confidential data into tickets:
1. Ask them to redact.  
2. Do not request live ERP credentials for digital SKUs.

---

## 6. Incident narrative (if a customer misuses agents)

Evidence Room provides design artefacts; customer configures production systems. Misuse (e.g. removing human payment gates) is outside intended use. Support should document guidance given and restate licence/disclaimer.

---

## 7. Merchant of record

Using Lemon Squeezy (or similar MoR) can improve checkout trust via tax handling and payment security — **not advice; verify fit with advisors.** Disclose MoR on checkout pages.

---

## 8. Domain & brand

State preferred domain evidenceroom.ai with **availability verification pending** until secured. Avoid shipping creatives that assert ownership of an unregistered domain.
