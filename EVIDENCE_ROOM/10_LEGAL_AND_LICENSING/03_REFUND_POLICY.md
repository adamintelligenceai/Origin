# Evidence Room — Refund Policy

**Version:** 0.9 draft — 20 September 2026  
**Status:** Commercial recommendation. **Not legal advice.**  
**Flag for lawyer:** EU/UK consumer cancellation (14 days), digital-content exceptions (if performance began with consent), US state laws, chargeback codes, Lemon Squeezy’s own refund rules, VAT reversal.  
**Store sentence:** If the OS is not the instrument you expected, email licence@evidenceroom.ai within 14 days. We refund Starter and Professional purchases that meet this policy. This is not a performance guarantee.

---

## 1. Principle

Refunds are for **fit of the instrument** — you received the wrong kind of thing, the file would not open, or the listing materially mis-described the contents.

Refunds are **not** for:

- failure to achieve savings, ROI, touchless rate, or a promotion to L3  
- disagreement with L0 as the default  
- needing a connector we never sold  
- “AI didn’t do AP for me”  
- change of mind after using the files as a client deliverable

We refuse a “365-day results guarantee.” That would be a banned promise.

## 3. How to ask

Email **licence@evidenceroom.ai** with:

1. Order ID  
2. Product name  
3. Reason in one paragraph (fit, file, listing)  
4. Attestation: “I will delete local copies of the templates. I have not used them as a client deliverable or to train a public model.”

We do **not** ask for invoice PDFs or ERP screenshots.

Target decision: five business days. Processor timing after that is not ours.

---

## 4. What we do when we grant

1. Instruct Lemon Squeezy (or successor) to refund.  
2. End the licence.  
3. Ask you to delete templates. You may keep *your* completed notes that do not reproduce our files. **Flag for lawyer.**  
4. Invalidate the key in our register.  
5. Do not automatically add you to a new marketing sequence.

If LS retains a fee on refund, we absorb it unless fraud is involved. Model a residual cost (see financial model). **Confirm with LS terms.**

## 6. Chargebacks

If you open a chargeback without emailing us, we will provide the processor with: listing copy (including “not software” and disclaimer), download logs, licence attestation, this policy.

We may refuse future sales after a chargeback we consider abusive. **Flag for lawyer.**

---

## 7. Consumer law override

If you are a consumer in a jurisdiction that grants a cancellation right we cannot exclude, **that law wins**. Digital-content rules may require a consent statement before download if we want to waive the right. **Counsel must draft the checkout tick-box.** Until then, prefer granting the 14-day fitness refund rather than fighting a consumer.

B2B purchases (Professional / Team on a company card) may be treated differently — **counsel**.

---

## 8. Affiliates and launch codes

Refunds reverse affiliate commission where the platform allows. LAUNCH20 purchases refund at the amount paid, not $199.

## 9. Language we will not use

- “100% satisfaction guaranteed”  
- “Guaranteed to save your AP team 60%”  
- “All sales final” as the only sentence (too sharp; may be illegal)  
- Fake limited refunds that expire in 2 hours
