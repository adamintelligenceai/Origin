## 1. Who we are

Evidence Room publishes AP Agent OS and related digital products at evidenceroom.ai.

**Contact for privacy requests:** privacy@evidenceroom.ai  
**Legal:** legal@evidenceroom.ai

Company legal name, address, and establishment will be inserted by counsel. The `.ai` domain is **not** a statement that we are established in Anguilla.

---

## 2. What this product does *not* need

We sell an operating-design zip and a readiness score. We do **not** need, and you **must not send us**:

- supplier or employee invoice PDFs  
- bank account or IBAN data  
- government ID numbers  
- production ERP extracts  
- special-category personal data

If you paste those into a support mail, we will ask you to delete the thread and not use them as evidence of purchase.

## 8. Your requests

Email privacy@evidenceroom.ai from the address we hold. We will not require an invoice PDF as identity.

You may ask (where the law gives you the right): access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent. You may complain to a supervisory authority — counsel will name the lead authority when establishment is known.

**Licence vs deletion.** If we delete your email, we may keep a hashed record of the licence key and order ID for anti-fraud and tax. **Flag for lawyer.**
