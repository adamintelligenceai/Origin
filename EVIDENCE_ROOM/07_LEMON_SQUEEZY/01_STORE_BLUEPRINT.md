## 3. Fees math (Lemon Squeezy)

### Base fee (sourced)
- **5% + $0.50** platform fee per transaction  
- **Merchant of Record** (LS)  
- **+1.5%** international  
- **+1.5%** PayPal  
- **+0.5%** subscriptions  
- **Marketing add-ons** extra if enabled  

Source: Lemon Squeezy Pricing + Fees help docs. Re-verify live pages at launch.

## 7. Refunds policy (commercial draft — CONFIRM)

**Proposed stance for one-time digital downloads:**

1. **Full refund** if file not delivered / corrupt and not remedied within 48 hours.  
2. **Refund window** (e.g., 14 days) if buyer has **not** substantially downloaded — align to LS + consumer law.  
3. **No refund** after download when description matches delivery (licence regret, “too strategic,” “wanted OCR software”) — state clearly on listing.  
4. **Chargebacks:** respond with delivery logs; **CONFIRM** LS process.  
5. **Team:** seat-count mistakes — offer upgrade/downgrade path rather than full refund when possible.

Place short refund summary on each product page; full policy on site.

## 8. Merchant of Record notes

Lemon Squeezy as **MoR** typically means:

- Buyer pays Lemon Squeezy.  
- LS handles aspects of sales tax/VAT collection as described in their docs.  
- You receive net payouts per LS schedule.  

**You still must:**
- Licence the IP correctly.  
- Honour consumer/business rights that apply.  
- Avoid prohibited product claims.  
- Keep privacy/terms accurate about who processes payment data.  

**CONFIRM with counsel + LS:** entity onboarding, payout country, prohibited geographies, services vs digital goods for Custom Blueprint.
