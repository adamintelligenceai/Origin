## 15. VAT / GST (high-level — not advice)

Lemon Squeezy’s MoR pitch is that they collect and remit certain digital-goods taxes in certain jurisdictions. Coverage, thresholds, and invoices depend on **their current terms**, Stripe Managed Payments, and where Evidence Room is established.

**You must confirm with a tax adviser:**

- Whether your products are treated as electronically supplied services  
- Whether the customer sees tax-inclusive or exclusive prices  
- What appears on the receipt (seller name: LS vs Evidence Room)  
- Reverse charge / B2B VAT for EU/UK entities  
- GST/NZ/AU/SG treatment  
- Whether Blueprint invoicing sits outside MoR  
- Sales tax if you have nexus independent of MoR  
- What happens if Stripe Managed Payments replaces LS tax engine

**Do not tell customers “tax is handled”** until an adviser says you may.

## 16. Merchant of Record implications (high-level)

If LS is MoR for a sale:

- They may be the *seller of record* on the receipt.  
- Chargebacks and certain consumer rights may run through them.  
- Your licence still governs *use* of the files.  
- Privacy: they are a processor / independent controller depending on counsel’s view — **flag**.  
- If they remitted VAT, your books still need an accountant.

If Stripe Managed Payments changes who is merchant, **re-write this section**.
