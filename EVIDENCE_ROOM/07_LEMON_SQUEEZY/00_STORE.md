# Why Lemon Squeezy first

Merchant of Record: LS calculates and remits many sales taxes / VAT and runs checkout, licence keys, and file delivery. Confirm with a tax professional how this interacts with *your* entity. This is not tax advice.

# Fees (vendor docs, accessed September 2026)

- 5% + $0.50 on **order total including tax**
- +1.5% international (non-US)
- +1.5% PayPal
- +0.5% subscriptions (if you add them later)
- +5% abandoned-cart recovery
- +3% affiliate (merchant side)
- Payout: US bank free; non-US bank 1%; PayPal fees as documented
- Official example: $20 product + 20% VAT = $24 → fee $2.06 → net $17.94

Source: https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees

# Upsells

Starter checkout: “Add Professional for $120 more” (total $199). Professional: “Team +$300”. No dark patterns. Blueprint is an application, not a forced upsell.

# Launch offer

First 100 Professional: $149 (25% off) with code `ROOM149`, 21 days. Do not discount Team below $399 or you train the wrong buyer.

# Affiliates

Enable LS affiliates at 15% for practitioners (consultants) only after licence language forbids resale of files. +3% LS fee. Sensible later, not day one.

# VAT/GST / MoR

LS as MoR typically collects and remits in many jurisdictions. Still confirm: your corporate income tax, permanent establishment, consumer law (refunds, EU digital content), and whether Team sales to businesses need extra terms. **Professional confirmation required.**

# Refund recommendation (not legal advice)

14 days if the buyer has not substantially used the materials. After a Team workshop is run, no refund. State this on the product page.
