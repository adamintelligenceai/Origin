Team Edition expands facilitation: `../../04_AP_AGENT_OS_TEAM/Workshop/`.

## 1. What to do

Use the right meeting type. Pre-read the pack. End with a decision enum and a storage path.

**How.** Scripts below. Timeboxes are **ILLUSTRATIVE**.

**Who.** Named chair. Named decision-maker (must be the Accountable for that decision, not “the room”).

**Wrong.** Vendor demo as the only agenda item. ROI promised in the room. Agent treated as Accountable.

**Control.** Decision page filed. Attendance + conflicts (SoD) noted.

**Measure.** Meetings without an artefact; decisions without an owner.

**Evidence.** `[BUYER]/Evidence/Meetings/[ID]/`.

---

## 2. Common rules (every meeting)

1. Four families of metrics stay separate if numbers are shown.  
2. Activity is not a win.  
3. Unsigned financials are not spoken as fact.  
4. “Let’s just turn it on” is out of order; cite the gate.  
5. Kill-switch and human classes are never parked as AOB.

## 3. Meeting types

### M1 — Mobilise (Phase 0) — 60 min

**Purpose.** Name A/R, first slice, store, tools.  
**Chair.** Transformation. **Decide.** Head of AP + sponsor.  
**Agenda:** sponsor intent (5); slice allow-list (20); owners (15); privacy/tools (10); evidence store (5); decisions (5).  
**Outputs.** Mobilise pack; RA draft owner.  
**Wrong.** Slice = “all AP.”

### M2 — Observation readout — 45 min

**Purpose.** Accept as-done vs as-written.  
**Chair.** PE / Transformation. **Decide.** Process owner.  
**Agenda:** coverage (10); unofficial rules (20); money-adjacent map (10); sign or gap list (5).  
**Outputs.** Signed coverage or extend Observe.  
**Wrong.** Jump to target-state map.

### M3 — Charter challenge — 45 min

**Purpose.** Accept or reject a charter.  
**Chair.** Control owner. **Decide.** Head of AP.  
**Agenda:** exclusions (10); L0–L4 table (10); tools/SoD (10); 90-day subset (5); checklist (10).  
**Outputs.** Charter version or Redesign.  
**Wrong.** L3 because the use case is “simple.”

### M4 — Historical go/no-go — 45 min

**Script:** `../Testing/01_HISTORICAL_TEST_SCRIPTS.md` Script G.  
**Decide.** Head of AP; Control veto on risk-control.

### M5 — Shadow daily huddle — 15 min

**Chair.** AP Manager.  
**Agenda:** identity clean; material disagrees; do-not-wait reminder.  
**Outputs.** Daily sheet sign.  
**Wrong.** Instructing staff to follow the agent.

### M6 — Pilot mid-point — 30 min

**Purpose.** Continue / tighten / stop — **not expand**.  
**Decide.** Same as Pilot A.  
**Agenda:** mix; overrides; incidents; hashes; verbs outside list.  
**Wrong.** Adding suppliers.

### M7 — Measure / increment — 60 min

**Purpose.** One increment or refuse.  
**Chair.** Head of AP. **Cash lines.** Controller present or pack has no $ claims.  
**Agenda:** four families (25); residuals (15); increment proposal (10); decision (10).  
**Outputs.** Measure pack decision.  
**Wrong.** Netting a breach against cycle time.

### M8 — Incident standup — 15–30 min

**Use.** `../Governance/03_INCIDENT_AND_OVERRIDE.md`.  
**Chair.** AP Manager. **Contain first.**  
**Wrong.** Close as training while identity still live.

### M9 — Weekly agent performance — 30 min

**Pack.** `../KPI_and_Measurement/03_WEEKLY_AGENT_REPORT.md`.  
**Chair.** AP Manager. **A.** Head of AP.  
**Wrong.** Vendor dashboard without dictionary IDs.

### M10 — Steering (sponsor) — 45 min

**Use Team Edition** `../../04_AP_AGENT_OS_TEAM/Executive/01_STEERING_COMMITTEE.md`.  
**Professional minimum:** Conservative business case if funding; Measure if live; no Upside headline.
