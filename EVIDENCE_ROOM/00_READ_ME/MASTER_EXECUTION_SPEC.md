## Executive Mandate

Build and ship a **premium, implementation-ready digital product ecosystem** that enables Finance leaders to design, govern, and scale AI agents across Accounts Payable — without replacing ERP, AP automation, or payment systems.

**Positioning statement (final):**  
> *Evidence Room is the operating system for building, governing, and scaling AI agents across Accounts Payable. Agents earn responsibility through demonstrated performance — not hype.*

**Brand idea (selected):**  
**Evidence over experimentation.** Finance AI that earns responsibility through measurable operating performance.

## Quality Gate (Ship Criteria)

Every artifact must score **≥9/10** on:

1. **Content** — Useful to an experienced CFO  
2. **Practicality** — Usable Monday after Friday purchase  
3. **Specificity** — Solves real AP problems by name  
4. **Design** — Premium institutional aesthetic  
5. **Credibility** — Evidence-based claims only  
6. **Differentiation** — Clearly not a prompt bundle  
7. **Coherence** — Single operating system  
8. **Usability** — Clear navigation and start path  
9. **Commercial value** — Materially exceeds price paid  

**Commercial tests:**
- Would a Finance Director processing 15,000 invoices/month pay $199 from their own budget?
- Could a Transformation leader run a credible workshop tomorrow with Team Edition?
- Does positioning clearly communicate "agent operating system" vs "prompt pack"?

## Agent Responsibility Model (Canonical)

| Level | Name | Capability |
|-------|------|------------|
| 0 | Observe | Review only; no recommendations acted upon |
| 1 | Recommend | Produce recommendations for humans |
| 2 | Prepare | Prepare actions; human approval required |
| 3 | Execute within guardrails | Pre-approved low-risk execution |
| 4 | Managed autonomy | Independent within boundaries; exception oversight |

**Principle:** Responsibility is earned through performance evidence. Full autonomy is never the default.

## Critical Product Principle

Every page must answer at least one of:

- What should I do?
- How should I do it?
- Who owns it?
- What can go wrong?
- How do I control it?
- How do I measure it?
- What evidence proves it works?
