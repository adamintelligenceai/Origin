## 2. Unit economics — Lemon Squeezy baseline

### Assumptions for digital SKUs (illustrative)
- Platform fee = **5% × order total + US$0.50**  
- Order total = list price **before** customer-facing tax for *seller net sketches below* OR include tax if you sell tax-inclusive — **confirm with LS settings + advisor**  
- Sketch below uses **list price as fee base** for simplicity (conservative if fees also apply to tax portion — LS docs show fee on total including tax in examples)  
- Ignore payout fees, chargebacks, refunds in base case; treat as sensitivity  
- No COGS beyond payment fees (digital)  
- Blueprint may be invoiced outside LS — model both paths

### Net proceeds per digital sale (baseline fee only)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Team | 499 | 24.95 | 0.50 | 25.45 | **473.55** |

### Blueprint (midpoint US$2,250)
If processed on LS with baseline fee only: fee ≈ 5% × 2250 + 0.50 = **113.00** → net ≈ **2,137**.  
If invoiced outside MoR: payment fees differ; tax/admin burden may rise — **advisor required**.

**Refunds:** A refund reverses revenue; platform fee treatment per LS policy at the time — verify. Model **5–10%** refund rate sensitivity on digital.

## 9. Decision rules

1. Do not discount Professional below US$199 as a habit — use rare launch windows.  
2. Do not put full OS on cheap marketplaces (cannibalisation).  
3. Revisit fees against live Lemon Squeezy docs each quarter.  
4. Engage a tax professional before treating MoR as “no tax work left.”
