# Evidence Room design system

**Wordmark.** EVIDENCE ROOM — letterspaced small caps, bronze on paper or paper on ink.

**Product mark.** AP AGENT OS sits under the wordmark in Inter, never as a cute logomark creature.

**Logo direction.** A thin bronze horizontal rule, not an icon. If an emblem is required later: a rectangular “room” frame with a single bronze baseline (the evidence line). No robots, brains, sparks, or neural nets.

**Colour.** Ink `#121614` · Paper `#F3EFE6` · Bone `#E6DFD2` · Bronze `#8C7349` · Slate `#4A524C` · Rule `#C9C2B4` · Signal `#7A2E24` · Ledger `#2C5A4A`.

**Type.** Display Noto Serif. Text Inter. Data JetBrains Mono.

**Charts.** Bronze for Evidence Room series, ink for baseline, slate for comparators. No 3D. No rainbow. Cite under the chart.

**Tables.** Ink header, paper/bone zebra, bronze underline on header.

**Callouts.** Bone panel, 2.2mm accent bar: bronze note, ledger action, signal risk, ink ownership.

**Covers.** Ink or paper field, bronze rule, serif title, mono product code, no photography at launch.

**Product-family architecture.** Same cover grid; tier expressed as a mono code (FREE / ST / PRO / TEAM / CUS) and a single bronze weight change — not a new colour per SKU.
