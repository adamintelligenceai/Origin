# EVIDENCE ROOM — Refined Master Execution Prompt (v1.0)

## Mission (one sentence)
Ship a complete, sellable **Evidence Room — AP Agent OS** digital-product ecosystem that a Finance Director can buy on Friday and use to redesign their AP agent operating model on Monday — without software, without hype, and without replacing their ERP.

## Non-negotiable product identity
**Evidence Room** is the operating system for designing, governing, measuring, and scaling AI agents across Accounts Payable.

It is **not**: a ChatGPT prompt pack, AI ebook, consulting jargon deck, screenshot gallery, autonomous payment system, accounting system, or ERP replacement.

It **is**: an implementation-ready toolkit for CFOs, Controllers, Heads of AP / Shared Services, and Finance Transformation leaders in organisations with complex AP (200–10,000+ employees; hundreds to tens of thousands of invoices/month; ERP-backed; control- and audit-sensitive).

## Commercial north star
1. Immediate digital-product revenue at near-zero marginal cost.
2. Progressive product ladder that qualifies demand for custom services.
3. Brand authority in Finance AI before any SaaS build.
4. AP-only now; architecture extensible later to AR, Close, Treasury, Controls, FP&A.

## Hard constraints (reject any artifact that violates these)
1. **Human accountability first.** Agents earn responsibility (L0→L4). Full autonomy is never default.
2. **Payment authorisation stays human.** No autonomous payment positioning.
3. **No invented statistics.** Every market claim goes in the Research Ledger with source, date, URL, independence flag.
4. **No employer/client IP.** IP-cleanliness checklist must pass before release.
5. **ERP-agnostic.** Useful across Dynamics, SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Workday and peers — never dependent on one.
6. **Every page answers ≥1 operating question:** What / How / Who owns / What can go wrong / How to control / How to measure / What evidence proves it.
7. **Quality bar:** CFO-useful, tomorrow-usable, specific, premium, evidence-based, differentiated, coherent, navigable, commercially over-delivered. Nothing ships below 9/10 on any gate.
8. **Voice:** intelligent, concise, credible, executive, specific, non-hype. Never promise guaranteed savings, fraud detection, compliance, accounting accuracy, autonomous payment safety, or ROI.

## Differentiation thesis (must be unmistakable)
Vendors sell AP stack features. Consultancies sell projects. Prompt marketplaces sell prompts.
**Evidence Room sells the agent operating layer** — charters, controls, KPIs, exception taxonomies, responsibility progression, and implementation method — that sits across any ERP/AP automation estate.
