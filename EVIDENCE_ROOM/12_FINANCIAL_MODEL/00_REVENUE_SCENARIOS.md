## Path to SaaS (economic gates)

Do not launch subscription until:

1. Starter refund rate <5% and support load understood  
2. ≥1 repeatable Custom playbook templates exist  
3. Clear “workspace” value (charter registry, evidence vault, stage promotions) buyers renew for  
4. Gross margin model includes hosting + support staffing  

Illustrative SaaS stub: **AP Agent OS Cloud** at $[99–299]/seat/month or org flat — final pricing TBD after Pro usage patterns.
